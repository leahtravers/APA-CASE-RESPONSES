# APA Researcher Inventory Agent Contract V54

Status: ACTIVE SUCCESSOR FOR CALIBRATION
Contract version: `RI-CONTRACT-V54`
Predecessor: `RI-CONTRACT-V53`
Effective date: 2026-09-16
Authority: Leah's standing Researcher Inventory calibration instruction, permitting durable-contract revision only when preserved failures demonstrate a generalizable requirement.

## Mission

Produce only a literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate from the supplied source. Inventory source-presented research coordinates and lightweight compounds. Do not interpret psychological meaning, score APA, infer protected threads, draw conclusions, promote records, mint APA IDs, or admit anything to sovereign or production fabric.

The worker never receives approved archetypes, gold outputs, expected answers, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, sealed holdout source during calibration, or holdout outputs.

## 1. Source-coordinate admission without lexical census

Read the entire source before answering the requested class. A unit belongs when the source presents a distinct reusable research coordinate of that class at a stable source-native grain. Local, background, nested, remembered, reported, descriptive, prospective, questioned, negated, hypothetical, figurative, and embedded material may belong.

Coverage does not mean turning every clause, complement, prepositional phrase, auxiliary, discourse marker, relation argument, or syntactically separable phrase into its own coordinate. A phrase is not independently selectable merely because grammar can isolate it.

Coverage also does not permit deleting a source-distinguished coordinate merely because it is nested inside, temporally co-present with, spatially related to, or grammatically dependent on another coordinate. A nested or unnamed span may still be independently selectable when it performs its own class-native research function.

Use this test before admission: after preserving literal wording and posture, would a researcher reasonably return to this span/frame as a distinct coordinate of THIS class? If yes, retain it even when it participates inside a larger event or relation. If no, leave it inside the larger source-native coordinate. Grammatical dependence is evidence to inspect, not an automatic reason to merge.

## 2. Exhaustive decomposition before relation-integrity collapse

The smallest complete source-native grain is semantic, not token-minimal, and exhaustive inventory precedes deduplication.

First scan every source clause, predicate, relation, referent, characterization, orientation, scene, and temporal frame for independent class-native eligibility. Only after that coverage pass may relation-internal fragments be collapsed.

Preserve one lexical relation as one VERB when matrix, infinitival, participial, particle, complement, negation, modal, reflexive, or coordinated wording together expresses one source-presented relation identity. Do not split one relation into syntactic subpredicates merely because each word could be parsed as a verb.

But do not use relation integrity to swallow a second source-presented relation. A nested, infinitival, coordinated, reported, or embedded predicate is independently retained when it carries its own reusable relation job, participant binding, posture, direction, or research function. Matrix versus embedded syntax alone neither forces a split nor forces a merge.

Likewise, an argument, complement, orientation phrase, state/value, scene, or temporal frame may become OBJECT/LABEL/LOCATOR/PLACE/TIME when it independently satisfies that class's coordinate function, even if it is unnamed or participates in a retained relation. Reject only material that has no independent class-native identity after context is restored.

## 3. PLACE

Retain distinct physical scenes or occurrence positions that can independently host represented material. Named and supported unnamed scenes may qualify; broad and contained scenes may coexist when genuinely distinct.

A movement endpoint, object-position phrase, contained location, origin/destination phrase, or orientation relation is not automatically PLACE. It does qualify when the source establishes a distinct physical scene or occurrence position that a researcher could revisit independently. Do not reject a supported scene solely because its wording is relational rather than a proper place name.

For supported unnamed PLACE, `source_wording` may be null and `source_cue` must be exact source text.

## 4. TIME

Retain distinct episodes, phases, periods, waits, transitions, recurring spans, remembered periods, present reflection, and supported prospective/future frames.

Relations inside one represented episode may share one TIME, but episode membership does not automatically collapse every source-distinguished temporal frame. Retain a separate TIME when the source establishes a distinct before/after, recurring, waiting, remembered, current, prospective, transition, duration, or other reusable temporal role. Do not mint a new TIME merely because a new action or predicate occurs; do not suppress one merely because it is unnamed or nested in a broader episode.

For supported unnamed TIME, `source_wording` may be null and `source_cue` must be exact source text.

## 5. PERSON

Retain the speaker and every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group, including relational, remembered, reported, prospective, and represented addressee roles. Resolve aliases, titles, kinship terms, pronouns, and coreference before deduplication. Speaker canonical key is `B`. Generic grammatical `you` is not PERSON unless a represented actor/addressee exists.

## 6. OBJECT

Retain distinct source-treated concrete or abstract referents that can be revisited as things/content: physical things, choices, decisions, relations treated as things, sets/categories, amounts, services/results, internal/figurative objects, comparison referents, and source-distinguished wholes/parts.

Do not create OBJECT for every clause content, complement, pronoun/deictic, proposition wrapper, event nominalization, relation argument, or fragment. However, do not suppress content merely because it also serves as a complement or argument: retain it when the source independently treats that content as a stable reusable referent. Prefer the source's stable referent rather than duplicate wrappers around it.

When a noun-shaped phrase functions primarily as characterization/state/value, use LABEL instead.

## 7. LABEL

Retain source-presented characterizations, states, values, classifications, evaluations, comparisons, identity/self-labels, relational statuses, corrections, polarity responses, and question-labels when the label/value itself is independently selectable.

Do not turn ordinary predicate negation, interrogative scaffolding, duration wording, discourse framing, relation wording, or every adjective/adverb into LABEL. But retain a nested or predicate-linked state/value when the source presents that value as an independently reusable characterization. Preserve the complete source value rather than a smaller fragment when the smaller fragment loses the characterization's identity or posture.

## 8. VERB

Retain every materially distinct lexical relation at the shortest COMPLETE source-native grain. Relation integrity outranks token minimalism; exhaustive relation coverage outranks syntactic convenience.

Keep together words that jointly identify one relation, including particles, required objects/complements, infinitival material, negation/modal material, serial material, or coordinated material when the source treats them as one relation increment.

Split when the source presents independently revisitable relation jobs, including nested, reported, infinitival, coordinated, or embedded predicates with their own semantic role. Do not merge two relations merely because one grammatically depends on the other, and do not split one relation merely because it contains multiple verb-shaped tokens.

Reporting, perception, cognition, stance, possession, questioning, saying/telling, finding, waiting, locative/copying, and figurative relations may belong when independently relation-bearing. A state/value belongs in LABEL when that is its primary function.

## 9. LOCATOR

Retain independently useful orientation relations for position, path, direction, origin/destination, entry/exit, proximity, containment, carrying/accompaniment, movement, recurrence context, internal orientation, or figurative/relational orientation.

Do not inventory every prepositional argument, beneficiary phrase, content complement, value, or locative-looking phrase. A locator must itself supply a reusable orientation relation. A locator may coexist with a PLACE when the same source material genuinely establishes both an orientation relation and an independent physical scene; overlap is not forbidden merely because the functions are related.

## 10. Cross-class type resolution

Classify by source function, not surface grammar:
- represented social actor -> PERSON;
- thing/content/referent -> OBJECT;
- characterization/state/value -> LABEL;
- lexical relation -> VERB;
- orientation relation -> LOCATOR;
- independently reusable physical scene/position -> PLACE;
- independently reusable episode/period/frame -> TIME.

Cross-class overlap is allowed only when the same wording genuinely performs two distinct class-native functions. Do not create overlap merely to maximize coverage, and do not prohibit overlap merely because two functions share wording.

## 11. Coverage, grain, type, excess

For every class run four passes in this order:
1. COVERAGE LEDGER: traverse the complete source and identify every plausible independent class-native coordinate before collapsing anything. Include nested and unnamed candidates when source-grounded.
2. RELATION/GRAIN: collapse only true syntactic fragments that lack an independent class-native job after context is restored. Preserve source-distinguished predicates, roles, frames, scenes, values, and referents even when they participate in a larger relation.
3. TYPE: resolve each retained coordinate by source function and preserve genuine cross-class functions without type inflation.
4. DEDUP/EXCESS: remove aliases, true same-coordinate restatements, pure function fragments, analyst-created wrappers, and duplicate decompositions. Do not remove a coordinate solely because it is a child, complement, argument, unnamed frame, or participant in another retained relation.

Do not use proposition-centrality as the admission test. Do not use grammatical separability as the admission test. Do not use grammatical dependence as the exclusion test.

## 12. Literal and posture lock

Every non-null `source_wording`, every `source_cue`, and every non-null `order_cue` must be a character-for-character contiguous source substring. Preserve source spelling, punctuation, dialect, questions, negation, uncertainty, correction, comparison, attribution, intention, hypothetical, recurrence, report, and prospective posture. Explicit short tags use only source words. Supported unnamed PLACE/TIME may use neutral navigation tags grounded by exact source cues. Never substitute synonyms or normalize dialect.

## 13. Ordering and qualities

Order by first source establishment after coreference, subject to apparatus speaker-first PERSON and broad-before-contained PLACE/LOCATOR only when genuinely established together.

`qualities_available` is boolean only. It is true only when qualities/descriptions are available for that coordinate or compound. Question, negation, uncertainty, intensity, or presence of a LABEL does not automatically make it true.

## 14. Compound reconstruction

Freeze primitive units before compounds. Scan source relations in order. Emit one smallest COMPLETE compound per distinct source-presented relation instance, using the frozen coordinates actually co-bound in that relation.

Compound construction does not replace primitive inventory. If a source relation contains several independently retained relations or coordinates, preserve those primitives first, then construct the compound(s) that represent their proven source binding. Do not collapse distinct primitive relations into one broad sentence bag merely to avoid overlap.

Do not emit nested subset alternatives for the same single relation, duplicate decompositions, every pair, combinatorial closure, or broad sentence bags. Matrix/reporting and embedded/content compounds may both survive when they are genuinely separate source relations. If one retained VERB already preserves one complete relation identity, do not create extra compounds merely by splitting that verb's internal syntax.

## 15. Calibration isolation

Return only the JSON required by the request schema. Never infer or reconstruct archetypes, expected counts, evaluator preferences, prior scored outputs, case-specific gold corrections, sealed holdout source during calibration, or holdout output.

## 16. Prohibited work

Never perform APA scoring, psychological interpretation, protected-thread analysis, conclusions, promotion, APA-ID creation, Oval Office research writing, sovereign admission, or database/fabric admission.

## 17. Successor effect

V54 prospectively supersedes V53 only for new Researcher Inventory calibration/certification work beginning 2026-09-16. V53 and all prior contracts and runs remain immutable historical evidence.

V54 retains V53's protection against syntactic over-fragmentation but corrects V53's demonstrated opposite failure: broad suppression of source-distinguished independent coordinates when they were nested, unnamed, grammatically dependent, temporally co-present, or participants inside a larger relation. The same under-inventory pattern appeared across both approved archetype classes in preserved V53 calibration output, making this a generalizable correction rather than a case-specific gold patch.

Immutable archetype hashes, hidden evaluation, worker/gold isolation, candidate-only status, exact-source literal lock, repeated paired-archetype gate, sealed Case 5 one-shot semantics, fresh-agent clean-room certification, and all promotion/APA-ID/database prohibitions remain unchanged.
