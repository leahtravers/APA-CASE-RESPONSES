The paired hidden-archetype diagnostic then failed semantically across both cases, the full attempt was preserved, no independent harness defect was established, and the sealed holdout was not reached.

V125's generalizable defect was not insufficient coverage alone. Its provisional admission surface remained too lexical: it kept many spans because they carried represented meaning even when they did not need independent coordinate identity in a distinct source-local binding, while source-local where/when support and some role-bearing nuclei were still missed because frame reconstruction came too late.

V126 therefore uses this controlling order:

> **FRAME FIRST, EDITOR COORDINATES SECOND. RECONSTRUCT DISTINCT SOURCE-LOCAL BINDINGS AND THE WHERE/WHEN SUPPORTS NEEDED TO KEEP THEM APART. THEN ADMIT ONLY THE SMALLEST SOURCE-NATIVE OR LAWFULLY RECONSTRUCTED COORDINATES THAT OCCUPY A DISTINCT ROLE IN AT LEAST ONE BINDING OR SUPPORT DISTINCTION.**

No archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, scored prior outputs, canonical gold extracts, case-specific corrections/examples, sealed holdout material, or indirect encoding of hidden answers are supplied to the worker.

## Mission

Produce a literal, lightweight Researcher Inventory of the complete supplied source in exactly seven primitive classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Then build selective compounds only from frozen registered primitives.

The inventory is a researcher-facing coordinate system at the source's natural editorial grain. It is neither a sparse summary nor a lexical census.

## 1. Read the whole source and resolve source-local frames

Read the complete source before emitting any class. Resolve obvious coreference and distinguish source-local represented frames/configurations.

A new local frame may be established when the source changes one or more of:

- participating actor/endpoints;
- operative action/state/relation;
- target/content;
- represented posture such as direct event, remembered/reported material, prospect, question, correction, comparison, uncertainty, or present reflection;
- where-support;
- when-support.

Do not output this internal frame map or private reasoning. Its purpose is only to prevent unrelated local material from collapsing together and to make support reconstruction precede final primitive admission.

A frame is not automatically one sentence, paragraph, clause, or discourse turn. Split only where the represented binding changes materially.

## 2. Reconstruct PLACE/TIME support before final primitive admission

For each source-local frame determine what spatial and temporal support is needed to keep its represented bindings correctly situated.

Create an explicit or unnamed `PLACE` or `TIME` coordinate only when it performs a genuine support job.

### Unnamed support gate

Create unnamed support when all are true:

1. the source differentiates a represented where-frame or when-frame from another represented frame;
2. at least one binding would otherwise be mislocated, conflated, or lose a source-established distinction;
3. no explicit source-native PLACE/TIME primitive supplies that support adequately;
4. an exact source cue anchors the support; and
5. the support can be described mechanically without inventing where or when.

Use null `source_wording`, an exact source cue, and a neutral mechanical short tag.

A local, one-use, remembered, reported, prospective, or present-reflection support may qualify.

### Explicit wording is evidence, not entitlement

A physical noun, destination phrase, spatial image, time adverb, duration phrase, event clause, question, or discourse segment is not automatically PLACE or TIME. It is PLACE/TIME only when its represented job is the spatial/temporal support of one or more bindings. Otherwise type it by its actual role or omit it.

## 3. Primitive admission gate: distinct binding role

After the internal frame/support map is stable, consider explicit source-native nuclei for primitive admission.

Admit a primitive only when it occupies at least one distinct role in a source-local binding:

- human/social participant or endpoint;
- independently handled thing/content endpoint;
- target-bearing characterization/state/status/evaluation;
- operative action/state/relation kernel;
- orientation/path/containment/direction/context relation;
- spatial support;
- temporal support.

**Represented meaning alone is not enough. Mention alone is not enough. Grammatical independence alone is not enough.**

Use this removal test:

> If this candidate disappeared as an independent coordinate, would a distinct source-established participant, endpoint/content handle, state/evaluation, relation, orientation, or support distinction in at least one binding be lost?

If no, omit it.

If uncertain, do not preserve by default. Re-run the removal test against the source-local binding.

Minor, ordinary, one-use, colloquial, idiomatic, uncertain, negated, remembered, reported, prospective, figurative, or peripheral wording may still qualify when it passes this role test.

## 4. Exact source nucleus and editorial grain

For every explicit primitive choose the smallest complete exact source-native unit that performs the admitted role without changing that role.

Keep together what functions together, including particles, reflexives, required complements, negation, modality, comparison, idiom, and orientation constructions when splitting would change identity.

Do not inflate a primitive into a whole proposition, rhetorical shell, or question when a smaller exact nucleus carries the role. Do not shrink a natural phrase until its relation, target, polarity, orientation, or idiomatic meaning changes.

Every non-null `source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be character-for-character source text.

Never normalize, polish, translate, diagnose, euphemize, substitute synonyms, regularize grammar, or lemmatize into a different surface form.

## 5. Type by binding role, not surface shape

Assign one primary class by the job the coordinate performs in the represented binding:

- spatial support -> `PLACE`
- temporal period/support -> `TIME`
- represented human/social actor or stable group -> `PERSON`
- independently represented thing/content handle -> `OBJECT`
- target-bearing characterization/state/status/evaluation -> `LABEL`
- operative action/state/relation kernel -> `VERB`
- orientation/path/containment/direction/context relation -> `LOCATOR`

Grammar is evidence, not authority. Concreteness is evidence, not authority. Spatial or temporal imagery is evidence, not authority.

A noun-shaped phrase may be LABEL or OBJECT rather than PLACE. A spatially worded phrase may be OBJECT/content or LOCATOR rather than PLACE. A verbal phrase may be OBJECT/content or LABEL. A common copular, speech, asking, positional, perceptual, or cognitive relation may be VERB when it actually organizes a selected binding.

Cross-class duplication is allowed only when the source genuinely establishes two distinct coordinate jobs, not merely because one phrase can be grammatically described two ways.

## 6. Class rules

### PLACE

Return only explicit or lawfully reconstructed spatial supports that answer where a represented binding is situated. Broad and contained supports may both qualify when the source differentiates them. Local and unnamed supports may qualify. Do not turn scenic physical nouns, destinations, comparisons, orientation phrases, or spatial images into PLACE unless they perform the support role.

### TIME

Return only explicit or lawfully reconstructed temporal supports that answer which differentiated represented period a binding occupies. Local event periods, remembered/reported periods, prospective periods, and source-established present reflection may qualify. Do not turn event clauses, actions, questions, discourse segments, durations, or time adverbs into TIME unless they perform the support role.

### PERSON

Return the speaker plus each distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when the actor is a participant, endpoint, possessor/beneficiary with independent role, remembered/reported actor, institutional actor, prospective actor, peripheral actor, or one-use actor in at least one binding. Reject generic/rhetorical/nonreferential person wording.

### OBJECT

Return concrete or abstract thing/content handles that function as independently represented endpoints/nodes in at least one binding. This can include source-distinguished parts, values, services/results, choices/decisions treated as things, internal content, named sets/categories, remembered/reported content, or figurative/quoted content when the source handles it as content.

A physical detail is not admitted merely because it is visible in the scene. It qualifies when it is separately acted on, checked, located, compared, possessed, moved, referred to, reasoned about, or otherwise used as an independent endpoint. Reject scenic furniture, generic noun phrases, bare pronouns/deictics, and nominalized actions with no independent endpoint/content job.

### LABEL

Return exact target-bearing characterization/state/status/evaluation coordinates. A LABEL may be one-use, uncertain, questioned, negated, corrected, rejected, comparative, colloquial, idiomatic, or figurative.

Use the smallest complete source phrase that preserves the actual state, target, polarity, comparison, or evaluation. Do not wrap the label in an entire proposition when a shorter exact nucleus carries the predication. Distinct source-local evaluation/polarity turns may remain distinct when each is independently predicated.

Reject decorative description, generic manner, detached intensity, and wording with no target-bearing state/evaluation role.

### VERB

Return exact operative action/state/relation kernels that organize at least one admitted binding. Use the smallest complete natural predicate nucleus and preserve required particles, complements, reflexives, negation, modality, comparison, or idiomatic structure when needed for identity.

Common, grammatically light, copular, positional, speech, asking, perceptual, cognitive, possessive, or relational wording may qualify when it actually links selected coordinates in a distinct binding.

Reject auxiliaries/support morphology with no distinct relation, discourse-management shells, true duplicate restatements, whole-clause wrappers, and lexical verbs whose removal would not erase a selected binding relation.

### LOCATOR

Return exact source-native orientation/context relations that independently position or orient coordinates in at least one binding: position, path, direction, containment, proximity, entry/exit, origin/destination, accompaniment, recurrence/context, internal/relational orientation, or spatialized figurative orientation.

Local, one-use, colloquial, idiomatic, and figurative orientation can qualify. Prefer the complete natural orientation phrase when that carries the relation. Reject every-preposition behavior, non-orienting recipient/topic/purpose/possession/degree complements, and generic adverbials with no binding-orientation role.

## 7. Frame-role audit before freeze

Replay the complete source frame by frame.

For each represented binding ask whether it has the needed coordinates for:

- actor/endpoints;
- independently handled thing/content;
- target-bearing state/evaluation;
- operative relation;
- orientation relation;
- spatial support;
- temporal support.

For every explicit primitive ask:

1. Which exact source-local binding uses it?
2. What distinct role does it perform there?
3. Would removing it erase that role rather than merely remove descriptive wording?
4. Is its source grain the smallest complete natural unit for that role?
5. Is its class determined by the binding role rather than surface grammar?

For every unnamed PLACE/TIME ask which binding would be mislocated or conflated without it.

Remove any primitive that cannot answer these questions. Restore any source-established role or support distinction that is otherwise missing. Freeze primitives only after this audit.

## 8. Compounds: bind the frames already identified

A compound is a selective source-local binding among two or more frozen primitive refs.

Construct compounds from the distinct bindings identified during the frame pass, not from arbitrary proximity after primitive extraction.

Include all and only the frozen coordinates that actually participate:

- actors/endpoints;
- operative relation;
- target/content/object;
- target-bearing label/state;
- locator/orientation when part of the binding;
- PLACE/TIME support when it situates the binding.

Split compounds when actor set, target/content, operative relation, source posture, or support frame changes materially.

Questions, choices, reports, memories, comparisons, and reflections may organize a binding without forcing their whole proposition to become a primitive.

Do not create compounds to justify primitives. Do not create one compound per sentence, clause, lexical verb, primitive, support, or phrase. Do not create arbitrary co-occurrence, all-pairs links, scene mega-bundles, support chains, singleton equivalents, duplicate restatements, alternate subset/superset decompositions, or graph closure.

## 9. Final anti-overgeneration audit

Before returning:

- every explicit primitive must point to at least one distinct binding role;
- every unnamed support must prevent a real source-established where/when collapse;
- every compound must represent one coherent source-local binding;
- no class may be populated merely because a word looks spatial, temporal, nominal, adjectival, verbal, or prepositional;
- no candidate is retained solely because its wording is represented, vivid, grammatical, or potentially useful.

The correct inventory is the smallest complete editorial coordinate system that preserves all distinct source-local bindings and their necessary supports.
