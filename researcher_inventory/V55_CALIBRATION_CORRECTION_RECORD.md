V55 therefore does not return to V53 wholesale and does not continue V54's breadth. It introduces a balancing distinction:

**Independent research-coordinate identity is stricter than semantic separability.**

A candidate may be meaningful, nested, directional, participant-bearing, or syntactically distinct without being a primitive Researcher Inventory coordinate. It is retained only when the source itself establishes a stable reusable class-native coordinate after coreference and context restoration.

## V55 correction

V55:

1. uses source-distinction and dependency ledgers instead of a mandate to enumerate every plausible semantic part;
2. keeps nested and unnamed coordinates when the source independently establishes them, while rejecting action-local implicit frames and syntactic decomposition;
3. distinguishes physical scenes/occurrence positions from surfaces, object parts, endpoints, and mere locator targets;
4. distinguishes episode/frame TIME from adverbial/action-local temporal material;
5. rejects generic/impersonal grammatical `you` as PERSON absent a stable represented addressee;
6. resolves deictic/anaphoric wrappers to antecedents instead of duplicating them as OBJECTs;
7. requires LABEL values to be independently selectable characterizations rather than every modifier fragment;
8. requires a separate VERB to have distinct source-native lexical-relation identity, not merely its own grammatical predicate;
9. allows LOCATOR wording beyond prepositional phrases while requiring reusable orientation/context function;
10. defines compounds as selective bindings among admitted coordinates rather than lossless syntactic parses.
