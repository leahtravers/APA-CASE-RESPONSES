## Generalizable finding

V132 correctly returned admission to class-local whole-source passes, but its floor test asked whether deleting a candidate would erase a materially distinct answer to the class-native researcher question. The paired failure shows that this remains too close to wording-level distinctness. A long source can supply many distinct linguistic answers without establishing that many distinct researcher coordinates.

The repeated cross-case pattern is:

1. explicit descriptive, grammatical, reporting, copular, deictic, topical, and clause-level phrases were often promoted as independent primitives merely because they supplied a distinct possible answer wording;
2. some lawful low-salience coordinates were still omitted, showing that the solution cannot simply restore a high standalone-importance threshold;
3. class typing remained vulnerable when spatial/characterizing wording was treated by surface form rather than by the source-established semantic identity;
4. primitive overcapture propagated into compound overconstruction, while omitted primitives made some archetypal bindings impossible.

The durable middle grain therefore requires a **class-local coordinate-warrant test**: a candidate must correspond to a distinct source-established semantic identity of that class, not merely to distinct wording. Exact wording is the evidence/cue for a coordinate, not proof that a new coordinate exists.

## V133 correction

V133 prospectively requires, for every emitted primitive:

1. **source anchor** — exact source evidence exists;
2. **class job** — the candidate answers the class-native researcher question;
3. **coordinate identity** — the source establishes a distinct referent, state/classification, relation, orientation, occurrence position, or episode/period frame at the class's natural grain;
4. **target/relata anchoring** — the worker can identify what the coordinate is about, belongs to, positions, relates, or distinguishes without inventing an analyst abstraction;
5. **non-cue test** — removing the candidate from the inventory would make that semantic identity unrecoverable after all other retained primitives are considered, rather than merely remove one lexical or grammatical way the source expressed an already represented identity;
6. **same-grain deduplication** — alternate cues, parse fragments, restatements, and overlapping wording for the same semantic job do not create additional coordinates.

This test is class-local. V133 does **not** restore V131's global semantic-unit ledger and does not require narrative centrality, standalone-card importance, repeated mention, or high salience.

## Class-general refinements

- `PLACE`: retain a distinct occurrence/participant position; unnamed positions qualify when they distinguish a real represented position, not merely because spatial wording appears.
- `TIME`: retain a distinct episode/period/phase/recurrence frame; do not convert every temporal token or clause-local adverb into a new frame.
- `PERSON`: retain stable represented social actors/groups after coreference; grammatical person/reference alone is not a new actor.
- `OBJECT`: require source-level referential identity. Grammar may name something without the source treating it as a separate referent; propositions, clause shells, vague placeholders, and decorative nouns are not automatically objects.
- `LABEL`: require a source-applied state/value/classification/characterization with a stable target or independently staged status. Modifiers and evaluative wording that only decorate another coordinate remain cues, not separate labels.
- `VERB`: retain one smallest complete source-native relation kernel per distinct asserted relation. Auxiliaries, copular/reporting shells, split predicate fragments, and lexical restatements of the same relation do not multiply coordinates.
- `LOCATOR`: require a distinct orientation/path/relative-position/context relation among coordinates or relative to a represented context. Ordinary argument attachment, topicality, deictic filler, and already-captured place/time support do not automatically create locators.
- Cross-class overlap is allowed only when the same source wording genuinely instantiates two different semantic identities/jobs, not because two grammatical readings are possible.
- Compounds are built from frozen primitives and project the minimal source-asserted binding needed to reconnect them. They are not lexical dependency graphs, co-occurrence bundles, alternate parses, or compensation for primitive errors.
