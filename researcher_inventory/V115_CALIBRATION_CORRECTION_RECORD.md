However, its replacement admission rule treated represented semantic content too readily as entitlement to a standalone primitive. ## Generalizable V115 requirement

A Researcher Inventory primitive is admitted only when all of the following hold:

1. **source grounding** — the source establishes the candidate in the requested class, or legitimately establishes an unnamed PLACE/TIME support slot;
2. **class-native function** — the candidate performs a real job of that class rather than merely being grammatically compatible with it;
3. **source-presented binding/frame role** — the candidate fills a distinct selectable role in a source-presented binding or frame at the natural resolution of that class; and
4. **class-local distinctness/natural grain** — it is not a true alias/coreferent repeat, support fragment, clause wrapper, modifier fragment, generic argument phrase, or mechanically split piece of a stronger natural coordinate.

The primitive does **not** need recurrence, broad salience, multiple compounds, narrative centrality, independent existence outside its proposition, or repeated mention. Local, nested, peripheral, one-use, remembered, reported, relational, uncertain, negated, prospective, figurative, and colloquial coordinates remain eligible when the source separately projects the class-native role.

The controlling distinction is:

- source-presented selectable role at natural class grain -> retain;
- semantic wording internal to a stronger retained coordinate/binding -> do not promote to a standalone row merely because it is meaningful or typable.

## Whole-source binding/frame correction

V115 requires reconstruction of a source binding/frame skeleton before class extraction. The skeleton contains source-differentiated scenes/frames, actors/referents, characterizations, operative relation edges, and independent orientations. It is an omission-control and grain-control device, not a clause parse or exhaustive proposition graph.

A primitive must attach to a role in that skeleton. The skeleton must not create a separate binding for every clause fragment, modifier, grammatical argument, prepositional phrase, lexical verb, or semantic implication.

## Support correction

PLACE/TIME support is retained for source-differentiated scene/frame slots, including legitimate unnamed support, but not multiplied for every action, clause, local transition, or duration/question phrase. Several relations may share one PLACE/TIME support when the source does not materially separate their scene/frame.

## OBJECT / LABEL / LOCATOR correction

- `OBJECT`: retain independently tracked/reified referents/content handles; do not nominalize every proposition, question, intention, or grammatical argument.
- `LABEL`: retain natural complete source-applied characterizations/states/evaluations; do not promote every adjective, adverb, intensifier, local modifier, or discourse stance.
- `LOCATOR`: retain independent orientation edges that actually position a retained participant/referent/relation/frame; do not promote every prepositional, recipient/topic/purpose, possession, degree, or temporal phrase.

## VERB correction

V115 retains one minimal complete predicate kernel for each distinct **source-presented operative relation edge**. One-use, ordinary, gestural, reporting, perception, cognition, question, obligation, location, and low-salience predicates remain eligible when they actually form a distinct source binding. But lexical meaning alone is insufficient: do not promote every grammatical/contentful verb token or split one natural relation into micro-relations/support fragments.

## Compound correction

After primitive freeze, compounds serialize only the materially distinct source-presented bindings from the same skeleton. Do not create compounds for every clause or sentence merely because semantic structure exists. Do not emit graph closure, arbitrary co-occurrence bundles, support chains, mega-bundles, subset/superset variants, or duplicate restatements.
