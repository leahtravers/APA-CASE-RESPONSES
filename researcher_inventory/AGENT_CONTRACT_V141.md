# APA Researcher Inventory Agent Contract V141

Status: `ACTIVE SUCCESSOR FOR CALIBRATION — NOT CERTIFIED`  
Contract version: `RI-CONTRACT-V141`  
Predecessor: `RI-CONTRACT-V140`  
Effective date: 2026-09-21  
Authority: Leah's standing Researcher Inventory calibration instruction

## Prospective correction basis

V140 verified both immutable Leah-approved archetype workbook hashes, passed all retained deterministic apparatus and prompt-harness tests, and then failed hidden semantic calibration on both approved archetypes. The failure is generalizable and semantic rather than mechanical: V140's class-by-class positive-coverage rule widened the precursor population so far that grammatical predicates, local moments, spatial-looking phrases, descriptive wording, and nominal content were repeatedly admitted merely because they could perform a plausible class role. At the same time, some genuine low-salience structural positions, frames, source-treated referents, characterizations, and orientation relations were still missed.

The durable lesson is not to return to salience filtering and not to enumerate every source-supported candidate before suppression. V141 therefore restores the **researcher-coordinate backbone** as the admission gate and makes coverage operate *inside that gated backbone*. Low salience, one use, background status, nesting, uncertainty, remembered/reported status, prospectivity, negation, or lack of an explicit place/time name remain non-disqualifying, but none of them converts lexical or grammatical material into a coordinate by itself.

No gold rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, scored predecessor outputs, canonical archetype extracts, case-specific hidden corrections/examples, or sealed holdout content are supplied to the worker.

## Controlling rule

> **READ THE WHOLE SOURCE FIRST. BUILD A RESEARCHER-COORDINATE BACKBONE BEFORE CLASS EXTRACTION. A PRIMITIVE EXISTS ONLY WHEN THE SOURCE STAGES A DISTINCT HANDLE THAT A RESEARCHER COULD INDEPENDENTLY RECONNECT, TRACK, CODE, COMPARE, OR BIND AT LIGHTWEIGHT WORKSHEET GRAIN. COVERAGE MEANS TEST EVERY HANDLE IN THAT BACKBONE; IT DOES NOT MEAN ENUMERATE EVERY NOUN, VERB, CLAUSE, PROPOSITION, MODIFIER, SPATIAL PHRASE, TEMPORAL PHRASE, OR POSSIBLE PARSE. PRESERVE GENUINE LOW-SALIENCE AND UNNAMED STRUCTURAL COORDINATES. TYPE BY SOURCE FUNCTION, NOT SURFACE FORM. USE THE SMALLEST COMPLETE SOURCE-NATIVE GRAIN. PRESERVE EXACT SOURCE WORDING AND POSTURE. FREEZE PRIMITIVES BEFORE COMPOUNDS. COMPOUNDS PRESERVE MATERIAL LOCAL BINDINGS AMONG FROZEN COORDINATES; THEY DO NOT CREATE, REPAIR, RETYPE, OR SUBSTITUTE FOR PRIMITIVES.**

## Mission and primitive classes

Produce only a literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate from the supplied source.

Primitive classes are exactly:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The inventory is richer than a sparse semantic summary and narrower than a lexical, grammatical, dependency, event, or proposition census.

## 1. Mandatory construction order — coordinate gate before coverage

1. Read the complete source once without extracting primitives.
2. Resolve strict coreference, source order, and materially distinct scene/episode shifts.
3. Build one internal **researcher-coordinate backbone** from the source's represented structure: potential structural positions, structural frames, stable actors/endpoints, source-treated things/content referents, source-applied characterizations, operative relations, and orientation/context relations.
4. Do not create one backbone item per sentence, clause, proposition, event mention, noun phrase, modifier, predicate, prepositional phrase, or parseable dependency.
5. For each backbone handle, determine its exact class-native job and smallest complete source-native identity.
6. For each requested class, perform a coverage pass over the backbone and the whole source to detect **missing handles of that class**, not additional lexical material. A newly noticed item may enter only if it independently passes the coordinate gate in Section 2.
7. Run cross-class typing/non-substitution and same-class identity/deduplication.
8. Run the structural-floor audit and anti-census ceiling audit.
9. Apply literal/posture lock and freeze primitive membership.
10. Build compounds from frozen primitives only.
11. Run final coverage, typing, lexical, compound, and isolation audits.

Do not begin from a compressed summary. Do not begin from an exhaustive proposition ledger. Do not begin from a class-specific lexical scan.

## 2. Universal coordinate gate

A primitive survives only when all applicable parts pass.

### 2.1 Source establishment

The source itself establishes the coordinate, or for lawful unnamed PLACE/TIME clearly stages the structural position/frame.

### 2.2 Distinct research handle

At lightweight worksheet grain, the item is a distinct source-staged handle a researcher can independently reconnect, track, code, compare, or bind. Ask: **what would a researcher point back to here as its own coordinate?**

One use is enough. Background placement is enough when the handle is genuine. Repetition, emotional salience, narrative centrality, explicit naming, and global importance are not required.

The following are never sufficient by themselves:

- a sentence or clause exists;
- a proposition is materially represented;
- a noun/adjective/verb/preposition is present;
- wording contains spatial or temporal language;
- a phrase can be nominalized;
- a parser isolates a predicate/dependency;
- a relation is grammatically complete;
- a modifier, complement, copula, support verb, modal, discourse marker, or reporting frame is present.

If the only reason to keep an item is “this is another thing the sentence says” or “this token can be typed as this class,” it fails the coordinate gate.

### 2.3 Class-native job

The coordinate must perform the native research job of the requested class: site/position, organizing frame, stable actor/endpoint, thing/content referent, characterization, operative relation, or orientation/context relation.

### 2.4 Natural identity and span

Use the smallest **complete** source-native unit that preserves the coordinate's identity. Do not reduce to an arbitrary token fragment and do not widen into a whole proposition merely for completeness.

Merge only true same-class same-identity coreference/repetition. Association, co-location, part/whole, container/content, actor/role, episode/subepisode, or semantic relatedness is not identity.

## 3. Coverage without census

Coverage is mandatory, but its object is the researcher-coordinate backbone.

After the first backbone pass, replay the whole source and ask class by class:

- Did I miss a distinct structural position/frame because it was unnamed or low-salience?
- Did I miss a stable actor or endpoint because it was brief, relational, possessive, offscreen, remembered, reported, or prospective?
- Did I miss a source-treated thing/content referent because it was ordinary or one-use?
- Did I miss a source-applied characterization because it was a question, rejection, correction, polarity, uncertainty, or colloquial phrase?
- Did I omit or fragment an operative relation kernel that connects retained coordinates?
- Did I miss an independently useful orientation/context relation because its wording was deictic, figurative, relational, or embedded?

A coverage correction adds only an item that passes Section 2. Coverage never licenses “retain every materially distinct X” when X means grammatical predicate, temporal mention, spatial phrase, noun phrase, characterization-like modifier, or clause.

## 4. Cross-class typing and non-substitution

Type by represented research function, never by surface form.

One source span may support more than one class only when the source presents genuinely different independently addressable roles at the relevant spans. Never create twins merely because grammar permits multiple descriptions.

A retained class cannot erase a separate coordinate in another class: OBJECT does not absorb LABEL; LABEL does not absorb VERB; PLACE/TIME do not absorb LOCATOR; VERB does not absorb its actor/object merely because they participate in the relation.

Spatial-looking wording can be OBJECT when the source treats it as content; temporal-looking wording can be LABEL/OBJECT/VERB when it does not organize a frame; descriptive wording is not automatically LABEL; noun-like wording is not automatically OBJECT; verb-like wording is not automatically VERB.

## 5. PLACE — structural sites and positions

`PLACE` inventories represented physical/institutional settings, occurrence positions, and participant/referent positions that **organize source material as sites**.

Retain a named or unnamed PLACE when the source distinctly stages a position at which a retained occurrence happens, a participant/referent is situated, a movement/waiting/interaction episode is anchored, or a remembered/reported/prospective/present occurrence is positioned.

Rules:

- Physical naming is not required.
- Broad and contained sites may both survive when each independently organizes material.
- Co-located participant/occurrence positions may remain distinct when the source stages them as separate research handles.
- A participant/referent position may be unnamed yet qualify when the staged relation makes that position independently reconnectable.
- The present-telling/reflection position qualifies only when the source distinctly stages that present occurrence.
- A physical noun, surface, container, body part, direction, figurative spatial expression, or movement phrase does not become PLACE merely because it can be located.

For lawful unnamed PLACE use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording.

## 6. TIME — structural organizing frames

`TIME` inventories represented episodes, periods, phases, attempts, transitions, recurrence spans, remembered/reported frames, prospective frames, and present telling/reflection frames that **organize source material as frames**.

Retain a TIME when the source groups or positions material within a distinct independently reconnectable frame.

Rules:

- No date, clock, duration, frequency word, or temporal noun is required.
- Short local episodes qualify only when they organize a distinct packet of source material, not merely because a clause occurs.
- Broad and nested frames may both survive when the source genuinely organizes material at both levels.
- Waiting, interaction/help/conversation, transition, recurrence, remembered/reported material, prospective possibility, and present reflection may establish frames when distinctly staged.
- Do not create TIME from tense/aspect, every action, every clause, every explicit duration/frequency phrase, or every narrated local moment.

For lawful unnamed TIME use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording.

## 7. PERSON — stable represented actors/endpoints

`PERSON` inventories `B` plus every distinct stable represented human or social actor/group after strict coreference.

Retain actors who act, speak, perceive, are acted upon, possess/receive, function as source/target/beneficiary/endpoint/companion/member, or participate in remembered/reported/prospective relations. Minor, brief, offscreen, relational, institutional, possessive, or group actors may qualify when source-established.

Exclude rhetorical/generic addressees, generic hypothetical roles, pronouns with no stable referent, and true aliases/coreference duplicates.

Order non-speakers by first independent represented participation rather than incidental possessive mention alone.

## 8. OBJECT — source-treated things and content referents

`OBJECT` inventories things and content referents the source independently handles, positions, possesses, selects, compares, evaluates, remembers, decides, questions, or points back to.

Lawful OBJECTs may include physical things, scene items, products, documents, parts, containers, amounts/values, sets, choices, decisions, plans, relationships-as-content, recurring situations, conditions, services, results, mental contents, comparison vehicles, and explicitly reified relations.

A referent may be low-salience or one-use. It still must be treated by the source as its own thing/content coordinate.

Suppress pure pronoun/deictic aliases, arbitrary noun fragments, grammatical complements with no independent referent role, clause shells created only because a proposition can be nominalized, and every noun phrase merely because it names something. Do not duplicate a setting as OBJECT when its only research job is PLACE.

## 9. LABEL — source-applied characterizations

`LABEL` inventories independently reconnectable source-applied states, qualities, statuses, identities, classifications, evaluations, comparisons, manners, polarities, corrections, rejections, and characterization questions.

Retain the smallest complete source-native wording carrying the characterization move. Preserve question, negation, uncertainty, rejection, correction, contrast, attribution, and material intensity where identity-bearing.

A questioned characterization and a separately staged response/rejection may both survive when each is its own characterization move.

Do not create LABEL from every adjective, adverb, modifier, colorful phrase, descriptive clause, quoted epithet, ordinary predicate, or rhetorical question. The characterization itself must be the research coordinate.

## 10. VERB — operative relation spine

`VERB` inventories **operative lexical relation kernels** that form the Researcher Inventory's relation spine.

A relation qualifies when at least one of these is true:

1. it materially connects two or more retained coordinates in a source-staged relation useful for reconnecting the case; or
2. it establishes a distinct state, transition, stance, perception, cognition, speech act, intention, comparison, movement, waiting, possession, or other relation that is itself an independently addressable research handle.

The unit is the smallest complete source-native lexical relation kernel preserving one admitted relation identity.

Rules:

- Do not harvest every grammatical verb, copula, auxiliary, speech frame, cognition frame, support relation, or clause predicate.
- A content-bearing verb is not enough by itself; it must belong to the operative relation spine under the coordinate gate.
- Keep required particles, reflexives, directionals, negation-bearing material, modal/control/infinitival material, idiomatic complements, or coordinated material when removing it changes or hollows out the admitted relation identity.
- Split matrix/embedded or coordinated predicates only when each is independently admitted to the relation spine.
- Copular/locative/state relations qualify only when that relation itself is an independently addressable coordinate, not merely because grammar forms a proposition.
- Speech/cognition/reporting predicates qualify when the act of saying/thinking/reporting itself is a retained relation, not merely a narrative wrapper around separately inventoried content.
- Do not swallow independent PERSON/OBJECT/PLACE/TIME/LABEL/LOCATOR arguments merely to make a clause.

## 11. LOCATOR — independent orientation/context relations

`LOCATOR` inventories materially useful source-native orientation/context relations: relative position, containment, direction, path, origin/destination, entry/exit, approach/departure, proximity/distance, accompaniment/carrying, embodied/internal orientation, recurring/situational context, and materially useful figurative/comparison orientation.

Retain a LOCATOR only when the orientation/context relation is independently reconnectable. Its span must be local to that relation.

Suppress bare prepositions/path particles, routine possession, recipient/topic marking, ordinary argument attachment, generic comparison support, and wording that merely restates another retained coordinate without adding an independent orientation/context job.

## 12. Literal and posture lock

Every source-derived non-null string must preserve source language character-for-character wherever the schema requires source text.

Never synonymize, lemmatize, repair spelling/grammar, normalize dialect, expand contractions, translate, polish, diagnose, standardize a group name, change number, or substitute a conventional expression. Do not replace source-native labels for people/groups/things with analyst-created category names.

`source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be exact source text where applicable. Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME may use null `source_wording`.

Questions, uncertainty, hypotheticals, negations, self-corrections, remembered possibilities, reported material, and proposed future actions remain in source posture. Inventorying does not assert truth or realization.

## 13. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean only. True means the source supplies material qualities/descriptions associated with the coordinate; false otherwise. Do not create or split primitives to justify the boolean. `Q` is never a primitive.

## 14. Ordering

Default within-class order is first material source anchor after filtering/coreference.

Tie rules:

- broad/whole setting before dependent/contained setting when introduced together;
- whole before dependent part at the same anchor unless the source stages the part first;
- PERSON follows first independent represented participation;
- remembered/reported/hypothetical/future frames remain at source position rather than external chronology.

The apparatus owns canonical IDs and deterministic numbering.

## 15. Structural floor audit

Replay the whole source using the researcher-coordinate backbone, not a fresh lexical scan. Ask whether the inventory lets a researcher reconnect the meaningful structural skeleton without substituting a summary.

Specifically check for under-resolution of:

- unnamed occurrence/participant/referent positions;
- broad and local organizing frames, waits, interactions, recurrence, transitions, remembered/reported/prospective frames, and present-reflection frames;
- minor stable actors/endpoints;
- low-salience concrete referents independently handled by the source;
- abstract choices/decisions/contents/relations treated as things;
- questioned/rejected/corrected source-applied characterizations;
- operative relations whose source-native kernel must remain complete;
- independently useful physical/figurative/context orientation relations.

If a distinct research handle is missing, add it in the correct class only after Section 2 passes. Do not widen or retype a neighboring primitive to compensate.

## 16. Anti-census ceiling audit

For every retained primitive ask:

1. What independent researcher coordinate does this serve?
2. What exact source material establishes that coordinate?
3. Is it retained for represented research function rather than lexical/grammatical form?
4. Could a researcher independently reconnect, track, code, compare, or bind it at worksheet grain?
5. Is this the smallest complete natural identity for that job?
6. Is it distinct from same-class identities already retained?
7. Did it enter because I enumerated all clauses, predicates, nouns, modifiers, spatial phrases, temporal phrases, descriptions, or prepositional relations?

If question 7 is yes and no independent coordinate survives, remove it. Never aim for a hidden row count or density.

## 17. Compound construction — selective research-significant bindings

Freeze primitives before compound work.

A compound is a selective local packet of frozen coordinates whose source binding is itself useful for reconnecting the case.

Create a compound only when:

1. the source binds two or more frozen primitives in a materially distinct relation/state/configuration;
2. that binding is independently useful as a research packet rather than merely sentence membership;
3. the packet contains only primitives actually active in that binding; and
4. it is not a duplicate/subset alias, alternate parse, dependency closure, pairwise/transitive closure, one-compound-per-primitive artifact, clause-support bundle, or scene-wide mega-bundle.

Where an admitted VERB materially connects retained coordinates, normally preserve that local binding when it adds a distinct research relation/configuration. Nonverbal compounds are allowed for independently significant characterizations/configurations.

Include PLACE/TIME only when they actively organize that binding. Include LABEL/LOCATOR only when active in that binding. A compound cannot create, delete, merge, retype, repair, or substitute for a primitive. If a necessary coordinate is missing, return to primitive review first.

Canonical compound IDs/order and `_Q` construction remain apparatus-owned.

## 18. Final contrast audit

### Too dense / census failure

Warning signs: explicit spatial wording typed PLACE without structural-site function; every local moment/action typed TIME; every noun phrase/content clause typed OBJECT; every description typed LABEL; every grammatical/copular/support/reporting predicate typed VERB; every prepositional/spatial phrase typed LOCATOR; compounds tracking most clauses or propositions.

### Too sparse / summary failure

Warning signs: missing unnamed positions; missing local or broad organizing frames; missing minor stable actors/endpoints; missing low-salience but independently handled referents; missing source-applied question/rejection/correction characterizations; fragmented or omitted operative relations; missing independent orientation/context relations; collapsing distinct research handles merely because they share a scene.

Correct toward the researcher-coordinate backbone, not toward target density.

## 19. Final audit

Before return verify:

- **Coordinate gate:** every primitive is independently reconnectable at lightweight worksheet grain.
- **Coverage:** every genuine backbone handle received a class-native test, including low-salience and unnamed structural handles.
- **Typing:** every retained coordinate is typed by source function rather than surface form.
- **Grain:** every span/frame is the smallest complete source-native identity for its coordinate.
- **Cross-class:** genuinely distinct jobs survive; alternate grammatical parses do not.
- **Literal:** source strings and posture are exact; no normalization or analyst category substitution occurred.
- **Compounds:** only selective local bindings among frozen active refs survive.
- **Candidate:** no promotion, scoring, APA identity, or database action occurred.

## 23. Holdout boundary

No calibration correction may be made from sealed holdout output.
