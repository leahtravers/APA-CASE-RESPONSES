## Generalizable requirement admitted

V82 establishes **SOURCE-STAGED SELECTABLE-COORDINATE INVENTORY WITH POSITIVE LEXICAL/ORIENTATION SATURATION AND SELECTIVE COMPOUND BINDING**.

The controlling construction order is prospectively:

1. read the whole source and resolve source order, coreference, scenes/periods, and represented relations;
2. run positive whole-source coverage for each requested class, including low-salience, nested, one-use, reported, remembered, prospective, uncertain, negative, and figurative material;
3. retain source-staged coordinates that provide a distinct class-native researcher-selectable handle, whether or not they later belong to a compound;
4. choose the smallest complete source-native grain for each retained handle;
5. allow cross-class overlap only where each projection supplies a genuinely distinct class-native handle;
6. suppress only function grammar, true aliases, unreified shells, mechanical class shadows, arbitrary analyst subparts, and duplicate/alternate tokenizations;
7. freeze all primitives; and
8. build compounds only as selective focal bindings over frozen primitives.

### Primitive coverage is broader than compound coverage

V81's independent-job phrasing was too easy to interpret as requiring each primitive to justify itself through a strong downstream relation or compound. V82 makes the distinction explicit: a primitive may be valid because the source makes it separately selectable even when it is incidental, descriptive, low-salience, or never compounded.

### Lexical relation saturation

A source may separately predicate multiple actions/relations inside one scene, sentence, control construction, report, or causal sequence. Sharing participants or an episode does not by itself make them one VERB. V82 therefore requires retention of distinguishable source-staged lexical relations at the smallest complete lexical grain, while still suppressing auxiliaries and true alternate tokenizations.

### Locator saturation without preposition census

A direct source-native position, containment, surface, direction, path, origin/destination, proximity/distance, accompaniment, recurrence, internal, or figurative orientation can be a valid LOCATOR even when it is syntactically simple and event-dependent. Provenance, ownership, association, attribution, and generic context do not qualify merely because they are prepositional.

### Labels versus qualities

Not every descriptive property is independently inventoried as LABEL. When a description merely supplies qualities of another retained coordinate, `qualities_available` is the lightweight mechanism unless the source separately stages or foregrounds the value/state/classification itself.

### Scene and episode anchors

PLACE and TIME retain source-staged scene/position and episode/period frames, including supported unnamed frames where material structure would otherwise disappear. They do not become generic shadows for every action, noun, temporal word, or movement phrase.

### Selective compounds

Compounds are not a primitive-coverage mechanism. After primitive freeze, create a compound only when multiple coordinates need binding to reconnect a focal source-level relation, state, episode event, comparison, or explicitly staged proposition. Standalone and descriptive primitives require no compound.
