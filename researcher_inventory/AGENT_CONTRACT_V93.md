## Prospective correction basis

Preserved V92 execution `35431679975-A1` verified the immutable Leah-approved Case 2 and Case 6 workbook hashes and passed deterministic apparatus validation before hidden semantic adjudication. Both archetypes then showed the same broad failure family: V92 treated materially represented local proposition detail as sufficient evidence for standalone primitive identity, producing large cross-class primitive overgeneration and derivative compound overgeneration. A smaller set of class-native coordinates was still missed.

V93 corrects that general rule without restoring V91's rejected recurrence/reuse prerequisite.

> **STANDALONE RESEARCH-COORDINATE IDENTITY, NOT SOURCE-DETAIL CENSUS.** Read and account for the whole source, but create a primitive only when the source gives that material a bounded identity in the requested class that is independently useful for source reconnection. A coordinate may occur once. Recurrence, reuse, multiple compounds, or repeated mention may support independence but are never required. Material that matters only as detail inside one proposition remains represented through the proposition/compound rather than receiving a redundant standalone row.

This contract contains durable general rules only. It does not expose approved archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, or sealed holdout content/output to the worker.

## Mission

Read the complete source and construct a lightweight researcher inventory in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Preserve literal source language and posture. Freeze primitives before compounds. Then reconstruct materially distinct source-local propositions from the frozen inventory.

This is a source-reconnection index, not a token/POS census, grammatical parse, interpretation, or minimal ontology.

## 1. Whole-source ledger does not equal primitive entitlement

Read the entire source before extracting. Silently account for materially represented settings, frames, actors, referents, characterizations, relations, and orientations so omissions are visible.

The ledger is only an omission-control device. **A source detail does not become a primitive merely because it is real, vivid, grammatical, or used in a proposition.**

## 2. Four-gate primitive test

For every proposed row, require all four:

### A. Source identity
The material is actually represented by the source and is not analyst-created.

### B. Class-native identity
It performs the requested class's job, not merely a neighboring class's job or a grammatical role.

### C. Standalone indexability
The coordinate can be selected as a bounded source handle without needing the whole surrounding proposition to make it into an entity/relation/frame/characterization/orientation. Removing the row would lose an independently reconnectable coordinate, not merely remove local proposition detail that remains recoverable in the compound.

### D. Editorial materiality
The coordinate contributes materially to reconnecting represented source structure. Incidental scenery, argument support, discourse scaffolding, grammatical decomposition, and redundant shadows do not qualify merely because they are source-present.

If any gate fails, omit the primitive.

## 3. One-use coordinates

**One-use is allowed. One-use is not automatic.**

A coordinate may appear once and still qualify when the source itself gives it bounded independent identity, for example through a distinct actor, explicitly treated referent, source-organizing episode/site, independently applied characterization, discrete predicate relation, or genuine orientation relation.

Do not require recurrence, multiple bindings, persistence across scenes, broad salience, or later reuse.

Conversely, do not retain a row solely because a phrase appears once and can be assigned a semantic class. If its only work is to complete one local proposition, keep that meaning in the compound.

## 4. Coreference, repetition, and cross-class overlap

Resolve true aliases/coreference and exact repeated mentions before counting coordinates.

Cross-class overlap is permitted only when the same source material performs two independently indexable jobs. Grammatical plausibility is not enough. If the second projection merely redescribes the same job, suppress the shadow.

## 5. Grain

Use the smallest complete source-native form that preserves the admitted coordinate's identity.

Do not absorb separable subjects, objects, places, times, labels, locators, or neighboring predicates merely to make a sentence. Do retain particles, complements, negation, modality, comparison, or other wording when removing it changes the coordinate's identity.

Do not mechanically split a source-native relation into matrix/support/embedded/copular fragments. Split only when each resulting piece remains an independently indexable relation after the surrounding clause is removed.

## 6. PLACE

PLACE is a represented site or occurrence-position that organizes where material occurs.

Retain broad/contained settings and other distinct source-organizing positions when they independently locate an episode, encounter, wait, report, remembered scene, or materially staged referent.

An unnamed PLACE may use `source_wording: null` with an exact source cue when a genuinely distinct occurrence-position is required.

Do not create PLACE from every surface, body part, location noun, movement implication, contemplated exit, object position, conversation mention, or present-reflection clause. A local spatial detail belongs only in the proposition unless the source makes the position independently indexable.

## 7. TIME

TIME is a represented episode/frame/period that organizes when material occurs.

Retain distinct source-organizing frames such as an initial episode, materially separate response/transition/wait, later report/conversation, recurring span, remembered frame, present reflection frame, or separately represented prospective frame.

Several actions may share one TIME. Do not create a TIME for each clause, predicate, question, hypothetical action, or local phase unless the source actually establishes a distinct frame. A time can occur once; recurrence is not required.

Unnamed TIME may use `source_wording: null` with an exact source cue.

## 8. PERSON

Retain `B` plus every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when the source gives that actor referential identity.

A one-use or offscreen actor may qualify because actor identity itself is independently indexable. Suppress only true aliases/coreferents and nonreferential/rhetorical addressees.

Do not invent actors from generic roles or pronouns that lack a represented referent.

## 9. OBJECT

OBJECT is a source-treated concrete or abstract referent with bounded independent referential identity.

Retain physical things, amounts/values, documents/orders/results/services, choices/decisions/next steps, sets/categories, explicitly reified situations/content, and internal represented objects when the source actually treats them as things that can be selected independently.

A one-use referent may qualify. But nounhood, concreteness, vivid scenery, possession, or appearance inside a proposition is not enough. Do not nominalize clauses, retain incidental scenic props merely because they are named, or create generic wrappers around more specific content.

If an item functions only as the argument/detail of one local proposition and the source does not otherwise establish it as an independent handle, keep it inside the proposition rather than minting a standalone OBJECT.

## 10. LABEL

LABEL is a source-applied characterization that is independently selectable as a characterization of a represented target.

Retain compact qualities, states, comparisons, identity terms, candidate labels, evaluations, rejections, or corrections when the source actually applies them as characterizations.

One-use characterizations may qualify. Do not inventory every adjective, adverb, intensifier, vivid phrase, rhetorical aside, whole clause, or predicate complement. A phrase is not a LABEL merely because it is descriptive.

Preserve negation, uncertainty, rejection, correction, comparison, and dialect when they are identity-bearing.

## 11. VERB

VERB inventories source-native predicate/relation edges, not grammatical verb tokens and not every clause.

Retain the smallest complete predicate construction that expresses an independently indexable action, state, stance, cognition, report, intention, question relation, modality, placement/existence relation, or happening.

### Relation-edge rule

- Prefer **one source-native relation edge** over a matrix/support/embedded decomposition when the pieces jointly express one proposition or one posture-bearing predicate.
- A matrix/control/support predicate is separate only when it contributes an independently selectable relation beyond merely scoping the embedded predicate.
- An embedded predicate is separate only when it remains a distinct represented relation rather than the lexical completion of the matrix/control construction.
- A copula whose semantic work is fully carried by an admitted LABEL or LOCATOR normally does not create a second VERB.
- Do not use an entire question or sentence as a VERB when a bounded lexical predicate carries the relation.
- Keep negation, modality, particles, and required complements when they define relation identity.
- Repeated/coreferential mentions of the same relation do not create duplicate rows.

A relation may occur once; recurrence is not required.

## 12. LOCATOR

LOCATOR is an independently indexable orientation/context relation: where, position, path, origin/destination, containment, internal orientation, or materially organizing situational context.

Retain the smallest complete construction that supplies the orientation relation.

Do not create LOCATOR from every prepositional phrase, recipient/topic marking, possession, comparison support, path-flavored verb, or clause merely because it contains spatial/context language. Movement can be a VERB without also becoming a LOCATOR unless the source separately establishes an orientation relation worth indexing.

A one-use locator may qualify when its orientation job is independently bounded.

## 13. Literal-language lock

Preserve source wording exactly wherever the schema expects source text. Do not normalize dialect, grammar, contractions, numbers, punctuation, uncertainty, negation, modality, or comparison.

`source_wording` must be exact when explicitly worded. Only genuinely unnamed structural PLACE/TIME coordinates may use `null`, anchored by an exact `source_cue`.

`researcher_note` should be null or minimal mechanical/coreference/unnamed-coordinate bookkeeping. Do not use notes to argue with the evaluator or add interpretation.

## 14. qualities_available

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean only. It does not create a LABEL, split a primitive, or justify retaining a coordinate.

## 15. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds, re-read the source and verify:

1. every material source trace was considered;
2. every retained row passes source identity, class-native identity, standalone indexability, and editorial materiality;
3. no row is retained solely because it appears in a proposition or can be grammatically classified;
4. no legitimate one-use coordinate was rejected merely for lacking recurrence/reuse;
5. aliases/repeats are merged;
6. cross-class overlap reflects two real independent jobs rather than duplicate projection;
7. PLACE/TIME are source-organizing sites/frames rather than micro-detail inventories;
8. VERB represents relation edges rather than matrix/embedded/token decomposition;
9. LOCATOR represents independent orientation rather than preposition/path census;
10. literal wording and posture are exact.

## 16. Compounds

After primitives are frozen, reconstruct materially distinct source-local propositions/events/states/reports/intentions/questions/comparisons/characterizations/orientations using **only retained units**.

A compound may preserve meaningful local proposition detail even when that detail did not earn a standalone primitive. The compound does not mint new units to spell every word of the sentence.

- Prefer one smallest complete semantic binding per materially distinct proposition/relation.
- Split different propositions; do not split grammatical fragments.
- Include only retained coordinates that materially belong to that proposition.
- Do not create graph closure, arbitrary pairwise combinations, subset/superset permutations, duplicate mega-bundles, or compounds merely because units coexist.
- A compound never repairs a missing primitive and never justifies an extra primitive.
