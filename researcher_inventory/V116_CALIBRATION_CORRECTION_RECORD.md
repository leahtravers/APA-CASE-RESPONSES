Conversely, meaningful wording does not become a primitive unless it occupies a class-native slot.

## Generalizable V116 requirement

V116 uses **RELATION-SLOT CLOSURE AT NATURAL CLASS GRAIN**.

1. Reconstruct the source as ordered represented relation/binding instances, not as sentences and not as a summary.
2. For each relation instance, identify only the seven-class slots the source actually establishes: participant, referent/content, characterization, operative relation, orientation, spatial support, and temporal support.
3. Retain each class-local slot once after coreference/deduplication, even when it is local, one-use, low-salience, nested, remembered, reported, prospective, uncertain, negated, figurative, or colloquial.
4. Do not promote wording merely because it is meaningful, grammatical, typable, or part of a relation. The proposed row must be the slot itself at that class's natural grain.
5. Perform **slot closure** before freezing primitives: every represented relation instance must be checked for missing class-native slots, and every proposed primitive must identify the relation slot that licenses it.

This rule is deliberately symmetric. It prevents pruning by salience and prevents overproduction by wording census.

## PLACE/TIME support clarification

PLACE/TIME support is neither `one broad scene only` nor `one support row per clause`.

Retain a distinct support coordinate when the source differentiates an occurrence position or temporal episode enough that reconnecting the represented relation requires distinguishing it from neighboring support. This may occur within one broader physical setting or narrative episode. Several relation instances may still share support when the source does not differentiate them.

Unnamed PLACE/TIME remains lawful only when the source establishes the distinct support role without naming it. No physical or temporal details may be invented.

## OBJECT/LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR clarification

- `OBJECT` is a referent/content slot, not every noun phrase, clause, question, or grammatical argument.
- `LABEL` is a source-applied characterization/state/evaluation slot, not every adjective, adverb, discourse stance, or descriptive fragment.
- `VERB` is the minimal complete operative relation kernel for a represented binding instance, not every lexical verb token and not a clause-sized paraphrase.
- `LOCATOR` is an independent orientation slot that positions a retained participant/referent/relation/frame, not every prepositional or relational phrase.

## Compound clarification

After primitive freeze, each materially distinct represented relation instance is serialized from the slots already frozen for that same instance. Compound construction does not create new primitives, graph closure, subset/superset variants, support-only links, or arbitrary co-occurrence bundles.
