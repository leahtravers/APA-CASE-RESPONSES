## V126 correction

V126 changes the semantic order of operations to:

> **FRAME FIRST, EDITOR COORDINATES SECOND. RECONSTRUCT THE SOURCE-LOCAL BINDINGS AND THEIR WHERE/WHEN SUPPORTS BEFORE FINAL PRIMITIVE ADMISSION. THEN KEEP ONLY THE SMALLEST SOURCE-NATIVE OR LAWFULLY RECONSTRUCTED COORDINATES THAT OCCUPY A DISTINCT ROLE IN AT LEAST ONE BINDING OR SUPPORT DISTINCTION.**

The successor requires:

1. **Internal frame map first.** Read the whole source and distinguish local represented configurations by changes in participant set, operative relation, target/content, represented posture, and source-established where/when support. The map is internal and is not emitted.
2. **Support reconstruction before final primitive admission.** Determine explicit or unnamed PLACE/TIME supports needed to keep distinct bindings from collapsing into the wrong where/when frame. Explicit spatial/temporal wording is evidence, not automatic PLACE/TIME entitlement.
3. **Role-bearing admission.** A lexical mention is not enough. A primitive survives only when removing it would erase a distinct participant/endpoint, independently handled thing/content, target-bearing state/evaluation, operative relation, orientation relation, or required support distinction in at least one source-local binding.
4. **No uncertainty-preservation default.** V125's instruction to preserve a candidate when uncertain is retired. When uncertain, test the candidate against the binding-role requirement rather than keeping it merely because it carries represented meaning.
5. **Type by binding role.** Surface grammar, concreteness, spatial imagery, or noun/verb shape do not control type. The same semantic theme can be typed differently depending on the job it performs in the represented binding.
6. **Editor grain.** Preserve exact lexical wording at the smallest complete source-native grain that carries the role. Do not inflate to proposition shells or fragment a natural relation/state until its identity changes.
7. **Binding-first compound construction.** Freeze primitives only after the frame/role audit. Then build compounds from the distinct source-local bindings already identified, using all and only their participating frozen coordinates and relevant supports.
8. **Anti-overgeneration audit.** Every explicit primitive must answer: which distinct binding role would disappear if this coordinate were removed? Every unnamed support must answer: which binding would become mislocated or conflated without this support? If neither can be answered, omit it.
