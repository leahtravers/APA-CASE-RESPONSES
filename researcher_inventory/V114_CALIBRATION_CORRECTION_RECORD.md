## Generalizable V114 requirement

A Researcher Inventory primitive is admitted when all of the following hold:

1. **source grounding** — the source explicitly establishes the class-native denotatum/relation/state/orientation, or the represented occurrence necessarily supplies an unnamed PLACE/TIME support coordinate;
2. **represented existence/function** — the candidate denotes or performs a distinct represented class-native job, even if it is mundane, single-use, background, local, low-salience, implicit-support, remembered, reported, prospective, uncertain, negated, or figurative; and
3. **class-local distinctness** — it is not merely an alias/coreferential repeat or same-class restatement of a retained coordinate.

Do **not** require recurrence, thematic importance, later reuse, independent salience, or a separately narratively emphasized role.

Reject only material whose class claim comes from grammar/discourse alone: auxiliaries, inflection/tense machinery, determiners, generic pronouns after coreference, connectives, fillers, discourse scaffolding, nonreferential/generic mentions, and spans that do not establish a distinct class-native denotatum/relation/state/orientation/support.

## Coverage correction

V114 requires a support-closure pass after whole-source segmentation:

- each differentiated represented episode/phase is checked for a distinct TIME support coordinate;
- each differentiated represented spatial scene/interaction is checked for a distinct PLACE support coordinate;
- unnamed support is allowed when the source establishes the occurrence but does not name the support;
- later interactions, remembered episodes, recurring spans, prospective situations, and present telling/reflection are not suppressed merely because they are structurally secondary.

This is a coverage test, not a requirement to fabricate support that the source does not establish.

## Functional typing correction

Type by represented job, not phrase shape.

- content/thing/referential handle -> `OBJECT` even when spatial, clausal, internal, relational, or figurative;
- characterization/state/evaluation/comparison/candidate label -> `LABEL` even when nominal or adverbial;
- scene/location support -> `PLACE`;
- episode/phase support -> `TIME`;
- meaningful represented lexical predicate/relation -> `VERB`;
- orientation/path/position/context relation -> `LOCATOR`;
- represented human/social participant -> `PERSON`.

Cross-class projection remains allowed when one exact source span genuinely performs more than one class-native represented function.

## VERB correction

V113's phrase "materially update" a represented relation is superseded. A lexical predicate is retained whenever it expresses a distinct represented event, state, action, communication, cognition, possession, perception, movement, question, intention, transition, gesture, or relation in the source. One-use and low-salience predicates qualify. Exclude only auxiliaries/tense-aspect machinery, pure copular carriage where the represented content is entirely a retained label, discourse/filler predicates with no represented relation, and true same-relation restatements.

## Compound correction

After primitive freeze, segment the source into represented proposition/relation instances and emit the smallest complete compound for each retained instance that is needed to preserve the source's represented structure. Include only frozen primitives that actually participate in that proposition/relation. Do not build graph closure or arbitrary co-occurrence bundles. Non-VERB label/state/question propositions may form compounds when they are represented structure not carried by another retained relation.
