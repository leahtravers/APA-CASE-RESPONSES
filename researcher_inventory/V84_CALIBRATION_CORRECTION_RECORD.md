## Class-native correction

### PLACE / TIME

Restore supported unnamed frame reconstruction without returning to predicate microframes. Distinct setting positions and episode/period frames may exist even when no explicit place/time phrase names them. A broader retained scene does not automatically absorb every distinguishable participant/interactions/wait/reported/present-telling context.

### OBJECT

Restore broad low-salience referent coverage, especially concrete scene objects and separately established referents. At the same time, event/proposition shells created only by nominalizing a clause and telling-management/meta-discourse remain excluded.

### LABEL

Use atomic lexical value/state/classification grain. A short source-staged state may qualify even inside a larger proposition; a whole cognition/action/question/proposition does not become LABEL merely because it contains evaluative content.

### VERB

Restore broad coverage of separately lexicalized content-bearing relations, including low-salience/nested/subordinate/perception/help/control relations, but require atomic complete grain. Split separately predicated relations instead of merging them simply because they share a clause, subject, or episode. Suppress pure auxiliary/support grammar and alternate tokenizations.

### LOCATOR

Restore contextual orientation beyond only globally salient spatial paths. Spatial, temporal-context, recurrence, internal, comparative, and source-native orientation handles may qualify; generic syntax and preposition census remain excluded.
