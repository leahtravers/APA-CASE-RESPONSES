> **A PRIMITIVE IS A SOURCE-NATIVE, MATERIAL-BINDING-CAPABLE RESEARCHER COORDINATE AT THE NATURAL EDITORIAL GRAIN. MAP MATERIAL BINDINGS AND THEIR SUPPORT FRAMES FIRST, THEN DERIVE PRIMITIVES FROM THE ROLES THE SOURCE ACTUALLY ESTABLISHES. No archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, calibration answers, canonical gold extracts, or sealed holdout material are supplied to the worker.

## Mission

Produce a literal, lightweight Researcher Inventory of the complete supplied source in exactly seven primitive classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Then build selective compounds only from frozen registered primitives.

The inventory is neither a sparse summary nor a lexical/clause census. It is a researcher-facing coordinate system at the source's own natural editorial resolution.

## 1. Read the whole source before answering any class

Read the complete source first.

Privately map:

- materially distinct represented situations/bindings;
- spatial support frames;
- temporal episode/support frames;
- participants and relation endpoints;
- things/content treated as distinct handles;
- applied characterizations;
- operative relations;
- orientations/context relations;
- reports, memories, questions, choices, contrasts, reflections, and prospective material.

Do not emit this private map. It is only an extraction scaffold.

Then answer only the requested class.

## 2. Material-binding-capable coordinate test

Admit a primitive only when the source itself establishes it as one independently useful researcher coordinate in the represented case structure.

A coordinate is `material-binding-capable` when it can occupy one coherent role in the private represented-binding/support map, such as:

- support frame;
- participant/endpoint;
- thing/content handle;
- applied characterization;
- operative relation kernel;
- orientation/context relation.

This does **not** require the primitive to appear in a final compound. A legitimate coordinate may remain unbundled.

It also does **not** require recurrence, persistence, global salience, narrative centrality, or entity-like identity. Local, one-use, mundane, uncertain, negated, questioned, hypothetical, prospective, remembered, reported, figurative, idiomatic, and colloquial coordinates may qualify.

Reject a candidate when its only entitlement is that the words are literal, grammatical, descriptive, rhetorically vivid, or pointable.

## 3. Editorial resolution

Use the source's natural lightweight editorial grain.

The correct unit is the smallest complete source-native form that performs the relevant researcher-coordinate job without losing its represented meaning.

Do not:

- split a natural idiom, phrasal relation, bound complement, negated construction, or orientation phrase so far that the coordinate's job is destroyed;
- inflate a coordinate into an entire sentence, clause, proposition, question shell, or rhetorical bundle when a smaller source-native nucleus carries the job;
- treat every lexical verb, noun phrase, adjective, prepositional phrase, temporal expression, deictic, modifier, or discourse fragment as a primitive.

## 4. Support frames first: PLACE and TIME

Before extracting any class, complete the spatial and temporal support map across the whole source.

### PLACE

Retain each materially differentiated source-established spatial support frame required to situate represented bindings.

A PLACE may be explicit or unnamed. It may be broad, contained/local, destination-like, remembered/reported, a later setting, the support of a represented object/interaction/wait/participant configuration, or a present-telling setting when the source itself establishes one.

Several bindings may share one PLACE when the source does not differentiate their support. Conversely, multiple unnamed PLACE rows may be required when the source establishes materially different support frames.

For unnamed PLACE use null `source_wording`, an exact source cue, and a neutral mechanical tag. Never invent where.

Do not turn every physical noun, destination phrase, or locator phrase into PLACE.

### TIME

Retain each materially differentiated source-established temporal episode/support frame required to situate represented bindings.

A TIME may be explicit or unnamed. It may include an attempt/condition episode, response phase, transition, wait, intended period, remembered/reported period, later conversation/report, recurring span, present-reflection frame, or prospective frame.

A temporal expression is not automatically a TIME. If it merely orients another episode, it may instead be LOCATOR/context or no separate primitive.

For unnamed TIME use null `source_wording`, an exact source cue, and a neutral mechanical tag.

## 5. PERSON

Retain the speaker plus each distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when that actor functions as a participant, anchor, possessor/beneficiary, relation endpoint, remembered/reported actor, institutional actor, prospective actor, peripheral actor, or one-use actor.

Reject rhetorical, generic, hypothetical-person wording that never becomes a represented actor coordinate.

## 6. OBJECT

Retain source-established concrete or abstract thing/content handles that function as independently usable relation endpoints or referential nodes in the represented map.

Qualifying OBJECT coordinates may include concrete things and source-distinguished parts, values/amounts, services/results, decisions/next steps treated as things, contemplated alternatives treated as choices, named sets/categories, remembered/reported content, and internal represented content handles.

Reject:

- every noun phrase merely because it is grammatical;
- generic pronouns/deictics;
- clause/proposition/question shells;
- explanations or discourse points merely because they can be nominalized;
- actions merely recast as nouns;
- descriptive fragments with no independently represented thing/content role.

## 7. LABEL

Retain distinct source-applied characterization events that perform a material role in the represented map.

LABEL may include states, statuses, identities, evaluations, comparisons, self-labels, candidate labels, rejections, corrections, contrasts, and materially expressed postures.

LABEL is target-bearing and occurrence-bound. A valid characterization may occur once and need not persist.

Preserve colloquial, idiomatic, uncertain, questioned, negated, corrected, rejected, and figurative characterization when the characterization itself is represented as a researcher coordinate.

Reject decorative color, incidental description, generic manner wording, detached modifiers/intensifiers, and scene-setting adjectives that do not function as an independently useful applied characterization.

## 8. VERB

Retain each distinct operative relation kernel that performs one material relation role in the represented map.

VERB may express action, state, cognition, perception, report/communication, intention, decision, possession, comparison, evaluation, movement, transition, gesture, existence/location, obligation, recurrence, or another represented relation.

A valid VERB may be one-use and need not recur or persist.

Reject:

- auxiliary/support verbs;
- filler/discourse predicates;
- lexical verb occurrences that do not constitute their own material relation role;
- proposition/question shells when a smaller source-native relation nucleus carries the job;
- empty copular/reporting scaffolding with no distinct relation contribution;
- duplicate restatements;
- whole-clause wrappers;
- rhetorical bundles;
- internal fragments of a stronger natural relation kernel.

Preserve particles, reflexives, negation, modality, and bound complements when needed for source identity. Preserve nonasserted posture.

## 9. LOCATOR

Retain each distinct orientation/context relation that performs one material role in the represented map.

LOCATOR may include position, path, direction, containment, proximity, movement orientation, accompaniment, origin/destination, entry/exit, recurrence/context, internal/relational orientation, and materially spatialized figurative orientation.

A valid LOCATOR may be one-use and need not persist.

Reject every-preposition/adverb behavior, recipient/topic/purpose/possession/degree complements, generic adverbials, isolated deictics without their own orientation role, and rhetorical location wording without a represented orientation job.

A spatial or temporal phrase may be LOCATOR rather than PLACE/TIME when it orients a support frame instead of constituting the support frame.

## 10. Functional typing by represented job

Type by the coordinate's primary represented job, not grammar or word shape:

- spatial support -> `PLACE`
- temporal episode/support -> `TIME`
- represented human/social actor -> `PERSON`
- independently represented thing/content handle -> `OBJECT`
- applied characterization/state/status/evaluation/comparison/identity -> `LABEL`
- operative happening/action/state/relation kernel -> `VERB`
- orientation/path/direction/containment/proximity/context relation -> `LOCATOR`

Cross-class duplication is allowed only when the source genuinely establishes two independently selectable jobs, not because one phrase can be linguistically described in several ways.

## 11. Literal lock and source posture

For every explicit primitive, preserve exact source-native wording.

Every non-null `source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be character-for-character source text.

Preserve colloquial language, dialect, spelling/grammar, idiom, figurative wording, negation, modality, uncertainty, question form, attribution, comparison, remembered/reported posture, hypothetical/prospective posture, correction, and rejection.

Never normalize, polish, translate, diagnose, euphemize, substitute synonyms, or lemmatize into a different surface form.

Researcher short tags are navigational only and must stay source-near.

## 12. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds:

1. replay the whole source in order;
2. verify every material binding has the spatial/temporal support the source actually differentiates;
3. verify PLACE/TIME/PERSON/OBJECT for source-established support/referential roles;
4. verify LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR for source-native characterization/relation/orientation roles;
5. restore legitimate local/one-use coordinates omitted only because they looked small, colloquial, mundane, uncertain, figurative, or nonpersistent;
6. remove candidates admitted only because they are literal surface spans, grammatical constituents, incidental descriptions, discourse/rhetorical material, redundant restatements, or proposition shells;
7. arbitrate OBJECT/LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR by primary represented job;
8. reduce relation-like units to the smallest complete natural source-native grain;
9. verify exact wording, coreference, class-local distinctness, and first-establishment order; and
10. freeze primitives.

Coverage and compression are equally mandatory.

## 13. Binding ledger and compounds

After primitive freeze, reconnect frozen coordinates to the private material-binding map.

A valid primitive may remain unbundled.

Emit a compound only when two or more frozen coordinates jointly form one source-local, materially complete researcher binding.

Use only coordinates actually participating in that binding. Include enough for material completeness without unrelated scene context.

Split when actor set, target/content, source posture, support frame, or independently represented relation changes materially.

Do not create:

- one compound per clause, verb, primitive, sentence, or phrase;
- arbitrary co-occurrence links;
- all-pairs links;
- graph closure;
- support chains;
- scene mega-bundles;
- duplicate restatements;
- alternate subset/superset decompositions of the same binding;
- singleton equivalents.

## 14. Qualities flag

`qualities_available` is only a yes/no availability flag showing that source qualities/descriptions exist for the coordinate. Do not explode qualities into a full APA parse merely to support Q. Do not use Q to replace LABEL rows.

## 15. Identity and ordering

Resolve true aliases/coreference before deduplication. Do not merge distinct coordinates merely because they are related, colocated, or lexically similar.

Code owns canonical output IDs. Supply only neutral identity keys when the apparatus asks for them.

Order by first source establishment after coreference, subject to speaker-first PERSON and genuine broad-before-contained support rules.
