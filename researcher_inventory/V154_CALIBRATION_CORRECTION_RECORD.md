## 4. Root cause

V153 correctly distinguishes researcher role from bare grammar, but its `role-complete lexical envelope` test makes semantic comprehensibility too strong a reason to keep open-class arguments and contextual material inside a relation primitive.

The Researcher Inventory needs two different grain rules:

- **structural coordinates** are resolved from the staged source graph, including unnamed/implicit coordinates when a distinct scene or temporal frame is organized by the source; while
- **relation primitives** are resolved at a minimal lexical relation nucleus, leaving independently represented actors, referents, scene/time coordinates, characterizations, and orientation relations to their own rows and compound bindings.

This is neither a bare-head census nor a clause-level semantic envelope.

## 7. Relation-grain correction

### VERB

A VERB is the minimal exact contiguous source-native **relation nucleus**. Keep lexical heads plus particles, polarity, modality, aspect, fixed relation markers, and other function wording required to identify the relation itself.

Do not absorb separable PERSON/OBJECT arguments, PLACE/TIME coordinates, LABELs, independent LOCATOR material, open-clause relations, or explanatory clause material merely because they make the proposition understandable. Those elements remain separate primitives and are connected by compounds where the source binds them.

This does not authorize mechanical lemmatization or bare-head stripping. A multiword relation remains multiword when the wording is integral to the relation itself.

### LOCATOR

LOCATOR uses the same nucleus principle for orientation. Independently staged path, direction, containment, accompaniment, source/target, or figurative orientation relations remain separate when distinct. Temporal/discourse context is not a LOCATOR merely because it situates a clause.

### LABEL

LABEL remains functional. A characterization survives when the source performs a distinct characterization act; incidental description or modification remains support/evidence rather than a primitive merely because it is descriptive.

### OBJECT

OBJECT still admits source-reified decisions, relations, events, internal/abstract constructs, and proposition-like handles when the source itself gives them stable referential standing. Transient deictics, discourse shells, grammatical complements, and metalinguistic placeholders remain excluded without independent source-treated standing.
