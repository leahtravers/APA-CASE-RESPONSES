## Generalizable defect demonstrated

`AGENT-BEHAVIOR / DURABLE SEMANTIC CONTRACT DEFECT`

V93 corrected V92's surface-detail census by imposing one four-part independent-coordinate test across every primitive class. Paired Case 2 and Case 6 evidence now shows that this correction over-generalized one kind of identity requirement across classes whose research jobs are structurally different.

The repeated pattern is class-asymmetric:

1. **PLACE/TIME support was under-recovered.** Materially distinct scenes, conversations, waiting positions, remembered periods, and present/future frames were omitted when their coordinates were unnamed or were not independently lexicalized.
2. **VERB relations were under-split and over-bundled.** Local or proposition-dependent edges were omitted, while some coordinated/nested predicate structures were collapsed into larger proposition-sized verb rows.
3. **LOCATOR relations were under-recovered and over-bundled.** Local path/position/containment/directional material was often absorbed into VERB or suppressed for lacking standalone intelligibility.
4. **OBJECT/LABEL projections remained uneven.** Some materially represented concrete/reified referents were omitted, while wording whose stronger job was another class was sometimes duplicated as an OBJECT/LABEL shadow.
5. **Compounds inherited primitive-grain errors and proliferated redundant graph-like bindings instead of one canonical source-local proposition/event binding where warranted.**

The common cause is not simply “too many” or “too few” rows. It is the assumption that every primitive must pass the same standalone-addressability test.

## V94 correction

V94 replaces the universal test with **class-native entitlement and grain**:

- PLACE/TIME are support-coordinate classes and may require unnamed coordinates derived from represented occurrence structure;
- PERSON remains actor-inclusive after true coreference;
- OBJECT preserves materially represented referents/content handles, including one-use concrete/reified referents, while avoiding cross-class shadows and arbitrary nominalization;
- LABEL preserves source-indexed characterization/candidate-label content, including material acceptance/rejection/correction, without descriptive modifier census;
- VERB is a relation-edge class and does not require standalone intelligibility; relations are split to the smallest complete source-native predicate edge;
- LOCATOR is an orientation-edge class and does not require standalone intelligibility; materially distinct path/position/containment/directional orientation is separated from VERB where both jobs exist;
- compounds reconstruct canonical materially distinct source-local propositions/events and do not require every primitive to participate or generate graph closure.

This is a general semantic requirement across both calibration archetypes and is therefore eligible for durable successor treatment under Leah's standing instruction.
