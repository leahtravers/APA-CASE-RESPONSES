## Mission

Read the complete source and recover a lightweight, source-native researcher inventory in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The target is **minimal sufficient frame-complete resolution**: preserve every distinct source-native coordinate needed to reconnect the source's represented frames while excluding grammar, discourse carriage, lexical census material, and redundant projections.

Do not perform APA scoring, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA Data Fabric/database mutation.

## 1. Whole-source frame segmentation first

Read the entire source before projecting any requested class.

Silently identify distinguishable represented frames and transitions, including where present:

- current events and interactions;
- waits, attempts, responses, changes, movements, and states;
- conversations, reports, memories, comparisons, questions, decisions, intentions, alternatives, and prospective situations;
- recurring or long-span relations;
- reflection or present-telling frames;
- participant changes;
- setting or phase changes.

Frame segmentation is semantic. It is not sentence segmentation, clause segmentation, or one-frame-per-verb.

A frame map is only an organizational aid. **Do not turn every mapped relation or slot into a primitive candidate.**

## 2. Research-bearing reconnectability gate

A primitive is eligible only when its exact source-present trace performs at least one distinct class-native research job in a represented frame.

### 2.1 Participant
A distinct human/social actor or stable group participates in, possesses materially within, is remembered/reported within, or is prospectively involved in represented structure.

### 2.2 Referential/content handle
A concrete or abstract source handle is acted on, checked, possessed, selected, contrasted, remembered, reported, considered, exchanged, questioned, decided about, located, or otherwise treated as a separately reconnectable thing/content relation.

### 2.3 Characterization
The source itself presents a distinct quality, state, identity, comparison, candidate label, correction, polarity, manner, or posture as represented content.

### 2.4 Episode support
PLACE/TIME support distinguishes a represented episode or phase. Exact geography or chronology may be unnamed; neutral support is allowed when it reconnects a genuinely distinct source frame without inventing facts.

### 2.5 Research-bearing relation kernel
A predicate expresses the identity of a distinct represented action, state, attempt, response, movement, cognition, decision, possession, comparison, question, or other relation that a researcher would need to reconnect that frame.

The fact that a verb, copula, report verb, speech verb, cognition verb, existential, modal, or state predicate is semantically interpretable does **not** make it a primitive. The relation itself must carry distinct frame information rather than merely carry grammar, narration, attribution, quotation, or content already represented by other coordinates.

### 2.6 Orientation
A source-present relation distinctly positions or contextualizes a coordinate/frame by containment, path, origin/destination, direction, proximity, accompaniment, recurrence, procedure, relation, mental/internal context, temporal position, or figurative/comparative orientation.

No route requires recurrence, narrative centrality, global indispensability, compound membership, or high salience.

## 3. Minimal sufficient set rule

Recover the **smallest complete set** of primitives that preserves all distinct represented research structure.

This means:

- do not omit a legitimate local or one-use coordinate merely because it is mundane;
- do not create a row merely because a clause contains an identifiable semantic relation;
- do not create one row per noun, verb, modifier, temporal phrase, spatial phrase, sentence, or clause;
- do not preserve both a proposition-sized wrapper and the smaller class-native coordinates that already carry its research information;
- do not duplicate identical semantic work across classes;
- do preserve cross-class projections only when each class carries genuinely different information.

`Minimal` does not mean globally indispensable. `Complete` does not mean exhaustive grammar.

## 4. Driver-versus-carrier test for VERB

For each possible VERB, determine whether the predicate is a **frame driver** or merely a **carrier**.

Retain the smallest complete predicate span when the relation itself establishes, changes, links, attempts, questions, decides, moves, possesses, perceives, evaluates, or otherwise constitutes a distinct represented frame relation.

Reject it when its only work is:

- auxiliary or tense/aspect support;
- copular scaffolding for a characterization already represented as LABEL;
- existential grammar without a separately represented event/state relation;
- ordinary report/speech framing whose only research content is the reported material;
- discourse carriage such as introducing or organizing narration;
- generic cognition framing whose only distinct content is already externalized;
- a proposition-sized wrapper around separable actors/content/labels/support/orientation;
- a redundant restatement of an already retained relation.

Copular, reporting, speech, cognition, perception, possession, state, or comparison predicates may still qualify when **that relation itself** is source-represented research structure. There is no automatic keep or automatic drop by verb type.

## 5. Episode-support recovery for PLACE and TIME

PLACE and TIME are support classes, not token inventories.

First identify source-differentiated episodes/phases. Then assign support only where it helps reconnect those episodes.

Retain as applicable:

- explicit broad settings;
- distinct local/contained settings doing different research work;
- unnamed location support for a distinct represented episode whose exact place is unstated;
- explicit periods/spans;
- unnamed time support for a distinct phase whose exact time is unstated;
- distinct remembered, reported, recurring, intended, prospective, and present-reflection phases when the source differentiates them.

Merge support coordinates doing the same job. Do not infer geography or chronology. Do not classify every physical noun, spatial phrase, temporal word, tense, duration, or connective as PLACE/TIME.

## 6. Class assignment by source-present research function

Resolve class **after** admission, by what the coordinate does in represented source structure:

- distinct human/social actor or stable group → `PERSON`;
- distinct concrete or abstract referential/content handle → `OBJECT`;
- source-present quality/state/identity/comparison/correction/posture → `LABEL`;
- location/episode support → `PLACE`;
- period/phase support → `TIME`;
- research-bearing relation kernel → `VERB`;
- orienting relation → `LOCATOR`.

Physical or spatial wording is not automatically PLACE. Nominal wording is not automatically OBJECT. A relational state can be LABEL when the source presents it as characterization. A phrase may be LOCATOR only when its research job is orientation rather than merely containing spatial or temporal language.

If two classes seem plausible, ask which class-native job the source actually gives the trace. Duplicate only when two genuinely different jobs remain.

## 7. Canonical source-native span and literal lock

After a coordinate qualifies and its class is resolved, choose the smallest exact source-native span that remains semantically complete for that class-native job.

Keep only what identity requires, including when necessary:

- particles and required prepositions;
- reflexives;
- light-verb support;
- essential complements;
- negation;
- modality;
- uncertainty;
- comparison;
- attribution;
- idiomatic wording;
- source posture.

Shrink clause/proposition wrappers when smaller coordinates carry the same research information. Expand a head fragment when the head alone distorts the source-present coordinate.

Never normalize, clean, improve, translate, diagnose, euphemize, paraphrase, lemmatize into a different surface form, or substitute synonyms.

For legitimate unnamed PLACE/TIME support, neutral mechanical tags are allowed only where the schema permits null source wording; `source_cue` must anchor the support to exact source text.

## 8. Class-native rules

### PLACE
Retain explicit settings and distinct local/contained support plus legitimate neutral unnamed location support for source-differentiated episodes. A physical object, surface, destination word, spatial noun, movement, or prepositional phrase is not PLACE unless its research job is episode/location support.

### TIME
Retain explicit periods and distinct semantic episode/phase support. Separate genuinely different phases and merge expressions doing the same support job. Do not inventory tense, duration, every temporal token, or one TIME per action/clause.

### PERSON
Retain the speaker and every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when the actor performs a represented participant/relation role. One-use, offscreen, remembered, reported, possessive, relational, institutional, and prospective actors may qualify. Suppress aliases and rhetorical/nonreferential addressees.

### OBJECT
Retain source-reified concrete and abstract referential/content handles. Mundane and one-use things qualify when the source uses them as distinct handles in research-bearing relations. Choices, decisions, relations, conditions, amounts, categories, alternatives, and internal/reported content may qualify when separately reified. Reject noun census, generic pronouns/deixis, incidental scenery, discourse organizers, and proposition wrappers without separate referential identity.

### LABEL
Retain distinct source-present characterization kernels: qualities, states, identities, comparisons, candidate labels, corrections, polarity, manner, or posture. Idiomatic, subtle, local, figurative, comparative, and one-use characterizations may qualify. Reject adjective/adverb/intensifier/colorful-language census and wording whose only role is stylistic or grammatical.

### VERB
Retain only research-bearing frame-driver relation kernels at the smallest complete predicate span. Apply the driver-versus-carrier test. Do not inventory every grammatical verb, copula, auxiliary, report/speech/cognition carrier, existential, or clause-sized proposition. Ordinary/simple predicates remain eligible only when the relation itself constitutes distinct represented research structure.

### LOCATOR
Retain distinct orienting constructions at the smallest complete span. Orientation may be spatial, contained, path/route, origin/destination, directional, proximal, accompaniment, recurring, procedural/relational, mental/internal, temporally positional, or figurative/comparative. Reject preposition/adverb/recipient/topic/duration/deixis/comparison census and duplicate orientation already doing no distinct work.

## 9. Semantic identity and deduplication

Primitive identity follows represented semantic identity, not mention count.

Merge true aliases, coreference, restatements, inflection-only repeats, and duplicate head/full-span variants representing one stable coordinate. Preserve distinct local occurrences/support when the source represents materially different frame roles.

Do not create analyst paraphrases beside literal source wording.

## 10. Primitive freeze audits

Repeat until stable, in this order:

1. **Frame segmentation audit** — distinguish semantic episodes/phases without converting clauses into frames mechanically.
2. **Reconnectability audit** — every retained primitive performs a distinct research-bearing class-native job; remove grammar/discourse candidates with no such job.
3. **Minimal-sufficient-set audit** — remove redundant wrappers and duplicated semantic work without suppressing legitimate local/one-use coordinates.
4. **VERB driver/carrier audit** — remove auxiliary/copular/reporting/discourse/cognition carriers unless the relation itself is research-bearing.
5. **PLACE/TIME support audit** — restore missing support for genuinely distinct episodes/phases; merge redundant support; do not invent geography/chronology.
6. **Functional-class audit** — classify by source-present research job rather than physical form or grammar.
7. **Atomic-span audit** — fix fragments and over-bundled proposition spans.
8. **Anti-tokenization audit** — remove noun/verb/modifier/spatial/temporal/preposition census behavior.
9. **Literal-lock audit** — remove normalization, synonyms, invented wording, and analyst inference.
10. **Semantic-dedup audit** — collapse true duplicates while preserving distinct local roles.

Freeze primitives only after all ten audits are stable.

## 11. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean only. It is true when source-present qualitative/descriptive material is available around a coordinate or compound under the schema. It is not confidence, importance, admission authority, or a demand to create a LABEL.

## 12. Selective compound construction

Only after all seven primitive classes are frozen, construct a lightweight set of multi-coordinate compounds.

Create a compound only when two or more frozen coordinates jointly reconnect one distinct source-local relation/event/frame and the bundle adds useful local binding beyond the primitives alone. Use the smallest set of frozen refs needed and preserve source-local semantic order/posture.

A primitive may remain unbundled.

Do not create graph closure, pairwise closure, one compound per primitive, one compound per clause, singleton-equivalent bundles, subset/superset permutations, redundant restatements, or scene mega-bundles. Compounds never justify missing, extra, or wrong-class primitives.

## 13. Completion standard

The target is **research-bearing, minimal-sufficient archetypal resolution**:

- whole-source semantic frame segmentation precedes projection;
- frame mapping guides but does not generate rows;
- every primitive has a distinct research-bearing class-native job;
- legitimate low-salience, local, one-use, remembered, reported, prospective, concrete, abstract, and figurative coordinates remain eligible;
- grammar, discourse carriage, and POS census behavior are excluded;
- VERBs are frame drivers rather than automatically admitted semantic predicates;
- PLACE/TIME preserve source-differentiated support frames, including legitimate neutral unnamed support;
- class assignment follows research function rather than form;
- every explicit row uses the smallest complete literal source-native span;
- semantic duplicates and proposition wrappers are removed;
- compounds reconstruct only a selective source-local set from frozen primitives.

Do not target hidden counts or infer hidden gold.
