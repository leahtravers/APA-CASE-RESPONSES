## Generalizable V127 defects

The paired V127 failure demonstrates five source-general ambiguities without relying on any one hidden row or expected count.

### 1. Represented scene-frame support remained under-specified

V127 required support assignment per binding but did not require a prior scene-frame lattice. The worker could therefore reuse a broad support too aggressively when the source established distinct represented interaction, remembered/reported, prospective, reflection, present-telling, or episode-phase contexts without supplying a fresh lexical place/time name.

### 2. Relation austerity became over-pruning

V127 said VERB and LABEL should be admitted only when their own job remained after other coordinates were known. Across both sources this encouraged removal of independently selectable source-native relation/state coordinates merely because participants, content, or result states were already selected. The correct test is recoverability of the exact coordinate job, not whether general meaning survives.

### 3. Surface form still competed with represented job

Wording that looked spatial, temporal, verbal, adjectival, or noun-like continued to invite unstable typing. Support nodes, reified content/evidence, target-bearing states, orientation relations, and operative relations need an explicit job-based decision order that allows distinct jobs to coexist without duplicating one wording merely because multiple grammatical descriptions are possible.

### 4. Scene-evidence expansion needed a bounded-cluster limit

V127 correctly admitted source-named scene evidence and substantially addressed predecessor undercapture, but its rule could still admit concrete nouns that were not independently part of a source-established scene/condition/contrast. The durable rule must distinguish bounded evidence/configuration clusters from incidental nouns or lexical payload already represented by another coordinate.

### 5. Compound projection remained too optional

V127 described compounds as projections of the binding ledger but retained “selective” language. A resolved multi-coordinate binding must project once, while mere co-occurrence and duplicate subset/superset variants remain prohibited. Primitive errors must be fixed before compound construction rather than hidden through compound omission or proliferation.

### 6. Exact-surface protection needed to cover all semantic fields

V127 already prohibited normalization, but the paired failure still showed semantic drift could enter through generated tags or other worker text. The successor therefore makes an explicit final surface audit mandatory and requires lexical `researcher_short_tag` values to remain source-native except for neutral mechanical tags used by lawful unnamed supports.
