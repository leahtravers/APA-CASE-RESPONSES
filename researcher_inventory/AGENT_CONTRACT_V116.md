## Prospective correction basis

V115 failed two successive paired Case 2 + Case 6 calibration executions under verified immutable archetype workbooks while the deterministic apparatus/harness suite passed. The recurring mixed pattern was under-admission of legitimate local coordinates together with over-admission of meaningful wording fragments. The defect is semantic and generalizable: `binding/frame role at natural class grain` allowed broad frame compression without requiring closure of the actual class-native slots in each represented relation instance.

V116 therefore uses one controlling rule:

> **RELATION-SLOT CLOSURE AT NATURAL CLASS GRAIN.** Reconstruct the source as ordered represented relation/binding instances. For each instance, retain the seven-class slots the source actually establishes. A slot may be local, one-use, nested, mundane, low-salience, remembered, reported, prospective, uncertain, negated, figurative, or colloquial. Meaningful wording does not become a primitive unless it is itself the class-native slot at that class's natural grain.

No archetype rows, counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, calibration answers, or sealed holdout material are supplied to the worker.

## Mission

Produce a literal, lightweight Researcher Inventory of the complete supplied source in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The inventory is a source-reconnection index. It is not a token/POS census, sentence parse, clause census, modifier census, semantic summary, exhaustive ontology, or graph closure.

Do not perform APA scoring, psychological interpretation, conclusions, promotion, APA-ID creation, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA database mutation.

## 1. Read the whole source and reconstruct relation instances

Read the complete source before extracting any class. Silently reconstruct the source as an ordered set of materially distinct represented relation/binding instances.

A relation instance is a source-presented action, state, communication, cognition, question, intention, possession, movement, comparison, evaluation, characterization, remembered/reported occurrence, prospective occurrence, or other represented relation that connects class-native roles.

Do not create a new relation instance merely because a sentence, subordinate clause, modifier, auxiliary, connective, or lexical token exists. Do not collapse several source-differentiated relations merely because they occur in one broad scene or narrative episode.

For each represented relation instance, identify only the slots the source actually establishes:

- human/social participant -> `PERSON`
- tracked referent/content -> `OBJECT`
- source-applied characterization/state/evaluation -> `LABEL`
- operative relation kernel -> `VERB`
- independent orientation -> `LOCATOR`
- spatial occurrence support -> `PLACE`
- temporal episode/phase support -> `TIME`

The relation-instance map is an omission-control and grain-control device. It is not shown in the output unless the apparatus schema requires a neutral key.

## 2. Primitive admission: relation-slot test

Retain a proposed primitive only when all of the following hold:

### A. Source grounding
The source explicitly establishes the coordinate in the requested class, or clearly establishes an unnamed PLACE/TIME support slot. Never invent analyst facts.

### B. Class-native function
The candidate performs the represented job of that class, not merely a grammatically compatible form.

### C. Relation-slot entitlement
The candidate occupies a distinct slot in at least one represented relation instance. Ask:

**What exact class-native slot does this candidate fill in the represented relation?**

If no answer exists without paraphrasing the source into a new analytic abstraction, reject it.

### D. Natural grain
Retain the slot itself, not wording nested inside it and not a larger clause wrapper around it. Use the smallest complete source-native span that preserves the slot's identity.

### E. Class-local distinctness
Resolve true coreference, aliases, and same-class restatements. Do not merge genuinely different coordinates merely because they share a scene, episode, target, or relation family.

A coordinate does not need recurrence, broad salience, later reuse, thematic importance, or multiple compounds. Local and one-use slots remain eligible.

## 3. Symmetric closure audit

Before primitive freeze, perform both sides of the audit.

### Missing-slot audit
Replay every represented relation instance in source order. For each instance, ask whether every class-native slot actually established by the source is represented in the relevant ledger. Check especially local, one-use, nested, remembered, reported, prospective, uncertain, negated, and unnamed-support roles.

### Excess-slot audit
For every proposed primitive, identify the exact relation instance and class-native slot that licenses it. Remove the row if it is only:

- wording carried inside another retained slot;
- a grammatical argument without independent class-native status;
- a clause/proposition wrapper not reified by the source;
- a modifier fragment rather than a characterization slot;
- a prepositional/recipient/topic/purpose phrase rather than an orientation slot;
- a lexical verb token that is not the operative relation kernel;
- a physical noun mistaken for scene support;
- a temporal phrase mistaken for a differentiated episode;
- discourse, filler, tense/aspect, auxiliary, article, conjunction, or other grammatical carriage;
- analyst-created interpretation or paraphrase.

The inventory passes only when both closure directions are satisfied.

## 4. Functional typing

Type by represented function, not part of speech or phrase shape.

- represented human/social actor -> `PERSON`
- tracked thing/content/referent -> `OBJECT`
- applied characterization/state/identity/evaluation/comparison/candidate label/correction -> `LABEL`
- scene/location support -> `PLACE`
- episode/period/phase support -> `TIME`
- operative lexical action/state/relation -> `VERB`
- independent position/path/direction/context/orientation -> `LOCATOR`

Cross-class overlap is allowed only when the same exact source span genuinely occupies different class-native slots.

## 5. PLACE — spatial support slots

Retain each spatial support coordinate the source differentiates sufficiently to reconnect a represented relation instance.

A distinct support coordinate may exist within a broader shared physical setting when the source distinguishes an occurrence position, participant position, destination, waiting position, later interaction position, remembered position, prospective position, or present-telling position that cannot truthfully be represented by the broader support alone.

Several relation instances may share one PLACE when the source does not differentiate their support.

Unnamed PLACE is permitted only when the source establishes a distinct spatial support slot without naming its physical details. Use null `source_wording`, exact source cue, and a neutral mechanical tag. Never invent a location.

Do not create PLACE merely from every physical noun, surface, object part, path phrase, or object-location mention. Physical thinghood is OBJECT; orientation is LOCATOR unless a spatial support slot is actually established.

## 6. TIME — temporal support slots

Retain each temporal support coordinate the source differentiates sufficiently to reconnect a represented relation instance: episode, phase, period, recurrence frame, remembered/reported episode, intended/prospective period, or present-reflection episode.

Several relation instances may share one TIME when the source does not separate their temporal support. Conversely, a broad episode does not erase a locally distinct temporal support when the source differentiates one.

Unnamed TIME is permitted only when a distinct episode/phase is established without a named time. Do not create TIME from every clause, predicate, grammatical tense, transition word, question, or duration phrase.

## 7. PERSON — participant slots

Retain the speaker and every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when that actor occupies a participant slot in a represented relation instance.

Direct, offscreen, relational, possessive/beneficiary, remembered, reported, institutional, prospective, peripheral, and one-use actors may qualify. Reject only nonreferential/rhetorical/generic person wording that never becomes a represented participant.

## 8. OBJECT — referent/content slots

Retain each concrete or abstract source-presented referent/content handle that occupies a tracked slot in a represented relation: something acted on, possessed, exchanged, located, checked, compared, selected, rejected, remembered, reported, contemplated, valued, or otherwise related as a thing/content node.

One-use and low-salience referents qualify when the source presents them as slots. Decisions, choices, plans, relations, values, sets/categories, internal content, and proposition-like content qualify only when the source itself reifies or tracks that content as a referential handle.

Do not nominalize every clause, question, intention, relation, grammatical argument, or discourse point into OBJECT.

## 9. LABEL — characterization slots

Retain each smallest complete source-applied characterization, state, status, identity, evaluation, comparison, candidate label, rejection, correction, or materially distinct manner/posture that occupies a characterization slot targeting a represented coordinate.

One-use, colloquial, idiomatic, uncertain, questioned, negated, corrected, rejected, and figurative labels may qualify.

Choose the natural complete judgment unit. Do not split a characterization into adjective/intensifier/polarity fragments, and do not inventory every descriptive modifier, adverb, discourse stance, or rhetorical flourish as LABEL.

## 10. VERB — operative relation kernels

Retain one minimal complete literal predicate kernel for each distinct represented relation instance that has an operative lexical relation.

Qualifying relations may express action, state, stance, cognition, perception, report/communication, intention, question, decision, possession, comparison, evaluation, movement, transition, gesture, existence/location, obligation, or another represented relation. One-use, ordinary, and low-salience relations qualify.

Use the smallest complete source-native kernel. Preserve particles, reflexives, negation, modality, or bound complements when required for relation identity. Split nested or coordinated predicates only when the source presents genuinely distinct operative relation instances.

Exclude pure tense/aspect auxiliaries, copular support whose represented content is fully carried by a LABEL and adds no relation, discourse/filler predicates with no represented edge, duplicate restatements, and lexical verb tokens that merely sit inside a stronger natural relation kernel.

## 11. LOCATOR — orientation slots

Retain each smallest complete exact source span that occupies an independent orientation slot in a represented relation instance: position, path, origin/destination, direction, containment, proximity, accompaniment/carrying, entry/exit, internal/mental/relational position, recurrence/context, or materially spatialized figurative/comparative orientation.

The locator must orient a retained participant, referent, relation, or frame. Do not inventory every prepositional phrase, recipient/topic/purpose argument, possession phrase, degree phrase, temporal phrase, or generic adverbial merely because it is relational wording.

## 12. Literal lock and posture

For every explicit primitive, use the smallest complete exact contiguous source-native span that preserves the coordinate's identity. Every non-null `source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be character-for-character source text.

Preserve negation, modality, uncertainty, question form, attribution, comparison, idiom/dialect, reported/recalled posture, hypothetical/prospective posture, and correction/rejection when semantically required.

Never normalize, improve, translate, diagnose, euphemize, paraphrase, lemmatize into a different surface form, or substitute synonyms.

## 13. Primitive freeze

After relation-slot closure:

1. replay all relation instances;
2. close missing slots;
3. remove wording-only excess;
4. verify PLACE/TIME support grain;
5. verify functional type assignment;
6. verify natural complete spans;
7. resolve coreference and same-class duplicates;
8. order by first source establishment subject to apparatus ordering rules;
9. freeze all seven ledgers.

Do not target a hidden count or infer a hidden archetype.

## 14. Compounds — serialize relation instances

Build compounds only after all primitive ledgers are frozen.

Emit one smallest complete compound for each materially distinct represented relation instance needed to reconnect the frozen coordinates. Use the retained VERB as backbone when one carries the relation. Include only the frozen PERSON/OBJECT/LABEL/PLACE/TIME/LOCATOR coordinates that actually occupy slots in that same relation instance.

A non-VERB characterization/state/question binding may form a compound only when it is distinct represented structure not already carried by another relation.

Do not emit graph closure, arbitrary co-occurrence links, all-pairs links, scene mega-bundles, singleton equivalents, subset/superset permutations, support chains, duplicate restatements, or compounds invented merely to justify a primitive.
