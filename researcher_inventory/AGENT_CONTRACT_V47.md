## 1. Scope

Produce the lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate only. Inventory the source; do not interpret it, score APA, infer psychological meaning, draw conclusions, promote records, mint APA IDs, or admit anything to sovereign/production fabric.

V47 preserves the immutable-archetype gate, literal/source-near lock, hidden-evaluator isolation, candidate-only boundary, failed-attempt preservation, deterministic harness checks, one-shot holdout gate, and clean-room certification requirements. It changes only prospective semantic unit admission/grain, cross-class preservation, and compound reconstruction.

## 2. Why V47 exists

V46 made global binding-role necessity the admission gate. Cross-case calibration under V46 verified both immutable approved archetypes and the deterministic apparatus, then failed hidden semantic adjudication on both cases. The recurring defect was systematic under-admission: source-presented referents, characterizations, predicate kernels, and orientations were absorbed into broader bindings or governing phrases; the lost unit ledger then caused compound failure.

The general V47 correction is **selectable semantic-atom admission**.

The inventory is neither a word/grammar census nor a minimum binding summary. Preserve each distinct source-established semantic coordinate of the requested class at the smallest complete function-bearing grain. Binding reconstruction is required for completeness, coreference, posture, and compound assembly, but a coordinate does not need to be globally necessary to an enclosing binding in order to survive.

A larger phrase, sentence, frame, or compound does not absorb smaller semantic atoms that the source separately establishes. Conversely, mere lexical divisibility is not enough: arbitrary fragments, support grammar, and material with no independent class-native semantic identity remain excluded.

## 3. Whole-source reconstruction before class extraction

Read the complete source before answering any requested class. Build silently:

1. a **frame map** of represented scenes, episodes, interactions, recollections, reports, hypothetical/prospective frames, recurring periods, and present telling/reflection;
2. a **referent map** of persons, concrete things, abstract contents, relations, choices, values, figurative contents, and other source-treated things;
3. a **predicate map** of source-presented actions, relations, reports, perceptions, cognitions, intentions, questions, transitions, states, and evaluations;
4. a **characterization map** of source-applied labels, comparisons, qualities, states, corrections, negations, questioned characterizations, and polarity responses;
5. an **orientation map** of source-presented location, path, direction, origin/destination, containment, accompaniment/carrying, recurrence, and figurative/relational orientation.

These maps are omission and reconciliation tools. They do not impose a minimum-summary filter.

## 4. Selectable semantic-atom admission test

Admit a candidate unit when all are true:

1. **Source support** — the source explicitly establishes it, or it is a permitted supported unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinate anchored to exact source text.
2. **Class-native semantic identity** — it performs a complete semantic job of the requested class rather than serving only as grammar or an arbitrary substring.
3. **Selectable independence** — a researcher could point to this coordinate as a distinct referent, participant, predicate/relation, characterization, place, time/frame, or orientation without inventing a category not present in the source.
4. **Natural atomic grain** — use the smallest complete source-near form that preserves that semantic job. Do not fuse separately established semantic jobs merely because they occur in one clause or binding.
5. **Distinctness** — after coreference, alias, occurrence, and same-function reconciliation, it is not the same coordinate already retained in that class.
6. **No analyst invention** — admission does not depend on interpretation, expected answers, hidden evaluation, or external theory.

Global narrative importance, recurrence, or necessity to summarize an enclosing binding is **not** required.

Local, one-use, nested, possessive, reported, remembered, descriptive, prospective, figurative, or subordinate material may qualify when it passes the semantic-atom test.

## 5. Component preservation without lexical census

When a source span contains several semantic jobs, preserve each independently established atom even if a larger relation also survives.

Examples of the general rule, without case-specific expected answers:

- a reporting predicate and the reported-content predicate are separate when both relations are explicitly represented;
- serial or coordinated predicates are separate when each contributes its own relation rather than functioning only as grammatical support;
- a tracked thing inside a larger content phrase is separate when the source also treats that thing as a referent;
- a characterization is separate from the person/object/relation it characterizes;
- an orientation may coexist with an action when the source separately establishes the movement/position relation.

Do **not** split:

- auxiliaries, tense/aspect support, determiners, complementizers, or bare argument markers;
- idioms or phrasal predicates whose pieces do not preserve the relation independently;
- arbitrary noun/adjective/preposition fragments with no separate semantic identity;
- the same coordinate into multiple rows merely because it is mentioned repeatedly.

The test is semantic-function independence, not token count.

## 6. Omission-first, then excess pass

For each requested class:

1. scan the complete source for every class-native semantic atom;
2. resolve coreference/aliases and repeated mentions;
3. preserve atoms embedded inside larger relations when they remain independently class-native;
4. add supported unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinates only when the source establishes a materially distinct scene/frame without naming it;
5. suppress only support grammar, arbitrary incomplete fragments, true duplicates, and analyst-created abstractions;
6. run a final omission pass;
7. run a final excess pass.

A broader retained unit or future compound is not itself a suppression reason.

## 7. Cross-class rule

Do not force one primary class to erase another independently valid class-native function.

The same or overlapping source span may support more than one class when each class has a distinct semantic job. Surface part-of-speech ambiguity alone is insufficient, but genuine cross-class function is allowed.

Within a class, deduplicate true aliases/coreference and same-function repetitions. Across classes, preserve independently established functions.

## 8. Class rules

### PLACE

Retain each distinct physical scene/location coordinate established for represented action, interaction, waiting, recollection, report, recurring activity, departure/destination, or present telling/reflection.

Broad and contained places may coexist when they are distinct location levels. Local and one-use scenes may qualify. A supported unnamed PLACE uses `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and a neutral navigation tag.

Do not turn every physical noun into PLACE when it functions only as a referent or orientation target.

### TIME

Retain each distinct represented episode, phase, period, recurrence span, recollected/reported interval, intended/hypothetical/prospective interval, transition phase, or present-telling/reflection frame established by the source.

Reconcile multiple cues for the same frame. Broad and contained times may coexist when they distinguish different represented material. A supported unnamed TIME uses `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and a neutral episode tag.

Do not create TIME from tense morphology, every transition word, or every action merely because events occur in time.

### PERSON

Retain the speaker and every distinct source-established human/social actor or stable actor group, including one-use, background, possessive, remembered, reported, prospective, or addressee roles.

Resolve pronouns, titles, kinship terms, and aliases before deduplication. Independent action or narrative centrality is not required.

### OBJECT

Retain every distinct source-established concrete, abstract, internal, relational, choice-like, set/category, value-like, figurative, or represented-content referent that the source treats as something trackable.

Nested and one-use referents may qualify when they have separate referential identity. A larger content phrase does not suppress a smaller tracked thing, and a smaller tracked thing does not automatically suppress a larger content coordinate when both are source-established.

Do not split arbitrary noun-like fragments that the source does not establish as referents.

### LABEL

Retain each distinct source-presented characterization, classification, comparison, state, manner, evaluation, questioned label, correction, acceptance/rejection, polarity response, or descriptive attribution.

Local and one-use characterizations qualify. Split separately presented characterizations even inside one clause. Preserve uncertainty, question, negation, correction, comparison, colloquial form, intensity, and epistemic posture.

Do not create LABEL from grammatical support or from wording that carries no independent characterization job.

### VERB

Retain each distinct **smallest complete source-supported predicate/relation kernel**: action, relation, possession, perception, communication/report, cognition, intention, question, modal relation, transition, location relation, evaluation act, or other operative predicate.

Do not let one broad verb phrase absorb several explicitly represented predicate relations. Outer and embedded predicates may both qualify. Serial, coordinated, reporting, cognition, perception, intention, and control constructions should be tested predicate-by-predicate for independent relation identity.

Keep particles, negation, reflexive material, or complements only when needed to preserve the natural predicate kernel. Arguments that are separately represented PERSON/OBJECT/LABEL/LOCATOR coordinates normally remain outside the VERB unit and are reconnected in compounds.

Exclude pure auxiliaries/support grammar and purely copular shells when the source-presented semantic job is exhausted by a LABEL/LOCATOR and no independent relation remains.

Inventorying a predicate never asserts that its event happened.

### LOCATOR

Retain each distinct smallest complete source-presented orienting span establishing position, path, direction, origin/destination, entry/exit, proximity, containment, accompaniment/carrying, recurrence orientation, or figurative/relational orientation.

Multiple LOCATORs may coexist inside one clause when they establish different orientations. A movement or position expression may have both VERB and LOCATOR functions when each independently passes its class-native test.

Do not retain bare prepositions or ordinary argument markers that do not form a complete orientation.

## 9. Literal lock and posture

- Every non-null `source_wording`, every `source_cue`, and every non-null `order_cue` must be a character-for-character contiguous source substring.
- Preserve punctuation, spelling, dialect, negation, uncertainty, question, correction, comparison, intention, hypothetical, recurrence, report, and prospective posture.
- For explicitly worded coordinates, `researcher_short_tag` may use only words already present in that coordinate's `source_wording` and/or `source_cue`.
- Supported unnamed PLACE/TIME may use a neutral navigation tag grounded by exact source cue.
- Copy literal spans from source; do not reconstruct them from memory.
- `qualities_available` is boolean metadata only and never creates a unit.

## 10. Ordering and identity

Code owns canonical IDs. The worker supplies neutral canonical keys only for alias/coreference merge.

Order units by first source establishment after coreference, subject to apparatus speaker-first PERSON and broad-before-contained PLACE/LOCATOR rules when genuinely established together. Do not reorder by importance or real-world chronology.

## 11. Unit freeze, then relation-instance compounds

Complete and freeze the unit ledger before compounds. Compounds may not create, suppress, merge, repair, rename, or retype units.

Then reread the source and emit compounds for **distinct source-presented relation instances** connecting two or more frozen coordinates.

A compound may represent an action, characterization, comparison, report, cognition, intention, question, possession, evaluation, orientation, frame-linked relation, or another explicit source binding. Nested or overlapping compounds may coexist when they encode genuinely different source-presented relations. A carrier relation does not automatically suppress its content relation, and a broader frame does not automatically suppress a smaller relation instance.

Use the smallest complete set of frozen refs needed for that relation while preserving source direction and posture.

Do not emit combinatorial subset closure, arbitrary co-occurrence bundles, every possible pair, or alternate compounds that merely restate the same relation.

## 12. Final adjudication

Before return:

1. reread the complete source;
2. rebuild frame, referent, predicate, characterization, and orientation maps;
3. perform an omission-first class-native scan;
4. check that broad phrases have not absorbed separately established semantic atoms;
5. check that local/nested/one-use coordinates were not dropped merely for lack of global binding necessity;
6. remove support grammar, arbitrary fragments, true duplicates, and analyst inventions;
7. verify class-native functions independently, including legitimate cross-class overlap;
8. verify natural atomic grain and exact-source locks;
9. freeze units;
10. rebuild source-presented relation-instance compounds from frozen units;
11. remove only duplicate/non-source-attested compound constructions;
12. verify source order and posture.

The target is a **complete lightweight selectable-semantic-atom inventory**: semantically comprehensive at natural class-native grain while excluding grammar census behavior and analyst invention.

Never target an expected count or infer a hidden archetype.
