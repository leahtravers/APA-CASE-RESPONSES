## Mission

Read the complete source and recover a lightweight, source-faithful researcher inventory in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The inventory is not a lexical census and not a complete semantic graph. It is a set of independently trackable research coordinates at natural class-native grain.

Do not perform APA scoring, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA Data Fabric/database mutation.

## 1. Whole-source experience map

Read the whole source before extracting any class. Silently map source-differentiated episodes/scenes, participants, acted-on or referred-to content, characterizations/states, relation edges, orientations, memories, reports, questions, intentions, alternatives, recurring relations, prospective situations, and present reflection.

This map prevents omission. It does not authorize one primitive per word, clause, noun phrase, predicate, modifier, spatial phrase, temporal phrase, or discourse move.

## 2. Two-gate primitive admission

A primitive is admitted only when both gates pass.

### Gate A — exact class-native source identity

The source establishes the candidate at the smallest complete natural literal span for its class, except legitimate unnamed PLACE/TIME support may use `source_wording = null` with an exact source cue and neutral mechanical tag.

### Gate B — independent research coordinate

The candidate must encode a distinct dimension of the represented experience that a researcher could track separately from neighboring wording or already-retained coordinates.

A coordinate qualifies when it supplies its own research axis as one of:

- a participant/social actor;
- a stable concrete, abstract, internal, relational, decision/choice, value, category, content, or figurative handle;
- a characterization/state/identity/evaluation/comparison/posture;
- an event/state/relation edge;
- a differentiated scene/location support coordinate;
- a differentiated episode/period/phase support coordinate; or
- an orientation that separately situates retained structure.

The test is **not** whether the candidate is important, central, repeated, or indispensable to understanding. A legitimate coordinate may be local, one-use, mundane, neutral, nested, subtle, remembered, reported, prospective, or low-salience.

The test is also **not** whether wording is independently interpretable or has a semantic role. Ask instead: **would retaining this candidate give the researcher a separate codeable dimension, rather than merely another lexical realization, detail, or fragment of a dimension already represented?**

## 3. Reject non-coordinate detail

Reject a candidate when it contributes no independent research dimension. This includes, as applicable:

- auxiliaries, tense/aspect/coplanar grammar, bare copular support, and grammatical shells;
- narration/discourse carriage, sequencing, filler, rhetorical organization, quotation/reporting scaffolds with no separately codeable relation;
- scene dressing, incidental props, descriptive color, intensification, flourish, or vivid wording that does not establish a separately trackable state/handle/orientation;
- arbitrary lexical decomposition, noun/predicate/adjective/preposition census;
- free-standing temporal/spatial/deictic wording that does not establish a differentiated support or orientation coordinate;
- proposition/clause wrappers whose research work is already carried by retained coordinates;
- duplicate projections, restatements, and alternate phrasings of the same coordinate.

A candidate is not admitted merely because it is real in the story, can be semantically typed, or could appear in a fully exhaustive semantic graph.

## 4. Projection splitting without atom explosion

When one span contains several genuinely different independent research coordinates, split it into the smallest complete class-native spans that preserve those coordinates.

Do not split grammar, particles, complements, modifiers, scene dressing, or stylistic material merely because linguistically separable. Preserve particles, negation, modality, uncertainty, attribution, comparison, idiom/dialect, and required complements when necessary to keep an admitted coordinate semantically complete.

## 5. PLACE and TIME — differentiated support coordinates

PLACE and TIME are support classes, not token inventories.

After mapping the source, preserve each distinct scene/location or episode/period/phase that functions as a separately trackable support coordinate for represented material. Broad and local support may coexist when they are genuinely different scene/phase dimensions.

Unnamed support is allowed when a scene/phase is clearly differentiated but unnamed. Use `source_wording = null`, exact source cue, and neutral mechanical tag.

Do not create PLACE from every physical noun, destination, or spatial phrase. Do not create TIME from every temporal token, duration, recurrence word, transition, tense, or action.

## 6. PERSON — participant coordinates

Retain the speaker and each distinct source-established human/social actor or stable group that forms a participant coordinate. Participation may be direct, offscreen, remembered, reported, relational, institutional, possessive within a material relation, or prospective.

Merge aliases/coreference. Reject rhetorical, generic, nonreferential addressees and incidental mentions that do not establish a participant coordinate.

## 7. OBJECT — stable research handles

Retain represented concrete, abstract, internal, relational, decision/choice-like, value-like, set/category, content, or figurative handles when the source treats them as stable things/content that a researcher could code separately.

A handle may qualify because it is acted on, possessed, exchanged, checked, contrasted, questioned, remembered, reported, selected, decided about, located, valued, or otherwise reified in a way that forms its own coordinate.

Reject noun census, generic pronouns/deixis, incidental props, arbitrary nominalizations, discourse topics, and proposition wrappers without a separate handle axis.

## 8. LABEL — separately trackable characterization/state

Retain the smallest complete source-native characterization when the source assigns a distinct quality, state, identity, evaluation, comparison, candidate/question label, correction, acceptance/rejection, polarity, manner, or posture that forms a separate research coordinate.

A LABEL may be idiomatic, colloquial, negated, uncertain, figurative, comparative, local, or one-use. Reject descriptive color, intensification, exclamation, rhetorical flourish, and modifiers that merely decorate an already represented coordinate.

## 9. VERB — separately trackable relation edge

Retain the smallest complete source-supported predicate when it establishes an event/state/relation edge that itself forms a distinct research coordinate.

Eligible relation families include actions, states, attempts, responses, movement, possession, perception, communication/report, cognition, intention, questions, decisions, comparison, evaluation, transition, and location/state relations.

Simple and one-use relations may qualify. Reject auxiliaries, bare copular/grammar shells, discourse organizers, stylistic narration, redundant restatements, and incidental predicates that add no separately trackable relation dimension.

Preserve required particles/complements for a qualifying relation without swallowing independently typed coordinates.

## 10. LOCATOR — separately trackable orientation

Retain exact source wording as LOCATOR when it separately situates a retained node, relation, or scene by spatial position, containment, path, direction, origin/destination, proximity, accompaniment, recurrence, procedure/relation, internal/mental context, temporal position, or figurative/comparative orientation.

Reject bare prepositions, argument/topic markers, discourse connectors, unanchored deictics, duplicated TIME support, and orientation wording that merely decorates or repeats an already represented coordinate.

## 11. Functional class assignment

Classify by research function, not surface grammar:

- human/social actor → `PERSON`
- thing/content/referential handle → `OBJECT`
- characterization/state/evaluation/identity → `LABEL`
- scene/location support → `PLACE`
- episode/period/phase support → `TIME`
- event/state/relation edge → `VERB`
- orientation/situating relation → `LOCATOR`

Cross-class overlap is allowed only when the exact same source span genuinely supplies two different independent research coordinates.

## 12. Atomic span and literal lock

For every admitted coordinate, choose the smallest exact contiguous source span that preserves its research identity.

Preserve required particles/prepositions, reflexives, complements, negation, modality, uncertainty, questions, attribution, comparison, idiom/dialect, and hypothetical/prospective/reported/corrective posture when semantically required.

Never normalize, clean, improve, translate, diagnose, euphemize, paraphrase, lemmatize into a different surface form, or substitute synonyms.

Every non-null `source_wording`, every `source_cue`, and every non-null `order_cue` must be a character-for-character contiguous source substring. Neutral mechanical tags are allowed only for legitimate unnamed PLACE/TIME support.

## 13. Identity, ordering, and deduplication

Primitive identity follows research-coordinate identity, not mention count.

Merge true aliases, coreference, same-coordinate repeats, inflection-only repeats, and duplicate head/full-span variants representing one coordinate. Do not merge genuinely distinct coordinates merely because similar or reconstructable from one another.

Code owns canonical IDs. The worker supplies neutral identity keys only for merge. Order by first source establishment after coreference, subject to apparatus speaker-first PERSON and genuine broad-before-contained support rules. Do not reorder by importance or real-world chronology.

## 14. Primitive freeze audit

Repeat until stable, in this order:

1. whole-source experience map;
2. research-coordinate audit — each retained primitive supplies a separate codeable dimension;
3. omission audit — check local, low-salience, one-use, remembered, reported, prospective, and nested dimensions;
4. support audit — check differentiated PLACE/TIME coordinates, including legitimate unnamed support;
5. anti-census audit — remove grammar, discourse carriage, incidental props, style/color, lexical decomposition, and non-coordinate fragments;
6. projection-splitting audit — split only genuinely different research coordinates;
7. functional-class audit;
8. atomic-span audit;
9. literal-lock audit;
10. semantic-dedup audit.

Freeze all primitives only after these audits are stable.

## 15. Relation-instance compounds

Only after all seven primitive classes are frozen, reread the source relation by relation.

For each retained VERB relation instance, emit the smallest complete compound linking only frozen coordinates that actually participate in that exact relation instance. Include participant, content, support, orientation, and characterization refs only when they play a role in that relation.

Additional non-VERB compounds are allowed only when the source represents a relation/state structure not already captured by a retained VERB compound.

Do not emit graph closure, every possible pair, arbitrary co-occurrence bundles, singleton equivalents, generic question closure, duplicate restatements, subset/superset permutations, scene mega-bundles, or compounds containing rejected non-coordinate details. Compounds never justify missing, extra, merged, renamed, or retyped primitives.

## 16. Completion standard

The inventory is complete when the whole source was read; every retained primitive has exact class-native source identity and a separate research-coordinate function; legitimate subtle/local support and relations are not pruned for importance; non-coordinate lexical/grammatical/descriptive/incidental material is excluded; class assignment follows function; literal wording/posture is preserved; and compounds reconstruct retained relation instances only from frozen coordinates.

Never target hidden counts or infer hidden gold.
