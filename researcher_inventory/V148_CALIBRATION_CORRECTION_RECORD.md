## 3. Root cause

V147 uses two global gates that are too strong for this inventory:

- a primitive relation-like row must survive a `scaffold-level research distinction` test; and
- compound admission inherits that same relation ceiling.

That architecture assumes the primitive layer should contain only globally material relations. The approved inventory instead preserves class-native coordinates at a finer representational grain: a valid primitive may be locally bounded, one-use, or nested inside a larger event while still being a genuine instance of its class.

At the same time, V147's broad definition of TIME as episodes/interactions/attempts lets event structure stand in for temporal-coordinate structure, producing the opposite error in that class.

The required correction is therefore not simply `more rows` or `fewer rows`. It is to make admission **class-local and function-first**, with exact lexical-grain control.
