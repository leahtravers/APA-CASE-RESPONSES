## Generalizable requirement admitted

V81 establishes **WHOLE-SOURCE COVERAGE WITH AN INDEPENDENT-RESEARCH-JOB ENTITLEMENT GATE**.

The worker must test the whole source for every requested class, but a positive candidate is not emitted until it passes one of three entitlement routes:

1. **RELATIONAL ROLE** — it fills an independent actor/referent/value/relation/orientation role in a materially represented relation;
2. **SCENE / EPISODE ANCHOR** — it locates a materially distinct physical/institutional scene or episode/period frame at class-native scale; or
3. **STANDALONE SOURCE IDENTITY** — the source treats it as an independently reconnectable thing, content, value, setting, relationship, choice, decision, recurring situation, comparison vehicle, or other stable coordinate.

A whole-source coverage pass means every plausible candidate receives a test. It does **not** mean every candidate that satisfies a broad class definition becomes a coordinate.

## Class-native corrections

### PLACE

PLACE remains scene/setting/occurrence-position scale. A movement phrase, path phrase, recurrence phrase, conversation wording, object support surface, or deictic alone is not PLACE. Supported unnamed PLACE is admitted only when it anchors a materially distinct scene/interaction/wait/present-telling position that would otherwise be lost.

### TIME

TIME remains episode/period scale. Group serial local actions inside the same represented episode. Do not create a separate TIME for every predicate, subordinate event, question, intention, or clause. A source-significant transition, recurring span, explicit period, remembered/reported scene, prospective frame, comparison frame, or present-telling frame may independently qualify.

### OBJECT

OBJECT requires independent source identity. Concrete things and source-reified choices, decisions, relationships, recurring situations, mental contents, amounts, and comparison vehicles may qualify. Event nominalizations, meta-discourse nouns, whole proposition shells, or embedded clause content do not qualify solely because they can be named.

### LABEL

LABEL requires a source-staged value/state/classification/evaluation/status of a coordinate or relation. Do not use LABEL as a general bucket for participant names, amounts, full questions, event propositions, discourse markers, or ordinary relation wording. A polarity response qualifies only when it directly stages acceptance/rejection/correction of a represented classification or value.

### VERB

VERB uses the smallest **complete lexical relation**, not every verbal token. Matrix/control/complement chains remain one relation when the embedded predicate merely completes the matrix relation. Serial or coordinated predicates split only when the source establishes genuinely independent relation jobs with distinct role structure or event identity. A single contemplated course of action may remain one complete relation even when coordinated wording appears.

### LOCATOR

LOCATOR requires an independently reconnectable orientation/path/context job. It may overlap a movement/deictic phrase only when the orientation can be queried separately from the action. Bare prepositional grammar or generic context is insufficient.

## Cross-class rule

Cross-class overlap is permitted only after **each class independently passes its own entitlement gate**. The fact that wording performs one valid job does not create entitlement in another class. PLACE and TIME are not generic context duplicates for every relation; LABEL is not a grammatical-value shadow for every predicate; LOCATOR is not every prepositional complement.

## Primitive and compound sequence

1. read the whole source and resolve coreference;
2. build coarse episode and material-relation ledgers;
3. run whole-source candidate coverage for each requested class;
4. apply the independent-research-job entitlement gate;
5. choose class-native grain and perform bounded cross-class projection;
6. remove aliases, grammatical support, duplicate projections, microframes, and un-reified wrappers;
7. lock literal source strings and freeze primitives;
8. build one materially complete local compound per represented material relation/state from frozen primitives only.

Compounds do not repair primitives. Do not emit pairwise closure, nested subsets, alternate tokenizations, one-compound-per-primitive, or scene-wide mega-bundles.
