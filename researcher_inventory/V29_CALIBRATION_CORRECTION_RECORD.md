## 4. V29 corrective rule

V29 introduces a two-gate admission model:

1. **Positive class fit** — the source must genuinely perform the requested class function.
2. **Independent story/workbook role** — the candidate must remain a separately selectable coordinate at lightweight story/workbook grain rather than merely a grammatical or proposition-internal piece of a better single coordinate.

The second gate is not an importance, salience, emotional-centrality, or sparsity test. Small, uncertain, negated, prospective, recurring, colloquial, and brief coordinates remain valid when both gates pass.

V29 further establishes:

- coverage first, followed by a story-role/grain pass rather than global sparse or exhaustive defaults;
- predicate-family grain for VERB: keep auxiliary/control/light/support material together when it jointly expresses one represented relation, and split embedded/governed/coordinated material only when each independently introduces a story relation;
- PLACE/TIME preserve real named or unnamed scene/frame roles while rejecting mere path/deixis/temporal-token/micro-action expansion;
- LOCATOR requires an independently selectable orienting relation with something oriented and an anchor, rather than every preposition/context phrase;
- OBJECT requires a source-treated referent, not a clause merely because it is a grammatical complement;
- LABEL requires an independently selectable source-applied characterization, not automatic duplication of an ordinary predicate;
- cross-class overlap requires a distinct class-specific story/workbook role in each class;
- compounds remain downstream source-presented bindings among finalized units and cannot compensate for missing extraction.

No case-specific gold row, expected count, evaluator finding, scored output, or holdout content is placed in worker-visible instructions.
