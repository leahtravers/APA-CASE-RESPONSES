## Mission

Produce only a literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate from the supplied source. Recover the source-present research-coordinate inventory at archetypal lightweight resolution while preserving wording and epistemic posture. The target is neither a minimal semantic graph nor an exhaustive grammatical parse.

Do not interpret psychological meaning, score APA, infer protected threads, draw conclusions, promote records, mint APA IDs, or admit anything to sovereign/production fabric. The worker never receives approved archetypes, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, sealed holdout source during calibration, or holdout outputs.

## 1. Research job, not grammar

Each retained primitive must have a source anchor and an independently useful research job in its class. Type assignment follows that research job rather than part of speech or surface syntax.

A span may support more than one class when each projection independently performs a useful job. Cross-class overlap is therefore allowed but never automatic. Do not suppress a valid LABEL because the same wording is verbal, a LOCATOR because it is multiword, or a PLACE/TIME because a broader scene coordinate already exists.

`Lightweight` means bounded source-coordinate coverage: preserve the useful distinctions the source establishes without turning every token, phrase, predicate, modifier, or parse into a coordinate.

## 2. Scene and frame ledger

Before other extraction, construct a source-order ledger of physical and temporal organization.

Retain PLACE/TIME coordinates for source-distinguished settings, positions, interaction phases, waits, remembered/reported scenes, relationship spans, recurring contexts, intended/prospective frames, and present telling/reflection frames when they organize different material.

Two coordinates may be co-located or nested and still both be retained when the source distinguishes their research job. Do not collapse a waiting position into a broad store setting merely because the physical location is the same; likewise do not split one source frame into a new TIME for every predicate, tense, duration token, or movement.

Supported unnamed PLACE/TIME anchors are allowed when a distinct represented coordinate exists but its exact name is unstated.

## 3. Primitive admission test

For each proposed primitive ask:

1. **SOURCE SUPPORT** — is the coordinate grounded in exact source wording/cue, or a supported unnamed PLACE/TIME anchor?
2. **CLASS JOB** — what independently useful research job does this projection perform in this class?
3. **SOURCE DISTINCTION** — does the source treat this coordinate as distinct enough to revisit or bind material, even if it overlaps another coordinate?
4. **BOUNDED GRAIN** — is this the smallest complete source-native unit for that job, rather than an alias, wrapper, parse fragment, or alternate decomposition?

Admit only when all four pass. Stable distinctness does not mean globally minimizing the graph; it means avoiding same-job duplicates while preserving source-distinguished coordinates.

## 4. PLACE

PLACE records a physical setting or position that materially locates represented material.

Retain named places and supported unnamed anchors for distinct participant/object positions, interactions, waits, reported/remembered scenes, prospective positions, or current telling settings. Co-located PLACE coordinates may both survive when they perform different source-distinguished locating jobs.

A concrete referent may also be OBJECT when thing-identity and place-position are independently useful. Resolve true aliases. Do not promote figurative-only language, descriptive nouns, or every path/movement endpoint into PLACE.

## 5. TIME

TIME records source-distinguished episodes, periods, interaction phases, waits, relationship spans, remembered/reported frames, recurring spans, intended/prospective periods, and present telling/reflection frames.

Nested or adjacent TIME coordinates may both survive when they organize different material. Several relations may share one TIME. Do not create TIME from every tense/aspect form, duration/frequency token, temporal adverb, predicate, or local action moment.

## 6. PERSON

Retain `B` and every source-distinguished represented human/social actor or stable group after coreference, including indirect, possessive, relational, offscreen, remembered, reported, prospective, or comparison actors when represented.

Resolve true aliases/coreference. Exclude generic rhetorical addressees and classification words that do not denote represented actors.

## 7. OBJECT

OBJECT records a source-distinguished concrete thing or stable abstract/content/figurative referent independently revisitable as an argument or research coordinate.

Retain stable choices, decisions, relations, feelings/content, propositions, internal referents, figurative entities, services/results, and source-treated abstractions when the source gives them thing-like identity. Preserve concrete things when their identity matters, even when another use of the same referent also supplies PLACE or LOCATOR structure.

Resolve aliases/coreference and repeated surface mentions to one coordinate where they are the same object. Reject one-off clause wrappers, grammatical complements, event restatements, and nominalizations that add no stable referent.

## 8. LABEL

LABEL records the smallest complete source-present characterization value: quality, state, evaluation, comparison, identity/classification, correction/rejection, polarity/status, or materially qualifying manner.

Type by characterization function, not grammar. Participles, verbal/stative phrases, idioms, questioned labels, negations, corrections, and comparisons may be LABEL when the value itself is a reusable coordinate. Preserve the smallest complete source-native wording needed for posture, intensity, uncertainty, or correction.

Do not retain incidental modifiers, discourse filler, or entire clauses when only a smaller characterization value performs the job.

## 9. VERB

VERB records the smallest complete exact source-native relation packet that materially binds coordinates.

Do **not** mechanically reduce a relation to one lexical head. Preserve multiword attempts, reports, cognition, stance, negation, particles, coordinated action, relation complements, waiting/posture, modal/prospective wording, or positional relation wording when those elements are part of the relation identity as expressed.

Do not split auxiliaries/support syntax or emit every parseable embedded verb. A matrix and embedded relation may both survive only when each independently binds coordinates. Arguments that are separate retained coordinates belong in compounds, but relation-defining source wording remains inside the VERB.

Never paraphrase `source_wording`.

## 10. LOCATOR

LOCATOR records the smallest complete source-native orientation/context coordinate that materially locates a retained entity or binding.

This includes physical position/path/direction/origin/destination, entry/exit, containment/support, accompaniment, recurrence-context, internal/figurative orientation, proximity/distance, or another source-present locating relation. A useful locator may be multiword and may overlap another class when orientation remains independently useful.

Reject preposition, recipient, topic, instrument, duration, frequency, discourse-marker, deictic, or temporal-token census when no orientation job exists.

## 11. Literal preservation lock

Every non-null `source_wording`, every `source_cue`, and every non-null `order_cue` must be a character-for-character contiguous source substring. Never substitute researcher tags, paraphrases, normalized dialect, grammatical repairs, or inferred wording.

Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinates may use `source_wording: null`, with an exact source cue. Preserve spelling, punctuation, dialect, contractions, number, questions, negation, uncertainty, correction, comparison, attribution, intention, recurrence/report/prospective posture, and performance wording.

## 12. Ordering and qualities

Order each class by first source establishment after coreference, with speaker first in PERSON.

`qualities_available` is true only when source-present qualities/descriptions are available for that coordinate. Quality availability does not force a separate LABEL, and a LABEL may still coexist with another class when it independently passes admission.

## 13. Proposition-level compound reconstruction

Freeze primitives before compounds. Re-read the source for materially distinct local propositions/bindings.

Create one materially complete compound for a distinct source-local relation, attribution, characterization proposition, question, position, comparison, or other research binding. Include all and only frozen coordinates materially co-bound in that proposition.

Prefer one complete proposition bundle over several nested partials. Multiple nearby relations, labels, and locators that jointly express one proposition should normally be represented together. Split only when the source establishes separately useful bindings with changed actor, argument, target, attribution, comparison, question, position, or frame.

A primitive may remain unbundled. Do not emit pairwise/exhaustive closure, one compound per primitive, alternate subset/superset decompositions, duplicate thematic bundles, or scene-wide supersets.

## 14. Final audits

**Scene/frame audit:** recover omitted source-distinguished PLACE/TIME coordinates, including co-located or nested anchors whose research jobs differ; remove mere token/predicate/time-word census.

**Class-job audit:** revisit every class by researcher job rather than grammar. Permit cross-class overlap only when each projection independently passes admission. Check coreference and duplicate surface mentions.

**Relation audit:** make each VERB the smallest complete source-native relation packet, neither atomized lexical head nor clause wrapper.

**Orientation audit:** recover contextual, recurrence, physical, internal, and figurative LOCATOR coordinates when orientation is independently useful; remove non-orientation fragments.

**Compound audit:** collapse nested/partial duplicates into the smallest materially complete proposition bundle and remove compounds without a distinct research binding.

Then verify literal source spans, source order, type assignment, qualities flags, compound references, and candidate-only boundaries.
