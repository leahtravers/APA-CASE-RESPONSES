THEN REMOVE ONLY TRUE SCAFFOLDING, DUPLICATE IDENTITY, OR SPANS WITH NO REPRESENTED COORDINATE JOB. ## Mission

Produce a literal, lightweight Researcher Inventory of the complete supplied source in exactly seven primitive classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Then build selective compounds only from frozen registered primitives.

The inventory is a researcher-facing coordinate system at the source's natural editorial grain. It is neither a sparse summary nor a lexical census. A coordinate does not need to be important, recurrent, central, polished, or explicit in analytical language. It needs a stable represented job in the source.

## 1. Read the whole source first

Read the complete source before answering any class. Resolve obvious coreference and notice changes in represented setting, period, participants, remembered/reported material, prospect, question, correction, comparison, uncertainty, and present reflection.

Do not emit private reasoning or a hidden scene model.

## 2. Pass A — coverage-first source sweep

Replay the source in order and provisionally mark every exact source-native nucleus that performs at least one represented researcher-coordinate job:

- human/social participant;
- concrete or abstract thing/content handle;
- target-bearing characterization/state/status/evaluation;
- operative action/state/relation kernel;
- orientation/path/containment/direction/context relation;
- explicit spatial support;
- explicit temporal period/support.

At this pass, **do not prune because a candidate feels minor, ordinary, one-use, local, obvious, grammatical, colloquial, uncertain, negated, remembered, reported, prospective, figurative, idiomatic, peripheral, or insufficiently important**. Coverage comes first.

Common or grammatically light wording is not automatically scaffolding. A relation can be ordinary and still be the exact source relation that connects represented coordinates. A local thing, state, or orientation can occur once and still be a valid coordinate.

Literal wording alone is not final entitlement, but salience is not entitlement either. The question at Pass A is: **does this exact source nucleus perform a represented coordinate job that a researcher could stably point back to without inventing language or meaning?**

## 3. Pass B — anti-census pruning

Only after Pass A coverage is complete, remove a provisional candidate when its only function is one of the following:

- pure narration/discourse management with no represented relation or content handle of its own;
- auxiliary/support morphology that contributes no distinct represented relation beyond a stronger natural predicate nucleus;
- generic or nonreferential pronoun/deictic wording with no stable represented endpoint or orientation;
- a proposition/question shell whose represented content is fully carried by other selected coordinates and which is not itself treated as content;
- detached intensity, filler, or rhetorical color with no target-bearing state/evaluation or orientation job;
- a true duplicate/restatement of the same class-local source identity after coreference;
- a fragment nested inside a stronger natural nucleus where splitting would destroy the source's represented job;
- wording that has no represented coordinate role and would be included only to make the inventory exhaustive at the token, phrase, or clause level.

When uncertain between keeping and pruning, preserve the candidate if the source gives it a distinct represented job or makes another selected coordinate depend on it. Do not prune merely because the candidate is mundane or appears only once.

## 4. Exact source nucleus and editorial grain

For every explicit primitive choose the smallest complete exact source-native unit that performs its represented job without destroying that job.

Keep together what functions together: phrasal relations, particles, reflexives, required complements, negation, modality, comparison, idiom, and orientation constructions when splitting would change the represented relation or state.

Do not inflate a primitive into a whole clause, proposition, rhetorical sentence, or question shell when a smaller exact nucleus carries the job. Do not shrink a natural phrase until its relation, target, polarity, orientation, or idiomatic meaning changes.

Every non-null `source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be character-for-character source text.

Never normalize, polish, translate, diagnose, euphemize, substitute synonyms, regularize grammar, or lemmatize into a different surface form.

## 5. Pass C — unnamed PLACE/TIME support reconstruction

After explicit candidates have been covered and pruned, reconstruct unnamed spatial and temporal support separately.

Create an unnamed `PLACE` or `TIME` support when all are true:

1. the source differentiates a represented where-frame or when-frame from another represented frame;
2. at least one surviving coordinate or compound would otherwise be materially mislocated, conflated with another frame, or lose a source-established distinction;
3. no explicit source-native PLACE/TIME primitive already supplies that support adequately;
4. an exact source cue anchors the support; and
5. the support can be described mechanically without inventing where or when.

The support may be local, one-use, ordinary, remembered, reported, prospective, or the present telling/reflection frame. It does **not** need to be globally important or to contain a large configuration.

Use null `source_wording`, an exact source cue, and a neutral mechanical short tag for unnamed support.

An action, question, decision, intention, proposition, remembered content, or rhetorical span is not itself `TIME` merely because it occurs or marks a discourse segment. It may establish evidence for an unnamed period when the source differentiates that period.

A concrete thing, destination phrase, spatial image, or orientation phrase is not itself `PLACE` merely because it is spatial. It may be another class while also helping establish an unnamed support.

Two supports are distinct when merging them would place one or more surviving coordinates in the wrong source-established where/when frame. Do not merge distinct local supports merely because the source omits a formal place or date name.

## 6. Type by represented job, not surface shape

Assign one primary class by the job the surviving nucleus performs in the represented source:

- spatial support -> `PLACE`
- temporal period/support -> `TIME`
- represented human/social actor or stable group -> `PERSON`
- independently represented thing/content handle -> `OBJECT`
- target-bearing characterization/state/status/evaluation -> `LABEL`
- operative action/state/relation kernel -> `VERB`
- orientation/path/containment/direction/context relation -> `LOCATOR`

Grammar is evidence, not authority. A noun-shaped phrase can be a LABEL or another class; a verbal phrase can be OBJECT/content or LABEL; a spatial phrase can be OBJECT/content or LOCATOR rather than PLACE; a temporal phrase can orient rather than constitute TIME.

Cross-class duplication is allowed only when the source genuinely establishes two distinct researcher-coordinate jobs, not because a phrase can be grammatically described in two ways.

## 7. Class rules

### PLACE

Return explicit or lawfully reconstructed spatial supports that answer **where a represented configuration is situated**. Broad and contained supports may both qualify when the source differentiates them. Local and unnamed supports may qualify. Do not turn every physical noun, destination, comparison, orientation phrase, or spatial image into PLACE.

### TIME

Return explicit or lawfully reconstructed temporal periods/supports that answer **which differentiated represented period a configuration occupies**. Local episode periods, remembered/reported periods, prospective periods, and a source-established present reflective frame may qualify. Do not turn every event clause, action, question, or discourse segment into TIME.

### PERSON

Return the speaker plus each distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when the actor functions as participant, relation endpoint, possessor/beneficiary, remembered/reported actor, institutional actor, prospective actor, peripheral actor, or one-use actor. Reject rhetorical/generic/nonreferential person wording.

### OBJECT

Return concrete or abstract thing/content handles that function as independently represented endpoints/nodes. This includes source-distinguished parts, amounts/values, services/results, choices/decisions treated as things, internal or mental content handles, named sets/categories, remembered/reported content, and figurative/quoted content when the source refers to it as content.

A physical detail qualifies when the source separately acts on, checks, locates, compares, possesses, moves, refers to, reasons about, or uses it as an endpoint. Reject scenic furniture that never acquires its own represented role, generic noun phrases, bare pronouns/deictics, and nominalized actions with no independent thing/content job.

### LABEL

Return exact target-bearing characterization/state/status/evaluation coordinates. A LABEL may be one-use, uncertain, questioned, negated, corrected, rejected, comparative, colloquial, idiomatic, or figurative. Preserve the complete natural characterization phrase when that is what the source applies.

Require a represented target or state being characterized. Reject decorative description, generic manner, detached intensity, and characterization wording with no represented predication/evaluation job.

### VERB

Return exact operative action/state/relation kernels that connect or position represented coordinates or establish a represented state. Use the smallest complete natural predicate nucleus and preserve particles, bound complements, reflexives, negation, modality, comparison, or idiomatic structure when needed for identity.

Do not create one VERB for every lexical verb, but do not reject a valid relation merely because it is common, grammatically light, perceptual, cognitive, positional, copular, possessive, or otherwise ordinary. Reject only auxiliaries/support morphology with no distinct represented relation, discourse shells whose content is carried elsewhere, true duplicate restatements, whole-clause wrappers, and fragments inside a stronger natural kernel.

### LOCATOR

Return exact source-native orientation/context relations that independently position or orient represented coordinates: position, path, direction, containment, proximity, entry/exit, origin/destination, accompaniment, recurrence/context, internal/relational orientation, or spatialized figurative orientation.

Local, colloquial, idiomatic, and one-use orientation phrases may qualify. Prefer the complete natural orientation phrase over a bare deictic when that phrase carries the relation. Reject recipient/topic/purpose/possession/degree complements, generic adverbials with no orientation job, and every-preposition behavior.

## 8. Coverage audit before freeze

Replay the source once more in order. For every represented segment ask whether any surviving coordinate is missing from these families:

- actor or social endpoint;
- separately handled thing/content/part/value/choice/internal handle;
- target-bearing characterization/state/evaluation;
- operative action/state/relation kernel;
- orientation relation;
- differentiated spatial support;
- differentiated temporal support.

For each surviving primitive ask:

1. What represented job does it perform?
2. What exact source nucleus or lawful unnamed-support cue entitles it?
3. Is the grain the smallest complete natural unit for that job?
4. Is it distinct after true coreference/restatement?
5. Is its class determined by source use rather than surface grammar?

Restore a candidate when it has a distinct represented job even if it is minor or one-use. Remove it only when the anti-census exclusions actually apply.

Freeze primitives only after this coverage audit.

## 9. Compounds after freeze only

A compound is a selective source-local binding among two or more frozen primitive refs.

Build a compound when the source represents one local relation, characterization, question, choice, report, remembered/reported event, comparison, or reflection whose participating frozen coordinates jointly form one coherent binding.

Include **all and only** frozen coordinates that actually participate in that local binding:

- actors/endpoints;
- operative relation;
- target/content/object;
- target-bearing label/state;
- locator/orientation relation when represented as part of the binding;
- PLACE/TIME support when it situates that binding in a source-distinguished frame.

Do not omit a participating support or orientation merely because it feels redundant. Do not add unrelated scene context merely because it is nearby in the source.

Split compounds when actor set, target/content, source posture, support frame, or independently represented relation changes. Questions, choices, reports, and reflections can organize a compound without forcing their whole clause to become a primitive.

Do not create compounds to justify primitives. Do not create one compound per sentence, clause, lexical verb, primitive, phrase, or support. Do not create arbitrary co-occurrence, all-pairs links, scene mega-bundles, support chains, singleton equivalents, duplicate restatements, alternate subset/superset decompositions, or graph closure.
