## 2. Generalized V146 defect

V146 correctly eliminated the V145 requirement that every relation-like primitive be independently reusable. It also corrected the runtime topology so all seven classes and compounds could be resolved from one whole-source semantic pass.

The corrected run nevertheless failed both approved archetypes with the same two-sided pattern:

- **structural undercoverage:** distinct source-staged positions, episode/frame coordinates, low-salience referents, and some class-native relations remained omitted;
- **relation overcoverage:** locally meaningful predicates, reports, questions, support/copular material, evaluative wording, orientation wrappers, and proposition-like content were admitted too readily as primitives and compounds;
- **typing instability:** spatial-sounding wording was sometimes typed as PLACE even when its represented function was another class;
- **compound inflation:** because the relation ledger itself was too broad, compound emission approximated semantic/event parsing rather than a lightweight research inventory.

The cross-case recurrence after the corrected one-turn harness demonstrates a generalizable semantic-selection defect rather than a deterministic harness failure.

## 3. Root cause

V146 made the relation ledger the principal admission engine. Its positive `research-bearing relation` criteria allowed nearly any explicit action, state, report, perception, cognition, question, evaluation, intention, or prospective relation to seed primitive slots when local meaning would be lost by omission.

That threshold is too close to semantic parsing. At the same time, tying PLACE/TIME and other structural coordinates to retained relations allowed low-salience or unnamed structural coordinates to disappear when no relation was first selected strongly enough to demand them.

The result is a paradoxical inventory that is simultaneously too semantically dense and structurally incomplete.

## 4. V147 corrective principle

V147 restores **frame/coordinate scaffold primacy** without restoring V145's over-strict independent-handle gate for relation-bound slots.

The new order is:

`represented frames -> structural PERSON/OBJECT/PLACE/TIME scaffold -> scaffold-bounded relation ledger -> relation-bound VERB/LABEL/LOCATOR slots -> structural recall -> relation ceiling -> identity reconciliation -> frozen primitives -> compounds`

The relation ledger is now a bounded reconstruction layer over the scaffold, not a census of everything the source predicates.

A relation-like primitive may remain one-use and relation-bound, but it must preserve a distinct scaffold-level connection/state/transition/contrast/posture. Local sentence meaning, semantic category membership, or grammatical predication alone is insufficient.

## 5. Structural correction

PLACE and TIME are explicitly treated as **semantic coordinates rather than lexical phrase classes**.

A distinct source-staged position or episode/frame may require an unnamed coordinate even when no noun/date names it. Conversely, explicit spatial or temporal wording does not become PLACE/TIME merely because it contains spatial/temporal vocabulary.

PERSON and OBJECT remain structural only when the source establishes a stable social endpoint or source-treated referent. Relations may not manufacture OBJECT/PLACE/TIME rows merely to complete their argument structure.

## 6. Relation ceiling

V147 adds a contrastive research-grain ceiling:

- retain a relation when it materially connects, changes, characterizes, orients, contrasts, or transitions the frozen scaffold or retained frame boundary;
- suppress matrix, reporting, control, support, copular, or cognitive wording when it merely scaffolds a more specific retained relation and adds no distinct scaffold-level relation of its own;
- suppress alternate parses, redundant re-expression, and clause-level propositions that add only local semantic detail;
- preserve brief/generic/negative/questioned/colloquial wording when it is the smallest exact identity of a genuinely retained scaffold-level relation.

This preserves the valid V146 relaxation for relation-bound atoms while adding the ceiling V146 lacked.

## 7. Typing correction

V147 strengthens functional typing:

- spatial-sounding wording is PLACE only when it denotes a represented where-coordinate;
- temporal-sounding wording is TIME only when it denotes a represented frame/episode coordinate;
- conceptual or referential `space/place/time` language may instead be OBJECT/LABEL/LOCATOR when that is the source function;
- LOCATOR preserves orientation without substituting for a distinct PLACE/TIME coordinate.

The rule is general and does not encode any case-specific gold answer.

## 8. Compound correction
