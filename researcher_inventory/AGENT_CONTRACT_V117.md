## Prospective correction basis

V116 completed two preserved paired Case 2 + Case 6 diagnostic executions under the same finalized V116 semantic contract and the same established evaluator family. In both executions the immutable Leah-approved workbook copies passed their required SHA-256 gates before semantic work, the deterministic apparatus/harness checks passed, and the paired semantic comparison failed before the sealed holdout was reached.

The repeated defect is generalizable. `RELATION-SLOT CLOSURE AT NATURAL CLASS GRAIN` still invited the worker to treat each locally represented relation instance as if it independently owed a complete seven-class slot inventory and a compound. That creates a micro-semantic census: internal clause/content wrappers and predicate fragments can be over-admitted, while the source-reconnection supports and natural complete bindings that an editor preserves across an interaction, episode, later report, recurrence, present reflection, or prospective frame can still be under-represented.

V117 therefore uses one controlling rule:

> **SOURCE-RECONNECTION COORDINATE CLOSURE AT NATURAL FRAME GRAIN.** Build a lightweight index of the source-native coordinates a researcher would select to reconnect the materially represented people, things/content, characterizations, relations, orientations, places, times, and complete bindings. Do not inventory every grammatical or semantic slot merely because it exists locally. Do not prune a coordinate merely because it is local, one-use, mundane, nested, remembered, reported, prospective, uncertain, negated, figurative, or colloquial.

No archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, calibration answers, or sealed holdout material are supplied to the worker.

## Mission

Produce a literal, lightweight Researcher Inventory of the complete supplied source in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The inventory is a source-reconnection index. It is not a token/POS census, sentence parse, clause census, semantic-role census, proposition census, event ledger, exhaustive ontology, or graph closure.

Do not perform APA scoring, psychological interpretation, conclusions, promotion, APA-ID creation, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA database mutation.

## 1. Read the whole source and build a reconnection-frame map

Read the complete source before extracting any class. Silently reconstruct a small whole-source map of the materially differentiated frames needed to reconnect the narrative or report.

A **reconnection frame** is not every sentence, clause, predicate, or semantic relation. It is a source-differentiated occurrence position, interaction, episode, reported/remembered frame, intended/prospective frame, recurrence context, comparison frame, later conversation/report, or present-reflection frame that organizes material coordinates.

Several relations may live inside one frame. A broad physical scene may contain several materially different local occurrence positions. A broad narrative period may contain several materially different episodes. Conversely, adjacent wording does not create a new frame merely because grammar changes.

Use the map only to prevent omission and over-fragmentation. Do not output the map unless the apparatus schema explicitly requests a neutral identity key.

## 2. Primitive admission: reconnection-coordinate test

Retain a proposed primitive only when all five conditions hold.

### A. Source grounding

The source explicitly establishes the coordinate in the requested class, or clearly establishes an unnamed PLACE/TIME support coordinate. Never invent analyst facts.

### B. Class-native function

The candidate performs the represented job of that class, not merely a grammatically compatible form.

### C. Reconnection value

The candidate is a selectable coordinate that materially helps reconnect a source-presented participant, referent/content handle, characterization, operative relation, orientation, spatial support, or temporal support at the inventory's natural resolution.

Ask:

**If this row were absent, would a researcher lose a materially distinct source coordinate rather than merely lose wording already recoverable inside a stronger retained coordinate or binding?**

A candidate may pass even when it is one-use, local, nested, ordinary, or low-salience. Reconnection value is not a recurrence, importance, or independence test.

### D. Natural grain

Retain the smallest complete source-native coordinate for the class. Do not split a natural predicate, characterization, orientation, or referential handle into internal fragments merely because the fragments are semantically meaningful. Do not wrap a larger clause around the coordinate merely because the clause explains it.

### E. Class-local distinctness

Resolve true coreference, aliases, and same-class restatements. Do not merge genuinely different coordinates merely because they share a scene, episode, target, or relation family.

## 3. Frame-support closure for PLACE and TIME

PLACE and TIME are support coordinates for reconnection, not just explicit location/time nouns and not one row per clause.

Before primitive freeze, replay the source by reconnection frame and ask whether each materially differentiated frame has the spatial and temporal support needed to reconnect it.

A distinct support coordinate may be warranted when the source materially differentiates, for example:

- a broad setting from a contained occurrence position;
- an object's materially tracked location;
- a destination or place another participant is located;
- a waiting position;
- a later interaction/conversation position;
- a remembered or reported position;
- a present-telling position;
- an initial attempt from a help/response episode;
- a departure/transition from the preceding interaction;
- a waiting episode;
- an intended period;
- a later report/conversation;
- a recurring span;
- a present reflection;
- a prospective/future frame.

These are categories, not a required checklist. Split only when the source differentiates the support enough that collapsing it would lose reconnection structure. Share a support row across multiple relations when the source does not materially differentiate their support.

Unnamed PLACE/TIME is lawful only when the source establishes the distinct support but does not name its physical or temporal details. Use null `source_wording`, an exact source cue, and a neutral mechanical tag. Never invent where or when.

## 4. Functional typing and overlap arbitration

Type by represented function, not part of speech, phrase shape, or metaphoric vocabulary.

- represented human/social actor -> `PERSON`
- tracked thing/content/referential handle -> `OBJECT`
- source-applied characterization/state/status/evaluation/comparison/identity -> `LABEL`
- operative lexical action/state/relation -> `VERB`
- independent position/path/direction/context/orientation -> `LOCATOR`
- spatial support -> `PLACE`
- temporal support -> `TIME`

When a span is ambiguous, ask what job the row performs in the source-reconnection index.

A state or evaluation does not become LOCATOR merely because its wording is spatialized. A relational phrase does not become OBJECT merely because it can be nominalized. A copular or support phrase does not become VERB merely because it contains a verb. A descriptive phrase does not become LABEL merely because it modifies something.

Cross-class overlap is allowed only when the same exact source span genuinely performs two different selectable jobs. Do not manufacture overlap from grammar alone.

## 5. PERSON — represented participant coordinates

Retain the speaker and every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when the actor materially participates in or anchors a source-presented relation/frame.

Direct, offscreen, relational, possessive/beneficiary, remembered, reported, institutional, prospective, peripheral, and one-use actors may qualify. Reject nonreferential/rhetorical/generic person wording that never becomes a represented participant.

## 6. OBJECT — tracked referent/content coordinates

Retain each concrete or abstract source-presented referential handle that is materially trackable across a binding or needed to reconnect what the source treats as a thing/content node.

Qualifying objects may include concrete things, parts, amounts/values, services/results, decisions/next steps, contemplated choices treated as choices, recurring relations treated as content, named sets/categories, remembered/reported content, and internal represented objects.

A phrase does **not** become OBJECT merely because it is a grammatical argument, pronoun, indefinite complement, question content, clause wrapper, discourse point, or semantically meaningful proposition. For abstract content, require that the source itself treat the content as a referential handle or selectable thing beyond the predicate wording that states it.

Use the smallest complete exact source span available. Do not replace source wording with an analyst noun phrase.

## 7. LABEL — source-applied characterization coordinates

Retain each smallest complete source-applied characterization, state, status, identity, evaluation, comparison, candidate label, rejection, correction, or materially distinct manner/posture that is independently selectable as a characterization of a represented target.

One-use, colloquial, idiomatic, uncertain, questioned, negated, corrected, rejected, and figurative labels may qualify.

Choose the natural complete judgment unit. Do not split intensifier, polarity, adjective, or adverb fragments from one natural characterization. Do not create LABEL from every descriptive modifier or from wording whose represented job is only the operative relation.

A span may support both LABEL and VERB only when the source genuinely presents both a selectable characterization and an operative relation; do not duplicate by default.

## 8. VERB — natural operative relation kernels

Retain the smallest complete literal predicate kernel for each **materially reconnective operative relation**, not every lexical verb and not every clause-level semantic relation.

A qualifying relation may express action, state, cognition, perception, report/communication, intention, question, decision, possession, comparison, evaluation, movement, transition, gesture, existence/location, obligation, or another represented relation when that relation is a useful source coordinate.

Preserve particles, reflexives, negation, modality, and bound complements when required for the relation's identity.

Keep a coordinated or bound predicate together when its parts jointly form one source-native candidate action, movement, choice, or relation kernel. Split coordinated/nested predicates when the source presents genuinely separate operative relations that remain independently selectable.

Do not retain:

- pure tense/aspect auxiliaries;
- copular/support carriage whose represented content is fully supplied by another retained coordinate and adds no material relation;
- interrogative/support predicates whose only job is grammatical framing of a separately represented measure/frame;
- discourse/filler predicates;
- duplicate restatements;
- lexical verb fragments nested inside a stronger natural predicate kernel.

## 9. LOCATOR — independent orientation/context coordinates

Retain each smallest complete exact source span that independently orients a represented participant, referent, relation, or frame.

Qualifying orientation may include position, path, origin/destination, direction, containment, proximity, accompaniment/carrying, entry/exit, recurrence/context, internal/mental/relational position, or materially spatialized figurative/comparative orientation.

A purpose/destination construction may qualify when it actually supplies direction/orientation rather than merely a grammatical complement. A recurrence clause may qualify when it supplies the recurring context in which a represented relation is located.

Do not inventory every prepositional phrase, recipient/topic phrase, possession phrase, degree phrase, temporal phrase, generic adverbial, or evaluative state as LOCATOR.

## 10. Literal lock and source posture

For every explicit primitive, use the smallest complete exact contiguous source-native span that preserves the coordinate's identity. Every non-null `source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be character-for-character source text.

Preserve negation, modality, uncertainty, question form, attribution, comparison, idiom/dialect, reported/recalled posture, hypothetical/prospective posture, and correction/rejection when semantically required.

Never normalize, improve, translate, diagnose, euphemize, paraphrase, lemmatize into a different surface form, correct the speaker's spelling/grammar, or substitute synonyms.

## 11. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds:

1. replay the reconnection-frame map in source order;
2. verify materially differentiated PLACE/TIME supports are represented without per-clause proliferation;
3. verify each retained PERSON/OBJECT/LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR has independent reconnection value at class-native grain;
4. restore local, one-use, remembered, reported, prospective, uncertain, negated, and figurative coordinates that were pruned only for low salience;
5. remove clause/content wrappers, modifier fragments, support verbs, and orientation fragments whose information is already carried by a stronger retained coordinate/binding;
6. arbitrate OBJECT/LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR by represented function;
7. verify natural complete spans, literal lock, coreference, and class-local distinctness;
8. order by first source establishment subject to apparatus ordering rules; and
9. freeze all seven ledgers.

Do not target a hidden count or infer a hidden archetype.

## 12. Compounds — materially complete source bindings

Build compounds only after all primitive ledgers are frozen.

A compound is a **materially complete source binding**, not a receipt that every primitive or predicate was inventoried.

Emit a compound when combining frozen coordinates materially reconnects a source-presented interaction, proposition, characterization, question, comparison, choice, reflection, or other complete binding that would otherwise be lost as structure.

Use these rules:

- group locally joint predicates, participants, referents, qualities, orientations, and support coordinates when the source presents them as one materially complete interaction or proposition;
- several retained VERBs may appear in one compound when they jointly describe one local interaction/binding;
- split compounds when participant set, target/content, support frame, source posture, or materially distinct judgment/action changes enough that one bundle would blur separate structure;
- a non-VERB characterization/state/question may form a compound when it is independently material structure;
- a valid primitive may remain unbundled when no material multi-coordinate binding requires it;
- never invent a compound merely to justify a primitive.

Do not emit one compound per sentence, clause, predicate, or relation instance. Do not emit graph closure, arbitrary co-occurrence, all-pairs links, scene mega-bundles, singleton equivalents, subset/superset permutations, support chains, or duplicate restatements.
