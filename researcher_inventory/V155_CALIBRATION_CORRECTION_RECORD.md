## 4. Root cause

V154 asks whether a candidate establishes one of seven class functions and then chooses class-native grain. Its `VERB inventories distinct source-native relation moves` formulation is broad enough to make ordinary predicates appear entitled to primitive status.

The correction must separate two questions that V154 still partially conflates:

1. **Admission:** does this occurrence function as a distinct researcher-addressable role in the represented situation graph?
2. **Span:** if admitted, what is the smallest exact source-native wording that preserves that role?

Minimal wording is a representation rule, not an admission rule.

A relation, descriptor, orientation phrase, temporal phrase, spatial phrase, or referential shell can be perfectly source-faithful and class-compatible while still belonging only to support/evidence rather than the primitive ledger.

## 7. Relation admission before relation grain

### VERB

VERB is not a predicate census.

A source-native action/state/process is admitted only when the source stages it as a distinct represented connection or state in the situation graph. Support, copular, reporting, cognition, intention, modal, and discourse verbs are neither categorically excluded nor automatically admitted. They survive only when the source stages that act itself as a researcher-addressable relation rather than using it as scaffolding around another relation.

Only after admission does V154's minimal relation-nucleus rule apply.

### LOCATOR

LOCATOR is not a preposition/path census.

An orientation is admitted only when it materially positions or relates a retained participant, referent, coordinate, movement, or comparison frame. Grammatical markers and incidental spatial/comparison wording remain support unless independently staged as orientation.

Only after admission is the minimal exact orientation nucleus selected.

### LABEL

LABEL requires a distinct characterization act applied to a retained coordinate/relation or itself staged as an addressable represented state/category. Incidental description, reaction, emphasis, and texture remain evidence/quality rather than primitives.

### OBJECT

OBJECT continues to allow abstract, relational, decision-like, event-like, and proposition-like handles when the source itself reifies them. Transient discourse/metalinguistic/grammatical shells remain excluded without independent source-treated standing.
