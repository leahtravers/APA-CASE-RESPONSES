1. **Packet inflation.** V144 permits a relation, characterization, or locally meaningful clause to constitute its own packet. That makes the packet-necessity counterfactual nearly tautological: once a local semantic item has been allowed to define or occupy a packet, removing it necessarily changes that packet. The result is lexical/grammatical leakage despite the nominal anti-census rule.
2. **Scaffold omission.** At the same time, unnamed scene positions, organizing phases, later-reporting positions, minor social endpoints, and other low-salience structural coordinates can still be omitted because the worker is deciding local semantic packets before it has frozen the narrative scaffold those packets must inhabit.
3. **Over-splitting of relations.** Verb/support/control/copular material is often emitted as independent relation atoms when the source-native research relation is a larger contiguous lexical kernel or when the support item has no independent research handle.
4. **Orientation leakage.** Spatial-, temporal-, or argument-looking expressions can become LOCATOR/TIME/PLACE rows merely because they situate a clause locally, instead of because they establish a durable orientation or scene coordinate in the inventory.
5. **Referent leakage.** Local proposition shells, discourse placeholders, and transient argument phrases can become OBJECT rows even when the source does not treat them as independently trackable referents.
6. **Downstream compound failure.** Compound mismatch is dominated by unstable primitive membership: compounds are assembled from extra local atoms while required scaffold coordinates are absent. No new deterministic compound-harness defect is demonstrated.
7. **Literal drift remains independently visible.** The failed V144 Case 6 output introduced forbidden normalizations. Exact-source and posture locks therefore remain mandatory and must not be weakened while correcting density.

The failure is case-independent: the contract lacks a stable distinction between a **research-inventory coordinate** and a merely **locally meaningful semantic/grammatical contribution**.

## V145 correction — frame scaffold before relation inventory

`RI-CONTRACT-V145` replaces packet-first primitive admission with **frame-scaffold coordinate resolution**.

The general rule is:

1. First resolve a bounded set of **research frames** only: distinct occurrences/interactions, remembered or reported episodes, standing relationships, periods/phases, prospective situations, and present reflection/telling frames that organize the source. A relation, predicate, modifier, noun phrase, or clause does not become a frame merely because it carries meaning.
2. Within those frames, freeze the **structural coordinate scaffold** before lexical relation extraction: stable PERSON endpoints, source-treated OBJECT referents, and PLACE/TIME coordinates that locate or organize the represented material. Unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinates remain lawful when the source clearly stages the position/frame.
3. LABEL, VERB, and LOCATOR are then admitted only when they independently characterize, connect, or orient frozen scaffold coordinates or define a retained frame boundary. They may not self-create a frame in order to justify themselves.
4. Every primitive must pass an **independent research-handle test**: after strict coreference, could a researcher point back to this unit as the same participant, referent, scene position/frame, characterization, relation, or orientation without needing to treat its host clause's grammar as the identity? If its identity is exhausted by local syntax/support or by a one-clause paraphrase, it is not a primitive.
5. For relation-like classes, preserve one smallest source-native semantic kernel per distinct research relation. Do not split auxiliaries, empty copulas, control/support verbs, or discourse/report scaffolding into separate primitives unless the source gives that relation its own independently trackable function.
6. Structural recall and density control are asymmetric: the recovery pass may restore omitted scaffold coordinates and independently trackable relation/characterization/orientation handles, but it may not harvest all class-shaped wording.
7. Compounds are built only after the scaffold and relation/description layers are frozen. They reconnect minimal represented research relations among those handles; they never create missing primitives or validate an extra local atom.

This correction is not a sparse-summary rule and not a hidden-count rule. Low-salience, unnamed, deictic, generic, one-use, reported, uncertain, negative, questioned, figurative, or context-dependent material can still qualify when it creates a distinct research handle under the same rule.
