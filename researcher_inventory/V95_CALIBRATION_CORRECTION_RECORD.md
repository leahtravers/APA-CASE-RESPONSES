- `OBJECT`: stable source-reified referent/content handle, not every noun phrase/pronoun/clause shadow;
- `LABEL`: distinct represented characterization/candidate characterization, not descriptive-language census;

- candidate label/rejection/correction units remain distinct when their semantic jobs are separately represented;
