## Generalizable requirement admitted

V77 changes the construction direction from **mention-first pruning** to **binding-first entitlement**.

A primitive is emitted only when it is entitled by one of three sources:

1. it fills an independent semantic role in a selected source-represented proposition/relation;
2. it is a PLACE/TIME anchor required to locate one or more selected propositions or materially distinct episodes; or
3. it is a standalone represented referent/value whose independent identity is established by recurrence/coreference, explicit reification, later reference, comparison, possession/evaluation, or another source relation even if it has no separate compound.

Mere mention, grammatical constituency, vividness, concreteness, lexical explicitness, or membership in a scene does not create entitlement.

V77 adds an explicit **removal test**: if deleting a candidate primitive leaves every selected proposition, episode distinction, independent referent, staged value, and genuine orientation reconstructable without loss, suppress it.

V77 also adds a **one-job arbitration rule**: when a span can be read under multiple classes, select the class demanded by its role in the represented proposition; duplicate projections require genuinely independent roles, not grammatical alternatives.

Compounds are now generated directly from the selected proposition ledger after primitive freeze: one compound per materially distinct proposition, no compound generated merely because a primitive exists.
