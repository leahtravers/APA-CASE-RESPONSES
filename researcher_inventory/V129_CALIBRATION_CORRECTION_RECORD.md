## Generalizable V128 defects

### 1. Exhaustive semantic planning exceeded the lightweight mission

V128 required an exhaustive binding ledger and a scene-frame lattice before selection. Across two materially different sources, that planning model encouraged the worker to preserve clause-level and lexical distinctions that were semantically real but not independently useful researcher coordinates. “Distinct semantic job” became too easy to satisfy.

### 2. Support reconstruction multiplied scene and episode scaffolding

V128 allowed distinct represented modes and episode phases to create support identities even without source-native place/time names. Although intended to prevent undercapture, the rule could turn narrative structure itself into PLACE/TIME inventory. Support should exist only when independently researchable or necessary to disambiguate otherwise admitted primary units.

### 3. Recoverability alone admitted too many predicates and relations

A lexical predicate, orientation phrase, or discourse relation can often carry information not exactly recoverable from surrounding nodes. V128's recoverability test therefore admitted many relations that were not at the source's natural editorial grain. Relation necessity must be paired with editorial independence.

### 4. Evidence-cluster language still over-admitted incidental nouns

V128 permitted source-named details inside bounded evidence/configuration clusters. The paired failure shows that this can elevate descriptive texture into multiple independent OBJECT rows. The source's own level of individuation must control; a cluster can matter without every noun inside it becoming a primitive.

### 5. Mandatory compound projection amplified primitive overcapture

V128 required every resolved multi-coordinate binding to project exactly once. Once the internal ledger was too fine-grained, the compound layer became graph-like and near-exhaustive. Compounds must represent independently researchable primary relations, not every available semantic binding or support edge.

### 6. Natural editorial grain needs an explicit ceiling as well as a floor

Prior contracts increasingly strengthened completeness to prevent undercapture. V128 shows that completeness without a strong density ceiling can overshoot. The durable successor therefore makes both errors explicit: a coordinate system may be too sparse, but it may also be too dense when rows exist only because of grammar, incidental detail, discourse scaffolding, or logically separable support.
