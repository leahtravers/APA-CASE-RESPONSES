# APA Researcher Inventory Agent Contract V94

Status: `ACTIVE SUCCESSOR FOR CALIBRATION`

Authority: Leah's standing Researcher Inventory calibration instruction.

## Prospective correction basis

V93 established an important protection against surface-detail census behavior, but paired Case 2 / Case 6 calibration demonstrated that its **single universal independent-addressability threshold** is not valid across all seven Researcher Inventory classes.

A Researcher Inventory primitive does not always need to be a standalone thing that remains intelligible apart from its proposition. The seven classes have different jobs:

- `PLACE` and `TIME` are **support coordinates** that organize where and when represented material occurs, including necessary unnamed scene/frame coordinates;
- `PERSON` is an **actor coordinate**;
- `OBJECT` is a **referential coordinate**;
- `LABEL` is a **characterization coordinate**;
- `VERB` is a **relation-edge coordinate** whose identity may depend on the coordinates it connects;
- `LOCATOR` is an **orientation-edge coordinate** whose identity may depend on the item/event it positions.

V94 therefore replaces the uniform standalone-addressability test with **CLASS-NATIVE ENTITLEMENT AND GRAIN**.

> **A primitive is admitted when the source materially establishes the class-native job of that primitive and preserving it is necessary to reconnect the represented source structure at the resolution of that class. Standalone intelligibility is not a universal prerequisite.**

This correction is prospective. It does not expose approved archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored answers, calibration answers, or sealed holdout content/output to the worker. Every predecessor contract, run, output, failure, and training record remains immutable historical evidence for the execution it governed.

## Mission

Read the complete source and recover source-native research coordinates in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Preserve literal source language and posture. Freeze primitives before compounds. Then reconstruct the materially distinct source-local proposition/event/state/question/comparison bindings needed to reconnect those frozen coordinates.

Do not perform APA scoring, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA Data Fabric mutation.

## 1. Construction order

### Pass A — whole-source scene and proposition map

Read the entire represented source before extracting any class. Silently map:

- represented scenes and occurrence positions;
- temporal episodes/frames;
- actors;
- concrete and abstract referents;
- explicit or candidate characterizations;
- relation edges;
- orientation/path/containment edges;
- materially distinct propositions, questions, comparisons, reports, and events.

This map is an omission-control device. It does not create a token/POS census.

### Pass B — classify by class-native job

Do not ask one generic question of every proposed row. Ask the question appropriate to the class:

- `PLACE`: does this coordinate organize **where** a represented occurrence, relation, conversation, reflection, or staged object/person is located?
- `TIME`: does this coordinate organize **when / in what episode or phase** represented material occurs?
- `PERSON`: is this a distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference?
- `OBJECT`: is this a materially represented concrete or abstract referent/content handle that the source treats as a thing, amount, option, event-content, relation-content, or figurative object?
- `LABEL`: is this source wording functioning as a retrievable characterization, state, status, manner, comparison, identity term, candidate label, acceptance/rejection, or correction?
- `VERB`: is this a materially represented relation edge connecting or predicating over source coordinates?
- `LOCATOR`: is this a materially represented orientation edge that positions, contains, directs, approaches, separates, recurs, or otherwise locates source material?

### Pass C — class-native completeness

For each class, cover the material source structure at that class's native resolution. Do not deliberately prune a legitimate coordinate merely because it is local, one-use, low-salience, meaningful only with its endpoints, or lacks a literal noun-like name.

Equally, do not multiply aliases, grammatical support, tokenization variants, or analyst-created abstractions.

### Pass D — class-native grain

Use the smallest complete source-native wording appropriate to the class.

- Entity/reference/characterization rows use the shortest wording that preserves the represented identity or posture.
- Relation rows separate the predicate edge from separable arguments.
- Orientation rows separate the orienting phrase/particle from the relation edge when both have distinct class jobs.
- Coordinated or nested relations are split when they are materially distinct edges.
- Keep particles, negation, modality, uncertainty, comparison, or complements only when they are part of the identity of that coordinate rather than a separable argument/edge.

### Pass E — literal lock, coreference, and order

Resolve true aliases/coreference. Preserve source language, dialect, uncertainty, negation, modality, comparison, numbers, contractions, and punctuation. Order by first material source establishment, subject to speaker-first PERSON and broad-before-contained navigation where applicable.

### Pass F — primitive freeze

Freeze the complete primitive inventory before compounds. Compounds may not invent missing primitives, erase admitted primitives, or retroactively justify unsupported rows.

### Pass G — proposition reconstruction

Re-read the source and create canonical source-local bindings for materially distinct propositions, events, states, reports, intentions, questions, comparisons, characterizations, and orientation relations among the frozen coordinates.

Create the smallest set of compounds that preserves those materially distinct bindings. A primitive does not need to appear in a compound merely because it exists. Do not generate graph closure, pairwise combinations, subset/superset permutations, or a separate compound for every descriptive detail.

## 2. PLACE — support coordinates for represented occurrence space

PLACE is not limited to literal place nouns and does not require a self-contained lexical label.

Retain:

- named broad and contained settings when they organize represented material;
- distinct occurrence positions for waits, encounters, conversations, reports, memories, reflections, performances, or physical actions;
- materially staged positions of actors/objects where the position is required to reconnect the occurrence;
- origins/destinations when the source establishes a distinct location role;
- necessary **unnamed** support positions when a materially distinct represented occurrence has a place even though the source does not name it.

An unnamed PLACE may use `source_wording: null` with an exact source cue and a neutral mechanical tag such as `place of [represented occurrence]`.

Do not turn every physical noun, surface, body part, path noun, container, figurative space, or locative wording into PLACE. A thing may remain OBJECT while a LOCATOR carries its positional relation.

## 3. TIME — support coordinates for represented episodes and phases

TIME is not limited to explicit dates/adverbs and does not require a literal temporal phrase.

Retain distinct source-organizing episodes/frames, including:

- episode starts or materially different phases;
- waits/transitions;
- later conversations or explanations;
- remembered periods;
- recurring spans;
- present telling/reflection;
- prospective periods;
- broader contextual periods when they independently organize represented material;
- necessary unnamed temporal support frames.

A TIME coordinate may be anchored by an event/episode cue rather than a literal temporal token. Several propositions may share one TIME. Do not create TIME merely for tense, every action, every adverb, or every duration phrase.

## 4. PERSON — represented actors

Retain `B` plus every distinct materially represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference.

Minor, one-use, offscreen, remembered, reported, relational, possessive, prospective, and institutional actors may qualify. Suppress true aliases/coreferent repeats and nonreferential/rhetorical addressees.

PERSON remains intentionally inclusive.

## 5. OBJECT — materially represented referents and content handles

OBJECT is a materially represented concrete or abstract referent that can be reconnected as a thing/content handle in the source.

Retain, when materially represented:

- concrete things, parts, products, documents, surfaces, contents, instruments, results, amounts, values, sets, categories, and one-use physical details;
- choices, decisions, next steps, plans, options, alternatives;
- relations, situations, event-content, internal content, or figurative objects when the source refers to them as things/content handles.

Do not require recurrence or high salience. Do not omit a concrete/reified referent merely because it appears only as an argument of one proposition.

Do not nominalize every clause, complement, question, action, adjective, or place expression. If an exact trace's only material job is already fully represented as PLACE, PERSON, LABEL, VERB, or LOCATOR and the source does not separately treat it as a referent, do not create an OBJECT shadow.

## 6. LABEL — source-indexed characterizations, not description census

LABEL preserves wording the source uses as a retrievable characterization or candidate characterization.

Retain, when materially functioning as such:

- identity/status terms;
- states and manners that materially characterize an actor/object/event;
- comparisons used as characterization;
- self/other labels;
- candidate feeling/meaning labels and explicit source uncertainty;
- immediate acceptance, rejection, correction, or polarity wording when it materially changes the candidate characterization;
- source wording whose characterization is itself part of the represented proposition rather than incidental background description.

Preserve exact question form, negation, rejection, intensity, comparison, and dialect when identity-bearing.

Do not inventory every adjective, adverb, decorative descriptor, rhetorical aside, or incidental evaluative phrase merely because it modifies something.

## 7. VERB — relation-edge completeness

VERB inventories **material source relations**, not standalone lexical handles and not whole propositions.

Retain each materially represented relation edge needed to reconstruct source propositions/events, including:

- actions and physical acts;
- states/placements/existence relations;
- perception/cognition/stance;
- reports/speech;
- intentions/needs/choices;
- social relations and interaction;
- local one-use actions;
- embodied/performance actions;
- nested relations when the nested edge itself contributes represented structure.

A VERB may be meaningful only together with its endpoints. That is normal for a relation edge and is not a reason to suppress it.

### Relation grain

Prefer the **smallest complete predicate edge**:

- split coordinated predicates when they are distinct relations;
- split a matrix and embedded relation when both materially contribute separate edges;
- leave PERSON/OBJECT/LABEL/PLACE/TIME arguments to their own coordinates;
- leave separable orientation/path/containment material to LOCATOR;
- keep a particle/complement only when removing it changes the predicate's identity;
- preserve negation/modality when it belongs to the relation's source posture.

Suppress pure auxiliaries, tense/aspect support, do-support, duplicate coreferent restatements, and full-clause bundles masquerading as one VERB.

Do **not** suppress a main or local relation merely because its semantics are simple, one-use, physical, or proposition-dependent.

## 8. LOCATOR — orientation-edge completeness

LOCATOR inventories represented orientation edges, not only phrases that make sense by themselves.

Retain materially represented orientation such as:

- `at` / position relations;
- approach/away/entry/exit/path;
- origin/destination;
- containment/support/surface position;
- directional particles such as back/out/up/over when they materially orient an event;
- recurring-context orientation;
- internal/mental orientation;
- temporal-position phrases when they orient an event;
- figurative/comparative orientation when it locates the source relation.

A LOCATOR may be local and may depend on its endpoints for full meaning. That is not a reason to suppress it.

Separate a LOCATOR from the VERB when the source contains both a relation edge and a materially distinct orientation edge. Suppress routine recipient/topic marking, purely grammatical prepositions, transitions, and argument markers that do not orient represented material.

## 9. Cross-class overlap

The same source wording may appear in more than one class when it performs genuinely different class-native jobs.

Examples of legitimate structural overlap include:

- a locative state functioning both as a characterization and as orientation;
- a movement construction contributing both a VERB edge and a LOCATOR edge;
- a represented physical item serving as OBJECT while its relation to another item is carried by LOCATOR.

Do not create cross-class duplicates merely because the phrase can receive several grammatical labels.

## 10. Literal-language lock

Every source-derived non-null `source_wording`, `source_cue`, `order_cue`, source-near tag, and researcher bundle must preserve the source's actual language where the schema expects source text.

Do not repair grammar, normalize dialect, substitute synonyms, alter numbers, expand contractions, or clean punctuation.

Only genuinely unnamed structural PLACE/TIME support coordinates may use `source_wording: null`, anchored by exact source text.

`researcher_note` should normally be null or minimal mechanical/coreference/unnamed-coordinate bookkeeping.

## 11. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds, verify:

1. the entire source was read;
2. PLACE/TIME scene and frame support is complete enough to distinguish materially different represented occurrences;
3. PERSON coverage includes every distinct represented actor after true coreference;
4. OBJECT coverage preserves materially represented concrete/reified referents without arbitrary clause nominalization or cross-class shadows;
5. LABEL coverage preserves source-indexed characterizations without descriptive modifier census;
6. VERB coverage contains the materially distinct relation edges and does not collapse multiple edges into proposition-sized phrases;
7. LOCATOR coverage contains materially distinct orientation edges, including local path/position/containment particles/phrases when represented;
8. one-use/local/proposition-dependent relation or orientation edges were not pruned for lack of standalone intelligibility;
9. literal wording/posture is exact;
10. aliases and duplicate projections are suppressed.

## 12. Compound reconstruction

Compounds reconnect frozen primitives into canonical source-local propositions/events/states/questions/comparisons.

For each materially distinct binding:

- use the primitive coordinates that belong to that represented relation;
- include applicable PLACE/TIME/LOCATOR/LABEL/VERB coordinates when those primitives are part of that binding;
- preserve source-local semantic order;
- set Q from source-present qualitative availability under the schema;
- integrate characterization/orientation into the same compound when it belongs to the same proposition rather than spawning redundant side compounds.

Do not:

- require every primitive to appear in a compound;
- create one compound for every noun/label pair;
- create pairwise graph closure;
- create nested subset/superset permutations for the same proposition;
- create mega-bundles spanning distinct propositions;
- invent primitives to complete wording.

## 13. Final audit

Before returning, verify:

1. class-native entitlement rather than one universal standalone-addressability test;
2. complete scene/frame support without place/time token census;
3. inclusive actor coverage after true coreference;
4. material referent coverage without arbitrary nominalization;
5. characterization coverage without modifier census;
6. relation-edge completeness with atomic predicate grain;
7. orientation-edge completeness with atomic orienting grain;
8. literal wording, dialect, uncertainty, negation, modality, numbers, and punctuation remain exact;
9. compounds reconstruct materially distinct source-local bindings without graph closure;
10. candidate-only boundary is preserved.

Never target hidden counts or infer hidden gold.

## 14. Calibration isolation and sealed holdout

Clean-room certification requires that fresh agent to pass in a new session without access to prior agent sessions or holdout output.
