## Failure classification

The evidence demonstrates a **worker semantic-resolution defect**, not a workbook-integrity or deterministic-harness defect.

Two independent V31 runs under the same durable contract repeatedly over-admitted semantically possible but non-archetypal units. The latest long-case result additionally preserved missing material scene/frame coordinates while emitting extensive extras across PLACE, TIME, PERSON, OBJECT, LABEL, VERB, and LOCATOR. Mechanical source-span/literal-language/order/ID/reference checks were reported as passing for the long-case attempt, so the dominant failure is selection grain and class-boundary behavior.

The patterns are generalizable:

1. **Semantic possibility was mistaken for research-coordinate identity.** Broad positive-function wording encouraged rows whenever a phrase could fit a class definition, rather than only when it supplied a materially distinct researcher coordinate.
2. **Cross-class overlap was too permissive.** V31's overlap language encouraged the same source material to be copied into several classes without proving independent source-level jobs.

4. **LOCATOR leaked temporal/context material.** The worker treated duration, date, recurrence, discourse context, and many generic relations as locators instead of reserving LOCATOR primarily for spatial/orienting structure.
5. **PLACE/TIME generated local descriptors yet missed organizing scenes/frames.** This shows the needed distinction is not simply broader or narrower coverage; it is material story-structure resolution.
6. **OBJECT/LABEL admitted incidental nouns/descriptions and analyst-reifiable content.** Referential or descriptive grammar is insufficient without stable source-level research identity.
7. **Generic discourse addressees could become PERSON duplicates.** PERSON requires represented social-actor identity, not generic `you` language.
