## Mission

Produce only the literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate requested by the schema.

Read the whole source, recover the source-native research handles that remain independently selectable in exactly seven classes, preserve literal language and posture, freeze those primitives, then bind them into lightweight local compounds. ## 1. Construction order

### Pass A — whole-source ledger

Read the entire represented source before extracting a class. Silently account for actual, remembered, reported, relational, recurring, reflective, intended, questioned, hypothetical, negated, corrected, figurative, and prospective material.

Accounting for material does not automatically entitle it to a standalone primitive.

### Pass B — class-native entitlement

For each plausible coordinate in the requested class ask both questions:

1. **Independent class job:** after participants, arguments, broad scene/frame, qualities, and neighboring coordinates are known, does this candidate still provide a distinct research handle of this requested class?
2. **Loss test:** if this candidate is omitted from this class, would a materially distinct class-native function disappear rather than merely remain represented in another primitive's exact wording/cue or in a canonical local compound?

Retain only when both answers are yes, except for required unnamed PLACE/TIME support frames defined below.

Low salience never defeats a real handle. Grammar, lexical explicitness, or salience alone never create one.

### Pass C — type and shadow audit

Type by the job the source coordinate actually performs. Do not create a second class projection merely because the same phrase contains spatial, temporal, evaluative, verbal, nominal, or prepositional material.

Cross-class overlap is allowed when each projection independently survives the entitlement test. Otherwise keep the dominant class-native handle and preserve the other material through qualities, exact source wording/cues, or the local compound.

### Pass D — minimal class-head grain

For every entitled primitive, choose the smallest complete source-native wording that preserves the class handle's identity.

Do not absorb separable arguments or neighboring coordinates merely to make a phrase read like a complete clause. Conversely, do not strip a required particle, complement, reflexive, modal/negative posture, or other material whose removal changes the identity of that class handle.

### Pass E — coreference, literal lock, order

Resolve true aliases/coreference. Preserve exact source language and posture. Order by first material source establishment subject to the class rules.

### Pass F — primitive freeze

Freeze primitives before compounds. A compound may not repair a missing primitive or justify an otherwise unsupported one.

### Pass G — canonical local compounds

Bind frozen primitives into the smallest source-local proposition/relation/state/orientation bundles useful for reconnection. Compounds reconstruct source-local relationships; they do not create new semantics.

## 2. General non-entitlement rules

Do not promote a span to a standalone primitive when its only independent role is:

- article, auxiliary, do-support, tense/aspect support, infinitival/control support, bare copula, or grammar-only material;
- an argument/participant that is separately represented as PERSON/OBJECT/PLACE/etc.;
- a modifier or degree phrase whose research work is adequately carried as a quality of an admitted coordinate;
- a temporal/sequencing fragment that merely locates a broader episode rather than defining a separately revisit-able period/frame;
- a phrase-internal preposition or complement that does not remain independently useful as orientation/context;
- a pronoun/deictic repeat, alias, repeated mention, or alternate tokenization;
- a clause/proposition/event shell that the source does not itself reify as an OBJECT;
- metadiscourse that only organizes the telling;
- a cross-class shadow whose class job disappears once the dominant admitted coordinate and local compound are known.

This list does not ban subtle, one-use, background, remembered, reported, prospective, questioned, negative, or figurative material when it independently passes the class-native entitlement test.

## 3. PLACE — site or necessary occurrence position

PLACE is a materially useful site or occurrence-position.

Retain explicit physical/institutional settings when they independently support navigation. Retain a contained site separately from its broad setting when the contained position itself remains a useful source-native handle.

Create an unnamed PLACE (`source_wording: null`) only when a materially distinct represented interaction, wait, report, remembered/comparison scene, object-location scene, or present telling/reflection requires its own occurrence-position to avoid collapsing into another scene.

Do not turn every spatial noun, surface, body part, path, object position, distance phrase, water/forest/boat mention, community descriptor, deictic, or figurative expression into PLACE. A thing can be spatially described without becoming an independent PLACE.

If the source gives a concrete position that matters independently (for example, the position of a represented object or destination of a represented movement), the position may qualify; use the smallest source-native place wording or an unnamed support position rather than the whole clause.

## 4. TIME — episode or period frame

TIME is a materially distinct episode/period frame that a later researcher could independently revisit.

Retain distinct attempts/conditions, response/help episodes, departures/transitions, waits, intended periods, later conversations/reports, recurring spans, relationship periods, remembered/comparison frames, present telling/reflection, and separately staged prospective/future frames when the frame itself remains independently useful.

Do not create TIME from every action, frequency phrase, duration, tense, sequencing phrase, or clause that merely occurs at a time. Several actions may share one frame. Split only when the source materially stages a new episode or period.

A named temporal phrase may be a TIME when it itself identifies the frame. An episode may instead require `source_wording: null` with an exact source cue when no compact temporal wording names it.

## 5. PERSON

Retain `B` plus every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when the source gives that actor a distinguishable participant, relational, reported, remembered, prospective, or institutional role.

Minor, possessive/relational, offscreen, remembered, reported, and prospective actors may qualify. Suppress true aliases and nonreferential/rhetorical addressees.

## 6. OBJECT — source-reified referent/content handle

OBJECT is a source-treated concrete or abstract thing with independent selectable identity.

Retain physical things/parts, products/documents/services/results, amounts/values, choices/decisions/plans/next steps treated as things, stable recurring relations/practices/relationships treated as objects of concern, represented sets/categories, comparison vehicles, and explicitly reified mental/content objects when the source itself gives them thing-like identity.

Do not create OBJECT merely because a noun phrase exists. Do not nominalize an unreified event, proposition, question, relation, modifier, or clause. Do not duplicate a PLACE whose only job is location or a LABEL whose only job is characterization.

When a phrase describes an OBJECT using a state/value, preserve the OBJECT head separately from an independently entitled LABEL only if each remains separately selectable. Do not turn the entire descriptive clause into a second OBJECT.

## 7. LABEL — independently staged value/state/posture

LABEL is an independently reusable characterization, value, state, classification, evaluation, comparison, identity, manner, status, correction/rejection, or explicitly questioned candidate value.

Admission is not adjective/modifier census. A descriptive word or phrase remains only `qualities_available` when it does not itself form a separately staged value/state a researcher could select after the characterized coordinate is known.

Use the shortest complete exact source span that preserves the staged value's posture. Preserve negation, uncertainty, question form, contrast, rejection, correction, and intensity when needed to avoid reversing or collapsing the source-staged value.

When the source explicitly stages a candidate value and then separately accepts/rejects/corrects it, the candidate and response may be distinct LABEL handles. Do not merge them merely because they address the same topic.

Do not create LABEL from whole questions/propositions merely because they contain an evaluation. Do not create a LABEL whose only content is a noun/category better typed PERSON/OBJECT/PLACE. `qualities_available` never substitutes for an independently entitled LABEL and never forces one.

## 8. VERB — relation entitlement, then lexical head grain

VERB is a materially represented relation/happening/state/stance/cognition/report that remains independently selectable after its participants, arguments, scene/frame, values, and orientation are known.

**First decide relation entitlement. Then choose the smallest complete lexical predicate head.**

### Entitlement

Retain ordinary, subtle, remembered, reported, intended, questioned, negative, hypothetical, recurring, and future relations when they remain distinct research edges. Reporting/cognition acts qualify when the act of seeing, saying, thinking, finding, asking, telling, recalling, or similar cognition/reporting is itself independently represented rather than merely scaffolding another relation.

Do not automatically create separate VERBs for every matrix/control/aspect predicate and embedded predicate. When one merely scopes/supports the other without a distinct research edge, retain the content-bearing relation rather than both. Split matrix and embedded predicates only when each independently passes the entitlement test.

Coordinated/sequential predicates may split when each contributes a different relation. Keep a semantically bound multiword predicate together when splitting would destroy one source-native relation.

### Minimal lexical head

Once entitled, exclude separable subject/object/PLACE/TIME/LOCATOR/LABEL arguments from `source_wording` and the short tag unless the material is required to identify the predicate itself.

Examples of the rule's form, not case answers:

- prefer a relation head such as `look at` over absorbing the object into the VERB when the object is separately represented;
- preserve a particle or required complement when removing it changes the predicate (`care about`, `walk away with`, `out of control of`, etc.);
- preserve negation/modal posture when it belongs to relation identity;
- do not expand a relation head to the whole clause merely to make it syntactically complete.

Suppress pure auxiliaries, do-support, empty copulas, and support/control fragments that fail entitlement. A quality copula whose only semantic content is an admitted LABEL normally does not require a duplicate VERB.

## 9. LOCATOR — independently useful orientation/context handle

LOCATOR is an independently selectable where/from/toward/path/containment/context/orientation relation.

Retain broad/contained setting relations, position/proximity, origin/destination, movement/path, entry/exit, accompaniment/carrying when locational, containment, recurring/situational context, and genuine internal/figurative/comparative orientation when the orientation itself remains useful after PLACE/TIME and relation arguments are known.

Use the smallest complete meaningful construction, not a bare preposition and not a whole clause when a smaller orientation phrase preserves the job.

A movement predicate and a LOCATOR may overlap when each independently does different research work. Do not suppress a true locator merely because wording also appears in a VERB or compound. But do not create LOCATOR from routine possession, recipient/beneficiary/topic marking, provenance, temporal adverbial material, or every phrase-internal preposition.

## 10. Literal-language lock

Every source-derived non-null `source_wording`, `source_cue`, `order_cue`, source-near tag, and researcher bundle must preserve the source's actual language where the schema requires source text.

Do not repair grammar, normalize dialect, substitute synonyms, alter numbers, expand contractions, or clean punctuation.

Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME support coordinates may use `source_wording: null`, anchored by exact source text.

`researcher_note` should normally be null or minimal mechanical/coreference bookkeeping. Do not use a note to smuggle interpretation or evaluator-oriented justification into the candidate.

## 11. Coreference, order, and qualities

Merge true aliases/coreferents even when wording changes. Repeated mentions do not create duplicate primitives.

Default within-class order is first material source anchor after coreference resolution, with speaker-first PERSON and broad-before-contained setting navigation where source structure supports it.

`qualities_available` is boolean only. It marks source-present descriptive material; it neither forces nor suppresses an independently entitled LABEL.

## 12. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds, inspect every retained primitive:

1. Does it independently pass its class-native entitlement test?
2. Is it typed by its actual class job rather than by surface grammar?
3. Is its lexical/source wording at the smallest complete class-head grain?
4. Have separable participants, arguments, values, places, times, and orientations been kept out of the primitive wording unless necessary to identity?
5. Is any related source material merely a quality/support/context shadow rather than a second primitive?
6. Would removing the primitive erase a distinct research handle rather than only change how a compound is verbalized?

Reject or resize any primitive that fails this audit before compound construction.

## 13. Compound reconstruction — canonical local bindings

After primitive freeze, create the smallest materially useful local bindings that reconnect admitted coordinates to represented source relations/states/orientations.

A compound should bind frozen coordinates that jointly express one source-local relation/proposition/state useful to reconnect the index. Use the admitted relation/value/orientation anchor and the participating PERSON/OBJECT coordinates plus only the PLACE/TIME/LOCATOR coordinates materially belonging to that binding.

Do not create:

- grammar-fragment compounds;
- arbitrary pairwise closure;
- duplicate subset/superset versions of the same local binding;
- broad scene mega-bundles;
- compounds merely because a primitive exists;
- compounds containing a primitive whose only reason for existence was that compound.

A source sentence may yield several compounds when it contains several independently represented relations. Several lexical words may also resolve to one compound when they express one local relation. Primitive entitlement and lexical grain are decided before compound construction.

## 14. Final audit

Before returning, verify:

1. the whole source was read and every material coordinate considered;
2. each emitted primitive independently passes class-native entitlement;
3. class overlap is independently justified rather than triggered by shared wording;
4. PLACE preserves required sites/occurrence positions without spatial census;
5. TIME preserves episode/period frames without action-by-action microtiming;
6. PERSON preserves every represented actor after true coreference;
7. OBJECT preserves source-reified things/content without noun/clause census;
8. LABEL preserves independently staged values/postures without modifier census;
9. VERB relation entitlement precedes splitting and each admitted verb uses minimal complete predicate-head grain;
10. LOCATOR preserves independently useful orientation without preposition census;
11. literal wording, dialect, posture, coreference, and order remain exact;
12. separable arguments/participants/context are not absorbed into primitive wording merely for grammatical completeness;
13. compounds use frozen units only and reconstruct local source relations without inventing semantics;
14. the result remains candidate-only.

Never target hidden counts or infer hidden gold.
