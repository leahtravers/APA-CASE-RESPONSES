## Prospective correction basis

V118 executed only after the immutable Case 2 and Case 6 workbook hashes were verified and all deterministic apparatus/harness tests passed. It then failed semantically on both approved archetype cases. The attempt and failure evidence were preserved. No new harness defect was established.

V118 made primitive admission depend too strongly on whether removing a row would destroy a materially complete binding. That conflated two different resolutions: the primitive ledgers are a literal researcher-reconnection index, while compounds are selective bindings among already frozen primitives. A valid source-native coordinate may remain independently selectable and useful at its own class grain even when a larger binding remains understandable without it.

V119 therefore separates the two gates:

> **PRIMITIVES = SOURCE-ADDRESSABLE CLASS COORDINATES. COMPOUNDS = SELECTIVE MATERIAL BINDINGS.**

No archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, calibration answers, or sealed holdout material are supplied to the worker.

## Mission

Produce a literal, lightweight Researcher Inventory of the complete supplied source in exactly seven primitive classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Then build lightweight compounds only from frozen registered primitives.

The inventory is not a sparse summary. It must preserve all materially useful researcher-selectable coordinates at the intended workbook resolution. It is also not a token/POS census, clause census, exhaustive semantic parse, rhetorical annotation, graph closure, APA analysis, or psychological interpretation.

## 1. Read the whole source before extracting a class

Read the complete source and understand its represented scenes, episodes, participants, things/content, characterizations, happenings/relations, orientations, questions, memories, reports, comparisons, decisions, reflections, recurring frames, and prospective material.

Then answer only the class requested by the apparatus.

Do not stop after the most salient items. Local, one-use, mundane, background, remembered, reported, uncertain, negated, questioned, hypothetical, prospective, figurative, and colloquial coordinates may all qualify.

## 2. Primitive admission: source-addressable independent selectability

A primitive is admitted when all applicable conditions hold:

### A. Source grounding

The coordinate is explicitly established by the source, except that PLACE and TIME may be unnamed when the source clearly establishes a distinct spatial or temporal support frame. Never invent facts.

### B. Class-native independent selectability

The coordinate performs a real job in its requested class and can stand as its own researcher-selectable ledger row without needing a sentence or clause wrapper.

A primitive does **not** need to be indispensable to a compound or to change a whole material binding when removed. Primitive completeness is judged at the class ledger level.

### C. Natural literal grain

Use the smallest complete source-native unit that preserves the coordinate's identity. Keep a natural multiword predicate, judgment, orientation, idiom, comparison, negation, question-label, or bound construction whole when splitting it would destroy its source-native identity.

### D. No grammatical/discourse debris

Reject wording whose only function is grammatical carriage, sentence completion, discourse management, a generic deictic wrapper, a clause/proposition wrapper, an intensifier detached from its characterization, or an internal fragment of a stronger retained coordinate.

### E. Nonduplication

Merge true aliases/coreference and true same-class restatements. Do not merge genuinely different coordinates merely because they share a scene, target, lexical family, or larger binding.

## 3. Coverage rule

The primitive ledgers are a researcher reconnection index. Ask:

**Could a researcher reasonably need to select this source-native coordinate later in order to reconnect where, when, who, what, how characterized, what happened/was related, or what orientation/context was represented?**

If yes, and the coordinate passes the class-native and natural-grain rules, retain it even if it is local, one-use, or not essential to a compound.

Do not prune a valid primitive merely because another retained row or compound makes the narrative broadly understandable.

## 4. Functional typing

Type by represented job, not by part of speech or lexical shape:

- spatial support -> `PLACE`
- temporal support -> `TIME`
- represented human/social actor -> `PERSON`
- independently selectable thing/content/referential handle -> `OBJECT`
- source-applied characterization/state/status/evaluation/comparison/identity -> `LABEL`
- independently inventoryable happening/action/state/relation -> `VERB`
- independently selectable position/path/direction/containment/proximity/orientation/context -> `LOCATOR`

Cross-class overlap is allowed only when the same exact source span genuinely performs two independently selectable class jobs. Do not duplicate merely because grammar permits alternate analyses.

## 5. PLACE — spatial support ledger

Retain every materially differentiated source-established place/scene support useful for locating represented material.

This may include:

- an explicit broad setting;
- a distinct contained/local position;
- the location of a represented object or interaction;
- a destination;
- a waiting position or scene;
- a remembered/reported location;
- a later conversation setting;
- a present-telling setting when the source establishes one.

Several events may share one PLACE when the source does not differentiate their support. A broad and contained support may both be retained when the source differentiates them.

Unnamed PLACE is permitted only when a distinct spatial support is clearly established but not physically named. Use null `source_wording`, an exact source cue, and a neutral mechanical tag. Never invent where.

Do not turn every physical noun, possession phrase, or locator phrase into PLACE.

## 6. TIME — temporal support ledger

Retain materially differentiated source-established temporal frames useful for reconnecting the represented material.

This may include:

- an attempt or condition frame;
- a help/response episode;
- a transition/departure phase;
- a waiting interval;
- an intended period;
- a remembered/reported period;
- a later conversation or report frame;
- a recurring span;
- a present-reflection frame;
- an explicitly represented future/prospective frame.

A TIME does not require a clock, date, or temporal noun. Several relations may share one TIME when their temporal support is not materially differentiated.

Unnamed TIME is permitted only when the source clearly establishes a distinct temporal frame without naming its details. Do not create TIME from grammatical tense, every transition word, or every predicate occurrence.

## 7. PERSON — participant ledger

Retain the speaker and every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when the actor participates in, anchors, is related to, is remembered/reported in, benefits from, possesses something material to, or is otherwise represented in source structure.

Direct, offscreen, relational, possessive/beneficiary, institutional, remembered, reported, prospective, peripheral, and one-use actors may qualify.

Reject rhetorical, generic, or nonreferential person wording that never becomes a represented actor.

## 8. OBJECT — thing/content ledger

Retain every materially represented independently selectable concrete or abstract thing/content handle.

Qualifying OBJECT coordinates may include:

- concrete things and source-distinguished parts;
- amounts/values;
- services/results;
- decisions and next steps when treated as things;
- contemplated alternatives when the source treats them as selectable choices;
- recurring relations treated as content;
- named sets/categories;
- remembered/reported content;
- internal represented objects or content handles.

Do not create OBJECT from every grammatical argument, pronoun, generic `this/that/it`, clause complement, question content, explanation, proposition wrapper, discourse point, or meta-reference. An action or clause becomes OBJECT only when the source itself treats it as a selectable thing/content node rather than merely expressing it through syntax.

## 9. LABEL — characterization ledger

Retain each independently selectable source-applied characterization, quality/state, status, identity, evaluation, comparison, self-label, candidate label, rejection, correction, contrast, or materially expressed posture.

Preserve local, one-use, colloquial, idiomatic, uncertain, questioned, negated, corrected, rejected, and figurative labels when the source presents them as characterizations.

Use the shortest complete exact source form that carries the characterization. Keep one natural judgment together.

Reject rhetorical color, descriptive flourish, generic manner wording, isolated intensifiers, and modifier fragments that do not stand as independently selectable characterizations.

LABEL inventory is separate from the `qualities_available` boolean.

## 10. VERB — happening/relation ledger

Source happenings and relations are independently inventoryable. Retain materially represented lexical predicate increments in source order at natural complete grain.

A VERB may express action, state, cognition, perception, report/communication, intention, question relation, decision, possession, comparison, evaluation, movement, transition, gesture, existence/location, obligation, recurrence, or another represented relation.

Preserve particles, reflexives, negation, modality, and bound complements when required for the predicate's source identity.

Split matrix/embedded or coordinated predicates only when they perform genuinely different independently selectable relation jobs. Keep a natural bound or coordinated predicate together when it forms one source-native relation.

Reject pure tense/aspect support, discourse/filler predicates, whole-clause wrappers, duplicate restatements, and lexical fragments nested inside a stronger complete predicate.

Inventorying a VERB never asserts that its event happened. Preserve questioned, negated, hypothetical, intended, reported, remembered, recurring, and prospective posture.

## 11. LOCATOR — orientation/context ledger

Retain materially useful independently selectable spatial, directional, containment, path, proximity, movement, accompaniment, origin/destination, entry/exit, recurrence/context, internal/relational, or materially spatialized figurative orientation.

Use the smallest complete meaningful exact source construction, not an isolated preposition.

A LOCATOR may coexist with a PLACE or VERB when it performs its own orientation job. It need not be indispensable to a compound.

Do not inventory every prepositional phrase, recipient/topic/purpose complement, possession phrase, degree phrase, temporal phrase, generic adverbial, isolated `there/here`, or rhetorical location metaphor that lacks an independently selectable orientation function.

## 12. Literal lock and source posture

For every explicit primitive, use exact source-native wording. Every non-null `source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be character-for-character source text.

Preserve colloquial language, dialect, spelling/grammar, idiom, figurative wording, negation, modality, uncertainty, question form, attribution, comparison, remembered/reported posture, hypothetical/prospective posture, correction, and rejection.

Never normalize, polish, translate, diagnose, euphemize, substitute synonyms, or lemmatize into a different surface form.

Researcher short tags are navigational only. They never replace literal source wording.

## 13. Qualities flag

`qualities_available` is only a yes/no availability flag showing that source qualities/descriptions exist for the coordinate. Do not explode qualities into a full APA parse merely to support Q. Do not use Q to replace LABEL rows.

## 14. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds:

1. replay the whole source in order;
2. verify all seven requested ledgers at their own class grain;
3. restore valid local/one-use coordinates omitted only because they were not globally salient or indispensable to a compound;
4. remove unsupported inference, grammatical/discourse wrappers, generic deictics, proposition/clause wrappers, internal fragments, and true duplicates;
5. arbitrate OBJECT/LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR by represented function;
6. verify materially differentiated PLACE/TIME support, including legitimate unnamed support;
7. verify natural complete spans, literal lock, coreference, class-local distinctness, and first-establishment ordering; and
8. freeze the primitive ledgers.

Do not target a hidden count or infer a hidden archetype.

## 15. Compounds — selective material bindings only

Build compounds only after primitive freeze.

The compound stage is intentionally stricter than primitive admission. A valid primitive may remain unbundled.

Emit a compound only when two or more frozen coordinates jointly form one materially complete source binding useful for reconnecting a represented interaction, happening, proposition, characterization, question, comparison, choice, reflection, report, memory, or prospective relation.

Rules:

- include only registered frozen coordinates actually participating in that binding;
- use enough coordinates to preserve the binding without adding unrelated context;
- split when participant set, target/content, support frame, source posture, or materially distinct judgment/action changes enough that one bundle would blur separate structure;
- group locally joint predicates when they jointly form one binding;
- do not create a compound merely to justify a primitive;
- do not create one compound per clause, predicate token, primitive, or sentence;
- do not emit partial subset/superset variants of the same binding;
- do not emit arbitrary co-occurrence, all-pairs links, scene mega-bundles, support chains, singleton equivalents, duplicate restatements, or graph closure.

If an apparatus prompt elsewhere asks broadly for each represented relation, this V119 compound rule controls the lightweight grain: serialize only materially complete researcher bindings, not an exhaustive relation census.

## 16. Identity and ordering

Primitive identity is class-local source identity, not mention count. Resolve true aliases/coreference before deduplication. Do not merge distinct coordinates merely because they are related or colocated.

Code owns canonical output IDs. Supply only neutral identity keys when the apparatus asks for them. Order by first source establishment after coreference, subject to speaker-first PERSON and genuine broad-before-contained support rules.
