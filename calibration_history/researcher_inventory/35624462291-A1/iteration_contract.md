## Prospective correction basis

V142 verified both immutable Leah-approved archetype workbook hashes, passed all retained deterministic apparatus and prompt-harness tests, and then failed hidden semantic calibration on both approved archetypes. The failure is generalizable and semantic rather than mechanical.

V142 correctly removed the V141 pressure to widen brief or context-dependent source wording into self-sufficient conceptual phrases. However, V142 made a different mistake: it allowed a locally class-native job inside a material local binding to act as the principal admission gate. The worker consequently treated large amounts of grammatically or lexically valid local material as inventory primitives. TIME, PLACE, OBJECT, LABEL, VERB, and LOCATOR all leaked toward local-census behavior, and compounds expanded with that precursor population. Genuine structural coordinates were still missed in parallel.

The durable correction is therefore not a return to self-contained wording and not another class-by-class census. V143 distinguishes **role independence from wording independence**. The worker must first select a lightweight **researcher-coordinate backbone** and its research-significant bindings without extracting primitive wording. Only an independently addressable class-native role on that backbone may become a primitive. After the role is admitted, the primitive must be realized with the smallest exact source-native atom, even when that atom is brief, deictic, generic, context-dependent, one-use, or not understandable outside its binding. A proposition, grammatical predicate, modifier, preposition, noun phrase, or local assertion does not become a researcher coordinate merely because it can be parsed or locally classified.

No gold rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, scored predecessor outputs, canonical archetype extracts, case-specific hidden corrections/examples, or sealed holdout content are supplied to the worker.

## Controlling rule

> **SELECT THE RESEARCH ROLE BEFORE YOU SELECT THE WORDS. READ THE WHOLE SOURCE, RESOLVE STRICT COREFERENCE, POSTURE, EPISODES, AND THE LIGHTWEIGHT RESEARCHER-COORDINATE BACKBONE. FIRST DECIDE WHICH DISTINCT PLACE, TIME, PERSON, OBJECT, LABEL, VERB, AND LOCATOR ROLES A RESEARCHER WOULD NEED TO RECONNECT THE CASE WITHOUT INVENTING OR RE-PARSING IT. A ROLE MUST BE INDEPENDENTLY ADDRESSABLE IN THAT RESEARCH GRAPH; THE WORDING THAT REALIZES IT DOES NOT HAVE TO BE INDEPENDENTLY INTELLIGIBLE. ONLY AFTER A ROLE IS ADMITTED MAY YOU MAP IT TO THE SMALLEST EXACT CONTIGUOUS SOURCE-NATIVE ATOM, OR TO A LAWFUL UNNAMED PLACE/TIME POSITION OR FRAME. NEVER ENUMERATE SOURCE ATOMS FIRST AND THEN PROMOTE THEM BECAUSE THEY FIT A CLASS. NEVER WIDEN A SHORT OR DEICTIC SOURCE ATOM JUST TO MAKE IT STAND ALONE. COVERAGE REPLAYS THE SELECTED RESEARCHER BACKBONE AND ITS RESEARCH-SIGNIFICANT BINDINGS, NOT EVERY CLAUSE, ASSERTION, PREDICATE, MODIFIER, PREPOSITIONAL PHRASE, OR LOCAL GRAMMATICAL BINDING. TYPE BY SOURCE FUNCTION. FREEZE PRIMITIVES BEFORE COMPOUNDS. COMPOUNDS REASSEMBLE SELECTED RESEARCH-SIGNIFICANT BINDINGS AMONG FROZEN COORDINATES; THEY DO NOT TRACK EVERY PROPOSITION AND THEY NEVER CREATE OR REPAIR PRIMITIVES.**

## 1. Mandatory construction order — backbone before atomization

1. Read the complete source once without extracting primitives.
2. Resolve strict coreference, source order, scene/episode shifts, reported/remembered/prospective posture, uncertainty, negation, correction, and materially distinct represented situations.
3. Build an internal **researcher-coordinate backbone** using role placeholders only. Do not copy source phrases yet. The backbone contains only distinct researcher-addressable roles and research-significant bindings needed to reconnect the case at lightweight worksheet grain.
4. A role placeholder may represent a site/position, organizing frame, stable actor/social endpoint, source-treated thing/content referent, source-applied characterization/polarity move, operative relation, or distinct orientation/context relation.
5. For each candidate role, apply the role-admission gate in Section 2. Reject proposition completeness, grammatical completeness, and local lexical coverage as reasons for admission.
6. Only after role membership is frozen provisionally, map each admitted role to the smallest exact contiguous source-native atom. For lawful unnamed PLACE/TIME, map the role to an exact source cue and neutral unnamed coordinate.
7. Run class-by-class **backbone coverage** to find omitted roles. Do not scan vocabulary looking for class-shaped phrases.
8. Run cross-class role review and same-class identity/deduplication.
9. Run the structural-floor audit and anti-census ceiling audit.
10. Apply literal/posture lock and freeze primitive membership.
11. Build compounds from frozen primitives by replaying only research-significant backbone bindings.
12. Run final coverage, typing, lexical, compound, and isolation audits.

Do not begin from a compressed summary. Do not begin from an exhaustive proposition ledger. Do not begin from seven lexical candidate lists. Do not begin by harvesting source atoms and asking which class they resemble.

## 2. Universal role-first admission rule

A primitive survives only when the **role** passes first and the **source realization** passes second.

### 2.1 Researcher-coordinate role gate

Before selecting any wording, ask whether there is a distinct class-native role that a researcher could address again without inventing a new concept or re-parsing the source.

A role qualifies when it is source-established and materially contributes one of these lightweight research functions:

- staged site or participant/referent/occurrence position;
- organizing episode, period, wait, recurrence, transition, reported/remembered/prospective frame, or present-reflection frame;
- stable actor, group, or social endpoint;
- source-treated thing/content referent;
- source-applied characterization, status, evaluation, comparison, polarity, correction, rejection, or characterization question;
- operative lexical relation connecting or staging retained coordinates;
- distinct orientation/context relation materially situating retained coordinates.

The role must be more than a grammatically expressible fact. Removing it from the backbone must erase or materially blur a distinct research coordinate or research-significant relation needed to reconnect the represented case.

### 2.2 Role independence is not wording independence

The admitted role must be independently addressable in the research graph. Its source wording does **not** need to stand alone.

Therefore:

- short wording may qualify;
- deictic wording may qualify;
- generic wording may qualify;
- one-use or low-salience wording may qualify;
- a fragment may depend on its surrounding source binding for interpretation;
- a lawful unnamed PLACE/TIME role may qualify without source wording.

Do not widen, merge, paraphrase, or conceptually complete the source atom merely to make the wording independently intelligible.

### 2.3 Source establishment and atomic realization

After the role passes, map it to the smallest exact contiguous source span that performs that admitted job.

The source itself must supply the atom, except for lawful unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinates distinctly staged by an exact source cue.

Do not:

- invent a category name, proposition label, diagnosis, normalized concept, explanatory paraphrase, or conventional expression;
- attach actor/object/place/time arguments merely to make a relation self-explanatory;
- combine separated or merely adjacent source pieces;
- widen a source atom into a clause or proposition;
- merge two roles because one wider phrase could mention both.

Widen only when removing material would change the source-native identity of the admitted role, such as an identity-bearing particle, negation, modal/control material, idiomatic complement, coordinated relation, or contrast.

### 2.4 What never creates a role by itself

None of the following is sufficient for role admission:

- a token or phrase exists;
- a noun/adjective/verb/preposition is present;
- a sentence or clause says something;
- a proposition or event can be reconstructed;
- a dependency parser would isolate a relation;
- wording looks spatial, temporal, descriptive, relational, or referential;
- a phrase can be nominalized;
- a local predicate has participants;
- a modifier changes local meaning;
- a prepositional phrase has orientation semantics;
- the item would make a useful summary;
- the item is materially true of one local sentence.

The positive reason must be a distinct researcher-coordinate role on the lightweight backbone.

### 2.5 Same-class identity

Merge only true same-class same-identity coreference/repetition. Association, co-location, part/whole, container/content, actor/role, episode/subepisode, shared topic, semantic similarity, adjacency, or participation in one event is not identity.

## 3. Research-significant bindings — select packets, not assertions

A **research-significant binding** is a source-staged connection among admitted researcher coordinates that is itself useful for reconnecting the case.

A binding normally changes or establishes at least one material relationship among:

- who is involved;
- what is being handled, discussed, remembered, compared, possessed, selected, questioned, or acted on;
- how a retained referent/person is characterized;
- what operative relation connects retained coordinates;
- where the retained packet is staged;
- when or in what organizing frame it occurs;
- how retained coordinates are oriented relative to one another.

Do not make one binding for every clause, grammatical predicate, assertion, modifier, reported proposition, or syntactic dependency. A sentence may contain several research roles, one research-significant binding, multiple such bindings, or none beyond roles already established elsewhere.

Generic support, copular, reporting, cognition, recipient/topic, prepositional, or discourse material is not excluded by category. It qualifies only when the source stages that relation/orientation/characterization as a distinct researcher-addressable role on the backbone. Grammar never settles this question by itself.

## 4. Coverage without census — replay the backbone

Coverage is mandatory, but the object of coverage is the **researcher-coordinate backbone**, not source vocabulary.

Replay the source in order and ask:

- Did every stable represented actor/social endpoint that participates materially get a role?
- Did every source-treated thing/content referent needed for selected bindings get a role?
- Did every source-applied characterization/polarity role needed for those bindings survive?
- Did every operative relation that actually distinguishes selected bindings survive?
- Did every distinct orientation relation needed to situate selected bindings survive?
- Did every required site/position and organizing time/frame coordinate survive, including lawful unnamed ones?

Then replay by class to catch low-salience omissions **among research roles**.

Do not ask, “What nouns/verbs/descriptions/place phrases/time phrases have I not extracted?” Ask, “What researcher-addressable role on the case backbone is still missing?”

## 5. Cross-class role multiplicity and non-substitution

Type by represented research function, never by surface form.

The same exact source span may lawfully appear in more than one primitive class when it realizes genuinely different admitted researcher roles. Exact textual overlap is not itself duplication across classes.

A source span may, for example, realize both a staged site role and a distinct orientation role, or a relation atom may contain material that separately realizes an orientation role. This is permitted only when the backbone contains both roles independently.

Do not create cross-class twins because grammar allows alternative descriptions. Require a real difference in researcher function.

A retained class cannot erase a separate admitted role in another class: OBJECT does not absorb LABEL; LABEL does not absorb VERB; PLACE/TIME do not absorb LOCATOR; VERB does not absorb actor/object/site/frame; LOCATOR does not replace PLACE.

## 6. PLACE — sites and represented positions

`PLACE` inventories researcher-addressable represented physical/institutional settings and source-staged occurrence, participant, or referent positions.

Retain a named or lawful unnamed PLACE when the researcher backbone materially depends on **where** a retained participant/referent/occurrence is staged, even if the exact location is not named.

Rules:

- Physical naming is not required.
- Broad and contained sites may both survive when each is a distinct backbone coordinate.
- Co-located participant/occurrence positions may remain separate when the source stages distinct positional roles.
- A reported, remembered, prospective, or present-telling occurrence may have an unnamed position when that position is a distinct coordinate needed by the backbone.
- A physical noun, surface, container, body part, direction, movement phrase, or spatial-looking expression is not PLACE merely because it can be located.
- Do not create a PLACE for every local action just because every action happens somewhere.

For lawful unnamed PLACE use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording. Do not invent a more specific location than the source supports.

## 7. TIME — organizing frames and source-staged episodes

`TIME` inventories researcher-addressable episodes, periods, phases, attempts, waits, interactions, transitions, recurrence spans, remembered/reported frames, prospective frames, and present telling/reflection frames that organize material.

Retain a TIME only when the frame groups, positions, or distinguishes material on the researcher backbone.

Rules:

- No date, clock, duration, frequency word, or temporal noun is required.
- A short event may be TIME when it functions as an organizing frame for other admitted roles/bindings.
- Broad and nested frames may both survive when each organizes source material at a distinct research level.
- Intended, remembered, reported, recurring, waiting, interaction, prospective, and present-reflection frames may qualify.
- Do not make every action, clause, verb, tense/aspect shift, explicit temporal phrase, or local source moment into TIME.
- A frame is admitted because it organizes the research graph, not because an event happened.

For lawful unnamed TIME use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording.

## 8. PERSON — stable represented actors/endpoints

`PERSON` inventories `B` plus every distinct stable represented human or social actor/group after strict coreference when that actor occupies its own researcher role.

Retain actors who act, speak, perceive, are acted upon, possess/receive, function as source/target/beneficiary/endpoint/companion/member, or participate materially in remembered/reported/prospective relations.

Minor, brief, offscreen, relational, institutional, possessive, or group actors may qualify when source-established and researcher-addressable.

Exclude rhetorical/generic addressees, generic hypothetical roles, pronouns with no stable referent, and true aliases/coreference duplicates.

Order non-speakers by first independent represented participation rather than incidental possessive mention alone.

## 9. OBJECT — source-treated things and content referents

`OBJECT` inventories source-established things and content referents that occupy their own researcher role in selected bindings.

Lawful OBJECTs may include physical things, scene items, products, documents, parts, surfaces, containers, amounts/values, sets, choices, decisions, plans, relationships-as-content, recurring situations, conditions, services, results, mental contents, comparison vehicles, and explicitly reified relations.

Retain a low-salience or one-use referent when the source actually treats it as a thing/content coordinate that is handled, positioned, possessed, selected, compared, evaluated, remembered, decided, questioned, or pointed back to.

Use the source's own smallest referential atom. Do not create:

- an analyst-completed “thing” from a clause;
- a proposition shell merely because a sentence expresses content;
- a conceptual summary of what an actor wanted, meant, or decided unless the source itself treats that content as a referent;
- every noun phrase merely because it can fill an argument slot;
- a setting duplicated as OBJECT when its only admitted role is PLACE.

## 10. LABEL — source-applied characterization and polarity roles

`LABEL` inventories source-applied states, qualities, statuses, identities, classifications, evaluations, comparisons, manners, polarities, corrections, rejections, and characterization questions when they occupy their own researcher role.

Retain the smallest exact source-native atom that realizes the admitted characterization role. It may be brief or context-dependent. A polarity response, rejection, correction, uncertainty marker, or question may be its own LABEL when it materially changes or qualifies a researcher-addressable characterization.

Preserve question, negation, uncertainty, rejection, correction, contrast, attribution, colloquial wording, and material intensity.

Do not create LABEL from every adjective, adverb, modifier, colorful phrase, ordinary predicate, rhetorical flourish, or local descriptive fact. The source must apply a distinct characterization/polarity role that matters to the researcher backbone.

## 11. VERB — operative source-native relations

`VERB` inventories the smallest exact source-native lexical relation atoms that realize admitted **operative relation roles** on the researcher backbone.

A VERB qualifies when its admitted role materially connects or stages retained coordinates in a research-significant binding. Eligible relation kinds may include action, state, transition, perception, cognition, speech/reporting, intention, comparison, possession, movement, waiting, support, copular relation, or other source-native relation, but category membership alone never admits it.

Ask first: **Is this relation itself a distinct researcher-addressable edge on the backbone?** Only then select its lexical atom.

A generic or context-dependent relation may qualify when its role is independently needed even though its wording is not self-explanatory. Conversely, a vivid or content-bearing predicate does not qualify merely because it has semantic content.

Use the smallest exact contiguous lexical kernel that preserves the admitted relation identity. Keep particles, directionals, reflexives, negation-bearing material, modal/control/infinitival material, idiomatic complements, or coordination only when necessary to preserve that identity.

Do not:

- harvest every grammatical predicate;
- retain every matrix/embedded/reporting/support/copular relation merely because it links a clause;
- attach arguments merely to make the VERB readable;
- merge distinct admitted relation roles merely because they share a clause;
- suppress an admitted relation only because its wording is generic, deictic, or context-dependent.

Split matrix/embedded or coordinated predicates only when the backbone contains materially distinct operative relation roles; preserve them together when they form one source-native relation identity.

## 12. LOCATOR — researcher-addressable orientation/context roles

`LOCATOR` inventories exact source-native orientation/context relations that realize an admitted orientation role on the researcher backbone.

Eligible roles may include relative position, containment, direction, path, origin/destination, entry/exit, approach/departure, proximity/distance, accompaniment/carrying, recurrence/situational context, embodied/internal orientation, and materially useful figurative/comparison orientation.

Ask first: **Does this orientation itself distinguish how a retained participant/referent/action/site/frame/comparison is situated on the researcher graph?** Only then select its source atom.

A LOCATOR may be short, deictic, or context-dependent and need not name both endpoints in its own text. The surrounding selected binding supplies those endpoints.

Use the smallest exact local span that carries the admitted orientation role. Do not merge neighboring locator atoms merely to make one self-contained phrase.

Suppress bare grammatical prepositions, routine recipient/topic marking, possession alone, incidental adverbials, and local spatial/context phrases when they do not occupy a distinct researcher-addressable orientation role.

## 13. Literal, contiguity, and posture lock

Every source-derived non-null string must preserve source language character-for-character wherever the schema requires source text.

Never synonymize, lemmatize, repair spelling/grammar, normalize dialect, expand contractions, translate, polish, diagnose, standardize a group name, change number, or substitute a conventional expression. Do not replace source-native labels for people/groups/things with analyst-created category names.

Do not concatenate noncontiguous source fragments into one primitive. Do not silently add omitted words. Do not broaden a fragment merely so a reader can understand it without context.

`source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be exact source text where applicable. Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME may use null `source_wording`.

Questions, uncertainty, hypotheticals, negations, self-corrections, remembered possibilities, reported material, and proposed future actions remain in source posture. Inventorying does not assert truth or realization.

## 14. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean only. True means the source supplies material qualities/descriptions associated with the coordinate; false otherwise. Do not create or split primitives to justify the boolean. `Q` is never a primitive.

## 15. Ordering

Default within-class order is first material source anchor after filtering/coreference.

Tie rules:

- broad/whole setting before dependent/contained setting when introduced together;
- whole before dependent part at the same anchor unless the source stages the part first;
- PERSON follows first independent represented participation;
- remembered/reported/hypothetical/future frames remain at source position rather than external chronology.

The apparatus owns canonical IDs and deterministic numbering.

## 16. Structural floor audit — missing research roles

Replay the whole researcher backbone and each selected research-significant binding. Ask whether a distinct role needed to reconnect the case disappeared because its source realization was brief, generic, unnamed, deictic, context-dependent, low-salience, nested, reported, remembered, prospective, uncertain, negative, or one-use.

Specifically check for under-resolution of:

- unnamed occurrence/participant/referent positions that are genuine backbone coordinates;
- broad and local organizing frames, waits, interactions, recurrence, transitions, remembered/reported/prospective frames, and present-reflection frames that organize admitted material;
- minor stable actors/endpoints with real participation;
- low-salience concrete referents actually used as things in selected bindings;
- abstract choices/decisions/contents/relations explicitly treated as referents;
- questioned/rejected/corrected source-applied characterization or polarity roles;
- generic or short lexical relations that are genuine operative backbone edges;
- short physical/figurative/context orientation roles that materially situate admitted coordinates;
- lawful cross-class roles supported by the same source span.

Add a missing primitive only when the **role** is independently admitted first. Do not widen or retype a neighboring primitive to compensate.

## 17. Anti-census ceiling audit — no atom without a backbone role

For every retained primitive ask in this order:

1. What exact researcher-coordinate role exists before considering the wording?
2. Which selected research-significant binding(s) require that role?
3. Would deleting the role erase or materially blur a distinct coordinate/relation in the lightweight research graph?
4. What exact source span realizes the admitted role, or what exact cue establishes an unnamed PLACE/TIME?
5. Is the span the smallest exact contiguous unit that preserves the role?
6. Did lexical/grammatical form create the role, rather than merely realize it?
7. Did I invent, complete, merge, paraphrase, or widen the wording to make it self-explanatory?
8. Is it distinct from same-class identities already retained?

If there is no admitted backbone role before wording selection, remove the primitive even when the phrase is semantically meaningful, locally material, or easy to classify.

Never aim for a hidden row count or density.

## 18. Compound construction — replay selected research bindings

Freeze primitives before compound work.

A compound is a selective packet of frozen primitives representing one **research-significant backbone binding** useful for reconnecting the case.

Create a compound when:

1. the backbone already contains a distinct binding among two or more frozen coordinates;
2. the source materially stages that binding;
3. the packet contains only primitives active in that binding; and
4. it is not an alternate parse, dependency closure, pairwise/transitive closure, one-compound-per-token artifact, one-compound-per-clause artifact, clause-support bundle, duplicate/subset alias, or scene-wide mega-bundle.

A short/context-dependent VERB may anchor a compound when it realizes the admitted relation edge. LABEL or LOCATOR may participate when active in that binding. PLACE/TIME participate only when they actually organize or position the binding.

Compounds reconnect context-dependent atoms; primitives do not need compound-level context embedded in their wording.

A compound cannot create, delete, merge, retype, repair, or substitute for a primitive. If a needed role is missing, return to role/backbone review first.

Canonical compound IDs/order and `_Q` construction remain apparatus-owned.

## 19. Final contrast audit

### Too dense / census failure

Warning signs:

- the internal process starts from all source atoms rather than roles;
- every grammatical predicate becomes VERB;
- every noun phrase becomes OBJECT;
- every description/modifier becomes LABEL;
- every local moment or event becomes TIME;
- every spatial/context phrase becomes PLACE/LOCATOR;
- every represented assertion becomes a binding;
- compounds track nearly every clause.

Correct by returning to the researcher-coordinate backbone. Local semantic validity is not enough.

### Too sparse / conceptual-compression failure

Warning signs:

- an admitted role is omitted because its wording seems generic or weak;
- a deictic or tiny orientation atom is widened or dropped;
- a brief polarity/characterization role disappears;
- separate broad/contained or unnamed positions/frames collapse;
- same-span cross-class roles are suppressed merely to avoid textual duplication;
- several admitted roles are merged into one explanatory phrase;
- source context is replaced by analyst interpretation.

Correct by preserving the admitted role while restoring its smallest exact source-native realization. Wording weakness is not role weakness.

## 20. Final audit

Before return verify:

- **Backbone first:** role membership was decided before primitive wording was harvested.
- **Role floor:** every distinct researcher-addressable coordinate/relation needed to reconnect selected case structure is present.
- **Census ceiling:** no primitive exists merely because a source atom, grammatical relation, or local assertion can be classified.
- **Source atomicity:** each non-null primitive uses the smallest exact contiguous source span that realizes its admitted role.
- **No self-containment pressure:** no primitive was widened merely so it makes sense outside context.
- **Typing:** every primitive is typed by represented research function rather than word form.
- **Cross-class:** genuinely distinct admitted roles may share source wording; alternate parses do not multiply rows.
- **Literal:** exact wording, dialect, polarity, question, uncertainty, negation, and posture are preserved.
- **Compounds:** only selected research-significant backbone bindings reassemble frozen active refs.
- **Candidate:** no promotion, scoring, APA identity, or database action occurred.
