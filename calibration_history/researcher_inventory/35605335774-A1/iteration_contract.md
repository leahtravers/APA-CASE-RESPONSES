## Prospective correction basis

V139 verified both immutable Leah-approved archetype workbook hashes and passed all retained deterministic apparatus and harness tests, then failed hidden semantic calibration on both approved archetypes. The failure showed a generalizable semantic-selection defect rather than a harness defect: the global `researcher addressability` gate suppressed many genuine low-salience, one-use, structural, relational, and lexical coordinates while still admitting some grammatical/descriptive material at the wrong class or span. Compound failures were largely downstream of primitive mismatch.

V140 therefore preserves V139's whole-source reading, literal lock, candidate isolation, anti-census protection, immutable-archetype boundary, and frozen-primitive compound architecture, but changes the semantic decision order. Each primitive class now receives a **positive class-native coverage pass first**, followed by a **bounded suppression pass**. The worker may suppress only named defect classes. Low salience, one use, background status, nesting, deictic/relational wording, remembered/reported status, prospectivity, uncertainty, negation, or lack of an explicit place/time name are not suppression reasons by themselves.

No gold rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, scored predecessor outputs, canonical archetype extracts, case-specific hidden corrections/examples, or sealed holdout content are supplied to the worker.

## Controlling rule

> **READ THE WHOLE SOURCE FIRST. FOR EACH REQUESTED CLASS, POSITIVELY RECOVER EVERY DISTINCT SOURCE-SUPPORTED COORDINATE THAT ACTUALLY PERFORMS THAT CLASS'S RESEARCH JOB AT LIGHTWEIGHT WORKSHEET GRAIN. THEN REMOVE ONLY TRUE ALIASES/COREFERENCE DUPLICATES, PURE GRAMMATICAL MACHINERY WITH NO CLASS JOB, UNSUPPORTED PARAPHRASE OR INFERENCE, ARBITRARY SUBPARTS, WHOLE-CLAUSE/PROPOSITION WRAPPERS NOT REIFIED BY THE SOURCE, OR DUPLICATE PROJECTIONS OF THE SAME RESEARCH JOB. TYPE BY SOURCE FUNCTION, NOT SURFACE FORM. USE THE SMALLEST COMPLETE SOURCE-NATIVE GRAIN. PRESERVE EXACT SOURCE WORDING AND POSTURE. FREEZE PRIMITIVES BEFORE COMPOUNDS. COMPOUNDS PRESERVE MATERIAL LOCAL BINDINGS AMONG FROZEN COORDINATES; THEY DO NOT CREATE, REPAIR, RETYPE, OR SUBSTITUTE FOR PRIMITIVES.**

## Mission and primitive classes

Produce only a literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate from the supplied source.

Primitive classes are exactly:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The target is neither semantic compression nor lexical/grammatical census. It is source-supported researcher-reconnectable coordinates at class-native resolution.

## 1. Mandatory construction order

1. Read the complete source once without extracting primitives.
2. Resolve strict coreference, broad source order, and materially distinct scene/episode shifts.
3. For each requested class independently, run a **positive coverage pass** across the whole source.
4. For every candidate, choose the smallest complete source-native grain that preserves its class job.
5. Type by represented research function, never by part of speech or lexical appearance alone.
6. Run a cross-class audit for genuinely different independently reconnectable jobs performed by overlapping source material.
7. Apply only the bounded suppression rules in Section 3.
8. Apply same-class identity/deduplication.
9. Run the structural floor and anti-census ceiling audits.
10. Apply literal/posture lock and freeze primitive membership.
11. Build compounds from frozen primitives only.
12. Run final coverage, typing, lexical, compound, and isolation audits.

Do not decide membership from salience first. Do not begin with a compressed summary or a proposition ledger and then try to recover omitted coordinates.

## 2. Universal positive admission rule

A primitive is positively eligible when:

- the source establishes it, or for lawful unnamed PLACE/TIME clearly stages the distinct structural position/frame;
- it performs the native research job of the class being requested;
- at lightweight worksheet grain it is a distinct source-supported coordinate a researcher can reconnect, code, compare, or bind to other retained coordinates; and
- its span/frame is the smallest complete natural identity for that job.

One use is enough. Background placement is enough when the class job is genuine. Repetition and global centrality are not required.

Explicit mention alone is not enough. A token, phrase, clause, or proposition does not qualify merely because it is grammatically identifiable.

## 3. Bounded suppression rule

After positive coverage, suppress only when at least one of these defects applies:

1. true same-class alias/coreference duplication;
2. pure auxiliary/function/discourse grammar with no independent class job;
3. unsupported inference, normalization, paraphrase, analyst-created abstraction, or invented wording;
4. arbitrary fragment/subpart with no independent source identity;
5. duplicate same-grain restatement or alternate tokenization of the same class job;
6. a whole-clause/sentence/question/proposition wrapper that the source does not itself treat as identifiable content, choice, plan, decision, relationship, situation, or object of thought;
7. a bare preposition/path particle with no independent orientation job;
8. an alternative grammatical parse that merely duplicates another retained projection's research job; or
9. material that does not actually perform the requested class's native role.

Do **not** suppress solely because a coordinate is low-salience, brief, one-use, local, background, nested, remembered, reported, hypothetical, prospective, uncertain, negative, deictic, relational, recoverable from a compound, or associated with another retained coordinate.

## 4. Grain, identity, and cross-class rules

Use the smallest **complete** source-native unit that preserves the research job.

- Merge only true same-class same-identity coreference/repetition.
- Association, co-location, part/whole, container/content, actor/role, episode/subepisode, or semantic relatedness is not identity.
- Whole and dependent part may both survive if each is independently source-treated.
- Broad and contained PLACE/TIME may both survive when each performs a distinct organizing role.
- Overlapping wording may support more than one class only when the source presents genuinely different reconnectable roles. Cross-class overlap is not automatically an error, but alternative grammatical parses of one job are.
- Decide class job before span compression. Do not force spatial-looking wording into PLACE, temporal-looking wording into TIME, descriptive wording into LABEL, or noun-like wording into OBJECT unless it actually performs that role.

## 5. PLACE — source-staged positions and settings

Run a positive whole-source PLACE pass.

Retain every distinct represented physical/institutional setting or occurrence position that organizes source material, including as applicable:

- broad and contained settings when both matter;
- interaction, help, waiting, conversation, departure, arrival, destination, or participant positions;
- another represented participant's scene/position;
- a source-staged position of a retained referent when the position itself is reconnectable;
- remembered, reported, prospective, imagined, comparison, or present-telling positions;
- lawful unnamed positions when a distinct scene/location job exists without an explicit place name.

An explicit spatial noun/phrase does not qualify merely because it sounds locative. A concrete object does not become PLACE merely because it can contain/support something.

For lawful unnamed PLACE use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording.

## 6. TIME — source-staged episodes and frames

Run a positive whole-source TIME pass.

Retain every distinct source-supported episode, phase, period, attempt, interaction/help/conversation episode, wait, transition, remembered/reported frame, recurrence span, prospective/future frame, comparison/imagined frame, stable period, and present reflection/telling frame when it independently organizes source material.

A distinct short local episode may qualify. A broad and nested frame may both qualify. No date, duration, frequency word, or explicit temporal noun is required.

Do not create TIME from tense alone, every verb/clause, bare `now/then`, or an explicit duration/frequency phrase that does not organize an independent frame.

For lawful unnamed TIME use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording.

## 7. PERSON — stable human/social actors

Run a positive whole-source PERSON pass.

Retain `B` plus every distinct stable represented human or social actor/group after true coreference, including minor, possessive/relational, offscreen, remembered, reported, prospective, institutional, or comparison actors when source-established.

Participation may include acting, speaking, perceiving, being acted upon, possessing/receiving, or functioning as source, target, beneficiary, endpoint, companion, or member of a represented relation.

Exclude only nonreferential/rhetorical/generic grammatical addressees and true aliases/coreference duplicates.

Order non-speakers by first independent represented participation rather than incidental possessive mention alone.

## 8. OBJECT — source-treated things and content referents

Run a positive whole-source OBJECT pass.

Retain independently source-treated concrete and abstract referents, including as applicable physical things, scene items, products, documents, parts, containers, amounts/values, sets, choices, decisions, plans, relationships, recurring situations, mental contents, comparison vehicles, conditions, services, results, and explicitly reified relations/content.

One-off and low-salience referents may qualify. Do not suppress a scene item simply because it supports or locates another thing if the source also treats it as an independent referent.

Reject pure pronoun/deictic aliases, arbitrary noun fragments, and whole proposition/clause wrappers unless the source itself reifies that course of action, situation, relationship, plan, decision, or content as an identifiable thing. Do not duplicate a setting as OBJECT when its only research job is PLACE.

## 9. LABEL — source-applied characterizations

Run a positive whole-source LABEL pass.

Retain source-staged values, states, qualities, classifications, identities, evaluations, comparisons, corrections, rejections, polarity responses, manner/state descriptions, statuses, and characterization questions when the characterization itself is an independently reconnectable source move.

Preserve question, negation, uncertainty, rejection, correction, contrast, attribution, and material intensity at the shortest complete source-native grain.

Do not create LABEL from every adjective, modifier, colorful phrase, descriptive clause, or ordinary predicate. Description is not enough unless the characterization itself is the research coordinate.

## 10. VERB — content-bearing lexical relation kernels

Run a positive whole-source VERB pass.

Retain every materially distinct content-bearing lexical relation/event that is part of the research relation spine, including as applicable movement, possession, experience, cognition, speech/reporting, perception, intention, comparison, waiting, stance, gesture, copular/locative/state relations, questioning, finding, thinking, saying/telling, and other represented predicates.

The unit is the **smallest complete lexical relation kernel** that preserves one admitted relation identity.

- Keep required particles, reflexives, directionals, negation-bearing material, modal/control/infinitival material, idiomatic complements, or coordinated material when removing it changes or hollows out the relation identity.
- Split matrix/embedded or coordinated predicates only when they are genuinely distinct admitted relations.
- Do not swallow independent PERSON/OBJECT/PLACE/TIME/LABEL/LOCATOR arguments merely to make a clause.
- Suppress only pure auxiliaries/function grammar and true same-relation duplicates. Do not suppress a genuine relation merely because it is local, background, embedded, one-use, reporting, cognition, copular, or state-like.

## 11. LOCATOR — orientation and context relations

Run a positive whole-source LOCATOR pass.

Retain materially distinct source-native orientation/context relations for relative position, containment, direction, path, origin/destination, entry/exit, approach/departure, proximity/distance, accompaniment/carrying, recurrence/situational context, embodied/internal orientation, and materially useful figurative/comparison orientation.

The LOCATOR span must be local to the orientation/context relation. Do not widen it into a whole explanatory or decision clause merely because the clause contains orientation language.

Bare prepositional grammar, ordinary argument attachment, possession, recipient/topic marking, and isolated particles are insufficient without an independent orientation/context job.

## 12. Literal and posture lock

Every source-derived non-null string must preserve source language character-for-character wherever the schema requires source text.

Never synonymize, lemmatize, repair spelling/grammar, normalize dialect, expand contractions, translate, polish, diagnose, change number, or substitute a standard expression.

`source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be exact source text where applicable. Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME may use null `source_wording`.

Questions, uncertainty, hypotheticals, negations, self-corrections, remembered possibilities, reported material, and proposed future actions remain in source posture. Inventorying does not assert truth or realization.

## 13. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean only. True means the source supplies material qualities/descriptions associated with the coordinate; false otherwise. Do not create or split primitives to justify the boolean. `Q` is never a primitive.

## 14. Ordering

Default within-class order is first material source anchor after filtering/coreference.

Tie rules:

- broad/whole setting before dependent/contained setting when introduced together;
- whole before dependent part at the same anchor unless the source stages the part first;
- PERSON follows first independent represented participation;
- remembered/reported/hypothetical/future frames remain at source position rather than external chronology.

The apparatus owns canonical IDs and deterministic numbering.

## 15. Structural floor audit

After class-local positive coverage and bounded suppression, replay the whole source without starting a fresh lexical census.

Ask whether every genuine class-native coordinate received a positive test, especially:

- unnamed or relational occurrence/participant/referent positions;
- short local and broad episodes, waits, interactions, recurrence, transitions, remembered/reported/prospective/present frames;
- minor stable actors/endpoints;
- low-salience concrete referents and source-treated abstract contents;
- source-applied questions, corrections, rejections, polarities, and states;
- complete lexical relation kernels;
- local orientation/context relations.

If a genuine distinct role is missing, add it in the correct class. Do not widen or retype a neighboring primitive to compensate.

## 16. Anti-census ceiling audit

For every retained primitive ask:

1. What native research job does it perform?
2. What exact source material establishes that job?
3. Is it retained for represented function rather than merely lexical/grammatical form?
4. Is this the smallest complete natural identity for that job?
5. Is it distinct from same-class identities already retained?
6. Would it disappear under one of the bounded suppression defects in Section 3?

If no class-native research job survives, remove it. Never target a hidden count or density.

## 17. Compound construction — positive local binding, then dedupe

Freeze primitives before compound work.

A compound is a local packet of frozen coordinates whose represented binding is itself useful for reconnecting the case.

Run a positive relation/configuration pass through source order. For each materially distinct local relation/state/configuration that binds two or more retained coordinates:

1. include the operative retained VERB/LABEL/LOCATOR coordinate(s) when active;
2. include retained PERSON/OBJECT/value roles actually participating;
3. include PLACE/TIME only when they actively organize that binding;
4. preserve the smallest materially complete local binding;
5. emit no primitive not active in that binding.

Then remove only true duplicate/subset aliases, alternate tokenizations/parses of the same binding, pairwise/transitive closure, one-compound-per-primitive artifacts, clause-support bundles, and scene-wide mega-bundles.

A compound cannot add, delete, merge, retype, or repair primitives. If a necessary coordinate is missing, return to primitive review first. Canonical compound IDs/order and `_Q` construction are apparatus-owned.

## 18. Final audit

Before return verify:

- **Coverage:** every source-supported class-native coordinate received a positive test before suppression.
- **Suppression:** every deletion fits one of Section 3's allowed defect classes.
- **Typing:** each retained coordinate is typed by source research job, not surface form.
- **Grain:** each source-native span/frame is the smallest complete identity for its job.
- **Cross-class:** genuinely distinct jobs survive; duplicate grammatical parses do not.
- **Coreference:** true aliases are merged without collapsing related-but-distinct roles.
- **Literal:** source strings and posture are exact.
- **Compounds:** materially distinct local bindings use frozen active refs only.
- **Candidate:** no promotion, scoring, APA identity, or database action occurred.

## 19. Worker isolation

The worker receives only this durable contract, one source case, researcher interest, and the contract-subordinate task/schema envelope admitted by the apparatus.

An unscored provisional output from another stateless pass may be used only when the governing apparatus explicitly allows it; it is never gold/evaluator feedback.

## 20. Apparatus boundary

The apparatus, not the worker, owns canonical IDs, deterministic numbering, exact-source validation, source ordering enforcement, alias merge enforcement, compound reference validation, `_Q` construction, SQL-ready shaping, retries, persistence, evaluator comparison, and failure handling.

The worker must not depend on preexisting canonical IDs or expected outputs.

## 21. Candidate-only boundary

All output remains Researcher Inventory candidate material.

Never promote to Oval Office, admitted research, or sovereign APA records; mint an APA Blockchain ID; write to APA database/fabric systems; perform APA parsing, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, scoring, executive analysis, or policy determination; or present candidate inventory as established truth.

## 22. Holdout boundary

The calibration worker must never receive the sealed holdout source, holdout answer, holdout score, or derivative example.

The sealed holdout may be opened only by the authorized holdout apparatus after the **same finalized contract and saved calibration lineage repeatedly pass both approved archetypes**. If the calibrated lineage passes the one-shot holdout, that lineage is retired. Clean-room certification then requires a brand-new saved agent booted only from this finalized durable contract, with no prior calibration sessions or holdout output, to run the sealed holdout in a new session.

No calibration correction may be made from sealed holdout output.
