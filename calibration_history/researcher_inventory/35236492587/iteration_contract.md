## 1. Coordinate-identity gate

Read the whole source before extraction. A primitive survives only when all of the following hold:

1. **source support** — exact source wording/cue, except a supported unnamed PLACE/TIME anchor;
2. **class identity** — it performs the semantic research job of its class rather than merely being grammatically expressible there;
3. **independent selectability** — it is a stable coordinate a researcher could address separately inside the source's represented map; and
4. **non-redundancy** — it is not an alias, duplicate mention, whole-clause restatement, support-grammar artifact, micro-fragment, or second projection with no separate class job.

A coordinate may be single-use. It does **not** need recurrence or usefulness outside its local proposition. The question is whether it has its own represented coordinate identity, not whether it appears more than once.

Do not confuse `single-use` with `local grammatical fragment`. A one-time person, object, value, relation, orientation, scene, or frame may survive; a whole proposition, reason clause, question wrapper, argument phrase, or event restatement does not become a primitive merely because it can be named.

## 2. Scene and frame ledger

Before extracting the other classes, reconstruct the source-order ledger of materially distinct represented settings and temporal/event frames.

### PLACE grain

PLACE records material physical or represented settings/scene anchors. Preserve explicit broad and contained settings when both organize the represented material. Preserve supported unnamed anchors when a distinct object location, destination, waiting position, later interaction/conversation, remembered scene, prospective scene, participant-position scene, or present telling requires a place coordinate even though the source does not name that place.

Do not create PLACE for every surface, body part, container edge, object support point, path point, movement endpoint, or phrase that merely contains spatial wording. A thing that functions only as an object does not become PLACE.

### TIME grain

TIME records materially distinct event/episode frames in source progression. Preserve source-staged attempts/conditions, help/response episodes, departures/transitions, waits, intended periods, later conversations/reports, recurring spans, remembered episodes, present reflection/telling, and prospective/future frames when they organize distinct represented material.

Do not mint TIME for every verb, subpredicate, local action phase, question, tense/aspect form, duration, adverb, or clause. Several relations may share one TIME. Split only when the source stages a materially distinct episode/frame.

Unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinates may use `source_wording: null` with an exact source cue. Never invent a location or time name.

## 3. PERSON

Retain `B` and every materially represented human/social actor or stable group after coreference, including indirect, possessive, relational, offscreen, remembered, reported, prospective, and comparison actors when represented.

Resolve true aliases/coreference. Exclude generic rhetorical addressees and classification words that do not denote represented actors.

## 4. OBJECT

OBJECT records materially represented independently selectable concrete or abstract things.

Retain, when source-supported, concrete wholes/parts, amounts/values, orders/services/results, decisions/next steps, contemplated choices treated as things, stable relations, situations, named sets/categories, plans, and internal/figurative represented objects.

Do not perform a noun or proposition census. Do not create OBJECT for:

- a whole assertion or question merely because it has content;
- a reason/explanation clause whose only job is relational;
- an event restated as a thing when its identity is already carried by TIME/VERB plus arguments;
- a transient argument phrase with no independent thing-like identity;
- a referent whose only source job is PLACE.

Single-use objects may survive when they have independent thing-like identity.

## 5. LABEL

LABEL records independently selectable exact source-present characterization values: quality, state, evaluation, comparison, identity/classification, correction/rejection, polarity/status, experiential/epistemic description, or materially qualifying manner.

Use the **shortest complete source-native value** that carries the characterization's posture. Preserve source-native uncertainty, question, negation, intensity, correction, contrast, and idiom when they belong to the value.

Do not promote an entire proposition, question sentence, reason clause, or predicate packet into LABEL when a smaller source-present characterization value carries the label job. Distinct atomic values may remain separate when the source presents them as separately meaningful characterizations.

LABEL inventory is separate from `qualities_available`.

## 6. VERB

VERB records materially represented lexical relation/predicate increments in source order.

Retain principal event/relation atoms and coordinated or embedded relations when each establishes its own represented relational job. Cognition, stance, report, recall, perception, intention, comparison, location, waiting/continuation, and other matrix relations are retained when they materially contribute epistemic, attributional, modal, or relational posture; do not discard them merely because another embedded predicate is also present.

Choose the **smallest semantically complete exact source-native lexical construction**. Keep a particle, negation, reflexive, or required complement only when removing it changes the relation identity. Ordinary subjects, objects, recipients, topics, and optional complements belong in compounds rather than inside the VERB wording.

Split coordinated predicates when they perform different relation jobs. Do not emit every parseable subpredicate, copular support, auxiliary, discourse wrapper, or clause-level paraphrase.

## 7. LOCATOR

LOCATOR records selected exact source-native orientation/context relations that independently position a retained person, object, event, or relation.

Retain material physical or figurative position, containment, support, proximity, path, direction, origin/destination, accompaniment, recurring-context, embodied/internal position, or other orientation when the orientation itself is independently selectable.

Use the smallest complete meaningful locating construction, not an isolated preposition.

Do not classify a phrase as LOCATOR merely because it contains movement or directional-looking language. If the phrase's only represented job is the predicate/action itself, keep it as VERB. A motion/state span may project into both VERB and LOCATOR only when the source separately represents both the relation and an independently useful orientation coordinate.

Recipients, topics, instruments, durations, frequency fragments, discourse markers, causal/reason phrases, and generic complements are not LOCATOR merely because they are prepositional or contextual.

## 8. Cross-class projection

Cross-class overlap is **permitted but conservative**.

The same exact source span may appear in more than one class only when each projection has a separately addressable class-specific research job. Technical defensibility is not enough. Do not duplicate a phrase into OBJECT/LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR simply because multiple grammatical analyses are possible.

Before keeping an overlap, ask internally: `If the other projection were removed, would this class still contribute a distinct research coordinate rather than restating the same local meaning?` If not, keep only the class that carries the actual research job.

A concrete referent may be both PLACE and OBJECT only when thing identity and setting identity are separately represented.

Within every class, merge true aliases/coreference and duplicate mentions.

## 9. Literal preservation lock

Every non-null `source_wording`, every `source_cue`, every non-null `order_cue`, and every source-derived short tag must preserve source language. Never substitute synonyms, grammatical repairs, normalized dialect, number changes, expanded contractions, inferred terminology, or semantically convenient replacements.

Every emitted source-derived string must be a character-for-character contiguous source substring where the schema requires source text. Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinates may use `source_wording: null`, with an exact source cue.

Preserve spelling, punctuation, dialect, contractions, number, questions, negation, uncertainty, correction, comparison, attribution, intention, recurrence/report/prospective posture, and figurative wording.

## 10. Ordering and qualities

Order each class by first source establishment after coreference, with speaker first in PERSON.

`qualities_available` is true only when source-present qualities/descriptions are available for that coordinate. Quality availability does not force or forbid a separate LABEL.

## 11. Compound reconstruction

Freeze primitives only after the coordinate-identity and scene/frame audits. Then re-read the source in order.

For each distinct source-local relation, attribution, characterization, question, position, comparison, decision, intention, or event binding that connects frozen coordinates, create one **materially complete local compound** containing all frozen coordinates actually co-bound in that semantic unit, including the applicable scene/frame anchor when material.

Compounds express bindings among already justified coordinates. They do not justify extra primitives and are not a second inventory of every clause.

Do not create nested partials, pairwise subsets, alternate decompositions, duplicate bundles, or scene-wide supersets. One source semantic unit gets one complete compound unless the source establishes separately useful bindings through changed actor, target, relation, attribution, comparison, question, orientation, or frame.

A primitive may remain unbundled.

## 12. Final audits

**Scene/frame audit:** recover materially distinct named or supported unnamed PLACE/TIME anchors, including participant/object location, destination, waiting, later interaction, present telling/reflection, recurring, and prospective frames; remove micro-sublocations and predicate-phase times.

**Independent-selectability audit:** remove whole-clause restatements, local argument/content fragments, and duplicate projections that have no separate coordinate identity. Do not delete a genuine single-use coordinate merely because it is local.

**OBJECT audit:** keep independently selectable concrete/abstract things, decisions/options/relations/situations; remove proposition and event-restatement objects.

**LABEL audit:** keep the shortest complete characterization value; remove whole predicate/proposition duplicates.

**VERB audit:** keep materially represented lexical relation increments, including stance/report/cognition when they add posture; remove auxiliary/support and clause-restatement inflation.

**LOCATOR audit:** keep independently useful orientation/context increments; remove motion/state duplication when orientation is not separately represented.

**Type/overlap audit:** for every cross-class overlap, identify the distinct class job internally; remove any projection that merely re-expresses another.

**Literal audit:** compare every emitted source-derived string against the source and repair any synonym, normalization, dialect change, number change, or invented wording before return.

**Compound audit:** one materially complete local binding from the frozen coordinates, without subset/alternate-decomposition inflation.

Then verify source order, qualities flags, compound references, candidate-only status, and holdout isolation.
