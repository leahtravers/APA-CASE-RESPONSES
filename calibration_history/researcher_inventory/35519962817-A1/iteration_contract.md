## Prospective correction basis

V117 executed under verified immutable Case 2 and Case 6 archetype workbooks and passed the deterministic apparatus/harness suite before semantic evaluation. It then failed semantically on both approved archetype cases. The failure is generalizable and belongs to worker behavior rather than the harness.

V117's `SOURCE-RECONNECTION COORDINATE CLOSURE` rule remained too permissive because a locally meaningful source span could be judged useful for reconnection even when the source had not individuated that span as a durable inventory coordinate. This produced a surface-fragment census in several classes while still omitting some low-salience but genuinely source-individuated coordinates.

V118 therefore uses one controlling rule:

> **MINIMAL SUFFICIENT BINDING BASIS AT SOURCE-INDIVIDUATED GRAIN.** First identify the materially distinct source bindings. Then retain the smallest set of source-individuated primitives needed to reconstruct those bindings without loss. A primitive is admitted only when the source itself presents it, explicitly or structurally, as a participant, referential handle, characterization, operative relation kernel, orientation, spatial support, or temporal support that changes at least one materially distinct binding. Semantic meaningfulness, grammatical separability, vivid wording, discourse usefulness, or sentence-reconstruction value alone is insufficient. Local and one-use coordinates remain lawful when they pass the same test.

No archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, calibration answers, or sealed holdout material are supplied to the worker.

## Mission

Produce a literal, lightweight Researcher Inventory of the complete supplied source in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The inventory is a compact source-reconnection basis. It is not a token/POS census, sentence parse, clause census, semantic-role census, proposition census, event ledger, discourse annotation, rhetorical-style inventory, exhaustive ontology, or graph closure.

Do not perform APA scoring, psychological interpretation, conclusions, promotion, APA-ID creation, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA database mutation.

## 1. Read the whole source and identify material bindings first

Read the complete source before extracting any class. Silently identify the materially distinct source bindings that a researcher would need to reconnect the narrative, interaction, report, memory, comparison, decision, reflection, or prospective frame.

A **material binding** is a source-presented relationship among represented coordinates whose loss would change who/what/how/where/when of a materially represented occurrence, state, judgment, question, comparison, choice, report, or reflection.

Do not create a binding merely because a sentence, clause, predicate, modifier, connective, quotation-like fragment, discourse marker, or rhetorical flourish exists. Several lexical relations may jointly form one material binding. One narrative frame may contain several materially different bindings.

The binding map is an omission-control and excess-control device. It is not output unless the apparatus schema explicitly asks for a neutral identity key.

## 2. Primitive admission — all gates must pass

Retain a proposed primitive only when every applicable gate below passes.

### A. Source grounding

The source explicitly establishes the coordinate in the requested class, or clearly establishes an unnamed PLACE/TIME support coordinate. Never invent analyst facts.

### B. Source individuation

The source must present the candidate as a distinct coordinate, explicitly or structurally, rather than merely contain words that could be interpreted as one.

A source-individuated coordinate is something that can stand in the inventory as a stable source-native handle for a represented actor, referent/content node, characterization, operative relation, orientation, spatial support, or temporal support.

Reject wording that exists only as:

- grammatical carriage;
- clause packaging;
- sentence-completion material;
- a discourse-management phrase;
- rhetorical color;
- a vivid but non-structural aside;
- an internal fragment of a stronger retained coordinate;
- or an analyst-created semantic decomposition.

### C. Material binding contribution

The candidate must change at least one materially distinct binding. Ask:

**If this row were removed while all other retained rows remained, would some material binding lose a participant, referential target/content node, characterization, operative relation, orientation, spatial support, or temporal support?**

If not, reject it as redundant or merely descriptive wording.

This is not a salience test. A local, one-use, mundane, background, remembered, reported, prospective, uncertain, negated, figurative, or colloquial coordinate may still qualify when it makes a nonredundant contribution to a material binding.

### D. Natural class grain

Retain the smallest complete source-native coordinate for the class.

Do not split a natural predicate kernel, characterization, orientation, or referential handle into meaningful internal fragments. Do not wrap a larger sentence or clause around a coordinate merely because the larger span explains it.

### E. Nonredundancy

If another retained coordinate of the same class already carries the same source-individuated job in the same binding structure, merge true restatements/coreference rather than multiplying rows.

Do not merge genuinely distinct coordinates merely because they share a target, scene, episode, or lexical family.

### F. Class-native function

Type by represented function, not by part of speech, syntax, metaphor, or what a phrase could be made to mean analytically.

## 3. Minimal sufficient basis audit

Before primitive freeze, perform two directions of review.

### Missing-coordinate audit

Replay each material binding in source order. Ask whether every source-individuated coordinate required to reconstruct that binding is present at natural class grain.

Restore a coordinate when its absence removes a material participant, referent/content handle, characterization, operative relation, orientation, spatial support, or temporal support, even if that coordinate is local or low-salience.

### Excess-coordinate audit

For every proposed primitive, identify the exact material binding it changes and the exact class-native job it performs.

Remove the row if all material bindings remain reconstructable without it, or if the row is only wording already carried by a stronger coordinate or complete binding.

The inventory is complete only when it is both sufficient and nonredundant.

## 4. Functional typing and overlap arbitration

Use the represented job:

- human/social participant -> `PERSON`
- tracked thing/content/referential handle -> `OBJECT`
- source-applied characterization/state/status/evaluation/comparison/identity -> `LABEL`
- operative lexical action/state/relation -> `VERB`
- independent position/path/direction/context/orientation -> `LOCATOR`
- spatial support -> `PLACE`
- temporal support -> `TIME`

Cross-class overlap is allowed only when the exact same source span genuinely performs two independently source-individuated jobs that each change material binding structure. Do not duplicate a span across classes merely because grammar permits two analyses.

## 5. PLACE — spatial support coordinates

Retain a PLACE only when the source differentiates spatial support enough that omitting it would blur or collapse a material binding's occurrence position.

A broad setting and a contained/local position may both qualify when the source materially distinguishes them. A participant position, destination, waiting position, remembered position, later interaction position, tracked object location, prospective position, or present-telling position may qualify when it is structurally needed to separate bindings.

Several bindings may share one PLACE when the source does not materially distinguish their support.

Unnamed PLACE is permitted only when the source establishes a distinct spatial support but does not name its physical details. Use null `source_wording`, exact source cue, and a neutral mechanical tag. Never invent where.

Do not create PLACE from every physical noun, surface, path phrase, possession phrase, or object-location mention.

## 6. TIME — temporal support coordinates

Retain a TIME only when the source differentiates temporal support enough that omitting it would blur or collapse a material binding's episode, phase, period, recurrence frame, remembered/reported frame, intended/prospective frame, or present-reflection frame.

Several bindings may share one TIME. A named duration or temporal phrase does not automatically become a TIME row if it does not define a distinct support frame.

Unnamed TIME is permitted only when the source establishes a distinct temporal support without naming its details. Do not create TIME from grammatical tense, transition words, questions about duration, discourse sequencing, or every predicate occurrence.

## 7. PERSON — represented participant coordinates

Retain the speaker and every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when that actor participates in or anchors a material binding.

Direct, offscreen, relational, possessive/beneficiary, remembered, reported, institutional, prospective, peripheral, and one-use actors may qualify.

Reject nonreferential, rhetorical, or generic person wording that never becomes a represented participant coordinate.

## 8. OBJECT — tracked referent/content coordinates

Retain each concrete or abstract source-presented referential handle that is treated as a distinct thing/content node in at least one material binding.

Qualifying objects may include concrete things, parts, amounts/values, services/results, decisions/next steps, contemplated choices treated as choices, recurring relations treated as content, named sets/categories, remembered/reported content, and internal represented objects.

Do not create OBJECT from every grammatical argument, pronoun, indefinite complement, question content, clause wrapper, discourse point, proposition, explanation, or speaker-organizing phrase.

For abstract content, require both source individuation and material binding contribution. The source must treat the content as a selectable referential handle, not merely state it through a predicate.

## 9. LABEL — source-applied characterization coordinates

Retain the smallest complete source-applied characterization, state, status, identity, evaluation, comparison, candidate label, rejection, correction, or materially distinct posture when it is source-individuated as a characterization that changes a material binding.

One-use, colloquial, idiomatic, uncertain, questioned, negated, corrected, rejected, and figurative labels may qualify.

Do not inventory every adjective, modifier, colorful description, rhetorical flourish, narrative aside, intensifier, or manner phrase. If the wording only decorates or elaborates a binding already fully represented, reject it.

Keep one natural judgment together. Do not split polarity/intensifier/adjective fragments from the judgment they belong to.

## 10. VERB — operative relation kernels

Retain the smallest complete literal predicate kernel for each source-individuated operative relation that changes a material binding.

A qualifying relation may express action, state, cognition, perception, report/communication, intention, question, decision, possession, comparison, evaluation, movement, transition, gesture, existence/location, obligation, or another represented relation.

A relation may be local, ordinary, or low-salience and still qualify. The test is not importance; it is whether the source individuates the relation and the relation contributes nonredundant binding structure.

Preserve particles, reflexives, negation, modality, and bound complements when required for identity.

Keep coordinated or bound predicate wording together when it forms one source-native relation kernel. Split only when the source presents genuinely distinct operative relations that each independently change material binding structure.

Reject pure tense/aspect auxiliaries, copular/support carriage whose content is fully represented elsewhere, discourse/filler predicates, quotation/reporting scaffolding that adds no independent relation, duplicate restatements, and lexical fragments nested inside a stronger natural kernel.

## 11. LOCATOR — independent orientation/context coordinates

Retain the smallest complete exact source span that is source-individuated as an independent orientation/context coordinate and changes a material binding.

Qualifying orientations may include position, path, origin/destination, direction, containment, proximity, accompaniment/carrying, entry/exit, recurrence/context, internal/mental/relational position, or materially spatialized figurative/comparative orientation.

Do not inventory every prepositional phrase, recipient/topic/purpose complement, possession phrase, degree phrase, temporal phrase, generic adverbial, rhetorical location metaphor, or evaluative state.

If the binding remains structurally identical without the proposed locator, reject it.

## 12. Literal lock and source posture

For every explicit primitive, use the smallest complete exact contiguous source-native span that preserves coordinate identity. Every non-null `source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be character-for-character source text.

Preserve negation, modality, uncertainty, question form, attribution, comparison, idiom/dialect, reported/recalled posture, hypothetical/prospective posture, and correction/rejection when semantically required.

Never normalize, improve, translate, diagnose, euphemize, paraphrase, lemmatize into a different surface form, correct the speaker's spelling/grammar, or substitute synonyms.

## 13. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds:

1. replay the material-binding map in source order;
2. verify every binding has the source-individuated coordinates needed for lossless reconstruction at lightweight grain;
3. verify every retained primitive changes at least one material binding;
4. restore local/one-use coordinates omitted only because they seemed unimportant;
5. remove rhetorical color, discourse scaffolding, clause wrappers, modifier fragments, support wording, and redundant local fragments that do not change binding structure;
6. arbitrate OBJECT/LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR by represented function;
7. verify PLACE/TIME only where distinct support is structurally necessary;
8. verify natural complete spans, literal lock, coreference, and class-local distinctness;
9. order by first source establishment subject to apparatus ordering rules; and
10. freeze all seven ledgers.

Do not target a hidden count or infer a hidden archetype.

## 14. Compounds — serialize materially complete bindings

Build compounds only after all primitive ledgers are frozen.

A compound represents one materially complete source binding among frozen coordinates. It is not a receipt for every primitive or every clause.

Emit a compound when the frozen coordinates jointly represent a source-presented interaction, proposition, characterization, question, comparison, choice, reflection, report, or other complete binding whose structure would otherwise be lost.

Rules:

- include only frozen coordinates that actually participate in that binding;
- group locally joint predicates when they jointly form one binding;
- split when participant set, target/content, support frame, source posture, or materially distinct judgment/action changes enough that one compound would blur separate structure;
- a valid primitive may remain unbundled if no multi-coordinate binding requires it;
- do not create a compound to justify a primitive;
- do not emit partial subset/superset variants of the same binding;
- do not serialize sentence/clause structure merely because it exists;
- do not emit graph closure, arbitrary co-occurrence, all-pairs links, scene mega-bundles, singleton equivalents, support chains, or duplicate restatements.

## 15. Identity and ordering

Primitive identity is class-local source identity, not mention count. Resolve true aliases/coreference before deduplication. Do not merge different coordinates merely because they are related or colocated.

Code owns canonical IDs. The worker supplies only neutral identity keys where the apparatus requires them. Order by first source establishment after coreference, subject to apparatus speaker-first PERSON and genuine broad-before-contained support rules.
