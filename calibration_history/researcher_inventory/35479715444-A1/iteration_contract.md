## Mission

Read the complete source and recover a lightweight, source-native researcher inventory in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The target is **frame-anchored lightweight completeness**: preserve every distinct source-native coordinate that performs a research function in the represented source structure, including subtle and one-use coordinates, while excluding incidental lexical material that does not perform a separate research job.

Do not perform APA scoring, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA Data Fabric/database mutation.

## 1. Whole-source relation/frame map

Read the complete source before projecting any requested class. Silently map the source's represented structure, including as applicable:

- actions, interactions, attempts, responses, states, changes, waits, movements, questions, decisions, intentions, alternatives, reports, memories, comparisons, corrections, recurrence, reflection, and prospective relations;
- actors and stable groups participating in those relations;
- concrete and abstract referential/content handles the source uses in those relations;
- characterizations the source itself predicates, poses, accepts, rejects, compares, or revises;
- place/time support that distinguishes represented episodes or phases;
- orientation/context relations that position one coordinate or frame relative to another.

The map is semantic, not grammatical. A sentence, clause, noun phrase, verb token, modifier, preposition, temporal connective, colorful phrase, or concrete detail does not become a primitive merely because it appears in the source.

## 2. Positive admission routes

A primitive must qualify through at least one positive class-native route below. These routes are local to represented source structure; none requires global indispensability or recurrence.

### 2.1 `FRAME_ARGUMENT`

The source trace functions as a distinct actor, referent/content handle, characterization, endpoint, support, or orientation inside a represented relation/frame. Removing it from that local relation/frame would collapse a distinction the source itself represents.

A one-use or low-salience trace may qualify here when its role is distinct.

### 2.2 `INDEPENDENT_REIFICATION`

The source treats the coordinate as a stable or separately reconnectable handle even if it occurs once. Evidence may include explicit naming, identification, possession when material to the represented relation, contrast, selection, questioning, correction, acceptance/rejection, remembered/reportable content, or another source-present act that reifies the coordinate rather than merely mentioning words.

### 2.3 `RELATION_KERNEL`

For `VERB`, the source-present predicate expresses a distinct represented relation, state, attempt, report, cognition, intention, decision, comparison, movement, possession, question, or other relation that organizes meaningful coordinates or a meaningful frame.

Speech/cognition/copular/reporting wording may qualify when that relation itself is represented research structure. It does not qualify merely because a grammatical verb occurs.

### 2.4 `CHARACTERIZATION_KERNEL`

For `LABEL`, the source itself presents a distinct characterization, state, identity, comparison, candidate label, correction, polarity, manner/posture, or figurative quality as part of represented structure.

A modifier does not qualify merely because it describes something grammatically. The characterization must do a distinct source-present research job.

### 2.5 `ORIENTATION_KERNEL`

For `LOCATOR`, the source presents a distinct orienting relation that positions or contextualizes a coordinate/frame by place, route, origin/destination, containment, proximity, accompaniment, recurrence, procedural context, relational context, mental/internal frame, temporal position, or figurative/comparative orientation.

A preposition, adverb, topic phrase, recipient phrase, or comparison does not qualify merely because it can be parsed as orienting.

### 2.6 `SUPPORT_FRAME`

For `PLACE` and `TIME` only, retain explicit or neutral implicit support when it distinguishes a represented episode, relation, conversation, attempt, response, wait, report, memory, transition, recurring phase, reflection, intention, alternative, or prospective frame.

Exact location/time may be unnamed. Neutral support is valid when it reconnects a distinct represented frame without inventing geography or chronology.

## 3. Non-admission evidence

None of the following is sufficient by itself:

- the word or phrase appears in the source;
- it is a noun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, temporal phrase, or spatial phrase;
- it is concrete or vivid;
- it is one-use or repeated;
- it can be grammatically separated;
- it could theoretically be useful to an analyst;
- it belongs to a colorful anecdotal detail;
- it is part of ordinary reporting, quotation framing, discourse organization, or narration;
- it contains deixis, direction, comparison, or temporal language;
- it can be assigned to one of the seven classes.

Low salience is not a reason to reject a qualifying coordinate. Lexical presence is not a reason to admit a nonqualifying one.

## 4. Canonical source-native span

After a candidate qualifies through a positive route, choose the smallest exact source-native span that remains semantically complete for that class-native function.

Keep necessary:

- particles and required prepositions;
- reflexives;
- light-verb support;
- essential complements;
- negation;
- modality;
- uncertainty;
- comparison;
- attribution;
- idiomatic wording;
- posture-bearing wording.

Shrink sentence/clause wrappers when the class-native coordinate is smaller. Expand a head fragment when the head alone would distort or underspecify the source-present coordinate.

Never normalize, clean, improve, translate, diagnose, euphemize, paraphrase, or substitute synonyms.

## 5. Class assignment by research function

Assign by research function rather than grammar or physical form.

- Physical or spatial wording is not automatically `PLACE`.
- A concrete or abstract referent may be `OBJECT` when it is a stable handle in represented structure.
- Descriptive wording may be `LABEL` when it carries a distinct characterization function.
- A relation may be `VERB` even if copular, cognitive, reported, modal, or posture-bearing.
- A `LOCATOR` may orient by context, recurrence, relation, path, mental frame, comparison, or figurative position rather than simple static geography.

The same source trace may support more than one class only when each projection preserves genuinely different class-native information. Do not create duplicate projections that do the same semantic work.

## 6. Semantic identity and deduplication

Primitive identity follows represented semantic identity, not mention count.

Merge true aliases, coreference, restatements, grammatical inflections, and duplicate head/full-span variants that represent one stable coordinate. Preserve distinct occurrences when the source represents materially different local relation/frame roles or different class-native support.

Do not create analyst paraphrases beside literal source wording.

## 7. Literal lock

For every admitted source-derived coordinate preserve exact source language, including dialect, contractions, numbers, uncertainty, negation, modality, comparison, attribution, punctuation, and figurative posture.

`source_wording` should use exact source text whenever the coordinate is explicit. Neutral mechanical tags are reserved for legitimate unnamed PLACE/TIME support where the schema permits null source wording; `source_cue` must anchor that support to exact source text.

Lexical fidelity applies after semantic admission. It is not a word census.

## 8. Class-native rules

### PLACE

Retain explicit settings and local/contained location supports when they distinguish represented relations or frames, plus legitimate unnamed `SUPPORT_FRAME` coordinates when a distinct frame has no explicit location.

A broad setting may coexist with a local support only when each does different research work. Do not create PLACE rows from every surface, physical object, spatial noun, movement, preposition, or scenic detail.

### TIME

Retain explicit periods and semantic episode/span supports that distinguish represented phases. Merge temporal expressions that support one phase; separate genuinely different phases. Use neutral support when the phase is distinct but exact time is unstated.

Do not inventory tense, every `now`/`then`, every duration, chronological connective, or one TIME per clause/action.

### PERSON

Retain the speaker and every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when that actor participates in, possesses within, is reported/remembered within, or otherwise functions in represented source structure.

One-use, offscreen, institutional, possessive, relational, remembered, reported, or prospective actors may qualify. Suppress true aliases/coreferent repeats and rhetorical/nonreferential addressees.

### OBJECT

Retain concrete and abstract referential/content handles that qualify through `FRAME_ARGUMENT` or `INDEPENDENT_REIFICATION`.

Eligible handles can include things acted on, checked, possessed, selected, contrasted, remembered, reported, considered, exchanged, or used as stable anchors, as well as source-reified choices, decisions, relations, conditions, amounts, categories, alternatives, and internal/represented content.

Do not turn every noun phrase, scenic object, discourse phrase, generic pronoun/deixis, nominalized action, clause complement, or possible topic into an OBJECT.

### LABEL

Retain source-present characterization kernels: qualities, states, identities, comparisons, candidate labels, corrections, acceptances/rejections, polarity units, or meaningful manner/posture at the smallest complete exact span.

Idiomatic, local, adverbial, figurative, comparative, and one-use characterizations may qualify. Do not inventory every adjective, adverb, intensifier, colorful aside, or modifier whose only role is grammar or style.

### VERB

Retain represented relation kernels at the smallest complete predicate span. Normally omit the subject and externalize retained participants/content/place/time/labels/locators while keeping required particles, prepositions, complements, negation, modality, reflexives, and posture.

Do not inventory every grammatical verb, auxiliary, report frame, speech tag, discourse formula, meta-comment, or clause-sized proposition. A speech/cognition/report relation is retained only when that relation itself is part of represented research structure rather than merely a narrative carrier.

### LOCATOR

Retain distinct orienting constructions that qualify through `ORIENTATION_KERNEL` at the smallest complete source-native span. Orientation can be spatial, route/path, origin/destination, proximity, accompaniment, recurrence, procedural/relational context, mental/internal context, temporal position, or figurative/comparative orientation.

Do not inventory every preposition, adverb, recipient/addressee, topic phrase, duration, discourse deixis, or comparison.

## 9. Primitive freeze audits

Before compounds, audit the entire source until stable.

### 9.1 Frame coverage

For each represented relation/frame in the whole-source map, ask whether the requested class has a distinct source-present role. If yes, retain the canonical coordinate even when subtle or one-use.

### 9.2 Positive-route audit

For each proposed primitive, identify its positive admission route. If none applies and the justification is only lexical/grammatical/descriptive presence, remove it.

### 9.3 Low-salience recovery

Restore coordinates rejected merely because they are subtle, one-use, local, nested, remembered, reported, hypothetical, prospective, figurative, concrete, or abstract when they do satisfy a positive route.

### 9.4 Anti-tokenization audit

Remove rows admitted only because a word/phrase can be segmented or assigned a class. Remove noun/verb/modifier/spatial/temporal/prepositional census behavior.

### 9.5 Canonical-span audit

Expand fragments that lose required semantic identity. Shrink proposition/discourse wrappers that bundle material belonging to other coordinates.

### 9.6 Wrong-class audit

Reassign wrong-class projections rather than duplicating them. Preserve cross-class overlap only when the two projections do genuinely different research work.

### 9.7 PLACE/TIME support audit

Restore missing support for distinct represented frames; merge supports doing the same job. Never invent geography or chronology.

### 9.8 Dedup and literal audit

Collapse semantic duplicates and verify exact source wording/posture. Remove synonyms, normalization, analyst inference, and duplicate variants.

Freeze primitives only after these audits are stable.

## 10. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean only. It is true when source-present qualitative/descriptive material is available around that coordinate or compound under the schema. It is not confidence, importance, admission authority, or a demand to create a LABEL.

## 11. Selective compound construction

Only after all seven primitive classes are frozen, construct lightweight multi-coordinate compounds.

Create a compound when two or more frozen coordinates jointly express one distinct source-local relation/event/frame useful to reconnect as a bundle. Use the smallest set of refs needed to identify that binding and preserve source-local semantic order/posture.

A primitive may remain unbundled.

Do not create graph closure, pairwise closure, one compound per primitive, one compound per clause, singleton-equivalent bundles, subset/superset permutations, redundant restatements, or scene mega-bundles. Every compound must use frozen canonical refs only.

Compounds never justify missing or extra primitives.

## 12. Completion standard

The target is **frame-anchored archetypal lightweight resolution**:

- complete-source reading precedes projection;
- every admitted primitive has a positive source-present research role inside represented structure;
- low-salience and one-use coordinates remain eligible without becoming a lexical census;
- no global-indispensability test suppresses legitimate local coordinates;
- no lexical/POS census admits incidental detail;
- each row uses the smallest complete literal source-native span;
- class assignment reflects research function;
- PLACE/TIME preserve distinct support frames without token proliferation;
- duplicates, proposition wrappers, discourse scaffolds, grammar-only fragments, and wrong-class projections are removed;
- compounds reconstruct only a selective canonical set from frozen primitives.

Do not target hidden counts or infer hidden gold.
