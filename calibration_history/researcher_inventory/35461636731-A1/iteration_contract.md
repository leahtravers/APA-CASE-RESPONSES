## Prospective correction basis

V95 added a universal semantic-sufficiency/removal gate to prevent lexical census behavior. Paired Case 2 / Case 6 calibration under V95 demonstrates that the gate is too coarse for the Researcher Inventory. It suppresses legitimate class-native coordinates merely because a coarse proposition can still be paraphrased without them, while its `complete semantic predicate relation` language encourages multiple source relations to be fused into proposition-sized VERB rows.

V96 therefore prospectively supersedes V95's universal removal/semantic-necessity gate and restores V94's **CLASS-NATIVE ENTITLEMENT AND GRAIN** as the controlling admission model, with one added resolution rule:

> **CLASS-NATIVE COORDINATE SUFFICIENCY. A primitive is entitled when the source materially establishes the native job of that class at the source's own local resolution. It does not have to be independently necessary to a coarse whole-proposition paraphrase. Local, one-use, low-salience, endpoint-dependent, unnamed-support, and wording-overlapping coordinates may be legitimate when they preserve different class-native jobs. Selection is still not a raw token/POS census.**

This correction is prospective. V95, V94, and every earlier contract/run remain immutable historical evidence for the executions they governed. Nothing in V96 exposes approved archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored answers, calibration answers, or sealed holdout material to the worker.

## Mission

Read the complete source and recover source-native research coordinates in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Preserve literal source language and posture. Freeze primitives before compounds. Then reconstruct canonical source-local proposition/event/state/question/comparison bindings from the frozen coordinates.

Do not perform APA scoring, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA Data Fabric mutation.

## 1. Construction order

### Pass A — whole-source map

Read the entire represented source before extracting. Silently map represented scenes/positions, temporal episodes/frames, actors, concrete/abstract referents, source characterizations, relation edges, orientation edges, and materially distinct propositions/events/questions/comparisons. This prevents omissions; it is not a token or POS census.

### Pass B — class-native entitlement

Ask the question appropriate to each class. Do not use one universal removal test.

- `PLACE`: does this coordinate organize where a represented occurrence, relation, actor, object, conversation, wait, memory, reflection, report, or action is situated?
- `TIME`: does this coordinate organize when or in what source episode/phase/context represented material occurs?
- `PERSON`: is this a distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference?
- `OBJECT`: is this a represented concrete/abstract referent or source-treated content handle?
- `LABEL`: is this wording functioning as a represented characterization, state, manner, comparison, identity term, candidate label, acceptance/rejection, or correction?
- `VERB`: is this a materially represented predicate/relation edge?
- `LOCATOR`: is this a materially represented orientation/position/path/containment/context edge?

A coordinate may qualify even when it is local, one-use, low-salience, unnamed, endpoint-dependent, or semantically overlapping with another class. The overlap must preserve a different class-native job, not merely a different grammatical label.

### Pass C — source-native grain

Use the smallest complete source-native unit appropriate to the class.

- Entity/reference/characterization rows use the shortest wording that preserves the represented identity/posture.
- VERB rows use the smallest complete **atomic predicate edge**, not an entire proposition or clause bundle.
- Split matrix and embedded relations when both materially contribute distinct edges.
- Split coordinated predicates when they are distinct relations.
- Leave separable PERSON/OBJECT/LABEL/PLACE/TIME arguments outside VERB.
- Leave separable position/path/containment/orientation outside VERB as LOCATOR, while allowing overlapping wording when both jobs genuinely exist.
- Keep particles, negation, modality, or complements only when removing them changes the identity/posture of that edge.

### Pass D — literal lock and coreference

Preserve source language, dialect, uncertainty, negation, modality, comparison, numbers, contractions, and punctuation exactly where source text is required. Resolve true aliases/coreference only. Do not clean or normalize source language.

### Pass E — primitive freeze

Freeze the full primitive inventory before compounds. Compounds may not invent missing primitives, erase admitted primitives, or justify unsupported rows retroactively.

### Pass F — proposition reconstruction

Re-read the source and create the smallest canonical set of source-local proposition/event/state/report/intention/question/comparison/characterization/orientation bindings needed to reconnect frozen primitives. Integrate coordinates that belong to the same represented binding. Do not create graph closure, pairwise combinations, subset/superset permutations, redundant side compounds, or proposition-sized primitive substitutes.

## 2. PLACE — support geography at occurrence resolution

Recover the support geography of represented occurrences, not just literal place nouns and not every physical noun.

Retain named broad/contained settings plus materially distinct occurrence positions for waits, encounters, conversations, reports, memories, reflections, performances, actions, staged people, and staged objects when location is part of how the source organizes the occurrence. Necessary unnamed support places may use `source_wording: null` with an exact source cue and neutral mechanical tag.

Do not suppress a local support place merely because a broader setting is already present. Do not turn every surface, body part, container, path noun, or figurative expression into PLACE.

## 3. TIME — support chronology at episode/phase resolution

Recover source-organizing chronology/episode support, including materially distinct local phases, waits/transitions, later conversations, remembered periods, recurring spans, present telling/reflection, prospective periods, contextual spans, and necessary unnamed frames.

A TIME may be anchored by an event cue instead of a literal time token. Several propositions may share one TIME, but a broad episode does not automatically erase materially distinct local phases. Do not create TIME for every tense, verb, adverb, or duration phrase.

## 4. PERSON — represented actors

Retain `B` plus every distinct materially represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference. Minor, one-use, offscreen, remembered, reported, relational, possessive, prospective, and institutional actors may qualify. Suppress true aliases/coreferent repeats and nonreferential/rhetorical addressees.

## 5. OBJECT — represented referents/content handles

Retain materially represented concrete or abstract referents/content handles, including one-use physical details, parts, products, documents, surfaces, contents, instruments, amounts, values, results, sets, choices, decisions, plans, options, relations/situations/event-content, and figurative/reified content when the source treats it as a thing or target of reference.

Do not require recurrence, high salience, or independent proposition-level necessity. Do not nominalize every clause/complement/question/action, and do not create a shadow OBJECT when another class fully carries the only source job and the source does not separately treat the trace as referential content.

## 6. LABEL — source-indexed characterizations

Retain source wording that functions as a represented characterization/candidate characterization, including identity/status, materially predicated state or manner, characterization comparisons, self/other labels, candidate feeling/meaning labels, and acceptance/rejection/correction/polarity when the source represents that characterization job.

A brief or local characterization may qualify. Do not require that it be central to the narrative. Preserve question form, negation, intensity, comparison, and dialect. Do not inventory purely decorative/background modifiers or whole clauses whose only job is already represented by other coordinates.

## 7. VERB — atomic relation-edge completeness

VERB inventories materially represented predicate/relation edges, not grammatical verb tokens and not proposition-sized semantic bundles.

Retain actions, states/placements/existence relations, perception/cognition/stance, reports/speech, intentions/needs/choices, social relations, local one-use actions, embodied/performance acts, and materially distinct nested relations.

Prefer the smallest complete **atomic source relation edge**. A relation may make full sense only with endpoints; that is normal. Split matrix/embedded and coordinated relations when each contributes represented structure. Leave separable arguments and orientation to their classes. Suppress pure auxiliaries, tense/aspect support, do-support, duplicate restatements, and whole-clause/proposition bundles masquerading as one VERB.

## 8. LOCATOR — atomic orientation-edge completeness

LOCATOR inventories materially represented orientation edges, including position, containment/support, origin/destination, path, approach/away/entry/exit, directional particles, recurring-context orientation, internal/mental orientation, temporal-position orientation, and figurative/comparative orientation when it actually positions represented material.

A LOCATOR may be local and endpoint-dependent. A movement/state construction may legitimately supply both a VERB edge and a LOCATOR edge, including overlapping wording, when the relation and orientation jobs are genuinely distinct. Suppress purely grammatical recipient/topic markers and transitions that do not orient represented material.

## 9. Cross-class overlap

Allow overlap only when the same literal trace preserves genuinely different class-native information. Do not suppress legitimate structural overlap merely to make classes textually disjoint. Do not multiply rows from grammatical ambiguity alone.

## 10. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds, verify:

1. PLACE/TIME cover source-support geography/chronology at materially distinct local resolution without raw mention census;
2. PERSON includes all distinct actors after true coreference;
3. OBJECT preserves represented one-use and recurring referents/content handles without arbitrary clause nominalization;
4. LABEL preserves source-indexed characterizations without decorative modifier census;
5. VERB contains atomic relation edges and no proposition-sized bundles;
6. LOCATOR contains atomic orientation edges and permits legitimate relation/orientation overlap;
7. literal wording/posture is exact;
8. aliases, duplicates, grammatical-support fragments, and analyst-created abstractions are suppressed.

## 11. Compound reconstruction

Create canonical source-local compounds from frozen coordinates. Use only coordinates that belong to that represented relation; integrate applicable PLACE/TIME/LOCATOR/LABEL support into the main binding rather than spawning redundant side compounds. Preserve source-local order and question/uncertainty posture. Set qualitative availability only under the schema's actual compound rule, not automatically because a referenced primitive could carry qualities.

Do not create graph closure, pairwise combinations, lexical-fragment compounds, subset/superset permutations, mega-bundles, or automatic propagation of all support coordinates.

## 12. Isolation and completion gates

Never target hidden counts or infer hidden gold. Never expose approved archetypes, evaluator findings, prior scored answers, calibration answers, or sealed holdout content/output to the worker. Case 5 remains inaccessible until repeated Case 2 + Case 6 passes under this same finalized contract. A calibrated-lineage holdout attempt, if eventually authorized by the workflow gate, is one-shot; clean-room certification requires a brand-new saved agent with only finalized durable instructions and a new session.

All outputs remain candidate research only. No promotion, Oval Office admission, APA-ID minting, or APA Data Fabric writing is authorized.
