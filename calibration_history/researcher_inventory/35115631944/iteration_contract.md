# APA Researcher Inventory Agent Contract V55

Status: ACTIVE SUCCESSOR FOR CALIBRATION
Contract version: `RI-CONTRACT-V55`
Predecessor: `RI-CONTRACT-V54`
Effective date: 2026-09-16
Authority: Leah's standing Researcher Inventory calibration instruction, permitting durable-contract revision only when preserved failures demonstrate a generalizable requirement.

## Mission

Produce only a literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate from the supplied source. Inventory source-presented research coordinates and lightweight compounds. Do not interpret psychological meaning, score APA, infer protected threads, draw conclusions, promote records, mint APA IDs, or admit anything to sovereign or production fabric.

The worker never receives approved archetypes, gold outputs, expected answers, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, sealed holdout source during calibration, or holdout outputs.

## 1. Coordinate identity is stricter than semantic separability

Read the entire source before selecting units. A unit belongs when the source establishes a stable, reusable research coordinate of the requested class at a source-native grain.

A span does **not** become a primitive merely because it can be grammatically isolated, semantically paraphrased, nested inside another relation, assigned a participant, given a direction, or recovered as implicit event structure. Semantic content may contribute to a compound without becoming its own primitive unit.

Use this admission test:

1. After coreference and local context are restored, does the source present this as an addressable coordinate of THIS class rather than merely material inside another coordinate?
2. Could a researcher return to it independently without first inventing a new wrapper, event, place, time, object, value, relation, or orientation that the source itself did not establish?
3. Does keeping it preserve a source distinction that would otherwise be lost, rather than merely preserve syntactic detail?

Retain when all applicable answers support independent coordinate identity. Reject when the candidate is only a function fragment, syntactic subpredicate, modifier fragment, relation argument, deictic wrapper, action-local implicit frame, or duplicated decomposition of a larger source-native coordinate.

## 2. Balanced coverage: neither suppression nor census

Coverage is complete source traversal, not exhaustive decomposition into every possible semantic part.

Perform two ledgers before final selection:

- **SOURCE-DISTINCTION LEDGER:** note every source-established actor, referent, characterization, lexical relation, orientation, physical scene/position, and temporal episode/frame that could carry independent research identity.
- **DEPENDENCY LEDGER:** mark candidates that exist only because another relation, modifier, argument, grammatical complement, deictic expression, or action implies them.

A dependency-ledger item is admitted only when the source independently establishes it as a reusable class-native coordinate. Grammatical dependence alone does not forbid admission, but neither does semantic separability justify it.

Preserve V54's valid correction: nested, unnamed, remembered, reported, questioned, negated, figurative, or embedded material may qualify. Restore V53's valid safeguard: do not turn syntax into a census.

## 3. PLACE

Retain source-established physical scenes or occurrence positions that can independently host represented material. Named and supported unnamed scenes may qualify. Broad and contained scenes may coexist only when each is independently represented as a scene/position rather than one being merely a surface, object part, endpoint, container, or locator target.

A represented interaction or episode may support an unnamed PLACE when the source establishes a coherent co-presence/context that is independently reusable even if no proper location name is supplied. Do not mint an unnamed PLACE for every action, clause, participant relation, or movement.

Do not promote floors, counters, walls, lines, sides, distances, object surfaces, movement endpoints, or similar subpositions to PLACE merely because something is located there. Such material may instead remain inside an OBJECT/LOCATOR/relation when that is its source function.

For supported unnamed PLACE, `source_wording` may be null and `source_cue` must be exact source text.

## 4. TIME

Retain distinct represented episodes, periods, phases, waits, remembered interactions, recurring frames, current reflection/telling frames, transitions, and prospective frames when the source establishes them as reusable temporal coordinates.

Do not create TIME for every predicate, adverb, duration phrase, aspect marker, purpose, question, simultaneity phrase, or action-local moment. Several relations inside one episode normally share its TIME unless the source marks a distinct before/after, remembered/current, recurring/singular, waiting/active, prospective/current, or otherwise independently reusable temporal frame.

A supported unnamed temporal frame may qualify when the source establishes the episode or phase even without a literal time noun. For supported unnamed TIME, `source_wording` may be null and `source_cue` must be exact source text.

## 5. PERSON

Retain the speaker and each distinct represented human/social actor or stable group. Resolve aliases, kinship terms, titles, pronouns, and coreference before deduplication. Speaker canonical key is `B`.

Generic, impersonal, rhetorical, idiomatic, or self-directed grammatical `you` is not PERSON merely because second-person wording appears. Retain an addressee only when the source establishes a stable represented person/addressee role.

## 6. OBJECT

Retain concrete or abstract referents that the source treats as stable things/content a researcher could revisit: physical things, explicit choices/decisions, amounts, services/results, relations treated as things, sets/categories, internal or figurative objects, comparison referents, and source-distinguished wholes/parts.

Do not create OBJECT for every complement, clause proposition, event, action nominalization, pronoun, deictic wrapper, relation argument, or descriptive fragment. Resolve `this`, `that`, `it`, and similar anaphoric/deictic wording to an established antecedent when the wording does not create a new referent.

A complement or embedded content may still be OBJECT when the source itself treats it as a stable independently revisitable referent. Prefer the stable source referent over duplicate proposition wrappers.

When noun-shaped wording primarily functions as a characterization/state/value, use LABEL instead.

## 7. LABEL

Retain source-presented characterizations, states, values, classifications, evaluations, comparisons, identity/self-labels, relational statuses, corrections, polarity responses, and question-labels when the value itself is independently selectable.

Do not inventory every adjective, adverb, degree marker, modifier, negation fragment, question scaffold, or predicate complement as a LABEL. Preserve the complete source value when a smaller modifier fragment would lose the characterization's identity, posture, or scope.

A spatial/deictic/value expression may function as LABEL when the source uses it as an independently selectable state/value rather than merely as a LOCATOR relation. Classify by source function, not part of speech.

## 8. VERB

Retain each materially distinct lexical relation at the shortest **complete source-native relation identity**.

Do not recursively decompose one relation into every verb-shaped token in control, raising, auxiliary, infinitival, participial, serial, complement, modal, negated, or coordinated syntax. Keep wording together when the source presents one relation increment.

Split an embedded/reported/coordinated relation only when the source establishes it as a second independently revisitable lexical relation—not merely because it has its own grammatical predicate, participant, or complement. A syntactic subpredicate may contribute to the parent relation or compound without becoming a separate VERB primitive.

Reporting, perception, cognition, stance, possession, questioning, saying/telling, waiting, finding, and figurative relations may qualify when they are independently relation-bearing. Pure state/value material belongs in LABEL when that is its primary source function.

## 9. LOCATOR

Retain independently reusable orientation/context relations for position, path, direction, origin/destination, entry/exit, proximity, containment, recurrence context, movement, accompaniment, internal orientation, or figurative/relational orientation.

A LOCATOR is not limited to a prepositional phrase; source wording such as an action-oriented destination, standing/placement expression, recurrence context, or temporal-position expression may function as LOCATOR when it supplies the orientation relation itself.

Do not inventory every preposition, beneficiary phrase, object-position phrase, relation argument, content complement, or directional token. Retain the orientation only when a researcher could independently use it to place/orient another retained coordinate or episode.

A LOCATOR may overlap wording with PLACE/TIME/LABEL only when the source genuinely supplies distinct class-native functions, not merely to maximize coverage.

## 10. Cross-class resolution

Classify by source function:
- represented social actor -> PERSON;
- stable thing/content/referent -> OBJECT;
- characterization/state/value -> LABEL;
- lexical relation -> VERB;
- reusable orientation/context relation -> LOCATOR;
- independent physical scene/occurrence position -> PLACE;
- independent episode/period/frame -> TIME.

Cross-class overlap is allowed only for genuinely distinct source functions. Surface grammar never decides type by itself.

## 11. Final selection passes

For each class:

1. **SOURCE COVERAGE:** traverse the complete source and collect source-established candidates.
2. **COREFERENCE/CONTEXT:** restore antecedents, actor identity, episode context, and literal posture before deciding grain.
3. **INDEPENDENCE:** apply the coordinate-identity test; remove candidates that exist only as syntactic/semantic decomposition of another coordinate.
4. **TYPE:** resolve source function without type inflation.
5. **DEDUP/EXCESS:** remove aliases, restatements, proposition wrappers, modifier fragments, action-local implicit frames, duplicated parent/child decompositions, and relation-internal fragments.
6. **LOSS CHECK:** confirm no source-distinguished coordinate disappeared merely because it was nested, unnamed, remembered, reported, or grammatically dependent.

Do not use proposition-centrality as the admission test. Do not use grammatical separability as the admission test. Do not use grammatical dependence as either an automatic inclusion or exclusion test.

## 12. Literal and posture lock

Every non-null `source_wording`, every `source_cue`, and every non-null `order_cue` must be a character-for-character contiguous source substring. Preserve source spelling, punctuation, dialect, questions, negation, uncertainty, correction, comparison, attribution, intention, hypothetical, recurrence, report, and prospective posture.

Explicit short tags use only source words. Supported unnamed PLACE/TIME may use neutral navigation tags grounded by exact source cues. Never substitute synonyms or normalize dialect.

## 13. Ordering and qualities

Order by first source establishment after coreference, subject to apparatus speaker-first PERSON and broad-before-contained PLACE/LOCATOR only when genuinely established together.

`qualities_available` is boolean only. It is true only when qualities/descriptions are available for that coordinate or compound. Question, negation, uncertainty, intensity, or presence of a LABEL does not automatically make it true.

## 14. Compound reconstruction is selective binding, not lossless parsing

Freeze the admitted primitive units before compounds. Scan source relations in order and emit the smallest complete compound for each source-presented relationship that materially binds two or more admitted coordinates.

A compound records proven binding among retained research coordinates. It does **not** require every lexical token or every syntactic subrelation in the sentence to have its own primitive unit. Omitted syntax may remain literal source material without primitive identity.

Do not emit:
- every clause;
- every pair;
- nested subset alternatives for one relation;
- duplicate decompositions;
- combinatorial closure;
- broad sentence bags merely to capture leftover words.

Matrix/reporting and embedded/content compounds may both survive only when they represent genuinely distinct source relations among admitted coordinates. If one retained VERB already preserves one complete relation identity, do not manufacture extra compounds from its internal syntax.

Compound `referenced_unit_refs` must reference only frozen admitted units, and `compound_expression` must reflect those bindings in source order.

## 15. Calibration isolation

Return only the JSON required by the request schema. Never infer or reconstruct archetypes, expected counts, evaluator preferences, prior scored outputs, case-specific gold corrections, sealed holdout source during calibration, or holdout output.

## 16. Prohibited work

Never perform APA scoring, psychological interpretation, protected-thread analysis, conclusions, promotion, APA-ID creation, Oval Office research writing, sovereign admission, or database/fabric admission.
