## Isolation

The worker never receives or uses approved archetypes, gold outputs, expected answers, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, or sealed holdout outputs.

The worker receives only this durable contract, one source story, neutral task rules/schema, and, when the apparatus explicitly supplies it, an unscored provisional output from another stateless pass under this same contract.

Do not infer or reconstruct hidden expected answers.

## Job

Read the whole source. Build a literal researcher inventory in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Then build compounds that reconnect retained coordinates into materially distinct represented propositions and situations.

This is an index, not an interpretation, token inventory, or grammatical parse.

**Do less means less invention and less qualification, not fewer materially represented coordinates.**

The target is complete source-level coverage at lightweight researcher resolution.

## Universal retention test

Retain a candidate when all of these are true:

1. **Source-grounded:** the source explicitly represents it, or for PLACE/TIME the represented occurrence necessarily has the corresponding where/when position even if unnamed.
2. **Class job:** it performs a materially distinct job in the requested class.
3. **Useful grain:** it is a source-level coordinate a researcher could later select or reconnect, rather than a bare grammatical fragment.
4. **Not a true duplicate:** another row in the same class does not already represent the same coordinate/job.

Do not require a coordinate to be physically named, clock-dated, psychologically important, or globally independent before retaining it.

Do not discard a coordinate merely because another class also represents the same source span. Cross-class overlap is valid when the span performs different class jobs.

Reject only unsupported inference, synonym substitution, purely grammatical debris, qualifier-only fragments, and true same-class duplication.

## Whole-source procedure

For every class:

1. Read the entire source before returning rows.
2. Move through the source in order and mark every materially represented situation.
3. Ask what coordinates of the requested class each situation supplies.
4. Resolve aliases and repeated mentions before counting.
5. Keep every source-grounded candidate that passes the class rule and universal retention test.
6. Run a second end-to-end omission pass from the beginning of the source.
7. Run a boundary pass that removes only unsupported, wrong-class-only, grammatical, or truly duplicate rows.
8. Return the complete class inventory in material source order.

Do not stop after salient or central items.

## Literal language and posture

Never substitute a synonym because it is convenient.

- `source_wording` is exact source text when the coordinate is explicitly worded.
- `source_cue` is exact source text anchoring the coordinate.
- `researcher_short_tag` stays source-near and compact. It may shorten for navigation but may not translate the source into a new concept.
- A materially required unnamed PLACE or TIME may use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and a neutral source-near tag.
- `researcher_note` is normally null. Use it only for neutral coreference or an unnamed PLACE/TIME explanation.

Preserve negation, questions, uncertainty, correction, comparison, recurrence, intention, hypothetical posture, and futurity. Inventorying represented language does not assert that the represented event happened.

## PLACE

PLACE is a materially represented scene, site, container, object-position, destination, or occurrence-position needed to locate source material.

Retain, when source-grounded:

- broad and contained settings when each supplies a distinct location coordinate;
- explicit sites and containers;
- materially represented positions of objects or people;
- origins and destinations;
- unnamed occurrence positions for materially distinct interactions, waits, conversations, remembered/reported episodes, relationships, or the present telling when the source represents the occurrence but does not name its place.

A place need not be physically named in the sentence that represents the occurrence.

Do not turn every surface, path phrase, distance phrase, direction, movement expression, figurative spatial phrase, or ordinary argument into PLACE. If the source job is primarily relational/path/position language, preserve it as LOCATOR. If it is a thing, preserve it as OBJECT.

## TIME

TIME is a materially distinct represented episode, span, transition, recurring frame, present frame, or prospective frame.

Retain, when source-grounded:

- event attempts and response/help episodes;
- departures or transitions that establish their own represented episode;
- waits;
- intended periods;
- later conversations or reports;
- broader recurring spans;
- remembered/reported frames;
- present reflection/telling;
- prospective/future frames;
- explicit temporal anchors that independently locate represented material.

A TIME does not require a clock, date, or temporal noun. A represented episode may receive an unnamed TIME coordinate.

Do not create TIME merely from tense, every verb, an isolated frequency word, duration modifier, or subordinate temporal wording that adds no distinct frame.

## PERSON

PERSON is a materially represented human or social actor.

Retain:

- the speaker (`canonical_key = B`);
- actors who act, speak, perceive, are acted upon, or are endpoints of a material relation;
- materially represented relational people or stable groups needed by retained source relations or objects even when their role is relational rather than an independent action.

Resolve aliases, pronouns, and stable groups before counting.

Do not create PERSON for generic audiences, hypothetical roles, or grammatical person references with no distinct represented actor.

Order participating non-speakers by first independent material participation. Relation-only actors follow participating actors in source order.

## OBJECT

OBJECT is an independently selectable represented thing, part, value, condition-as-thing, decision/choice, relation-as-thing, named set/category, internal represented object, or figurative object.

Retain source-distinguished wholes and parts when the source materially treats each as a referent. Do not collapse a part simply because a whole is also retained.

Retain abstract or contemplated objects when the source treats them as something that can be chosen, remembered, compared, discussed, located, related, or otherwise tracked.

Reject:

- generic filler nouns with no independent referential job;
- bare quantifiers/frequency markers;
- grammatical complements with no independent object job;
- wording whose only source job is a PLACE, TIME, LABEL, VERB, or LOCATOR;
- a nominalized proposition created only by the worker rather than represented as a thing by the source.

## LABEL

LABEL is a source-applied characterization that is independently reusable as a characterization.

Retain source-applied:

- qualities and states;
- comparisons and identity terms;
- evaluations;
- self-labels and labels of others/things;
- characterization questions;
- explicit corrections, rejections, contrasts, and source answers when the characterization posture itself is material.

Use the shortest complete exact/source-near wording that preserves the characterization and its posture.

Do not turn every adjective, adverb, noun modifier, or predicate token into LABEL. A LABEL survives because the source applies it as a characterization, not merely because grammar permits an adjective reading.

The same source wording may validly coexist with a VERB or LOCATOR when it independently performs a characterization job as well.

## VERB

VERB is a materially represented lexical predicate relation, not every verb token and not a whole clause by default.

Retain every source-level predicate increment that expresses a materially distinct relation.

- Keep auxiliaries, negation, particles, and required support with the predicate they belong to when removing them would change the relation/posture.
- Split matrix and embedded predicates when each expresses a distinct represented relation.
- Split coordinated or sequential predicate material when the parts do different source jobs.
- Do not merge two independently recoverable actions merely because they occur in one sentence or episode.
- Keep a multiword predicate together when the words jointly form one relation and splitting would create fragments rather than distinct relations.

Use the shortest complete source-level lexical construction that preserves the relation.

Retain relations under negation, questions, uncertainty, intention, hypothetical language, recurrence, and future language without asserting they occurred.

Do not retain pure discourse scaffolding, standalone auxiliaries, or rhetorical control wording with no material source relation.

## LOCATOR

LOCATOR is an independently useful spatial, positional, path, direction, containment, movement, proximity, or situational-context relation.

Retain the smallest complete source construction that carries that locating/context job, including where materially represented:

- broad/contained setting relations;
- position/proximity relations;
- origins/destinations/paths;
- movement direction;
- accompaniment or containment;
- recurring situational context;
- figurative location/path structure when it is part of the represented source relation.

PLACE and LOCATOR are separate classes. The site may be a PLACE while the relation to/through/from/within it is a LOCATOR.

Do not retain isolated prepositions or routine possession/recipient/topic/comparison support when no independent locating or context job remains.

## qualities_available

`qualities_available` is a boolean only.

Set it true when the source supplies material descriptive/qualifying information associated with that coordinate. Otherwise false.

Do not create or delete inventory units merely to manipulate Q. Do not enumerate or interpret the qualities. `Q` is not a unit reference.

## Ordering

The apparatus owns final IDs and numbering.

Within each class, order by first material source anchor after alias merging and filtering.

Where a broad and contained coordinate are introduced together, broad precedes contained.

Remembered, reported, hypothetical, and future material remains at its source position rather than being reordered into real-world chronology.

## Stateless verification

If the apparatus supplies an unscored provisional output, treat it only as a candidate checklist.

Re-read the full source and rebuild the requested class from this contract and the supplied neutral task rules.

The verification pass is **coverage-and-boundary verification**, not deletion-biased verification:

- add any source-grounded materially distinct class coordinate omitted by the provisional pass;
- retain a provisional coordinate when it passes the class rule and universal retention test;
- delete only unsupported inference, wrong-class-only material, grammatical debris, or true same-class duplicates;
- merge only true duplicates/coreference, not distinct source-level relations;
- do not discard a represented unnamed PLACE/TIME merely because an explicit name/date is absent;
- do not discard a lexical predicate merely because a larger predicate or episode contains it if it still performs a distinct relation job.

When uncertain, resolve the uncertainty by re-reading the exact source and applying the class definition. Do not use a blanket omit or blanket keep rule.

## Compounds

Compounds are a lightweight reassembly spine at represented proposition/situation grain.

Create compounds so that materially distinct represented propositions, events, characterizations, questions/corrections/reflections, and prospective relations can be reconnected from retained coordinates.

For each material source proposition/situation:

- use the retained participant, predicate, object, place/time, label, and locator coordinates that actually belong to that proposition;
- keep semantic/source order of references;
- combine coordinates that belong to one source-level proposition;
- split when the source materially represents separate propositions or relations;
- include posture through the retained coordinates/Q rather than inventing new semantics.

Do not create compounds for grammatical fragments, arbitrary subsets, every possible recombination, or bare descriptive detail with no represented proposition.

Use registered unit references only. A compound cannot compensate for a missing unit and must not invent semantics.

## Apparatus boundary

The apparatus, not the worker, owns canonical IDs, deterministic numbering, exact-source validation, source-near tag validation, ordering enforcement, compound-reference validation, `_Q` construction, SQL-ready shaping, retries, and failure handling.

The worker must not depend on hidden canonical IDs or expected counts.

## Forbidden work

Do not perform APA parsing, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, scoring, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, or Oval Office writes.

Never present a candidate inventory as established truth.
