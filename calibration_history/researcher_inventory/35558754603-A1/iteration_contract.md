## Mission

Produce a literal, lightweight Researcher Inventory of the complete supplied source in exactly seven primitive classes:

1. `PLACE`

4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`

The target is neither sparse semantic compression nor lexical census. It is a source-faithful researcher coordinate system at class-native resolution.

## 1. Whole-source positive coverage before suppression

Read the complete source before emitting any class. Resolve obvious coreference and broad source order.

For every requested class, scan the complete source and positively test every plausible source-supported candidate. Low salience, one-use occurrence, background placement, nesting, remembered/reported status, prospectivity, figurative use, uncertainty, negation, or lack of an explicit name are never sufficient reasons to suppress a candidate that performs a real class-native job.

A candidate may qualify by either route:

### A. Standalone source identity

The source treats it as an identifiable actor, thing/content, state/value, relation, orientation, setting, episode/period, choice, relationship, recurring situation, or other stable coordinate.

### B. Bound-coordinate identity

The candidate performs a genuine class-native role inside a material source-established relation/state/event and its exact coordinate identity would be lost if only the surrounding primitives or compound gist remained.

A bound coordinate does **not** have to merit a standalone narrative-summary card. Its role in the represented binding can be enough.

## 2. Suppression is bounded and enumerated

After positive coverage, suppress only when the candidate is one of the following:

- true alias/coreference duplicate;
- pure auxiliary, determiner, conjunction, discourse glue, or function grammar with no class-native content job;
- bare preposition/path particle with no orientation job;
- duplicate same-grain tokenization/restatement of one coordinate;
- analyst-created abstraction, paraphrase, inference, diagnosis, or normalized wording;
- arbitrary subpart with no source-supported coordinate identity;
- whole-clause/question/proposition shell not treated by the source as identifiable content, choice, decision, relationship, situation, or object of thought;
- PLACE/TIME microframe created only from a clause/predicate boundary rather than a distinct occurrence position or episode/period;
- cross-class duplicate that does not perform a genuinely different represented job;
- compound variant generated only by pairwise closure, nesting, subset/superset expansion, or alternate tokenization.

Do not invent additional austerity tests. In particular, do not suppress a real coordinate merely because another primitive or compound preserves the general gist.

## 3. Grain and typing

Use the smallest **complete source-native grain** that preserves the class job. Keep required particles, reflexives, negation, modality, comparison, directional/relational complements, and idiomatic material when splitting changes coordinate identity.

Type by represented job, not part of speech:

- `PLACE` = represented setting or occurrence position;
- `TIME` = represented episode, phase, period, recurrence span, or temporal frame;
- `PERSON` = represented human/social actor or stable group;
- `OBJECT` = represented concrete or abstract referent/content;
- `LABEL` = target-bearing state, value, quality, classification, evaluation, comparison, correction, or status;
- `VERB` = content-bearing operative relation/event/state kernel;
- `LOCATOR` = orientation/path/context relation.

One source span normally receives one primitive identity per genuine job. Cross-class overlap is allowed only when the same source material truly performs different reconnectable jobs.

## 4. PLACE

Run a positive whole-source PLACE pass.

Retain distinct source-supported settings and occurrence positions, including broad and contained settings when both matter; interaction/wait/conversation positions; destinations/departures/arrivals; another participant's represented scene; spatially staged positions of retained things; remembered/reported/prospective/comparison scenes; and present-telling location when represented.

A PLACE may be admitted because it materially anchors a represented relation even when it is not independently interesting as a standalone setting.

Supported unnamed PLACE is allowed when a materially distinct occurrence position/scene exists but the source supplies no adequate lexical name. Use null `source_wording`, exact source cue, and neutral mechanical short tag. Do not invent substantive spatial facts.

Do not create PLACE merely from movement/path wording, a support surface, or a clause boundary when no distinct represented position exists.

## 5. TIME

Run a positive whole-source TIME pass.

Retain distinct source-supported attempts, action/interaction episodes, waits, transitions, remembered/reported scenes, recurring spans, stable periods, prospective/future frames, comparison/imagined frames, and present reflection/telling when they organize represented material.

A TIME may be admitted because it materially anchors a represented relation even when it is not independently interesting as a standalone period.

Supported unnamed TIME is allowed when a materially distinct episode/phase exists without explicit temporal naming. Group serial local actions inside one episode when no material temporal distinction exists; do not create a TIME from every predicate, tense cue, clause, frequency word, or duration question.

## 6. PERSON

Retain `B` plus every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when the actor participates in, possesses/benefits from, is targeted by, is remembered/reported in, or otherwise provides a real social coordinate for represented material.

Minor, possessive/relational, offscreen, remembered, reported, prospective, and institutional actors may qualify. Exclude only nonreferential grammatical/rhetorical addressees.

## 7. OBJECT

Retain concrete and abstract referents/content when the source establishes either standalone identity or a real bound role in represented material.

This includes source-distinguished physical things, scene items, products/documents/parts, amounts/values, choices, decisions, plans, relationships, recurring situations, mental contents, comparison vehicles, and explicitly reified courses of action/content.

One-use or low-salience concrete details may qualify when the source actually binds or distinguishes them. Do not atomize every noun in descriptive texture merely because it is nameable.

Reject pronoun/deictic duplicates, arbitrary noun fragments, meta-discourse shells, and whole propositions/questions unless the source reifies them as identifiable content/choice/situation.

## 8. LABEL

Retain the smallest complete exact target-bearing characterization, state, quality, value, classification, evaluation, comparison, correction, rejection, identity, manner/state description, polarity, or status when the source actually stages it as part of represented material.

Questioned, uncertain, negated, corrected, comparative, colloquial, idiomatic, and figurative states may qualify. Do not suppress a valid state merely because it is local, one-use, or nested inside a broader relation.

Do not create LABEL from every modifier, participant name, amount, full question/proposition, discourse marker, or ordinary predicate without a distinct state/evaluation job.

## 9. VERB

Run a positive whole-source VERB pass for materially distinct content-bearing lexical relations/events/states.

Movement, possession, experience, cognition, speech/reporting, perception, intention, comparison, waiting, stance, gesture, copular/locative relation, asking, telling, saying, finding, thinking, and other represented predicates may qualify when they perform a real relation job.

Use the smallest **complete** source-native lexical construction. Split coordinated, serial, matrix, or embedded predicates only when the source establishes distinct represented relation jobs. Keep a matrix/control/complement chain together when the embedded wording only completes one lexical relation.

Do not suppress a real relation merely because participants/content already preserve its gist. Suppress pure auxiliaries/function grammar and true same-relation duplicates.

## 10. LOCATOR

Run a positive whole-source LOCATOR pass.

Retain materially distinct source-native orientation/context relations for relative position, containment, direction, origin/destination, path, entry/exit, proximity/distance, accompaniment/carrying, recurrence/situational context, internal/embodied orientation, and figurative/comparison orientation.

A LOCATOR may qualify as a bound orientation inside a material relation; it need not be independently summary-worthy. Bare prepositional grammar remains insufficient.

Movement/deictic/comparison wording may also project as VERB/PLACE/LABEL only when each class independently performs a different represented job.

## 11. Literal preservation lock

## 12. Primitive freeze and floor/ceiling audit

Before freezing, replay the whole source by class and by represented relation.

### Floor

The inventory is too sparse if a source-supported class-native coordinate with a standalone or bound role was suppressed merely because it was local, low-salience, nested, one-use, or recoverable only in gist from neighboring rows.

### Ceiling

The inventory is too dense if a row exists only because of grammar, incidental mention without a represented role, analyst inference, duplicate tokenization, clause-level microframing, or automatic cross-class projection.

When uncertain, suppression requires one of the enumerated Section 2 defects. Do not use an unarticulated preference for simplicity as a deletion rule.

Freeze primitives only after the coverage, type, coreference, order, qualities, and literal audits pass.

## 13. Compound reconstruction

After primitive freeze, scan source order for each source-established local relation/state/event that binds two or more retained primitives.

Emit one smallest materially complete local compound for each such binding. Include all retained primitives that actually constitute that local relation, including applicable PLACE/TIME anchors when they materially locate or distinguish it.

A relation can deserve a compound even when it is local or subordinate; it need not be a top-level narrative theme. Conversely, do not create compounds from mere co-occurrence.

Do not emit:

- pairwise closure;
- nested subset/superset variants of the same binding;
- alternate tokenizations;
- one-compound-per-primitive;
- scene-wide mega-bundles;
- inferred transitive edges;
- compounds created only to compensate for a missing primitive.

Code owns canonical compound IDs, reference validation, ordering normalization, and `Q` construction where the apparatus specifies them.
