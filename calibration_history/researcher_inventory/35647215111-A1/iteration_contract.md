## Prospective correction basis

V144 verified both immutable Leah-approved archetype workbook hashes, passed all retained deterministic apparatus and prompt-harness tests, and then failed semantic calibration on both approved archetypes. The failure was two-sided: structural coordinates were omitted while locally meaningful grammatical/semantic material was over-admitted. Compound mismatch followed unstable primitive membership, and literal normalization also appeared. No deterministic harness defect was demonstrated.

The general defect is that a locally meaningful relation, characterization, or clause can too easily become its own "packet" and thereby justify its own primitives. V145 therefore replaces packet-first admission with **frame-scaffold coordinate resolution**. The Researcher Inventory is a bounded ledger of independently trackable research handles, not a complete semantic parse, proposition inventory, dependency graph, lexical census, or summary.

No gold rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, scored predecessor outputs, canonical archetype extracts, hidden case-specific corrections/examples, or sealed holdout material are supplied to the worker.

## Controlling rule

> **BUILD THE RESEARCH FRAME SCAFFOLD BEFORE EXTRACTING RELATION WORDING. FIRST RESOLVE THE SOURCE'S DISTINCT OCCURRENCES, INTERACTIONS, PERIODS, STANDING RELATIONSHIPS, REMEMBERED/REPORTED/PROSPECTIVE FRAMES, AND PRESENT TELLING/REFLECTION FRAMES. THEN FREEZE THE STRUCTURAL COORDINATES THAT THOSE FRAMES REQUIRE: STABLE PERSON ENDPOINTS, SOURCE-TREATED OBJECT REFERENTS, AND PLACE/TIME POSITIONS OR FRAMES. ONLY AFTER THAT MAY LABEL, VERB, AND LOCATOR BE ADMITTED, AND ONLY WHEN THE EXACT SOURCE-NATIVE ATOM INDEPENDENTLY CHARACTERIZES, CONNECTS, OR ORIENTS A FROZEN COORDINATE OR DEFINES A RETAINED FRAME BOUNDARY. A TOKEN, PHRASE, PREDICATE, MODIFIER, PREPOSITION, CLAUSE, OR PARSEABLE PROPOSITION NEVER EARNS A ROW MERELY BECAUSE REMOVING IT WOULD CHANGE LOCAL SENTENCE MEANING. EACH ROW MUST BE AN INDEPENDENT RESEARCH HANDLE. PRESERVE THE SMALLEST EXACT CONTIGUOUS SOURCE-NATIVE IDENTITY, TYPE BY SOURCE FUNCTION, FREEZE PRIMITIVES BEFORE COMPOUNDS, AND NEVER USE COMPOUNDS TO REPAIR PRIMITIVE MEMBERSHIP.**

## Mission and primitive classes

Produce only a literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate from the supplied source.

Primitive classes are exactly:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The inventory is deliberately between two failures:

- **too sparse:** conceptual summary that loses source-staged research coordinates;
- **too dense:** lexical/grammatical/semantic census that inventories every locally meaningful item.

Do not aim for a row count or density. Resolve the source at the research-handle level.

## 1. Research frames are the scaffold

A **research frame** is a bounded represented occurrence, interaction, period/phase, standing relationship, remembered/reported episode, prospective situation, or present telling/reflection frame that organizes source material and can be revisited as the same represented context.

A research frame is not created merely because:

- a clause has a predicate;
- a sentence makes an assertion;
- a participant does one small action;
- an adjective or label is applied;
- a preposition supplies local orientation;
- a noun phrase can be treated as a referent;
- a semantic relation can be paraphrased.

Frames may nest when the source genuinely stages both a broad and a contained organizing context. Several sentences may belong to one frame. A sentence may move among frames. Reported, remembered, hypothetical, uncertain, negative, corrected, questioned, or prospective material may form or occur inside a frame without becoming asserted fact.

The same source must yield the same internal frame scaffold regardless of which primitive class the request asks you to return.

## 2. Mandatory construction order

1. Read the complete source once without returning primitives.
2. Resolve strict coreference, source order, speaker posture, reported/remembered/prospective material, uncertainty, negation, correction, and scene/episode transitions.
3. Identify the bounded research frames in source order. Do not create one frame per clause, predicate, characterization, or dependency.
4. Build the **structural coordinate scaffold** across those frames:
   - stable PERSON endpoints;
   - source-treated OBJECT referents;
   - PLACE coordinates that establish represented sites/positions;
   - TIME coordinates that establish organizing episodes/periods/phases.
5. Freeze the scaffold provisionally.
6. For the requested class, admit only candidates that pass the independent research-handle gate in Section 3.
7. For LABEL, VERB, and LOCATOR, require direct attachment to a frozen scaffold coordinate or a retained frame boundary. These classes may not create a frame merely to justify themselves.
8. Run the structural-recall audit to restore any omitted low-salience but genuine research handles.
9. Run the anti-census audit to remove local semantic/grammatical leakage.
10. Apply literal/posture lock and freeze primitive membership.
11. Build compounds only from frozen primitives by replaying minimal represented research relations inside retained frames.
12. Run final typing, lexical, frame, compound, and isolation audits.

Do not start from all words, all phrases, all clauses, all predicates, or all possible semantic roles.

## 3. Independent research-handle gate

A primitive survives only if all applicable parts pass.

### 3.1 Source grounding

The source itself must establish the candidate. Use exact source-native wording wherever the primitive is named. Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinates may use null `source_wording`, anchored by an exact source cue.

Reject analyst-created category names, diagnoses, normalized concepts, proposition labels, explanatory paraphrases, conventional substitutions, noncontiguous composites, or semantic completions.

### 3.2 Class-native function

The candidate must actually perform the requested class's function in the source. Surface grammar or part of speech does not decide the class.

### 3.3 Independent-handle test

Ask:

**After strict coreference and without treating the host clause's grammar as the item's identity, can this candidate be pointed back to as the same participant, referent, scene position/frame, characterization, relation, or orientation in later research reconstruction?**

A candidate normally survives when it establishes a distinct handle that a researcher could reuse to reconstruct who/what/where/when/how-characterized/how-related/how-oriented a retained frame contains.

A candidate normally fails when its identity is exhausted by:

- local syntactic support;
- an auxiliary or empty copula;
- a discourse connector;
- routine argument marking;
- a generic preposition or adverb whose only job is local grammar;
- a one-clause proposition shell;
- a transient pronoun/demonstrative resolved to another handle;
- a modifier whose only identity is decoration inside one phrase;
- a semantic completion invented by the analyst;
- a token-level split of a larger source-native relation.

Meaningfulness is not enough. Changing one sentence's meaning is not enough. The unit must have a distinct research identity.

### 3.4 Frame/scaffold connection

A retained primitive must either:

- be part of the structural scaffold itself; or
- independently characterize, connect, or orient one or more frozen scaffold coordinates; or
- define a genuine retained frame boundary.

LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR cannot self-create a frame in order to satisfy this condition.

### 3.5 Minimal source-native identity

Use the smallest exact contiguous span that preserves the primitive's source-native identity.

For a relation-like primitive, keep particles, negation, modal/control material, infinitival material, reflexives, directionals, or idiomatic complements only when removing them changes the identity of the relation. Conversely, do not split support material into extra primitives when the source-native relation is one larger lexical kernel.

Do not attach actors, objects, places, or times merely to make a primitive self-explanatory.

### 3.6 Same-class distinctness

Merge only true same-class identity/coreference/repetition. Association, co-location, nesting, part/whole, container/content, actor/role, shared topic, semantic similarity, or participation in the same frame does not by itself establish identity.

## 4. Structural scaffold classes

### PLACE — represented sites and positions

PLACE inventories distinct physical/institutional settings and represented positions of occurrences, participants, referents, or present telling when those positions are part of the research scaffold.

Retain PLACE when the source establishes a distinct **where-coordinate** needed to locate a retained frame or scaffold item.

Rules:

- Physical naming is not required.
- A source cue may establish an unnamed position.
- Broad and contained sites may both survive when each has a distinct organizing role.
- Co-located actors/referents may have distinct unnamed positions only when the source stages those positions as distinct research coordinates.
- Reported, remembered, prospective, or present-telling material may have its own position when the source structure requires one.
- Do not create PLACE from every surface, container, body part, direction, distance word, movement phrase, or spatial-looking expression.
- Do not create PLACE merely because every event must happen somewhere.

For unnamed PLACE use null `source_wording`, an exact source cue, and a neutral mechanical short tag/note. Never invent specificity.

### TIME — organizing frames and phases

TIME inventories distinct episodes, periods, phases, attempts, waits, interactions, transitions, recurrence spans, remembered/reported/prospective frames, and present telling/reflection frames when they organize retained material.

Retain TIME when it supplies a distinct **when/frame-coordinate** that groups, positions, separates, nests, or transitions research material.

Rules:

- No date, clock, duration, tense, or temporal word is required.
- Broad and contained frames may both survive when each organizes material distinctly.
- A short occurrence may be TIME when the source uses it as an episode/phase boundary.
- Explicit temporal wording is not automatically TIME.
- Do not convert every action, discourse transition, tense/aspect shift, adverb, recurrence word, or clause into TIME.

For unnamed TIME use null `source_wording`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording.

### PERSON — stable represented actors and social endpoints

PERSON inventories `B` plus every distinct stable human/social actor or group after strict coreference when the source gives that actor/group an independent represented role in at least one retained frame.

Participation may include acting, speaking, perceiving, being acted upon, possessing, receiving, benefiting, accompanying, or serving as a stable relation endpoint. Action or speech is not required.

Exclude rhetorical/generic addressees, hypothetical role placeholders, unstable pronouns with no stable referent, and true aliases/coreference duplicates.

Order non-speakers by first independent represented participation rather than incidental mention alone.

### OBJECT — source-treated things and content referents

OBJECT inventories source-established concrete or abstract referents that the source treats as independently trackable things inside retained frames.

An OBJECT may be physical, documentary, quantitative, relational, situational, decisional, mental-content, comparative, or otherwise abstract when the source itself reifies/treats it as a referent.

Retain when the source handles, positions, possesses, selects, compares, evaluates, notices, remembers, decides about, questions, appraises, revisits, or points back to the same referent.

Reject:

- every noun phrase merely because it can fill an argument slot;
- proposition shells created from clauses;
- analyst-completed summaries of what someone meant/wanted/decided unless the source treats that content as a referent;
- unstable pronouns/demonstratives that resolve to another handle;
- a setting duplicated as OBJECT when its only retained function is PLACE;
- a one-use argument phrase whose identity is exhausted by its host relation.

Use the smallest source-native referential atom.

## 5. Relation/description/orientation classes

These classes are extracted only after the structural scaffold is provisionally frozen.

### LABEL — independently trackable source-applied characterization

LABEL inventories source-applied states, qualities, statuses, identities, classifications, evaluations, comparisons, manners, polarities, corrections, rejections, intensifications, and characterization questions when the characterization itself is an independent research handle attached to a scaffold coordinate or retained frame.

Preserve uncertainty, question, negation, rejection, correction, contrast, attribution, colloquial wording, and material intensity.

Reject every adjective/adverb/modifier merely because it changes local phrase meaning. A LABEL must be something the source treats as a characterization worth independently pointing back to, not syntactic decoration.

### VERB — independently trackable source-native relation

VERB inventories the smallest exact source-native lexical relation kernels that independently connect or change frozen scaffold coordinates or define a retained frame relation.

Retain a relation when it establishes a distinct research handle for an action, state, transition, perception, cognition, report, intention, possession, movement, wait, or other relation that can be revisited as that same relation in reconstruction.

Do not harvest every grammatical predicate.

In particular:

- auxiliaries, empty copulas, support verbs, control verbs, infinitival markers, reporting scaffolds, and discourse predicates do not receive separate rows merely because they are verbs;
- keep them inside a larger contiguous relation kernel when they are necessary to preserve that relation's identity;
- split matrix/embedded or coordinated material only when the source genuinely establishes separately trackable research relations;
- do not split one source-native relation into multiple token-level rows;
- do not suppress a legitimate relation merely because its wording is brief, generic, deictic, colloquial, negative, uncertain, or context-dependent.

### LOCATOR — independently trackable source-native orientation/context relation

LOCATOR inventories exact source-native orientation/context relations that materially situate a frozen coordinate or retained frame and are independently useful in research reconstruction.

Eligible functions may include relative position, containment, direction, path, origin/destination, entry/exit, approach/departure, proximity/distance, accompaniment/carrying, recurrence/situational context, embodied/internal orientation, source/target orientation, and materially organizing figurative/comparison orientation.

Reject bare/routine prepositions, recipient/topic marking, possession alone, local argument wrappers, and incidental adverbials whose identity is exhausted by the host clause.

A locator may overlap wording with PLACE/TIME/LABEL/VERB only when the same exact span performs a genuinely distinct orientation function. Cross-class overlap is not automatic.

## 6. Cross-class multiplicity and non-substitution

Type by represented source function, never by part of speech.

The same exact source span may lawfully appear in multiple primitive classes only when it performs genuinely different independently trackable functions.

Do not create cross-class twins merely because multiple grammatical analyses are possible.

No class absorbs another retained function:

- OBJECT does not absorb LABEL;
- LABEL does not absorb VERB;
- PLACE/TIME do not absorb LOCATOR;
- VERB does not absorb actor/object/site/frame;
- LOCATOR does not replace PLACE/TIME.

## 7. Literal, contiguity, and posture lock

Every source-derived non-null string must preserve source language character-for-character wherever the schema requires source text.

Never synonymize, lemmatize, repair spelling/grammar, normalize dialect, expand contractions, translate, polish, diagnose, standardize a group/category name, change number, or substitute a conventional expression.

Do not concatenate noncontiguous fragments. Do not silently add omitted words. Do not broaden a fragment merely so it reads well outside context.

`source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be exact source text where applicable. Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME may use null `source_wording`.

Questions, uncertainty, hypotheticals, negations, self-corrections, remembered possibilities, reported material, and proposed future actions remain in source posture. Inventorying does not assert truth or realization.

## 8. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean only. True means the source supplies material qualities/descriptions associated with the coordinate; false otherwise.

Do not create or split primitives to justify the boolean. `Q` is never a primitive.

## 9. Ordering

Default within-class order is first material source anchor after filtering/coreference.

Tie rules:

- broad/whole setting before dependent/contained setting when introduced together;
- whole before dependent part at the same anchor unless source order stages the part first;
- PERSON follows first independent represented participation;
- remembered/reported/hypothetical/future frames remain at source position rather than external chronology.

The apparatus owns canonical IDs and deterministic numbering.

## 10. Structural-recall audit — recover scaffold, not vocabulary

After initial selection, replay every retained frame and the whole source. Restore any genuine research handle omitted merely because it was unnamed, brief, generic, deictic, context-dependent, low-salience, one-use, nested, possessively introduced, reported, remembered, prospective, uncertain, negative, corrected, questioned, or figurative.

Check especially for:

- unnamed participant/referent/occurrence positions required to locate retained frames;
- broad/local time frames, waits, interactions, transitions, recurrence, remembered/reported/prospective frames, and present telling/reflection frames;
- minor stable actors/groups/endpoints, including beneficiary/possessive/relational endpoints;
- low-salience source-treated referents that are independently trackable;
- source-applied characterization handles that independently qualify a scaffold coordinate;
- compact source-native relation kernels that independently connect scaffold coordinates;
- compact orientation/context handles that independently situate scaffold coordinates;
- lawful same-span different functions.

The recovery audit does not loosen the independent-handle gate. It cannot harvest all class-shaped wording.

## 11. Anti-census audit — remove host-clause leakage

For every retained primitive ask:

1. What retained research frame contains or is organized by this handle?
2. Which frozen scaffold coordinate does it establish, characterize, connect, or orient?
3. Can the item be pointed back to independently of its host clause's grammar?
4. Is its identity source-established rather than analyst-completed?
5. Is this the smallest exact contiguous source-native identity?
6. Did grammar, part of speech, lexical vividness, proposition parsing, or local sentence semantics create it instead of a research handle?
7. For LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR, did the item self-create a frame in order to justify itself?
8. Did I split one source-native relation into auxiliary/support/token fragments?
9. Did I invent, normalize, paraphrase, merge, or widen the source wording?
10. Is it truly distinct from same-class identity/coreference/repetition?

If questions 1-4 do not have a source-grounded answer, remove the primitive even when the phrase is meaningful.

Never aim for a hidden count.

## 12. Compound construction — minimal frame relation replay

Freeze primitives before compound work.

A compound represents one minimal source-staged research relation among two or more frozen primitives inside a retained frame. It reconnects existing handles; it does not create handles.

Create a compound only when:

1. the source stages a distinct relation/characterization/position/frame connection among frozen primitives;
2. the connection is independently useful for research reconstruction rather than merely a grammatical dependency;
3. the compound includes the active frozen primitives in that minimal relation and excludes inactive neighbors; and
4. it is not an alternate parse, pairwise/transitive closure, one-compound-per-token artifact, automatic one-compound-per-clause artifact, duplicate/subset alias, or scene-wide mega-bundle.

A retained frame may yield several compounds when it contains several distinct research relations. Several clauses may contribute to one compound when they realize one retained relation.

VERB, LABEL, and LOCATOR participate when active in the relation. PLACE/TIME participate when they actually locate or organize it.

A compound cannot create, delete, merge, retype, or substitute for a primitive. If a required handle is missing, primitive selection failed and must be corrected before compounds.

Canonical compound IDs/order and `_Q` construction remain apparatus-owned.

## 13. Final contrast audit

### Too dense / census failure

Warning signs:

- every clause or predicate creates its own frame;
- every verb token becomes VERB;
- auxiliaries/copulas/support/control material split into separate VERBs;
- every noun phrase or discourse placeholder becomes OBJECT;
- every modifier becomes LABEL;
- every temporal word/action becomes TIME;
- every spatial/context phrase becomes PLACE/LOCATOR;
- local argument marking is inventoried;
- compounds track nearly every clause.

Correct by returning to the frame scaffold and independent-handle gate.

### Too sparse / abstraction failure

Warning signs:

- unnamed site/frame coordinates disappear because no noun names them;
- minor stable actors/endpoints disappear because they do not act or speak;
- low-salience source-treated referents disappear because they are mundane;
- a source-applied characterization/relation/orientation handle is omitted because it is brief or generic;
- broad and contained organizing frames collapse when the source stages both;
- later reporting/present reflection loses its own coordinates;
- several distinct source handles are replaced by one conceptual summary.

Correct by replaying the structural-recall audit under the same independent-handle gate.

## 14. Final audit

Before return verify:

- **Stable frames:** the same bounded research-frame scaffold governed the source regardless of requested class.
- **Scaffold first:** PERSON/OBJECT/PLACE/TIME coordinates were resolved before relation-like extraction.
- **Independent handles:** every primitive has an independently trackable source identity.
- **No self-justifying packet:** LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR did not create a frame solely to justify themselves.
- **Structural recall:** low-salience/unnamed/deictic/context-dependent genuine handles were not suppressed.
- **Census ceiling:** no primitive exists merely because a lexical/grammatical/semantic item can be classified.
- **Relation kernels:** one source-native relation was not fragmented into support/auxiliary/token rows.
- **Source atomicity:** every non-null primitive uses the smallest exact contiguous source-native span preserving identity.
- **Typing:** every primitive is typed by represented source function.
- **Cross-class:** different functions may share wording; alternate parses do not multiply rows.
- **Literal/posture:** exact wording, dialect, polarity, question, uncertainty, negation, correction, and posture are preserved.
- **Compounds:** only minimal retained research relations reassemble frozen active refs.
- **Candidate only:** no promotion, APA identity, database action, or substantive psychological interpretation occurred.

## 18. Holdout boundary

No calibration correction may be made from sealed holdout output.
