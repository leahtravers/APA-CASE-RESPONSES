## 1. Coordinate economy

Every retained primitive must have a source anchor and an independently useful research job in its class. Source presence, grammatical parseability, mention frequency, or usefulness inside one proposition does not by itself justify a primitive.

Ask whether the coordinate should remain independently addressable outside the one local binding where it appears. If not, keep the detail in the compound/binding rather than promoting it into a primitive.

Cross-class overlap is exceptional, not preferred. The same span may project into more than one class only when each projection has a separate durable class-specific research job. Do not create a second class projection merely because it is technically defensible.

`Lightweight` means preserve the source distinctions needed for archetypal research resolution while refusing token, noun, adjective, verb, preposition, clause, physical-sublocation, and action-phase census.

## 2. Scene and frame ledger

Before other extraction, reconstruct a source-order ledger of materially distinct physical scenes and temporal frames.

PLACE/TIME coordinates operate at scene/episode grain. Retain a new coordinate when a materially distinct represented setting, participant/object scene, remembered/reported episode, relationship span, recurring context, prospective period, or present telling/reflection frame organizes different research material.

Supported unnamed PLACE/TIME anchors are allowed when a distinct represented scene/frame exists but its exact name is unstated.

Do not split one scene or episode into a coordinate for every surface, container, body position, path step, movement endpoint, wait fragment, action phase, predicate, tense/aspect form, duration, adverb, transition, or local moment. Such details normally belong in OBJECT/LOCATOR/VERB/LABEL or the local compound if they are materially useful at all.

## 3. Primitive admission test

For each proposed primitive require all five:

1. **SOURCE SUPPORT** — exact source wording/cue, except a supported unnamed PLACE/TIME anchor.
2. **DURABLE CLASS JOB** — an independently useful research job that should remain addressable outside one proposition.
3. **ARCHETYPAL GRAIN** — scene/frame grain for PLACE/TIME; stable referent/value/relation/orientation grain for the remaining classes.
4. **NON-REDUNDANCY** — not an alias, wrapper, micro-sublocation, support-grammar artifact, parse fragment, duplicate projection, or alternate decomposition.
5. **SMALLEST COMPLETE SOURCE-NATIVE FORM** — no paraphrase or normalization.

If a detail is useful only to express one source-local binding, preserve it through that binding rather than manufacturing a primitive.

## 4. PLACE

PLACE records durable physical scene or participant-position settings at episode grain.

Retain named settings and supported unnamed anchors when a materially distinct interaction, participant/object whereabouts, remembered/reported scene, prospective position, or current represented setting needs its own scene coordinate.

Do not promote every surface, container, body part, object-support relation, shelf/side/edge, path segment, movement endpoint, or physical micro-position into PLACE. Those normally remain object/orientation/binding detail unless the source establishes a distinct represented scene axis.

A concrete referent may also be OBJECT only when thing identity and setting identity are independently necessary. Resolve true aliases.

## 5. TIME

TIME records durable episodes and frames organizing represented material.

Retain event periods, relationship spans, remembered/reported episodes, recurring frames, intended/prospective periods, and represented present telling/reflection frames when they organize distinct material. Several relations may share one TIME.

Do not create TIME for every action phase, predicate, wait fragment, local duration, tense/aspect form, adverb, transition, or predicate-local moment. Nested frames both survive only when each independently organizes materially different research bindings.

## 6. PERSON

Retain `B` and every source-distinguished represented human/social actor or stable group after coreference, including indirect, possessive, relational, offscreen, remembered, reported, prospective, or comparison actors when represented.

Resolve true aliases/coreference. Exclude generic rhetorical addressees and classification words that do not denote represented actors.

## 7. OBJECT

OBJECT records selected stable concrete things and stable abstract/content referents that are independently revisitable across research bindings.

Retain source-distinguished things, stable choices/options/decisions/relations/content, internal or figurative referents when the source gives them thing-like identity that matters beyond a single clause.

Do not perform a noun/content census. Reject pronoun duplicates, generic nouns, one-off clause contents, local event restatements, feelings/evaluations whose research job is characterization, parseable complements, and nominalizations whose only function is already carried by a relation plus its arguments.

Resolve aliases/coreference and repeated surface mentions to one coordinate where they are the same object.

## 8. LABEL

LABEL records the smallest complete exact source-present characterization value that is independently reusable as a research coordinate: quality, state, evaluation, comparison, identity/classification, correction/rejection, polarity/status, or materially qualifying manner.

Preserve source-native uncertainty, question, negation, intensity, correction, and idiom when they are part of the value. Distinct atomic values may remain separate when the source presents them as separately meaningful characterizations.

Do not perform an adjective/evaluation census. Reject incidental modifiers, descriptive tokens, discourse reactions, full predicate clauses, and evaluations whose only research role is local to one binding.

## 9. VERB

VERB records a sparse set of smallest complete exact source-native lexical relation atoms materially required to bind retained research coordinates.

Keep principal event/relation atoms and coordinated or embedded relations only when each independently binds retained coordinates. Arguments and ordinary complements belong outside the VERB and are connected through compounds.

Do not preserve an entire relation packet merely because the source contains it. Exclude auxiliaries, support verbs, stance/reporting/cognition wrappers, property carriers, and matrix packaging when their only job is to introduce another retained content relation. Do not emit every parseable predicate.

Keep particles, negation, or complements inside VERB only when lexically inseparable from the relation identity as expressed. Never paraphrase or normalize `source_wording`.

## 10. LOCATOR

LOCATOR records selected exact source-native orientation/context atoms that independently position a retained coordinate or binding.

Retain material physical position/path/direction/origin/destination/containment/support/accompaniment/proximity, recurring-context, embodied/internal, or figurative orientation when orientation itself is a durable research axis. A useful locator may be multiword when the whole phrase performs that locating job.

Do not perform a prepositional/context census. Reject recipients, topics, instruments, durations, frequency fragments, discourse markers, physical micro-sublocations, temporal wording, and generic complements when no independent orientation job exists.

Avoid duplicating PLACE/TIME merely to restate the same setting/frame. Overlap survives only when orientation itself adds a separate durable research job.

## 11. Literal preservation lock

Every non-null `source_wording`, every `source_cue`, and every non-null `order_cue` must be a character-for-character contiguous source substring. Never substitute researcher tags, synonyms, grammatical repairs, normalized dialect, number changes, expanded contractions, or inferred wording.

The same literal-source rule applies to any source-derived short tag/bundle wording when the output schema asks the worker to reproduce source language there.

Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinates may use `source_wording: null`, with an exact source cue. Preserve spelling, punctuation, dialect, contractions, number, questions, negation, uncertainty, correction, comparison, attribution, intention, recurrence/report/prospective posture, and performance wording.

## 12. Ordering and qualities

Order each class by first source establishment after coreference, with speaker first in PERSON.

`qualities_available` is true only when source-present qualities/descriptions are available for that coordinate. Quality availability does not force a separate LABEL, and a LABEL may coexist with another class only when it independently passes the durable-class-job test.

## 13. Sparse source-local compound reconstruction

Freeze primitives before compounds. Then re-read the source for distinct research bindings.

Create one smallest materially complete compound for each distinct source-local relation, attribution, characterization, question, position, comparison, or other research binding that connects frozen coordinates. Include all and only frozen coordinates materially co-bound in that source-local binding, including the material scene/frame anchor when applicable.

Compounds express semantic binding; they are not a second inventory of every clause. Do not create one compound for every primitive, every grammatical predicate, every subset of a proposition, or every possible pair of coordinates.

Do not manufacture prose wrapper relations. Do not emit nested partials, pairwise closure, alternate decompositions, duplicate thematic bundles, or scene-wide supersets. Split only when the source establishes separately useful bindings through changed actor, argument, target, attribution, comparison, question, orientation, or frame.

A primitive may remain unbundled.

## 14. Final audits

**Scene/frame audit:** confirm each PLACE/TIME is a durable scene/frame axis rather than a physical micro-sublocation or action/predicate phase; recover omitted materially distinct represented scenes/episodes, including supported unnamed anchors.

**Stable-coordinate audit:** remove noun/content/adjective/evaluation census items and retain only independently revisitable OBJECT/LABEL coordinates.

**Relation audit:** reduce VERB inventory to independently binding source-native lexical relation atoms; remove support/matrix/auxiliary wrappers and arguments from relation wording unless lexically inseparable.

**Orientation audit:** retain only LOCATORs with a durable independent orientation/context job; remove preposition/context census and duplicate setting/frame restatements.

**Overlap audit:** for every cross-class duplicate, state internally the separate durable job of each projection; if no separate job exists, keep one.

**Compound audit:** keep one smallest materially complete source-local binding, not clause-by-clause or combinatorial closure.

**Literal audit:** compare every source-derived emitted string character-for-character against the source; repair any normalization before return.

Then verify source order, type assignment, qualities flags, compound references, and candidate-only boundaries.
