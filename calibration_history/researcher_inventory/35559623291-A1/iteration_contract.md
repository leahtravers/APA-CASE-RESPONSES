On the longer source especially, nearly any grammatical, descriptive, topical, copular, metaphorical, or discourse relation could become a primitive and compound. V131 keeps V130's improved recall while restoring a gate at the level that belongs in a Researcher Inventory: **the represented semantic unit**.

A primitive does not need to deserve a standalone summary card. But it must either have standalone source identity or fill a real role/support in a researcher-reconnectable represented semantic unit. Language does not become a semantic unit merely because it forms a clause, predicate, modifier, prepositional phrase, comparison fragment, or reporting frame.

No gold rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, scored prior outputs, canonical archetype extracts, case-specific hidden corrections/examples, or sealed holdout content are supplied to the worker.

## Controlling rule

> **READ THE WHOLE SOURCE. FIRST BUILD A SOURCE-NATIVE LEDGER OF RESEARCHER-RECONNECTABLE REPRESENTED SEMANTIC UNITS AT NATURAL GRAIN. A UNIT MAY BE LOCAL OR LOW-SALIENCE, BUT IT MUST ESTABLISH OR CHANGE AN ACTION, INTERACTION, EXPERIENCE, COGNITION, CHOICE, RELATIONSHIP, STATE, EVALUATION, COMPARISON, SCENE CONFIGURATION, ORIENTATION, OR REIFIED CONTENT THAT THE SOURCE ACTUALLY REPRESENTS. THEN, FOR EACH CLASS, RETAIN THE EXACT SOURCE-SUPPORTED COORDINATES THAT HAVE STANDALONE SOURCE IDENTITY OR FILL REAL ROLES/SUPPORTS IN THOSE UNITS. DO NOT REQUIRE STANDALONE IMPORTANCE. DO NOT PROMOTE GRAMMAR, DISCOURSE SCAFFOLDING, DECORATIVE TEXTURE, OR EVERY MODIFIER/CLAUSE INTO A UNIT. TYPE BY REPRESENTED JOB. FREEZE PRIMITIVES. PROJECT EACH RETAINED MULTI-COORDINATE SEMANTIC UNIT ONCE AS A COMPOUND.**

## Mission

Produce only a literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate from the supplied source.

Primitive classes are exactly:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The target is neither plot-summary compression nor lexical/grammatical census. It is a source-faithful coordinate system from which a researcher can reconnect to represented material without substituting analytic interpretation.

## 1. Construction order

Use this order and do not invert it:

1. Read the complete source.
2. Resolve obvious coreference and broad source order.
3. Build the represented-semantic-unit ledger under Section 2.
4. For each requested primitive class, run a complete positive source pass against that ledger plus standalone source identities.
5. Apply class-native type/grain rules.
6. Apply bounded suppression and deduplication.
7. Perform cross-class audit.
8. Lock literal source strings and freeze primitives.
9. Build compounds from the frozen primitives and retained semantic-unit ledger only.
10. Run floor/ceiling audit before output.

## 2. Represented semantic-unit ledger

The unit ledger is **smaller than a clause ledger and larger than a plot summary**.

A unit qualifies when the source materially represents at least one of these jobs:

### 2.1 Action / change / occurrence

A participant or referent does, undergoes, gets, loses, moves, waits, changes, arrives, leaves, obtains, rejects, chooses, attempts, or otherwise participates in a source-established occurrence.

### 2.2 Social interaction / communication

A social act qualifies when the act itself matters as represented interaction: asking, telling, responding, refusing, helping, directing, taking, giving, confronting, waiting on another, or similar interaction.

A bare reporting frame such as `said/thought/told` does not automatically deserve its own unit when its only function is introducing content already represented elsewhere. Retain the speech/cognition act when it has its own participant/recipient/content relation or materially changes the interaction.

### 2.3 Cognition / intention / choice / self-management

A thought, uncertainty, decision, intention, comparison, plan, question, reflection, or self-management relation qualifies when the source represents it as content or stance rather than merely using discourse filler.

### 2.4 State / evaluation / classification

A source-staged state, value, status, evaluation, correction, identity, uncertainty, comparison, or polarity qualifies when it characterizes a retained coordinate or relation in a researcher-reconnectable way.

### 2.5 Scene-defining configuration

A scene description qualifies as a unit when it establishes a materially represented configuration needed to reconnect the episode: who/what is where, a source-significant physical arrangement, or a represented environmental configuration that participates in later meaning/action.

Decorative texture does not become a unit merely because it is vivid.

### 2.6 Orientation / path / position

A source-established direction, containment, approach/departure, origin/destination, relative position, recurrence context, accompaniment, internal/embodied orientation, or figurative comparison orientation qualifies when that relation itself helps reconnect represented material.

### 2.7 Reified content / relationship / situation

A choice, plan, recurring situation, relationship, mental content, comparison vehicle, question, decision, or other abstract material qualifies when the source treats it as an identifiable thing rather than as a mere clause shell.

### 2.8 What is not a semantic unit by itself

Do not make a separate unit merely from:

- existential/coplanar scaffolding such as generic `there is`, `is/was`, or `I am` when another primitive already carries the represented job;
- a clause boundary;
- an auxiliary or grammatical complement;
- a bare attribution/reporting frame whose content is the actual unit;
- narrator discourse management such as repetition, filler, or saying-that-one-is-saying unless the communication act itself matters;
- a descriptive adjective/modifier with no researcher-reconnectable state/evaluation role;
- an incidental noun with no role in a retained unit and no standalone source identity;
- a topical `about/for/to/of/with` phrase that supplies ordinary grammar rather than orientation/context as a research coordinate;
- the internal pieces of a simile/metaphor when only the comparison as a whole has represented identity;
- every tense, frequency word, deictic, or preposition;
- restating the same relation at several overlapping token spans.

Narrative centrality is **not** required. A local, one-use, remembered, background, nested, prospective, colloquial, figurative, or low-salience unit can qualify. The question is represented semantic identity, not importance.

## 3. Primitive entitlement

A primitive is retained by one of two routes:

### Route A — standalone source identity

The source treats it as an identifiable actor, referent/content, value/state, lexical relation, orientation, setting, episode/period, relationship, choice, recurring situation, comparison vehicle, or other stable coordinate.

### Route B — semantic-unit role/support

The primitive fills a real class-native role or support in a retained semantic unit and removing it would erase a source-supported `who`, `what`, `state/value`, `relation/action`, `orientation`, `where`, or `when` distinction from that unit.

The primitive does **not** need a standalone summary card. But participation in mere grammar or decorative description is not Route B.

## 4. Bounded suppression

After positive coverage, suppress only:

- true alias/coreference duplicates;
- pure auxiliary/function/discourse grammar;
- duplicate same-grain tokenizations of one job;
- analyst-created abstraction, paraphrase, inference, normalization, or diagnosis;
- arbitrary subparts with no independent source identity or semantic-unit role;
- unreified whole-clause/question/proposition shells;
- PLACE/TIME microframes created only from individual predicates/clauses;
- cross-class projections that answer no genuinely different researcher question;
- duplicate compound subsets/supersets or graph-closure variants.

Do not suppress a valid primitive merely because neighboring primitives preserve the gist. Do not retain a candidate merely because it is linguistically explicit.

## 5. PLACE

`PLACE` is a represented physical/institutional setting or occurrence position, including lawful supported unnamed positions.

Retain:

- settings of retained episodes/semantic units;
- participant interaction/wait/conversation positions;
- destinations/departures/arrivals when represented as positions;
- broad and contained settings when each provides a different navigation job;
- another participant's represented scene;
- remembered/reported/prospective/comparison settings;
- present-telling location when the source establishes one;
- source-significant spatial arrangement when it is itself a retained scene-defining unit.

Supported unnamed PLACE may represent a real scene/position whose name is absent. Use `source_wording: null`, exact source cue, and neutral mechanical tag.

Do **not** classify as PLACE merely because wording is spatial. Physical objects/surfaces, abstract `spaces`, distance metaphors, comparison vehicles, body parts, walls/lines/water, or other spatial nouns remain OBJECT/LABEL/LOCATOR as their represented job requires unless the source actually uses them as an occurrence setting.

## 6. TIME

`TIME` is a represented episode, phase, period, recurrence span, transition, prospective/comparison frame, or present-reflection/telling frame.

Retain TIME when it organizes one or more retained semantic units as an episode/period distinction. A temporal frame may be unnamed when the episode is distinct but no explicit name exists.

Do not create a new TIME from every tense cue, frequency word, `now/then`, duration phrase, subordinate action, question, or clause. Explicit temporal wording is evidence, not automatic entitlement. Group serial actions into the same frame until the source establishes a material episode/phase distinction.

Prospective recurrence/future-similar-situation frames may qualify when the source explicitly represents them as an ongoing/future research frame.

## 7. PERSON

Retain `B` plus every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when that actor fills a social, possessive, recipient, beneficiary, source, target, remembered/reported, comparison, or other role in a retained semantic unit, or has standalone social identity.

Minor, offscreen, possessive/relational, remembered, reported, prospective, and institutional actors may qualify. Exclude nonreferential/rhetorical addressees.

## 8. OBJECT

`OBJECT` is a concrete or abstract referent/content with standalone source identity or a real argument/content role in a retained semantic unit.

Retain, when source-established:

- physical things and scene items used or positioned in retained units;
- products/documents/parts;
- amounts/values when treated as referents;
- choices, decisions, plans, relationships, recurring situations;
- mental contents/questions/comparison vehicles when reified;
- conceptual or figurative `spaces/places/things` when the source treats them as abstract referents rather than occurrence settings.

Do not emit every noun in descriptive texture. Exclude ordinary pronoun/deictic duplicates, arbitrary fragments, meta-discourse nouns, and clause shells unless source-reified.

## 9. LABEL

`LABEL` is the shortest complete source-native expression of a source-staged value, state, quality, classification, evaluation, comparison, correction, rejection, identity, manner/state description, polarity, or status attached to a retained coordinate or relation.

Preserve meaningful question, negation, uncertainty, contrast, intensity, correction, and attribution.

A classification question and its polarity/correction may be distinct LABEL jobs when the source stages them separately; do not automatically merge a question and answer into one label.

Do not emit every adjective or colorful descriptor. An attributive modifier qualifies only when the source stages the property/evaluation as a researcher-reconnectable state or when it is necessary to distinguish a retained semantic unit.

## 10. VERB

`VERB` is the smallest complete source-native lexical relation/event/state kernel for a retained semantic unit.

Retain materially distinct action, movement, possession, experience, cognition, perception, intention, social interaction, waiting, stance, comparison, or other operative relations.

Preserve required particles, reflexives, negation-bearing construction, and complements when removing them changes the relation identity.

Do not emit every verbal token. Suppress:

- pure auxiliaries;
- existential/copular scaffolding when LABEL/PLACE/OBJECT carries the represented job and the copula adds no separate relation;
- repeated metanarrative `say/think/know` frames whose only job is introducing already-retained content;
- fragments split from one complete control/complement relation;
- duplicate restatements of the same relation.

Speech/cognition verbs remain valid when the act itself has distinct interaction/content/stance identity.

## 11. LOCATOR

`LOCATOR` is a source-native orientation/path/context relation that independently helps reconnect a retained semantic unit.

It may be prepositional, adverbial, deictic, verb-like, colloquial, or idiomatic. Type by orientation job, not grammatical form.

Retain relative position, containment, origin/destination, direction/path, entry/exit, proximity/distance, accompaniment/carrying, recurrence/situational context, embodied/internal orientation, and figurative/comparison orientation when the relation itself matters.

Do not emit topical or argument-marking grammar (`about`, `for`, `to`, `of`, `with`, etc.) merely because it links words. The phrase must establish an orientation/context relation, not just attach content to a verb/noun.

## 12. Cross-class audit

The same source wording may appear in more than one class only when it genuinely answers different researcher questions.

Ask independently:

- who/social actor?
- what referent/content?
- what state/value/classification?
- what operative relation/action?
- what orientation/path/context?
- where/what occurrence position?
- when/what episode/period?

Do not infer a second class from grammatical possibility. Do not forbid overlap when the source truly performs both jobs.

## 13. Literal lock

Every explicit semantic primitive preserves source-native wording character-for-character wherever the schema requires source text.

Never synonymize, lemmatize, repair grammar/spelling, normalize dialect, expand contractions, clean punctuation, translate, diagnose, or invent wording.

Only lawful unnamed PLACE/TIME supports may use null `source_wording` and neutral mechanical tags. `source_cue` and `order_cue` remain exact source evidence.

## 14. Primitive floor/ceiling audit

Before freeze, replay each retained semantic unit and ask:

### Floor

Would deleting a candidate erase a source-supported actor/referent/state/relation/orientation/scene/episode distinction of a retained semantic unit or a standalone source-reified coordinate? If yes, keep it.

### Ceiling

Would deleting a candidate lose only grammar, discourse scaffolding, decorative texture, a duplicate parse/tokenization, an incidental mention with no unit role, or a class projection already fully represented by the same job? If yes, suppress it.

The floor protects local low-salience material. The ceiling protects against source-length-sensitive census.

Freeze primitives only after class coverage, type, coreference, order, qualities, and literal audits.

## 15. Compound reconstruction

Each retained semantic unit that binds two or more frozen primitives projects **once** as the smallest materially complete local compound.

Include the frozen roles/supports that actually constitute or materially locate that unit. Standalone primitives need no compound.

Do not create compounds for:

- mere co-occurrence;
- grammar-only attachments;
- descriptive texture not admitted as a semantic unit;
- pairwise graph closure;
- every possible subset/superset;
- alternate tokenization;
- one-compound-per-primitive;
- scene-wide mega-bundles;
- inferred transitive relations;
- compensation for missing primitives.

Canonical compound ordering/IDs and `Q` construction remain code-owned.

## 16. Final audit

Before output verify:

1. every represented semantic unit is source-supported and at natural grain;
2. the unit ledger is neither a clause census nor a plot summary;
3. every primitive has standalone source identity or a real role/support in a retained unit;
4. every plausible source-supported class candidate received a positive test;
5. PLACE/TIME represent occurrence/episode frames, not spatial/temporal words by default;
6. LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR represent semantic jobs, not modifiers/predicates/prepositions by default;
7. abstract/figurative spatial nouns are typed by job, not surface spatial vocabulary;
8. question/correction/polarity distinctions are preserved when separately staged;
9. all source-derived strings are literal;
10. every retained multi-coordinate semantic unit projects once, with no duplicate closure;
11. output remains candidate-only.

Never target hidden counts or infer gold.

## 17. Qualities and order

Order by first source establishment after coreference. Use speaker-first PERSON and broad-before-contained support only where source navigation warrants it.
