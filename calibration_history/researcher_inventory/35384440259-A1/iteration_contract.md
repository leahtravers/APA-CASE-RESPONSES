## Mission

Produce only the literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate requested by the schema.

Recover the source's represented research coordinates and the smallest source propositions that bind them. The target is not a semantic summary, but it is also not a token/grammar/preposition census.

Never interpret psychological meaning, score APA, infer protected threads, draw conclusions, promote records, mint APA IDs, write sovereign/admitted data, or write an APA database.

## 1. Construction order

Read the whole source before extracting.

### Pass A — source proposition map

Silently map the source in order as local propositions/events/states/orientations. For each local source proposition identify, where present:

- represented participants/referents;
- content-bearing lexical relation(s);
- explicitly staged value/state/classification;
- setting/occurrence position;
- episode/period frame;
- material orientation/locator.

This proposition map is only a construction aid. Do not output propositions as extra OBJECTs merely because they can be named.

### Pass B — primitive projection

Project only source-supported coordinates into PLACE, TIME, PERSON, OBJECT, LABEL, VERB, and LOCATOR using the class rules below. A coordinate qualifies when it is explicitly source-established or is a necessary unnamed PLACE/TIME support frame for a materially distinct proposition/scene that would otherwise lose its occurrence context.

Do not require every coordinate to be independently interesting in isolation. A low-salience one-use coordinate may be necessary because it preserves the represented topology of a local source proposition.

### Pass C — primitive audit and freeze

Resolve coreference, exact source grain, type, source order, qualities, lexical fidelity, duplicates, and class shadows. Freeze primitives before compounds.

### Pass D — canonical compounds

Reconstruct one canonical local binding for each materially represented proposition/state/orientation whose frozen coordinates participate together. Do not create alternate subset variants.

## 2. Admission rule — source establishment plus topology

Retain a candidate when at least one is true:

1. **Explicit coordinate:** the source names or directly stages it as a participant, referent, lexical relation, value/state, setting, episode/frame, or orientation; or
2. **Necessary occurrence frame:** an unnamed PLACE or TIME is needed to keep a distinct represented interaction/event/report/reflection/comparison scene from collapsing into another scene.

Reject only when the candidate is merely:

- an alias/coreferential duplicate;
- function grammar or auxiliary support with no distinct relation;
- an analyst-created nominalization of a clause/proposition;
- a modifier fragment already carried as a quality and not separately staged as a value/state;
- metadiscourse that only organizes the telling;
- an automatic cross-class shadow;
- a duplicate or alternate tokenization of the same coordinate.

Do not use salience, frequency, dramatic importance, or standalone conceptual interest as an admission test.

## 3. PLACE

PLACE represents physical/institutional settings and source-supported occurrence positions.

Retain broad and contained explicit settings when both are represented. Also reconstruct an unnamed PLACE (`source_wording: null`) for a materially distinct interaction, wait, report, remembered scene, comparison scene, or present-telling/reflection scene when its occurrence position is represented by the source but not separately named.

Do not create PLACE from every spatial noun, object position, path, distance, body part, water reference, or figurative expression. Those may be OBJECT, LABEL, or LOCATOR instead.

## 4. TIME

TIME represents event/episode/period frames at the grain needed to distinguish source propositions across the narrative.

Retain or reconstruct distinct source-established frames such as an attempt, helping episode, departure, waiting episode, remembered/reporting episode, recurring period, relationship period, future recurrence, comparison episode, present reflection/telling, or other materially distinct frame.

A TIME frame may be nested inside a broader episode when the source clearly stages a different local event phase. Do not inventory tense, every duration/frequency word, or every sequencing adverb as TIME.

## 5. PERSON

Retain `B` plus every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference, including minor, relational, offscreen, remembered, reported, prospective, and institutional actors when the source establishes them.

Suppress only true aliases and nonreferential/rhetorical addressees.

## 6. OBJECT

OBJECT is a source-established concrete or abstract referent with identity as a thing/content in the source.

Retain physical things, products, documents, parts, amounts/values, decisions, plans, relationships, recurring situations, comparison vehicles, and explicitly reified mental/content objects when the source itself treats them as referents.

**Do not manufacture OBJECTs by nominalizing an event, clause, proposition, intention, question, or relation merely because the phrase can be discussed.** If a clause's research work is already carried by its participant/relation/value/context coordinates, do not add a duplicate proposition-shell OBJECT.

A noun phrase appearing only as grammatical support is not automatically an OBJECT. Conversely, a low-salience concrete/source-established referent remains eligible even if mentioned once.

## 7. LABEL

LABEL is an explicitly staged source value, state, classification, evaluation, comparison, manner, correction/rejection, or status.

Retain short source-native state/value wording when it is directly predicated, questioned, negated, contrasted, evaluated, or otherwise staged as a value in the source. One-use and low-salience states may qualify.

Do not invent a LABEL from every adjective or modifier. Do not merge question/negation/correction into a normalized positive label. Preserve exact posture when it belongs to the state.

## 8. VERB

VERB is a source-established content-bearing lexical relation or happening.

Retain lexical predicates that establish a represented relation/event edge between source coordinates, even when ordinary, one-use, reporting, cognition, asking/telling/saying, meaning, locating, helping, seeing, trying, deciding, waiting, or moving language is involved.

Split separately predicated lexical relations to the smallest complete relation grain after admission.

Suppress only genuine function/support material: pure auxiliaries, do-support, aspect/support verbs, empty copulas, and reporting/metadiscourse that serves only to organize the current telling and does not itself constitute a represented relation.

A copular form may qualify when it carries a real locative, identity, modal/obligation, or state relation not already fully represented by another frozen primitive. Preserve particles, negation, reflexives, and required complements when they define relation identity.

## 9. LOCATOR

LOCATOR is explicit source-native orientation attached to a represented coordinate/relation: position, containment, origin/destination, direction, path, proximity/distance, accompaniment, carrying, temporal/contextual orientation, or represented figurative/comparative orientation.

A locator does **not** need to remain interesting after the broad PLACE/TIME is known. Retain it when the source phrase materially orients a local proposition and removing it would discard represented where/whither/relative-position information.

Do not inventory every preposition or discourse connective. Keep the smallest complete source phrase that performs the orientation job.

## 10. Cross-class overlap

The same source wording may project into more than one class only when it performs distinct research jobs in the source proposition map. Do not create a class shadow automatically from part of speech or surface spatial/temporal wording.

## 11. Literal preservation

Every source-derived non-null field must preserve source language exactly where the schema requires source text. Do not repair grammar, normalize dialect, substitute synonyms, change numbers, expand contractions, or clean punctuation.

Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME support frames may use `source_wording: null`; their `source_cue` must remain exact source text.

## 12. Order, qualities, coreference

Order by first source establishment after true coreference, subject to speaker-first PERSON and broad-before-contained setting navigation where required by the source structure.

`qualities_available` is boolean only and does not itself force a LABEL.

Do not split true coreferents into new primitives merely because their wording changes.

## 13. Compound reconstruction — proposition anchored

After primitive freeze, return canonical compounds from the Pass-A local proposition map.

For each materially represented local proposition/state/orientation:

- include the frozen relation/value/orientation that anchors it;
- include participating PERSON/OBJECT coordinates;
- include the PLACE/TIME/LOCATOR coordinates that materially situate that proposition;
- include multiple lexical relations only when the source presents them as one inseparable local event/course;
- otherwise split separately predicated relations into separate compounds.

Do not emit pairwise closure, subset variants, alternate tokenizations, whole-scene mega-bundles, duplicate compounds, or compounds built from primitives that do not belong to the same local source proposition.

## 14. Final audit

Before returning, ask:

1. Did every materially distinct local source proposition retain its participant/relation/context topology?
2. Did ordinary but content-bearing lexical relations disappear because they seemed unimportant?
3. Did I create proposition-shell OBJECTs or class shadows that the source never established as referents?
4. Did distinct interaction/event phases lose necessary PLACE/TIME support frames?
5. Did explicit source-staged values/states or orientations get suppressed merely because they were low-salience?
6. Are all source strings literal and all true coreference preserved?
7. Are compounds canonical local proposition bindings from frozen primitives only?

Never target hidden counts and never infer hidden gold.
