## Prospective correction basis

Preserved V91 execution `35428079437-A1` verified the immutable Leah-approved Case 2 and Case 6 SHA-256 archetype gates and passed the deterministic apparatus tests before calibration. Its first completed semantic archetype showed broad under-resolution: V91's requirement that a primitive be durable, reusable, revisited, or useful beyond one local proposition caused materially represented class-native traces to be suppressed across several classes, with corresponding compound loss. A separate Case 6 attempt failed at transport/session waiting because a transient connection reset escaped the recovery wrapper; that runtime defect is corrected separately and is not worker-semantic evidence.

V92 therefore makes one generalizable semantic correction while retaining the opposite boundary learned from V90's overproduction:

> **MATERIAL SOURCE-TRACE ENTITLEMENT, THEN CLASS-NATIVE GRAIN. A primitive does not need recurrence, reuse, multi-binding durability, or repeated mention. Retain a materially represented trace when it performs a distinct research job in the requested class at source-native grain. Omit it only when its proposed class job is merely grammar, a true alias/coreferent repeat, unsupported analyst abstraction, a fragment with no class-native identity, or a redundant class shadow that adds no distinct research function.**

The Researcher Inventory is a lightweight source-reconnection index. It is broader than a minimal ontology and narrower than a token/POS census. One-use source material may be a legitimate coordinate. Source presence alone is still not sufficient: the trace must do a real class-native job.

V92 does not expose approved archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored answers, or sealed holdout content/output to the worker. Every predecessor contract, run, output, failure, and training record remains immutable historical evidence for the execution it governed.

## Mission

Read the complete source and recover materially represented source-native research coordinates in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Preserve literal source language and posture. Freeze primitives before compounds. Then reconstruct source-local proposition/event/state/question/comparison bindings from the frozen coordinates.

Do not perform APA scoring, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA Data Fabric mutation.

## 1. Construction order

### Pass A — whole-source trace ledger

Read the entire represented source before extracting any class. Silently account for every materially represented:

- setting or occurrence-position;
- episode, event phase, period, recurrence, remembered/report frame, present frame, or prospective frame;
- human/social actor;
- concrete or source-reified referent/content handle;
- characterization, status, manner, comparison, candidate label, rejection, or correction;
- action, relation, state, stance, cognition, report, intention, question, negation, or modality;
- spatial, path, containment, situational, comparative, internal, or recurring-context orientation.

Include actual, remembered, reported, relational, recurring, reflective, intended, questioned, hypothetical, negated, corrected, figurative, and prospective material when materially represented.

The ledger is an omission-control device. It does not automatically create primitives.

### Pass B — material class-job entitlement

For each proposed primitive ask:

1. **Source identity:** is this trace materially represented in the source rather than supplied by analysis?
2. **Class job:** does it perform a distinguishable job of this requested class at source-native grain?
3. **Nonredundancy:** after true coreference and exact repetition are resolved, would omitting this row remove a materially represented job of this class rather than only remove grammar or duplicate the same class job?

Retain when all three answers are yes.

**Do not require** that the trace:

- recur;
- be revisited;
- participate in multiple compounds;
- persist across scenes;
- be a broad scene anchor;
- remain useful outside the single proposition in which it occurs.

A one-use trace can be a correct primitive when it independently performs the requested class job.

### Pass C — census and shadow suppression

Suppress only when the proposed primitive is:

- a true alias/coreferent or repeated mention already represented by the same primitive;
- an article, tense/aspect auxiliary, do-support, punctuation, or other grammar-only material with no semantic class job;
- a token fragment that is not a complete class-native coordinate;
- an analyst-created abstraction or inferred fact absent from the source;
- a generic discourse wrapper whose only function is to point to more specific source content;
- a duplicate class projection whose supposed second job disappears once the primary coordinate is known;
- a mechanically different tokenization of the same class-native trace.

Do **not** suppress merely because the trace appears once, is low-salience, is nested inside a larger clause, or is also represented in another class or compound.

### Pass D — class-native grain

For each entitled primitive use the smallest complete source-native wording that preserves that class job.

Do not absorb separable subjects, objects, places, times, labels, locators, or neighboring predicates merely to make a complete sentence. Conversely, retain particles, complements, negation, modality, comparison, or other wording when removing it changes the coordinate's identity.

Different classes may legitimately project overlapping literal wording when each projection performs a distinct job.

### Pass E — literal lock, coreference, and order

Resolve true aliases/coreference. Preserve source language, dialect, uncertainty, negation, modality, comparison, numbers, contractions, and punctuation. Order by first material source establishment subject to speaker-first PERSON and broad-before-contained setting navigation where applicable.

### Pass F — primitive freeze

Freeze the complete primitive inventory before compounds. Compounds may not invent missing primitives, erase entitled primitives, or retroactively justify unsupported ones.

### Pass G — local proposition reconstruction

Re-read the source and create the smallest complete source-local binding for each materially distinct proposition, event, state, report, intention, question, comparison, characterization, or orientation relation that the frozen coordinate map needs to reconnect.

## 2. Cross-class overlap

Cross-class overlap is allowed when the same source wording independently performs two different class jobs.

Do not force one semantic home merely to reduce row count. Do not create overlap merely because grammar permits two labels. Apply the material class-job test independently to each class.

Examples of permissible **forms**, not case answers:

- a movement construction can be a VERB relation and a LOCATOR/path orientation;
- a source-staged position can be PLACE and a characterization if the source actually uses both jobs;
- a state expression can be LABEL and part of a VERB relation when each remains independently selectable;
- a recurring context can supply TIME and LOCATOR functions.

## 3. PLACE — represented sites and occurrence-positions

PLACE is a source-relevant site or occurrence-position used to locate materially represented content.

Retain, when materially represented and distinguishable:

- broad and contained settings;
- object positions when the source materially stages where an object is;
- origins or destinations materially implicated by movement;
- positions of distinct waits, encounters, conversations, reports, or remembered scenes;
- present-telling/reflection position when it is a separate represented occurrence-position;
- unnamed support positions needed to preserve a distinct occurrence even when the exact physical place is unstated.

For an unnamed required position use `source_wording: null` and anchor with an exact source cue. The researcher tag may neutrally identify the occurrence-position without inventing facts.

Do not turn every physical noun, surface, body part, path word, or figurative expression into PLACE. The row must represent a location/position job, not mere spatial vocabulary.

## 4. TIME — materially distinct frames and event phases

TIME is a represented when-frame useful for reconnecting the source. It may be an episode or a materially distinct event phase even without clock/date wording.

Retain, when materially represented and distinct:

- an initial condition/attempt;
- a response/help episode;
- a departure/transition phase;
- a wait;
- an intended activity period;
- a later conversation/report;
- a recurring span or relationship period;
- a remembered/comparison frame;
- present reflection;
- a separately represented prospective/future recurring frame.

Several local relations may share one TIME. Split only when the source materially changes the represented phase/frame.

Do not create TIME merely from tense, every clause, every action, a duration question that only asks about an existing frame, or a broad container episode that adds no distinct temporal organization beyond its admitted subframes.

Unnamed frames may use `source_wording: null` with an exact cue.

## 5. PERSON — represented actors

Retain `B` plus every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when the actor materially participates in a direct, relational, possessive, remembered, reported, offscreen, prospective, or institutional role.

Low salience or one-use mention does not defeat a real represented actor. Suppress only true aliases/coreferent repeats and nonreferential/rhetorical addressees.

## 6. OBJECT — independently represented referents/content handles

OBJECT is a source-treated concrete or abstract thing with a distinct referential job.

Retain, as applicable:

- physical things and materially distinguished wholes/parts;
- products, documents, services, results, orders, amounts, values, sets, categories;
- choices, decisions, next steps, plans, alternatives, options;
- stable relations/practices/relationships treated as things the source talks about;
- explicitly reified situations/events/content;
- internal represented objects or containers when explicitly source-reified;
- figurative objects when the source gives them referential identity.

A one-use referent may qualify. Do not require persistence or repeated mention.

Do not nominalize every clause or noun phrase. Do not duplicate a PLACE whose only job is location or a LABEL whose only job is characterization. Generic wrappers are omitted when they add no referential job beyond the specific content they point to.

## 7. LABEL — source-applied characterizations

LABEL is an independently selectable source-applied characterization, state, status, manner, comparison, identity term, candidate label, rejection, or correction.

A characterization may qualify even when it occurs once or describes one local proposition. Durability/reuse is not required.

Retain the shortest complete exact wording that carries the characterization's posture. Preserve negation, uncertainty, question form, rejection, correction, intensity, comparison, and dialect when they are part of identity.

Do not split a characterization into separate rows merely because it contains an intensifier or modifier. Do not create LABEL from every adjective/adverb when the wording has no distinguishable characterization job.

`qualities_available` does not replace an entitled LABEL and does not create one by itself.

## 8. VERB — source-native relation increments

VERB inventories materially represented predicate/relation increments, not every grammatical verb token and not only broad event summaries.

Retain a source-native relation when it expresses a distinguishable action, state, stance, cognition, report, intention, question, modality, placement/existence relation, or happening. One-use relations qualify.

Semantically active matrix/support/copular/control predicates may qualify when they contribute a distinct relation such as attempt, intention, perception, cognition, report, obligation, location, or stance. Embedded predicates may also qualify when they express a different relation. Do not eliminate one merely because both occur inside one clause.

### Verb grain

Use the smallest complete lexical predicate construction that preserves the relation.

- keep particles and required complements when needed for identity;
- keep negation/modal posture when it changes the represented relation;
- split coordinated or nested predicates when each performs a distinct relation job;
- keep one multiword predicate when splitting would create grammar debris or destroy one relation;
- suppress pure auxiliary/support material that contributes no semantic relation of its own.

Do not inflate the predicate into the entire clause merely to include arguments already represented elsewhere.

## 9. LOCATOR — represented orientation/context relations

LOCATOR is a materially represented orientation relation: where, which position, path, origin/destination, containment, internal orientation, comparison, or situational context.

Retain, when it has a distinct orientation job:

- broad and contained setting relations;
- position/proximity;
- movement/path, entry/exit, toward/away constructions;
- origin/destination;
- accompaniment/carrying when orientational;
- containment;
- recurring/situational context;
- internal/mental orientation explicitly represented by the source;
- figurative/comparative orientation when the source uses it as locating structure.

A LOCATOR can occur once. It can contain verbal material when the complete construction is what supplies the orientation. It may overlap a VERB or PLACE when each performs a different job.

Suppress routine recipient/topic/argument marking and phrase-internal prepositions that provide no independent locating/context relation.

## 10. Literal-language lock

Every source-derived non-null `source_wording`, `source_cue`, `order_cue`, source-near tag, and researcher bundle must preserve the source's actual language where the schema expects source text.

Do not repair grammar, normalize dialect, substitute synonyms, alter numbers, expand contractions, or clean punctuation.

Only genuinely unnamed structural PLACE/TIME support coordinates may use `source_wording: null`, anchored by exact source text.

`researcher_note` should normally be null or minimal mechanical/coreference/unnamed-coordinate bookkeeping. Do not use notes for interpretation or evaluator-facing argument.

## 11. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean indicating that source-present qualitative/descriptive material is available around the coordinate under the schema. It is not confidence and does not create or suppress primitives.

## 12. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds, verify:

1. every materially represented source trace was considered in every plausible requested class;
2. each retained row has source identity, a distinguishable class job, and is not merely a duplicate/grammar artifact;
3. no row was suppressed solely because it is one-use, low-salience, or represented inside a compound;
4. no row was added solely because a token can be grammatically classified;
5. literal wording and source posture are exact;
6. true aliases/coreferents are merged;
7. cross-class overlaps are retained only when each class job is real;
8. PLACE/TIME support coordinates preserve distinct source organization without broad-frame duplication;
9. VERB and LOCATOR preserve source-native relation/orientation grain without token/preposition census.

## 13. Compound reconstruction

Compounds reconnect frozen primitives into materially distinct local propositions/events/states/reports/intentions/questions/comparisons/characterizations/orientations.

For each local binding:

- include the frozen participants/referents materially belonging to that relation;
- include applicable PLACE/TIME/LOCATOR/LABEL/VERB coordinates;
- preserve source-local semantic order;
- set Q from source-present qualitative availability under the schema.

Prefer proposition-level coverage over a few broad scene summaries. Split materially distinct propositions. Keep pieces together when they jointly express one source-native proposition.

Do not create arbitrary pairwise graph closure, every subset/superset permutation, duplicate mega-bundles, or compounds for rejected coordinates. A compound may not invent a primitive.

Before returning, verify:

2. material source-trace entitlement applied without durability/reuse prerequisite;
