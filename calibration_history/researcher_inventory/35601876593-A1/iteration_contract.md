## Prospective correction basis

V138 verified both immutable Leah-approved archetype workbook hashes and passed all retained deterministic apparatus/harness tests, then failed hidden semantic calibration on both approved archetypes. The V138 representation-ledger architecture improved global coherence in principle but made **materially represented assertions/configurations** the precursor population for extraction. That population is broader than the lightweight Researcher Inventory. A source can represent many true, complete, distinct propositions that do not each deserve a worksheet coordinate, while also implying unnamed structural PLACE/TIME coordinates that do deserve one.

The demonstrated defect is durable semantic selection, not the deterministic harness. V139 therefore keeps whole-source understanding but replaces exhaustive assertion/configuration ledgering with a **researcher-coordinate backbone**. The worker must identify the source-native handles a researcher would independently track at lightweight worksheet grain, including lawful unnamed structural positions/frames, without turning every represented proposition, clause, modifier, spatial phrase, temporal phrase, or predicate into inventory.

No gold rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, scored predecessor outputs, canonical archetype extracts, case-specific hidden corrections/examples, or sealed holdout content are supplied to the worker.

## Controlling rule

> **READ THE WHOLE SOURCE BEFORE EXTRACTION. BUILD ONE INTERNAL RESEARCHER-COORDINATE BACKBONE, NOT A SENTENCE, CLAUSE, TOKEN, OR PROPOSITION LEDGER. KEEP ONLY SOURCE-STAGED HANDLES THAT ARE INDEPENDENTLY ADDRESSABLE AT LIGHTWEIGHT RESEARCH WORKSHEET GRAIN: STRUCTURAL POSITIONS, STRUCTURAL FRAMES, STABLE ACTORS, SOURCE-TREATED THINGS/CONTENTS, SOURCE-APPLIED CHARACTERIZATIONS, OPERATIVE RELATION KERNELS, AND ORIENTATION/CONTEXT RELATIONS. A REPRESENTED ASSERTION DOES NOT CREATE A ROW MERELY BECAUSE IT IS TRUE OR COMPLETE. A WORD OR PHRASE DOES NOT CREATE A ROW MERELY BECAUSE IT LOOKS SPATIAL, TEMPORAL, DESCRIPTIVE, NOUN-LIKE, OR VERB-LIKE. UNNAMED PLACE/TIME MAY STILL BE REQUIRED WHEN THE SOURCE STRUCTURALLY STAGES A DISTINCT POSITION OR FRAME. PRESERVE EXACT SOURCE WORDING AND POSTURE. FREEZE PRIMITIVES, THEN BUILD COMPOUNDS ONLY FOR RESEARCH-SIGNIFICANT BINDINGS AMONG FROZEN COORDINATES, NOT FOR EVERY SOURCE PROPOSITION.**

## Mission and primitive classes

Produce only a literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate from the supplied source.

Primitive classes are exactly:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The inventory is richer than a sparse semantic summary and narrower than a lexical, grammatical, dependency, event, or proposition census.

## 1. Construction order — researcher-coordinate backbone first

1. Read the complete source once without extracting primitives.
2. Resolve only strict coreference and source order.
3. Build one internal **researcher-coordinate backbone** from the source's represented structure. Track potential structural positions, structural frames, stable actors/endpoints, things/content referents, characterizations, operative relations, and orientation/context relations.
4. Do **not** create one backbone item per sentence, clause, assertion, event mention, predicate, modifier, or parseable relation. The backbone is a map of candidate research handles, not a transcription of everything represented.
5. Resolve class-native role and natural identity across the whole source before deciding membership.
6. Apply the universal admission test and the class-specific rules below.
7. Run cross-class typing/non-substitution and same-class identity/deduplication.
8. Run the structural floor audit and anti-census ceiling audit.
9. Freeze primitive membership and exact source strings.
10. Build compounds only from frozen primitives and only for research-significant source bindings among them.
11. Run final literal, posture, typing, compound, and isolation audits.

## 2. Universal admission test

A primitive survives only when all applicable parts pass.

### 2.1 Source establishment

The source itself establishes the coordinate, or for lawful unnamed PLACE/TIME the source clearly stages the structural position/frame.

### 2.2 Class-native role

The candidate performs the native role of exactly the class being considered: site/position, frame, actor/endpoint, thing/content referent, characterization, operative relation, or orientation/context relation.

### 2.3 Researcher addressability

At lightweight worksheet grain, a researcher can independently point to, track, code, compare, or reconnect this coordinate as its own source-staged handle. One-use and low-salience coordinates may qualify. Repetition or global centrality is not required.

A candidate fails this test when its only reason to exist is that the source contains a grammatical clause, a complete proposition, an explicit noun/adjective/verb/preposition, or a phrase that can be re-parsed as another class.

### 2.4 Natural identity and span

Use the smallest complete source-native unit that preserves the represented role. Merge only true same-class same-identity coreference/repetition. Association, co-location, part/whole, container/content, actor/role, episode/subepisode, or semantic relatedness is not identity.

For overlapping wording, type the exact role carried by the exact span. A noun-like content referent and a larger orientation relation may both survive only when the source independently stages both roles. Do not create cross-class twins from the same wording merely because multiple grammatical descriptions are possible.

## 3. Anti-census invariant

The following facts are never sufficient by themselves for admission:

- a sentence or clause exists;
- a proposition is materially represented;
- a token has a part of speech;
- wording contains a spatial or temporal term;
- wording is descriptive;
- a phrase can be nominalized;
- a parser isolates a predicate/dependency;
- a relation is grammatically complete;
- a modifier, complement, support verb, copula, modal, or reporting frame is present.

Ask instead: **what independent researcher coordinate does this contribute to the case map?** If the answer is only “this is another thing the sentence says,” suppress it.

Do not compensate for census suppression by collapsing lawful unnamed structural PLACE/TIME coordinates or low-salience source-treated referents. Structural and referential roles are determined globally, not by lexical salience.

## 4. Cross-class typing and non-substitution

Type from represented research role, not surface form.

One source span may support more than one class only when the source presents genuinely different independently addressable roles at the relevant spans. Never create twins merely because grammar permits multiple descriptions.

A retained class cannot erase a separate role in another class: OBJECT does not absorb LABEL; LABEL does not absorb VERB; PLACE/TIME do not absorb LOCATOR; VERB does not absorb its actor/object merely because they participate in the relation.

When spatial-looking wording is functioning as a source-treated thing/content, it is not PLACE merely because it sounds spatial. When a larger phrase independently expresses orientation/context, that relation may be LOCATOR. Apply the same role-first principle to every class without relying on surface lexical appearance.

## 5. PLACE — structural positions

`PLACE` inventories represented **sites, occurrence positions, and participant/referent positions** that organize source material.

Retain a named or unnamed PLACE when the source structurally stages a distinct position at which a tracked occurrence happens, a participant/referent is situated, a movement/waiting episode is anchored, or a remembered/reported/prospective/present occurrence is positioned.

Rules:

- Physical naming is not required. A source-staged position may be unnamed.
- An explicit spatial noun or phrase is not enough. It must function as a structural site/position.
- Co-located positions may remain distinct when the source stages them as independently addressable positions.
- Contained and broader settings may both survive when each independently organizes source material.
- A participant/referent position may qualify even if the position is only inferable from the source-staged relation.
- The present telling/reflection position may qualify only when the source distinctly stages that present occurrence.

Do not create PLACE from surfaces, body parts, directions, figurative spatial wording, generic location words, or objects merely because they can be physically located.

For lawful unnamed PLACE use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording.

## 6. TIME — structural frames

`TIME` inventories represented **episodes, periods, phases, transitions, recurrence frames, remembered/reported frames, prospective frames, and present telling/reflection frames** that organize source material.

Retain a TIME when the source groups or positions material within a distinct independently addressable frame.

Rules:

- No date, clock, duration, frequency word, or temporal noun is required.
- Short local episodes qualify when distinctly staged as organizing frames.
- Broader and nested frames may both survive when the source genuinely organizes material at both levels.
- Intention, recurrence, remembered material, reported material, future possibility, and present reflection can establish frames without asserting realization.
- A transition may establish a new frame when the represented situation changes materially.
- An explicit temporal expression does not qualify merely because it tells when something happened; it must function as an independently useful frame at worksheet grain.

Do not create TIME from tense/aspect alone, every sentence/verb, every explicit duration/frequency phrase, or every local moment mentioned in narration.

For lawful unnamed TIME use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording.

## 7. PERSON — stable represented actors/endpoints

`PERSON` inventories `B` plus every stable represented human or social actor/group that functions as an independently addressable participant after strict coreference.

Retain actors who act, speak, perceive, are acted upon, possess/receive, serve as source/target/beneficiary/endpoint, or participate in remembered/reported/prospective relations. Minor, offscreen, brief, relational, institutional, or group actors may qualify.

Suppress rhetorical/generic addressees, generic hypothetical roles, pronouns with no stable actor, and true aliases/coreference duplicates.

Order non-speakers by first independent represented participation, not incidental possessive mention alone.

## 8. OBJECT — things and content referents

`OBJECT` inventories source-treated **things and content referents** that are independently addressable as what the source is handling, positioning, possessing, selecting, comparing, evaluating, remembering, deciding, questioning, or pointing back to.

Lawful OBJECTs include concrete items/parts/containers/artifacts/amounts/values/sets/results and abstract decisions/choices/next steps/relations-as-content/conditions/services/mental contents when the source treats them as referents.

Low salience is not decoration when the source materially handles the referent as a distinct thing/content coordinate.

Suppress:

- pure coreferential pronouns;
- clause shells created only because a proposition can be nominalized;
- metacommentary about speaking/thinking with no separately handled content referent;
- grammatical complements with no independent thing/content role;
- every noun phrase merely because it names something.

## 9. LABEL — source-applied characterizations

`LABEL` inventories independently addressable **source-applied characterizations**: states, qualities, statuses, identities, classifications, evaluations, comparisons, manners, polarities, corrections, rejections, and characterization questions.

Retain the smallest complete source-native wording that carries the characterization role. Preserve negation, question status, uncertainty, contrast, rejection, correction, and material intensity where identity-bearing.

A questioned characterization and a separately staged response/rejection may each survive when they are distinct characterization moves.

Do not create LABEL for every adjective, adverb, descriptive clause, evaluative word, quoted epithet, or comparison. The characterization itself must function as a source-applied research handle, not merely modify another coordinate in passing.

## 10. VERB — operative relation spine

`VERB` inventories source-represented **operative lexical relation kernels** that form the relation spine of the Researcher Inventory.

A relation qualifies when, at lightweight worksheet grain, it is independently useful for reconnecting retained researcher coordinates or is itself an independently addressable source-staged relation. The existence of a represented proposition or grammatical verb is not enough.

The unit is the **smallest complete lexical relation kernel that preserves one admitted relation identity**.

Rules:

- Do not harvest every grammatical verb, copula, auxiliary, speech frame, cognition frame, or clause predicate.
- Keep support, modal, aspectual, control, infinitival, particle, negation, coordinated, or embedded material together whenever removing it changes, reverses, hollows out, or fragments one admitted relation identity.
- Split matrix and embedded predicates only when each is independently admitted to the research relation spine.
- Split coordinated predicates only when the source stages distinct admitted relation jobs; otherwise retain the complete coordinated construction needed for one relation identity.
- Semantically bound multiword predicates stay together.
- Copular/modal/possessive/locative/state constructions qualify only when that relation itself is an admitted research handle; do not emit them merely because they form a complete proposition.
- Speech/cognition/reporting predicates qualify when the act of saying/thinking/reporting is itself an independently addressable relation, not merely a narrative wrapper around content.
- Questions, negations, hypotheticals, intentions, recurrence, uncertainty, and future language preserve their supplied posture.

Do not swallow independent actor/object/place/time/label/locator arguments into the VERB merely to make a clause. The kernel is relation identity, not full sentence membership.

## 11. LOCATOR — independent orientation/context relations

`LOCATOR` inventories independently useful **orientation and context relations**.

These may include physical/relative position, containment, path, direction, approach/departure, origin/destination, proximity/distance, accompaniment, embodied/internal position, recurring/situational context, and materially useful figurative orientation.

Retain a LOCATOR only when orientation/context itself is an independently addressable relation. An explicit preposition or spatial phrase is not enough.

A LOCATOR may overlap a PLACE/TIME/OBJECT/VERB only when the exact span/function contributes a separate orientation/context role. Suppress routine possession, recipient/topic marking, argument attachment, bare comparison support, isolated prepositions, and wording that merely restates another retained coordinate without adding orientation.

## 12. Literal and posture lock

Preserve source wording character-for-character wherever source text is required. Never synonymize, lemmatize, repair grammar/spelling, normalize dialect, expand contractions, translate, polish, diagnose, or substitute a standard expression.

`source_wording` must be exact source text when explicitly worded. `source_cue` and `order_cue` must be exact source text. Only lawful unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinates may use null `source_wording`.

Questions, uncertainty, hypotheticals, negations, self-corrections, remembered possibilities, reported material, and proposed future actions remain in their source posture. Inventorying does not assert truth or realization.

## 13. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is only a mechanical boolean. True means the source supplies material qualities/descriptions associated with the coordinate; false otherwise. Do not create/split/interpret qualities to justify it. `Q` is never a primitive.

## 14. Ordering

Default within-class order is first material source anchor after filtering/coreference.

Tie rules:

- broad/whole setting before dependent/contained setting when introduced together;
- whole before dependent part at the same anchor unless the source stages the part first;
- PERSON follows participation order;
- remembered/reported/hypothetical/future frames remain at source position rather than external chronology.

The apparatus owns canonical IDs and deterministic numbering.

## 15. Structural floor audit

Replay the whole source using the frozen researcher-coordinate backbone, not a fresh word scan. Ask whether the inventory would let a researcher reconnect the source's meaningful structural skeleton without substituting a summary.

Specifically check for under-resolution of:

- unnamed occurrence/participant/referent positions;
- broad and local episodes, recurrence, remembered/reported/prospective frames, transitions, and present-reflection frames;
- minor but stable actors/endpoints;
- low-salience concrete referents that are independently handled;
- abstract choices/decisions/contents/relations treated as things;
- questioned/rejected/corrected source-applied characterizations;
- admitted relation kernels whose source-native wording must remain complete;
- independently useful physical/figurative/context orientation relations.

If a distinct research role is missing, add the primitive in the correct class. Do not widen a neighboring primitive to compensate.

## 16. Anti-census ceiling audit

For every retained primitive ask:

1. What independent researcher role does this coordinate serve?
2. What exact source material establishes that role?
3. Is the reason for retaining it its represented research function rather than its lexical or grammatical form?
4. Could a researcher point to or reconnect it independently at lightweight worksheet grain?
5. Is this the smallest complete natural source-native identity for that role?
6. Is it distinct from same-class identities already retained?
7. Did it enter only because I enumerated all assertions, clauses, predicates, nouns, modifiers, spatial phrases, or temporal phrases?

If the answer to question 7 is yes and no separate research role survives, remove it.

Do not aim for a target count or density. Correct toward source-native research coordinates.

## 17. Compound construction — research-significant bindings

Freeze primitives before compound work.

A compound is a **selective packet of frozen coordinates whose source binding is itself useful for reconnecting the case**. Compound construction follows the admitted research relation/configuration spine, not the set of all represented propositions.

Create a compound when:

1. the source binds two or more frozen primitives in a materially distinct relation or configuration;
2. that binding is useful to preserve as a research packet rather than merely recoverable as sentence membership;
3. the packet uses only primitives actually active in that relation/configuration;
4. it is not a duplicate/subset view, alternate parse, dependency closure, or scene-wide bundle.

Where an admitted VERB materially connects retained coordinates, normally preserve that binding unless doing so would merely duplicate another compound or add no independent relation/configuration. Nonverbal compounds are allowed for independently significant characterizations/configurations.

Include PLACE/TIME anchors only when they actively organize the binding. Include LABEL/LOCATOR only when active in that binding. Do not create a compound merely to justify a primitive or repair a missing one; return to primitive review first.

Do not create one compound for every sentence, clause, grammatical verb, parser dependency, pairwise relation, or transitive closure.

Canonical compound IDs/order and `Q` construction remain apparatus-owned.

## 18. Final contrast audit

### Too dense / census failure

Warning signs: explicit spatial wording typed PLACE without structural-site function; every local moment typed TIME; every noun phrase typed OBJECT; every description typed LABEL; every grammatical/copular/support/reporting predicate typed VERB; every prepositional/spatial phrase typed LOCATOR; compounds tracking most clauses or propositions.

### Too sparse / summary failure

Warning signs: missing unnamed positions; missing local or broad structural frames; missing minor actors; missing low-salience but independently handled referents; missing source-applied question/rejection/correction labels; fragmented or omitted admitted relations; missing orientation/context relations; collapsing distinct research handles merely because they share a scene.

Correct toward the researcher-coordinate backbone, not toward target density.
