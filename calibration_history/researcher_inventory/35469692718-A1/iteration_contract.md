# APA Researcher Inventory Agent Contract V99

Status: `ACTIVE SUCCESSOR FOR CALIBRATION`  
Contract version: `RI-CONTRACT-V99`  
Predecessor: `RI-CONTRACT-V98`  
Effective date: 2026-09-19  
Authority: Leah's standing Researcher Inventory calibration instruction.

## Prospective correction basis

V98 retained source-native semantic grouping and added bidirectional binding completeness. Its paired Case 2 + Case 6 execution nevertheless demonstrated a generalizable admission-layer instability: broad completeness pressure can re-expand toward lexical/grammatical role census while still missing legitimate source-organizing support coordinates. Immutable workbook verification and all deterministic harness tests passed before semantic mismatch, so this remains a worker-semantic defect rather than a harness defect.

V99 retains V97/V98 semantic grouping, class-native entitlement, literal preservation, material-binding reconstruction, append-only history, holdout isolation, and clean-room certification controls. It replaces undifferentiated completeness pressure with one controlling rule:

> **EDITORIAL ADMISSION PROOF. Every primitive must enter through exactly one semantic admission lane: BINDING_CORE, QUALITATIVE_SUPPORT, or IMPLICIT_SUPPORT. A primitive that fits none is excluded. Binding completeness is judged at this editorial layer, not by lexical, grammatical, phrase, clause, mention, or role census.**

This is prospective only. V98 and every earlier contract/run remain immutable historical evidence. Nothing in V99 exposes archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, calibration answers, or sealed holdout material to the worker.

## Mission

Read the complete source and recover source-native research coordinates in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Preserve literal source language and posture. Reconstruct the source's materially distinct proposition/event/state/report/intention/question/comparison/characterization/orientation bindings. Freeze a complete but non-fragmented primitive inventory. Then construct the smallest canonical set of source-local compounds from those frozen coordinates.

Do not perform APA scoring, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA Data Fabric mutation.

## 1. Whole-source semantic map

Read the entire source before extracting primitives. Silently map materially distinct represented bindings: propositions, events, states, reports, intentions, questions, comparisons, characterizations, and orientation relations.

For each binding identify only semantically represented roles, not grammar. Typical roles may include actor, relation, target/content, characterization, occurrence support, episode support, orientation, and question/uncertainty posture.

A semantic map is never a token, POS, noun-phrase, clause, verb, preposition, adverb, spatial-mention, temporal-mention, or modifier census.

## 2. Three admission lanes

Every proposed primitive must have one and only one admission proof.

### 2.1 `BINDING_CORE`

Admit when the coordinate is needed to reconstruct a materially represented binding in its class-native role. Removing it would make that binding unreconstructable, collapse two distinct represented roles, lose a source-present relation/posture, or force an analyst-created substitute.

Examples of role kinds, not answer templates: represented actor, semantic relation, target/content handle, characterization, occurrence place, episode/time, or orientation.

### 2.2 `QUALITATIVE_SUPPORT`

Admit when the source independently represents a stable referent, characterization, scene/episode detail, object/content handle, or orientation that materially qualifies a represented binding or scene even when it does not need to appear as an explicit compound reference.

A qualitative-support primitive must still be independently reconnectable and source-native. It is not licensed merely because a word is vivid, concrete, descriptive, repeated, grammatically separable, or useful to an analyst.

`QUALITATIVE_SUPPORT` is the lane for source-present qualitative/detail structure that may remain available through the schema's qualitative signal without forcing compound proliferation.

### 2.3 `IMPLICIT_SUPPORT`

Admit only for `PLACE` or `TIME` when a materially distinct occurrence, conversation, wait, memory, reflection, report, performance, or episode has a distinct support slot but the exact place/time is not specified in the source.

For an implicit support coordinate:

- `source_wording` may be null when the schema permits it;
- preserve an exact source cue showing the supported occurrence/episode;
- use a neutral mechanical short tag that states only the support function;
- do not invent a location, date, chronology, actor, or substantive fact absent from the source.

Broad scene/episode support and local implicit support may both exist only when they organize materially distinct represented occurrences or phases.

## 3. Universal exclusion proof

Exclude a proposed primitive when its only basis is any of the following:

- lexical occurrence;
- grammatical token or POS identity;
- auxiliary/copular/do-support/infinitival support without a separate semantic relation;
- preposition, particle, adverb, article, determiner, intensifier, or discourse marker without a distinct class-native job;
- generic pronoun or deixis that merely points to an already represented referent;
- clause complement or noun phrase merely because it can be segmented;
- physical surface/body part/container/path/spatial phrase merely because it is spatial;
- tense/duration/transition phrase merely because it is temporal;
- rhetorical scaffolding or comparison scaffolding without its own represented characterization/orientation job;
- duplicate mention, true alias, coreferent restatement, or paraphrastic restatement of the same semantic coordinate;
- analyst-created abstraction not represented by the source.

A real local, one-use, low-salience, unnamed, nested, or endpoint-dependent coordinate is not excluded merely because of those properties. It must still pass one admission lane.

## 4. Semantic identity and deduplication

Primitive identity follows semantic identity, not mention count.

Create one primitive for the same stable represented actor, referent, characterization, support coordinate, or orientation when later mentions are true coreference/restatement.

Create separate relation primitives with identical or similar wording only when the source represents materially distinct relation occurrences or bindings and each is independently reconnectable by its own actor/target/time/episode context.

Do not collapse genuinely distinct occurrences merely because the same word is repeated. Do not multiply one coordinate merely because it is mentioned repeatedly.

## 5. Source-native grain

For each admitted coordinate select the smallest **complete semantic unit** that performs its class job, never the smallest lexical/syntactic fragment.

A split is valid only when every resulting row has its own independently reconnectable semantic job. If pieces only jointly express one relation, orientation, characterization, referent, or support coordinate, keep them grouped.

Leave separable arguments outside a VERB. Leave separable orientation to LOCATOR only when it carries information distinct from the VERB relation identity. Cross-class overlap is allowed only when the same literal trace genuinely preserves two different class-native jobs.

## 6. Literal lock

After selection, source-derived wording is literal-locked.

Preserve source dialect, contractions, numbers, uncertainty, negation, modality, comparison, punctuation, and source posture. Do not normalize, improve, clean, translate, diagnose, euphemize, or replace wording with a preferred synonym.

Use source-near short tags where a stable source phrase exists. Neutral mechanical tags are reserved for legitimately implicit support coordinates whose exact place/time is unspecified.

## 7. Class-native rules

### PLACE

Recover geography that organizes materially represented occurrences. Admit explicit named/contained settings, materially distinct local occurrence positions, and `IMPLICIT_SUPPORT` places for distinct occurrences whose exact place is not supplied.

Do not inventory every spatial phrase, surface, body part, container, object position, or physical noun. A broad setting does not erase a genuinely distinct local occurrence support, but locality alone does not create a PLACE.

### TIME

Recover chronology/episode support that organizes materially distinct represented material. Admit explicit periods/phases, distinct waits/transitions/remembered or prospective episodes, present telling/reflection when materially represented, and `IMPLICIT_SUPPORT` temporal frames when exact timing is not supplied but episode identity is distinct.

Do not inventory every tense, duration word, transition, `now/when/while/since`, or temporally worded clause.

### PERSON

Retain every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when it occupies a material actor role. Minor, one-use, offscreen, remembered, reported, relational, possessive, prospective, or institutional actors may qualify.

Suppress rhetorical/nonreferential addressees and true aliases/coreferent repeats.

### OBJECT

Retain source-treated concrete or abstract referents/content handles that are either `BINDING_CORE` or independently source-native `QUALITATIVE_SUPPORT`.

One-use and abstract objects may qualify. Do not create OBJECT rows merely from every noun phrase, pronoun, deictic, clause complement, action nominalization, or semantic trace already fully carried by another class.

Generic forms such as `it`, `this`, `something`, `anything`, and similar wording qualify only when that occurrence itself is reified as a distinct source referential handle rather than grammatical anaphora.

### LABEL

Retain represented characterizations, candidate characterizations, identity/status terms, states/manners, comparisons, acceptances/rejections/corrections, and polarity units when they are `BINDING_CORE` or independently source-native `QUALITATIVE_SUPPORT`.

Select complete characterization units. Do not inventory every adjective, adverb, intensifier, decorative phrase, or rhetorical flourish.

### VERB

VERB inventories materially represented semantic predicate/relation units, not verb tokens.

Use one complete semantic relation per independently represented relation job. Keep necessary auxiliaries, negation, modality, particles, light-verb support, infinitival/complement material, or comparison material with the relation when they are required for its identity/posture.

Split matrix/embedded/coordinated material only when each side is a distinct independently reconnectable relation. Repeated relation wording may yield separate rows only for distinct source events/bindings. Suppress pure grammatical support and discourse formulas.

### LOCATOR

LOCATOR inventories materially represented orientation/topology relations: position, containment/support, origin/destination, path, approach/away/entry/exit, direction, internal/mental orientation, temporal-position orientation, and figurative/comparative orientation when it actually positions represented material.

Use one complete semantic orientation unit, not every preposition/particle/adverb. A local or endpoint-dependent orientation may qualify through `BINDING_CORE` or `QUALITATIVE_SUPPORT` when it is independently represented. Do not duplicate a VERB job unless the orientation preserves different information.

## 8. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds, audit every row and every material binding.

### Admission audit

For every primitive, record privately which lane admits it: `BINDING_CORE`, `QUALITATIVE_SUPPORT`, or `IMPLICIT_SUPPORT`. If none applies, remove it.

### Binding completeness audit

For every material binding, confirm all core class-native roles required to reconnect it are present. Restore only the missing semantic coordinate at complete-semantic-unit grain. Do not respond to a missing role by enumerating neighboring grammar.

### Support audit

Check whether a materially distinct occurrence/episode needs an implicit PLACE/TIME support coordinate. Create one only when the source represents the distinct occurrence/episode but leaves the exact place/time unspecified.

### Dedup audit

Collapse true aliases, coreferent restatements, duplicate semantic coordinates, and paraphrastic duplicates. Preserve genuinely distinct relation occurrences.

### Fidelity audit

Confirm source wording/posture is exact for source-derived rows and that no forbidden normalization or analyst-created abstraction has entered the inventory.

Repeat until all audits pass.

## 9. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean indicating that source-present qualitative/descriptive material is available around the coordinate or compound under the schema. It is not confidence, importance, uncertainty, or admission authority.

It does not create or suppress primitives.

For compounds, set it according to actual source-present qualitative/descriptive support around that binding. A qualitative/support primitive may remain available to the binding without being forced into the compound's explicit reference list. Conversely, the presence of a LABEL or support primitive does not automatically force `qualities_available=true` unless the schema's source-present qualitative condition is actually met.

## 10. Compound reconstruction

After primitive freeze, re-read the source and create the smallest canonical set of materially distinct source-local compounds corresponding to the private binding map.

Compound references should normally use `BINDING_CORE` coordinates needed to identify/reconstruct that binding, plus only those support/qualification/orientation coordinates that materially distinguish it. `QUALITATIVE_SUPPORT` primitives do not automatically propagate into compound references; their availability may instead be reflected by the qualitative signal where appropriate.

Do not create a compound merely because a primitive exists. Do not create graph closure, pairwise combinations, lexical-fragment compounds, subset/superset permutations, redundant restatements, or mega-bundles.

Preserve source-local semantic order, uncertainty/question posture, negation, and modality.

## 11. Isolation and completion gates

Never target hidden counts or infer hidden gold. Never expose approved archetypes, evaluator findings, prior scored answers, calibration answers, or sealed holdout content/output to the worker.

Case 5 remains inaccessible until repeated Case 2 + Case 6 passes occur under this same finalized V99 contract and saved-agent lineage. A calibrated-lineage holdout attempt, if eventually authorized by the workflow gate, is one-shot. Clean-room certification requires retirement of that lineage and creation of a brand-new saved agent bootstrapped only from finalized V99 instructions, in a new session with no prior holdout output or calibration-session access.

All outputs remain candidate research only. No promotion, Oval Office admission, APA-ID minting, or APA Data Fabric writing is authorized.
