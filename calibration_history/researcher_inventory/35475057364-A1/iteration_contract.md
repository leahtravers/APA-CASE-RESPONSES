# APA Researcher Inventory Agent Contract V100

Status: `ACTIVE SUCCESSOR FOR CALIBRATION`  
Contract version: `RI-CONTRACT-V100`  
Predecessor: `RI-CONTRACT-V99`  
Effective date: 2026-09-19  
Authority: Leah's standing Researcher Inventory calibration instruction.

## Prospective correction basis

V99 introduced an editorial admission proof with binding-core, qualitative-support, and implicit-support lanes. Its paired Case 2 + Case 6 execution nevertheless demonstrated a generalizable semantic-coordination defect: separate class passes can still disagree about the source's material binding structure, broad qualitative-support language can admit incidental descriptive/rhetorical material, and a valid coordinate in one class can still be treated as though it substitutes for a distinct class-native facet in another. Immutable workbook verification and all deterministic apparatus/harness tests passed before the mismatch, so this is a worker-semantic defect rather than a harness or workbook defect.

V100 retains semantic admission, source-native grain, literal preservation, immutable-archetype isolation, append-only history, holdout isolation, and clean-room certification controls. It adds one controlling method:

> **BINDING-LEDGER PROJECTION. Read the complete source and silently construct one whole-source ledger of materially distinct represented bindings. Every class inventory is a projection of that same ledger plus only narrowly admitted independently revisitable support coordinates. A coordinate in one class never substitutes for a distinct class-native facet in another class.**

This successor is prospective only. V99 and every earlier contract/run remain immutable historical evidence. Nothing in V100 exposes archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, calibration answers, or sealed holdout material to the worker.

## Mission

Read the complete source and recover source-native research coordinates in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Preserve literal source language and posture. Recover a complete but non-fragmented primitive inventory from the source's materially distinct bindings, freeze that inventory, and then construct the smallest canonical set of compounds that reconstruct those same bindings.

Do not perform APA scoring, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA Data Fabric mutation.

## 1. Whole-source binding ledger

Before answering any requested class, read the entire source and silently build a binding ledger. Rebuild this same conceptual ledger on every class pass from the complete source; do not reason only from the local sentence containing a candidate.

A ledger entry is a materially distinct source-presented binding such as an event, state, report, intention, question, comparison, characterization, reflection, correction, prospective relation, recurring relation, or orientation relation.

A sentence, clause, noun phrase, verb token, modifier, prepositional phrase, discourse marker, or vivid detail is not automatically a binding.

For each ledger entry identify only roles actually represented by the source, as applicable:

- actor or social participant;
- semantic relation/predicate;
- concrete or abstract referent/content handle;
- characterization/state/comparison/correction;
- occurrence PLACE support;
- episode/phase TIME support;
- orientation/topology relation;
- source posture such as negation, uncertainty, question, intention, hypothetical, recurrence, memory, report, or prospectivity.

The ledger is semantic, not grammatical. Do not construct a token, POS, phrase, clause, modifier, spatial-mention, temporal-mention, noun, verb, or preposition census.

## 2. Class projection rule

Each requested class is a projection of the whole-source ledger.

For a primitive to be admitted, it must satisfy one of the following:

### 2.1 `BINDING_CORE`

The primitive performs a distinct class-native role required to reconstruct or distinguish a material ledger entry. Removing it would make a represented role disappear, collapse two materially distinct bindings, lose source posture, or force an analyst-created substitute.

### 2.2 `INDEPENDENT_SUPPORT`

The primitive is not required in the explicit core of a compound but is an independently revisitable source-native research coordinate. It may be admitted only when at least one of these semantic conditions is true:

- the source reuses or corefers to it as the same stable coordinate across represented material;
- the source explicitly contrasts it with another represented coordinate;
- the source questions, corrects, rejects, accepts, names, identifies, or explicitly characterizes it;
- it independently participates as an argument, endpoint, orientation, or support coordinate in a material binding;
- it materially distinguishes one retained binding or phase from another.

Mere vividness, concreteness, descriptiveness, narrative color, rhetorical force, lexical recurrence, grammatical separability, or possible analyst usefulness is not enough.

### 2.3 `IMPLICIT_SUPPORT`

Available only to `PLACE` and `TIME`. Admit when a materially distinct represented occurrence, conversation, wait, memory, reflection, report, performance, transition, recurring span, intention, or prospective phase requires a distinct support slot but the source does not specify the exact place/time.

For an implicit support coordinate:

- `source_wording` may be null when the schema permits it;
- `source_cue` must be exact source text anchoring the supported occurrence/phase;
- use a neutral mechanical short tag that states only the support function;
- do not invent a location, date, chronology, actor, cause, or substantive fact.

A broad setting/episode does not erase a genuinely distinct local support role. Conversely, a new clause does not create a new implicit support coordinate unless it represents a materially distinct occurrence/phase.

If a proposed primitive fits none of these three routes, exclude it.

## 3. No cross-class substitution

Classes preserve different research functions. A coordinate legitimately admitted in one class does not automatically satisfy or suppress another class's distinct role.

The same literal source trace may therefore support more than one primitive across different classes when each primitive preserves genuinely different class-native information. For example, one source phrase can contain both a semantic action relation and a directional/orientation relation; retaining one does not erase the other if both are materially represented.

Cross-class overlap is not permission to duplicate meaning mechanically. Admit both only when the class-native functions are different and independently reconnectable.

Never prune a PLACE because a LOCATOR mentions the same wording, a LOCATOR because a VERB contains the same wording, an OBJECT because a LABEL characterizes it, or a TIME because a VERB implies its phase, when the source represents distinct class-native roles.

## 4. Completeness followed by compression

Use this order, every time:

1. **Map bindings.** Build the whole-source ledger.
2. **Project roles.** Identify the requested class-native coordinates needed by the ledger.
3. **Restore support.** Add only qualified `INDEPENDENT_SUPPORT` and legitimate `IMPLICIT_SUPPORT` coordinates.
4. **Check cross-class facets.** Ensure another class has not been used as a substitute for a distinct requested-class role.
5. **Compress.** Remove lexical, grammatical, rhetorical, duplicate, and scene-color remainder that performs no admitted semantic job.
6. **Freeze.** Do not add primitives merely to make later compounds easier.

Completeness is judged against the binding ledger, not against the words of the source. Compression occurs only after binding roles have been accounted for.

## 5. Universal exclusion proof

Exclude a proposed primitive when its only basis is any of the following:

- lexical occurrence or repetition;
- token, POS, phrase, clause, noun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, or modifier identity;
- auxiliary/copular/do-support/infinitival support without its own semantic relation;
- discourse scaffolding, narrative filler, intensification, rhetorical flourish, or descriptive color without an admitted semantic role;
- generic pronoun/deixis that merely points to an already represented referent;
- a clause complement or noun phrase merely because it can be segmented;
- a body part, surface, container, path, object position, or physical noun merely because it is spatial;
- tense, duration, transition wording, or temporal connective merely because it is temporal;
- a comparison scaffold without its own represented characterization/orientation;
- a duplicate mention, true alias, coreferent restatement, or paraphrastic restatement of the same semantic coordinate;
- an analyst-created abstraction not represented by the source.

A coordinate is not excluded merely because it is local, one-use, low-salience, unnamed, remembered, offscreen, nested, endpoint-dependent, possessive, prospective, or figurative. Those properties are neutral. The coordinate must still pass an admission route.

## 6. Semantic identity and deduplication

Primitive identity follows semantic identity, not mention count.

Create one primitive for the same stable represented actor, referent, characterization, support coordinate, or orientation when later mentions are true coreference/restatement.

Create separate relation primitives with identical or similar wording only when the source represents materially distinct relation occurrences/bindings and each is reconnectable through its own source-local participants/content/support/posture.

Do not collapse distinct occurrences merely because wording repeats. Do not multiply one coordinate merely because it is mentioned repeatedly.

Deduplication is primarily within a class. Do not use deduplication as cross-class suppression.

## 7. Source-native grain and literal lock

For each admitted coordinate select the smallest complete semantic unit that performs its class job, never the smallest lexical/syntactic fragment and never an unnecessarily broad sentence-sized bundle.

A split is valid only when each resulting row has its own independently reconnectable semantic job. If words jointly express one semantic relation, characterization, orientation, referent, or support coordinate, keep the necessary package together.

For source-derived wording:

- preserve exact source language, dialect, contractions, numbers, uncertainty, negation, modality, comparison, attribution, punctuation, and posture;
- do not normalize, improve, clean, translate, diagnose, euphemize, summarize, or replace with a synonym;
- do not introduce a convenient analyst label when a source-near tag is available;
- reserve neutral mechanical tags for legitimate implicit PLACE/TIME support whose exact wording is absent.

A light verb, auxiliary, particle, complement, reflexive, negation, or modality remains with a relation when needed for that relation's semantic identity/posture. Do not split it into a separate VERB merely because grammar permits a split.

## 8. Class-native rules

### PLACE

Recover geography/support that organizes materially represented occurrences. Admit explicit broad/contained settings, materially distinct local occurrence positions, source-reified object locations when they organize a binding, and legitimate `IMPLICIT_SUPPORT` places for distinct occurrences whose exact place is not supplied.

Do not inventory every spatial phrase, surface, body part, container, physical noun, or object position. A broad setting does not erase a distinct local occurrence support, but locality alone does not create a PLACE.

### TIME

Recover chronology/episode support that organizes materially distinct represented material. Admit explicit periods/phases, distinct attempts, responses, departures/transitions, waits, later reports/conversations, recurring spans, present reflection/telling, intentions, and prospective phases when they are distinct ledger support roles. Use `IMPLICIT_SUPPORT` when exact timing is unspecified.

Do not inventory every tense, duration word, temporal connective, or clause with temporal language. A TIME coordinate marks a distinct represented phase/span, not a grammatical tense.

### PERSON

Retain every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when it occupies a material social/actor/possessor/beneficiary/endpoint role in the source. Minor, one-use, offscreen, remembered, reported, relational, possessive, prospective, or institutional actors may qualify.

Suppress rhetorical/nonreferential addressees and true aliases/coreferent repeats.

### OBJECT

Retain source-treated concrete or abstract referents/content handles that are `BINDING_CORE` or pass `INDEPENDENT_SUPPORT`.

Abstract decisions, choices, relations, internal represented objects, amounts/values, sets/categories, and other nonphysical content may qualify when the source reifies them as independently reconnectable handles. Do not create OBJECT rows merely from every noun phrase, pronoun, deictic, clause complement, action nominalization, descriptive noun, or semantic trace already lacking its own object role.

Generic forms such as `it`, `this`, `something`, `anything`, and similar wording qualify only when that occurrence itself is reified as a distinct source referential handle rather than grammatical anaphora.

### LABEL

Retain complete source-presented characterizations, candidate characterizations, identity/status terms, states/manners, comparisons, acceptances/rejections/corrections, and polarity units that are `BINDING_CORE` or pass `INDEPENDENT_SUPPORT`.

A characterization may be adjectival, nominal, adverbial, comparative, phrasal, questioned, negated, or corrected. Select the complete characterization unit. Do not inventory every modifier, intensifier, colorful descriptor, rhetorical flourish, or descriptive clause.

### VERB

VERB inventories materially represented semantic predicate/relation units, not verb tokens.

Use one complete semantic relation per independently represented relation occurrence. Keep necessary auxiliaries, negation, modality, particles, light-verb support, complements, reflexives, comparison material, or posture-bearing material with the relation when they are required for semantic identity.

Split matrix/embedded/coordinated material only when each side represents a distinct independently reconnectable relation. Do not split a light relation from the complement that gives it its material meaning. Repeated relation wording may yield separate rows only for genuinely distinct source events/bindings. Suppress pure grammatical support and discourse formulas.

### LOCATOR

LOCATOR inventories materially represented orientation/topology relations: position, containment/support, origin/destination, path, approach/away/entry/exit, accompaniment, direction, internal/mental orientation, temporal-position orientation, and figurative/comparative orientation when it actually positions represented material.

Use one complete semantic orientation unit, not every preposition/particle/adverb. A local or endpoint-dependent orientation may qualify as `BINDING_CORE` or `INDEPENDENT_SUPPORT`. A VERB and LOCATOR may share wording when the source simultaneously represents an action relation and a distinct orientation facet. Do not duplicate a VERB when there is no extra orientation information.

## 9. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds, audit the requested class against the whole-source ledger.

### Binding-role coverage audit

For every material ledger entry, ask whether the requested class has a represented role. If yes, confirm that role is retained at complete-semantic-unit grain. Restore only the missing semantic coordinate, not neighboring grammar.

### Independent-support audit

For each non-core primitive, identify the exact revisitation condition that admits it. If it is merely vivid, descriptive, concrete, rhetorical, repeated, or segmentable, remove it.

### Implicit-support audit

For PLACE/TIME, check each materially distinct occurrence/phase for an absent but required support slot. Add only neutral support coordinates anchored by exact source cues. Do not create one merely because a clause lacks an explicit place/time expression.

### Cross-class facet audit

Ask whether a coordinate was omitted only because another class carries overlapping wording. If the omitted coordinate preserves a genuinely distinct requested-class role, restore it. If it would merely duplicate meaning, keep it excluded.

### Excess audit

After coverage is complete, scan the entire proposed class and remove anything that cannot identify its ledger role or narrow independent-support basis.

### Dedup audit

Collapse true aliases, coreferent restatements, duplicate semantic coordinates, and paraphrastic duplicates within the class. Preserve genuinely distinct relation occurrences and support phases.

### Fidelity audit

Confirm exact source wording/posture for source-derived rows and remove any synonym, normalization, analyst-created abstraction, or unsupported inference.

Repeat until all audits pass.

## 10. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean indicating whether source-present qualitative/descriptive material is available around the coordinate or compound under the schema. It is not confidence, importance, uncertainty, salience, or admission authority.

It does not create or suppress primitives.

A source-present quality may set the boolean without becoming its own primitive unless it independently satisfies the LABEL or other applicable class rule. Conversely, a retained LABEL or support primitive does not automatically force the boolean to true for every neighboring coordinate.

## 11. Compound reconstruction from the ledger

After primitive freeze, reconstruct the same whole-source binding ledger using only frozen units.

Create the smallest canonical set of compounds corresponding to materially distinct ledger entries. Normally create one compound for one materially distinct binding when multiple retained coordinates are needed to reconnect that binding. A primitive may remain unbundled when it is not needed in an explicit compound.

Each compound should use only the minimal frozen coordinates needed to identify/reconstruct that binding, plus support/characterization/orientation coordinates that materially distinguish it. Do not attach every unit sharing a sentence, episode, actor, or scene.

Do not create:

- graph closure;
- pairwise combinations;
- one compound per primitive;
- lexical-fragment compounds;
- subset/superset permutations of the same binding;
- redundant restatements;
- mega-bundles spanning several ledger entries.

Preserve source-local semantic order and posture, including uncertainty, questions, negation, intention, recurrence, memory, comparison, and prospectivity.

Before return, compare compounds back to the private ledger: every materially distinct binding that needs a compound is represented once at canonical grain, and no compound exists without a ledger entry.

## 12. Isolation and completion gates

Never target hidden counts or infer hidden gold. Never expose approved archetypes, evaluator findings, prior scored answers, calibration answers, or sealed holdout content/output to the worker.

Case 5 remains inaccessible until repeated Case 2 + Case 6 passes occur under this same finalized V100 contract and saved-agent lineage. A calibrated-lineage holdout attempt, if eventually authorized by the workflow gate, is one-shot. Clean-room certification requires retirement of that lineage and creation of a brand-new saved agent bootstrapped only from finalized V100 instructions, in a new session with no prior holdout output or calibration-session access.

All outputs remain candidate research only. No promotion, Oval Office admission, APA-ID minting, or APA Data Fabric writing is authorized.
