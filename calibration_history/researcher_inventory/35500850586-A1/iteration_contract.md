## Prospective correction basis

V112 workflow run `35499571386` verified both Leah-approved immutable archetype workbooks against their required SHA-256 values without repair and passed every deterministic apparatus/harness test, then failed hidden semantic comparison on both approved archetype cases. The complete failed attempt remains preserved append-only under `calibration_history/researcher_inventory/35499571386-A1/`. The sealed holdout was not reached.

The failure is worker-semantic, not workbook-integrity or deterministic-harness behavior. V112 correctly removed V111's inventory-global economy rule, but its replacement made **class-local selectability by itself** the admission threshold. That threshold is too permissive: language can be classifiable as a place, time, object, label, verb, or locator without constituting a distinct represented research coordinate. The result was systematic census-like over-admission, type drift, and compound proliferation across both calibration archetypes.

The generalizable V113 correction is a **class-local representation-bearing coordinate threshold**. Cross-class overlap remains allowed and another class or future compound is never by itself a reason to omit a valid class-native projection. But a candidate must do more than fit a class definition: it must establish a stable, source-grounded coordinate in the represented situation that a researcher could independently point to inside that class. Grammar, narration carriage, incidental wording, scene color, and semantically classifiable fragments do not become primitives merely because they can be named.

V113 therefore retains V112's orthogonal seven-class architecture, exact-source literal lock, functional typing, same-class deduplication, candidate isolation, and relation-instance compound rule, while replacing the two-gate primitive test with a three-gate test: **exact source identity + representation-bearing coordinate + class-local distinctness**.

No archetype rows, hidden counts, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, calibration answers, or sealed holdout material are supplied to the worker.

## Mission

Read the complete source and recover a **lightweight, source-faithful researcher inventory** in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The inventory captures represented research coordinates, not every lexical item that could be classified. It is not a token census, parse tree, semantic graph, summary, or list of all nouns, verbs, modifiers, prepositional phrases, scenes, or clauses.

Do not perform APA scoring, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA Data Fabric/database mutation.

## 1. Whole-source map before extraction

Read the whole source before extracting any class. Silently map the represented situation: differentiated scenes and episodes, participants, stable referential content, acted-on or contemplated things, represented characterizations/states, relation instances, orientations, memories, reports, questions, intentions, alternatives, recurring relations, prospective situations, and present reflection.

This map is not itself the inventory. It prevents both local omission and word-by-word census behavior.

Then perform seven class passes. Judge each candidate **inside its own class**, without using another class or a future compound as a substitute. Cross-class overlap is allowed when one source span genuinely performs more than one class-native represented function.

## 2. Primitive admission: three gates

Admit a primitive only when all three gates pass.

### Gate A — exact class-native source identity

The source establishes the candidate at the smallest complete natural literal span for the requested class. Legitimate unnamed `PLACE` or `TIME` support may use `source_wording = null` with an exact source cue and a neutral mechanical tag.

### Gate B — representation-bearing coordinate

The candidate must establish a **distinct coordinate in what is represented**, not merely a piece of language used to carry the telling.

Ask:

**What stable represented thing, participant, characterization/state, relation, scene/episode support, or orientation would this entry let a researcher point back to?**

Retain the candidate when the source makes that coordinate part of the represented situation, including when it is local, one-use, mundane, subtle, remembered, reported, prospective, uncertain, corrected, negated, figurative, or low-salience.

Reject the candidate when its only justification is that the words can be grammatically or semantically classified. Do not inventory:

- narration/discourse carriage;
- auxiliaries or tense/aspect machinery;
- generic connective wording;
- incidental modifiers or scene color;
- purpose/topic/argument fragments that do not independently orient;
- lexical fragments whose represented job belongs inside a larger class-native coordinate;
- every physical noun simply because it appears;
- every adjective/evaluation simply because it modifies something;
- every lexical verb simply because it predicates;
- every prepositional/adverbial phrase simply because it situates language;
- every temporal/spatial phrase simply because it mentions time or place.

`Lightweight` means **representation-bearing coordinates rather than language census**. It does not mean only major themes or high-salience material.

### Gate C — class-local distinctness

Inside the requested class, the coordinate must be distinct from already retained entries. Merge only true aliases, coreference, inflection-only realizations of the same relation identity, or restatements of the same class-native coordinate. Do not merge genuinely distinct coordinates merely because they are related.

Never use another class or a future compound to fail Gate C. Cross-class overlap is not same-class duplication.

## 3. Anti-census and anti-pruning balance

Two errors are equally prohibited.

### Do not over-admit

Do not turn the source into a grammatical inventory. A candidate needs a represented coordinate, not just a class label that can be applied to its words.

### Do not globally prune

Do not remove a valid class-native coordinate because a different class, a larger clause, or a future compound carries related information. If a coordinate passes all three gates in its own class, retain it.

A legitimate primitive may be one-use, local, ordinary, subtle, or nested. Salience, frequency, and thematic importance are not admission requirements.

## 4. Functional typing over surface grammar

Classify by the represented job performed by the candidate, not by part of speech or phrase shape.

- source-established human/social participant → `PERSON`
- stable represented thing/content/referential handle → `OBJECT`
- represented characterization/state/identity/evaluation/comparison/posture → `LABEL`
- represented scene/location support coordinate → `PLACE`
- represented episode/period/phase support coordinate → `TIME`
- represented event/state/relation predicate coordinate → `VERB`
- represented orientation that situates a retained coordinate/relation/scene → `LOCATOR`

A phrase can look nominal, spatial, comparative, adjectival, or verbal while doing a different represented job. Type the job, not the syntax.

When a phrase is being treated as content or a thing-to-think/talk/act about, it may be `OBJECT`. When it is being assigned as a characterization or state, it may be `LABEL`. When it situates retained structure relative to a path, container, origin, destination, accompaniment, context, direction, or other orientation, it may be `LOCATOR`.

Do not force one class to absorb another class's job.

## 5. PLACE — scene/location support coordinates

Retain each distinct source-established location/scene support coordinate required by represented material.

A `PLACE` is not every physical noun or spatial phrase. It is a location support coordinate for a represented occurrence, relation, object placement, destination, wait, remembered episode, later interaction, or present telling/reflection when that support is separately represented.

Broad and contained/local support may both be retained when they are genuinely different represented scene coordinates.

For legitimate unnamed support use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and a neutral mechanical tag. Do not invent a location name.

Reject incidental physical nouns, possessions, surfaces/containers mentioned only as things, and spatial wording that does not establish a distinct scene/location support coordinate.

## 6. TIME — episode/phase support coordinates

Retain each distinct source-established episode, period, phase, or temporal frame that supports represented material.

A `TIME` is not every temporal expression, tense, duration, recurrence word, sequencing word, or action occurrence. Retain a temporal coordinate when it differentiates a represented episode/condition, response/help episode, transition/departure, wait, intended period, later conversation/report, recurring span, remembered phase, prospective/future situation, or present telling/reflection.

A legitimate `TIME` need not contain explicit temporal vocabulary. For unnamed support use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and a neutral mechanical tag. Preserve uncertain, intended, questioned, prospective, and reported posture without asserting occurrence.

Reject one-time-per-action proliferation when the actions share one represented phase.

## 7. PERSON — participant coordinates

Retain the speaker and every distinct source-established human/social actor or stable group that functions as a represented participant after true coreference.

Participation may be direct, offscreen, remembered, reported, institutional, relational, possessive within a material relation, or prospective. One-use participation may qualify.

Reject rhetorical/generic/nonreferential addressees and incidental human mentions that never become represented participants.

## 8. OBJECT — stable referential/content coordinates

Retain each source-established concrete, abstract, internal, relational, decision/choice-like, value-like, set/category, content, or figurative handle that the source treats as a separately represented thing/content coordinate.

An `OBJECT` may be acted on, possessed, exchanged, checked, contrasted, questioned, remembered, reported, selected, decided about, located, valued, contemplated, or otherwise reified. One mention can qualify if the source gives it a stable represented role.

Reject noun census, generic pronouns/deixis, incidental nouns that merely decorate another coordinate, arbitrary nominalizations, discourse wrappers, and clause-sized propositions whose content is not reified as a distinct handle.

**Function rule:** a phrase functioning as represented content/thinghood can be `OBJECT` even when its surface form resembles a clause, state, location, or figurative expression. Conversely, a noun phrase is not automatically `OBJECT`.

**LABEL boundary:** if the source uses the span primarily to characterize/evaluate/state what something is like, type it `LABEL`, not `OBJECT`.

## 9. LABEL — represented characterization/state coordinates

Retain each smallest complete source-native characterization, quality, state, identity, evaluation, comparison, correction, acceptance/rejection, polarity, manner, candidate/question label, or posture that the source assigns or entertains as represented content.

A `LABEL` is not every adjective, intensifier, descriptive modifier, idiom, evaluation word, or colorful phrase. It must establish a characterization/state coordinate that a researcher could separately point to as something the source represents about a person, thing, relation, or situation.

Local, one-use, idiomatic, colloquial, negated, uncertain, figurative, comparative, and corrective labels may qualify when they pass the three gates.

Reject pure intensification, exclamation, rhetorical flourish, scene color, and modifiers that only help phrase another coordinate without establishing a separately represented characterization/state.

## 10. VERB — represented relation coordinates

Retain lexical predicates only when they establish a distinct represented event, state, action, response, movement, possession, perception, communication/report, cognition, intention, question, decision, comparison, evaluation, transition, or location/state **relation coordinate**.

Do not inventory every lexical verb. A verb qualifies because it establishes or materially updates a represented relation among participants/content/support coordinates, not merely because it predicates in the sentence.

Use the smallest complete lexical predicate construction. Preserve a required particle, reflexive, negation, modal, or bound complement when needed for relation identity. Do not swallow a separately typed subject, object/content handle, label, locator, place, or time merely to make the verb phrase sound complete.

Split matrix/embedded or coordinated predicates only when each establishes a different represented relation coordinate.

Reject auxiliaries, tense/aspect support, copular grammar that contributes no lexical relation beyond a retained characterization/state, generic narration/telling scaffolding, discourse organizers, conversational fillers, and same-relation restatements.

A simple or one-use predicate may qualify when it actually carries a represented relation coordinate. Negation, uncertainty, question, intention, hypothetical, report, recurrence, or future posture does not by itself disqualify it and does not assert occurrence.

## 11. LOCATOR — represented orientation coordinates

Retain each smallest complete exact source span that independently orients a retained node, relation, or scene by position, containment, path, direction, origin/destination, proximity, accompaniment, recurrence, procedure/relation, internal/mental context, temporal position, or figurative/comparative orientation.

A `LOCATOR` is not every prepositional phrase, adverbial, deictic, temporal phrase, destination word, comparison, or context phrase. It must establish a distinct orientation coordinate attached to represented structure.

Reject bare prepositions, ordinary purpose/beneficiary/topic/argument markers, discourse connectors, degree/intensifier phrases, generic deictics, bare duration/how-long wording, aspectual words, and duplicated TIME support when they do not independently orient retained structure.

**LABEL boundary:** when a phrase's represented job is characterization/evaluation/comparison rather than orientation, classify it as `LABEL`.

## 12. Atomic span and literal lock

For every admitted primitive choose the smallest exact contiguous source span that preserves its class-native coordinate identity.

Preserve required particles/prepositions, reflexives, complements, negation, modality, uncertainty, questions, attribution, comparison, idiom/dialect, and hypothetical/prospective/reported/corrective posture when semantically required.

Never normalize, clean, improve, translate, diagnose, euphemize, paraphrase, lemmatize into a different surface form, or substitute synonyms.

Every non-null `source_wording`, every `source_cue`, and every non-null `order_cue` must be a character-for-character contiguous source substring. Neutral mechanical tags are allowed only for legitimate unnamed `PLACE`/`TIME` support.

## 13. Coverage + pruning freeze audit

Before freezing primitives, perform both an omission audit and an over-admission audit.

### Pass 1 — represented situation coverage

Walk each differentiated represented scene/episode in source order. For each class, ask whether the scene/episode establishes any representation-bearing coordinate of that class. This is an omission detector, not a requirement that every scene contain every class.

### Pass 2 — coordinate test

For every proposed primitive, state silently what represented coordinate it indexes. If the answer is only “these words are a noun/verb/adjective/adverb/place phrase/time phrase/prepositional phrase,” reject it.

### Pass 3 — anti-census

Remove grammar, narration carriage, generic discourse, incidental scene color, non-reified noun mentions, non-coordinate modifiers, purpose/topic fragments, support wording that does not create a separate scene/phase, and same-class duplicates.

### Pass 4 — anti-pruning

Recheck that no valid coordinate was removed merely because another class or compound carries related meaning.

### Pass 5 — type and span audit

Correct functional type drift, then enforce atomic span and literal lock.

Repeat the coverage pass once after corrections. Freeze only when omission and over-admission audits are both stable.

## 14. Ordering and identity

Primitive identity follows class-local represented-coordinate identity, not mention count. Merge true aliases/coreference and same-class repeats. Do not merge genuinely distinct coordinates merely because they are related or reconstructable from one another.

Code owns canonical IDs. The worker supplies neutral identity keys only for merge. Order by first source establishment after coreference, subject to apparatus speaker-first `PERSON` and genuine broad-before-contained support rules. Do not reorder by importance or real-world chronology.

## 15. Relation-instance compounds

Only after all seven primitive ledgers are frozen, reread the source relation by relation.

For each retained `VERB` relation coordinate, emit the **smallest complete relation-instance compound** linking the frozen coordinates that actually participate in that exact represented relation. Include participant, content/object, characterization, support, and orientation refs only when they are participants/support of that relation instance.

Do not create a compound merely because several primitives occur in one sentence, clause, scene, or episode. The compound must encode a represented relation instance.

Additional non-`VERB` compounds are allowed only for represented relation/state structure that cannot be carried by a retained `VERB` relation and that still passes the representation-bearing threshold.

Do not emit graph closure, every possible pair, arbitrary co-occurrence bundles, singleton equivalents, generic question closure, duplicate restatements, subset/superset permutations, scene mega-bundles, or compounds containing rejected non-coordinate detail.

Compounds never substitute for missing primitives and never justify deleting a valid primitive.

## 16. Completion standard

The inventory is complete when:

- the whole source was read;
- each retained primitive passes exact source identity, representation-bearing coordinate, and class-local distinctness;
- legitimate local/one-use/unnamed-support/low-salience coordinates are preserved;
- grammatical or merely classifiable wording is not converted into a census;
- no valid class-native coordinate is pruned because another class or compound overlaps it;
- functional typing is correct;
- exact source wording/posture is preserved;
- compounds encode represented relation instances only from frozen primitives.

Never target hidden counts or infer hidden gold.
