## Prospective correction basis

V141 verified both immutable Leah-approved archetype workbook hashes, passed all retained deterministic apparatus and prompt-harness tests, and then failed hidden semantic calibration on both approved archetypes. The failure is generalizable and semantic rather than mechanical.

V141 correctly rejected a lexical census, but its requirement that each primitive be an independently reconnectable, self-sufficient researcher coordinate was still too abstract. The worker repeatedly compressed source-native micro-coordinates into broader conceptual phrases, omitted context-dependent but structurally active atoms, and treated local relation/orientation fragments as too weak unless they were independently intelligible outside their source binding. At the same time, it still invented some broad content handles from clauses or propositions. Compound mismatch was downstream from unstable primitive membership and grain.

V142 therefore replaces the self-sufficient-coordinate gate with **source-atomic local-binding resolution**. A primitive need not be independently meaningful outside its source context. It must instead be an exact source-native atom, or a lawful unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinate, that performs a distinct class-native job in the represented local binding structure. Context dependence, brevity, deictic wording, generic relation wording, one use, and low salience are not disqualifying. Analyst-created paraphrase, proposition reification, and phrase completion remain prohibited.

No gold rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, scored predecessor outputs, canonical archetype extracts, case-specific hidden corrections/examples, or sealed holdout content are supplied to the worker.

## Controlling rule

> **READ THE WHOLE SOURCE FIRST. RESOLVE THE SOURCE AS A SEQUENCE OF LOCAL BINDINGS AND STRUCTURAL FRAMES. INVENTORY THE SMALLEST EXACT SOURCE-NATIVE ATOMS THAT ACTUALLY PERFORM PLACE, TIME, PERSON, OBJECT, LABEL, VERB, OR LOCATOR JOBS IN THOSE BINDINGS. AN ATOM MAY BE SHORT, DEICTIC, CONTEXT-DEPENDENT, GENERIC, OR NOT SELF-EXPLANATORY OUTSIDE ITS SOURCE; IT DOES NOT NEED INDEPENDENT IMPORTANCE. DO NOT COMPLETE IT INTO A PARAPHRASE OR MERGE NEARBY ATOMS JUST TO MAKE IT STAND ALONE. COVERAGE MEANS REPLAY EVERY MATERIAL LOCAL BINDING AND ASK WHICH CLASS-NATIVE ATOMS AND LAWFUL UNNAMED FRAMES/POSITIONS ARE REQUIRED TO REPRESENT IT. IT DOES NOT MEAN ENUMERATE EVERY TOKEN, NOUN, VERB, CLAUSE, PROPOSITION, MODIFIER, PREPOSITIONAL PHRASE, OR POSSIBLE PARSE. TYPE BY SOURCE FUNCTION. ALLOW THE SAME SOURCE SPAN IN MORE THAN ONE CLASS ONLY WHEN IT PERFORMS GENUINELY DIFFERENT RESEARCH JOBS. PRESERVE EXACT SOURCE WORDING AND POSTURE. FREEZE PRIMITIVES BEFORE COMPOUNDS. COMPOUNDS REASSEMBLE SELECTIVE MATERIAL LOCAL BINDINGS FROM FROZEN ATOMS; THEY NEVER CREATE OR REPAIR PRIMITIVES.**

## Mission and primitive classes

Produce only a literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate from the supplied source.

Primitive classes are exactly:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The inventory is richer than a sparse semantic summary and narrower than a lexical, grammatical, dependency, event, or proposition census.

## 1. Mandatory construction order — local bindings before class coverage

1. Read the complete source once without extracting primitives.
2. Resolve strict coreference, source order, scene/episode shifts, reported/remembered/prospective posture, and materially distinct local bindings.
3. Build an internal **local-binding ledger**. A local binding is a source-staged relation, state, characterization, orientation, occurrence packet, or frame in which one or more source-established roles are materially connected.
4. For each local binding, identify the smallest source-native atoms that perform material participant, referent, characterization, relation, orientation, site, or frame jobs. For lawful unnamed PLACE/TIME, identify the structural position/frame from an exact source cue.
5. Do not require each atom to be self-contained or independently intelligible outside the binding. The binding supplies context; the primitive supplies the exact local coordinate.
6. Do not create one atom merely because every token, phrase, clause, noun, predicate, modifier, or preposition can be parsed. An atom must perform a class-native research job in the represented binding structure.
7. Replay the whole source class by class to find omitted class-native atoms or unnamed structural positions/frames. Coverage additions must satisfy Section 2.
8. Run cross-class role review and same-class identity/deduplication.
9. Run the structural-floor audit and anti-census ceiling audit.
10. Apply literal/posture lock and freeze primitive membership.
11. Build compounds from frozen primitives only.
12. Run final coverage, typing, lexical, compound, and isolation audits.

Do not begin from a compressed summary. Do not begin from an exhaustive proposition ledger. Do not begin from a class-specific lexical census.

## 2. Universal source-atomic admission rule

A primitive survives when all applicable parts pass.

### 2.1 Source establishment

The source itself supplies the atom, or for lawful unnamed PLACE/TIME distinctly stages the structural position/frame through exact source evidence.

Do not invent a category name, proposition label, normalized concept, inferred diagnosis, explanatory paraphrase, or conventional expression merely because it would summarize the source well.

### 2.2 Local-binding role

The atom must perform at least one distinct class-native research job in the represented source structure:

- site or position;
- organizing time/frame;
- stable actor or social endpoint;
- thing/content referent;
- source-applied characterization or polarity move;
- lexical relation kernel;
- orientation/context relation.

A primitive does **not** need to be independently important, globally salient, repeated, named, or understandable without its surrounding binding. A short response, generic reporting relation, deictic fragment, path particle with lexical content, or context-dependent wording can qualify when it performs a material class-native job.

### 2.3 Atom rather than proposition

Retain the smallest exact contiguous source span that performs the admitted class job.

Do not:

- widen a source atom into a self-explanatory sentence or proposition;
- combine separated or merely adjacent source pieces to make a more readable primitive;
- replace source wording with a conceptual summary;
- attach arguments to a relation merely to make it understandable;
- merge two distinct same-class atoms because they occur in the same sentence or scene.

Widen only when removing material would change or hollow out the source-native identity of the admitted job, such as an identity-bearing particle, negation, modal/control material, idiomatic complement, coordinated relation, or contrast.

### 2.4 Same-class identity

Merge only true same-class same-identity coreference/repetition. Association, co-location, part/whole, container/content, actor/role, episode/subepisode, shared topic, semantic similarity, or adjacency is not identity.

### 2.5 What is insufficient by itself

The following alone never licenses a primitive:

- a token or phrase exists;
- a sentence or clause exists;
- a noun/adjective/verb/preposition is present;
- a proposition can be reconstructed;
- wording looks spatial or temporal;
- a phrase can be nominalized;
- a parser isolates a dependency;
- a modifier or complement is grammatically meaningful;
- the item would make a useful summary.

The positive reason must be its actual class-native role in the source's local binding or structural frame.

## 3. Coverage without census — replay bindings, not vocabulary

Coverage is mandatory.

Replay the source in order and, for each material local binding or frame, ask:

- Which actors/endpoints participate?
- Which things/content referents are actually handled, positioned, selected, compared, discussed, possessed, evaluated, remembered, or acted on?
- Which exact lexical relation atoms connect or stage them?
- Which exact characterization/polarity moves are applied?
- Which exact orientation/context atoms materially situate them?
- Which site/position and organizing time/frame coordinates are required, including lawful unnamed ones?

Then replay by class to catch low-salience omissions.

Coverage must preserve source-atomic micro-coordinates when they perform a real job, even if they are brief, generic, or context-dependent. Coverage must not turn into “retain every materially distinct noun/verb/description/place phrase/time phrase.”

## 4. Cross-class role multiplicity and non-substitution

Type by represented research function, never by surface form.

The same exact source span may lawfully appear in more than one primitive class when it performs genuinely different class-native jobs that are separately needed in the local binding graph. Exact textual overlap is not itself duplication across classes.

Examples of the *kind* of distinction, without prescribing any case answer:

- a source span may name a site and also express orientation toward that site;
- wording may characterize something and also locate/orient it;
- a lexical movement expression may serve as a VERB relation while a contained directional fragment serves as LOCATOR.

Do not create cross-class twins merely because grammar allows alternative descriptions. Require a real difference in research job.

A retained class cannot erase a separate job in another class: OBJECT does not absorb LABEL; LABEL does not absorb VERB; PLACE/TIME do not absorb LOCATOR; VERB does not absorb its actor/object/site/frame; LOCATOR does not replace PLACE.

## 5. PLACE — sites and represented positions

`PLACE` inventories represented physical/institutional settings and source-staged occurrence, participant, or referent positions.

Retain a named or lawful unnamed PLACE when a source binding materially depends on **where** a retained participant/referent/occurrence is staged, even if the exact location is not named.

Rules:

- Physical naming is not required.
- Broad and contained sites may both survive.
- Co-located participant/occurrence positions may remain separate if the source stages distinct positional roles.
- A reported, remembered, prospective, or present-telling occurrence may have its own unnamed position when the source distinctly stages that occurrence.
- Do not require an unnamed position to be independently interesting outside the event it positions.
- A physical noun, surface, container, body part, direction, figurative location, or movement phrase is not PLACE merely because it can be located.

For lawful unnamed PLACE use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording. Do not invent a more specific location than the source supports.

## 6. TIME — organizing frames and source-staged episodes

`TIME` inventories represented episodes, periods, phases, attempts, waits, interactions, transitions, recurrence spans, remembered/reported frames, prospective frames, and present telling/reflection frames that organize material.

Retain a TIME when the source establishes a distinct frame used to group or position a material local binding, even if the frame is brief or unnamed.

Rules:

- No date, clock, duration, frequency word, or temporal noun is required.
- A short event may be TIME when the source treats it as a distinct episode/frame that organizes other retained atoms.
- Broad and nested frames may both survive when each organizes source material.
- Intended, remembered, reported, recurring, waiting, conversation, prospective, and present-reflection frames may qualify.
- Do not make every action, clause, verb, tense/aspect shift, or explicit temporal phrase into TIME. The item must organize a binding or source packet as a frame.

For lawful unnamed TIME use `source_wording = null`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording.

## 7. PERSON — stable represented actors/endpoints

`PERSON` inventories `B` plus every distinct stable represented human or social actor/group after strict coreference.

Retain actors who act, speak, perceive, are acted upon, possess/receive, function as source/target/beneficiary/endpoint/companion/member, or participate in remembered/reported/prospective relations. Minor, brief, offscreen, relational, institutional, possessive, or group actors may qualify when source-established.

Exclude rhetorical/generic addressees, generic hypothetical roles, pronouns with no stable referent, and true aliases/coreference duplicates.

Order non-speakers by first independent represented participation rather than incidental possessive mention alone.

## 8. OBJECT — source-treated things and content referents

`OBJECT` inventories source-established things and content referents that participate as their own role in local bindings.

Lawful OBJECTs may include physical things, scene items, products, documents, parts, surfaces, containers, amounts/values, sets, choices, decisions, plans, relationships-as-content, recurring situations, conditions, services, results, mental contents, comparison vehicles, and explicitly reified relations.

Retain a low-salience or one-use referent when the source actually handles, positions, possesses, selects, compares, evaluates, remembers, decides, questions, or points back to it.

Use the source's own smallest referential atom. Do not create:

- an analyst-completed “thing” from a whole clause;
- a proposition shell merely because the sentence says something;
- a conceptual summary of what an actor wanted, meant, or decided unless the source itself treats that content as a referent;
- an arbitrary noun fragment with no referent role.

Do not duplicate a setting as OBJECT when its only research job is PLACE.

## 9. LABEL — source-applied characterization and polarity atoms

`LABEL` inventories source-applied states, qualities, statuses, identities, classifications, evaluations, comparisons, manners, polarities, corrections, rejections, and characterization questions.

Retain the smallest exact source-native atom that carries the characterization move. It may be brief or context-dependent. A polarity response, rejection, correction, uncertainty marker, or question may be its own LABEL when it materially accepts/rejects/qualifies a characterization in the local binding.

Preserve question, negation, uncertainty, rejection, correction, contrast, attribution, colloquial wording, and material intensity.

Do not create LABEL from every adjective, adverb, modifier, colorful phrase, ordinary predicate, or rhetorical flourish. There must be a source-applied characterization/polarity job.

## 10. VERB — source-native relation atoms

`VERB` inventories the source's lexical relation atoms that materially constitute local bindings.

A VERB qualifies when it does at least one of the following:

1. connects retained participants/referents/sites/frames in a represented relation;
2. stages a retained actor/referent in an action, state, transition, perception, cognition, speech/reporting act, intention, comparison, possession, movement, waiting, or other material relation; or
3. supplies the lexical relation by which a material source packet is reported or linked, even when the relation word is generic or context-dependent.

The atom need not be independently interesting outside the binding. Generic relations can qualify when they are the actual source relation connecting retained coordinates.

Use the smallest exact contiguous lexical kernel that preserves the source relation identity. Keep particles, directionals, reflexives, negation-bearing material, modal/control/infinitival material, idiomatic complements, or coordination only when necessary to preserve that relation identity.

Do not:

- attach actor/object/place/time arguments just to make the VERB self-explanatory;
- merge separate predicates merely because they share a clause;
- suppress a relation solely because it is a reporting, cognition, support, copular, possession, or generic verb when it materially binds retained coordinates;
- harvest every grammatical verb when it does not perform a material local-binding job.

Split matrix/embedded or coordinated predicates when the source stages materially distinct relation jobs; preserve them together when they form one source-native relation identity.

## 11. LOCATOR — source-native orientation/context atoms

`LOCATOR` inventories exact source-native orientation/context relations that materially situate a retained participant, referent, action, site, frame, or comparison.

Eligible jobs include relative position, containment, direction, path, origin/destination, entry/exit, approach/departure, proximity/distance, accompaniment/carrying, recurrence/situational context, embodied/internal orientation, and materially useful figurative/comparison orientation.

A LOCATOR may be short, deictic, or context-dependent. It does not need to name both endpoints inside its own text. The surrounding local binding supplies those endpoints.

Use the smallest exact local span that carries the orientation job. Do not merge neighboring locator atoms merely to make one self-contained phrase.

Suppress bare grammatical prepositions with no independent orientation content, routine recipient/topic marking, possession alone, and wording that merely repeats another coordinate without a distinct orientation/context job.

## 12. Literal, contiguity, and posture lock

Every source-derived non-null string must preserve source language character-for-character wherever the schema requires source text.

Never synonymize, lemmatize, repair spelling/grammar, normalize dialect, expand contractions, translate, polish, diagnose, standardize a group name, change number, or substitute a conventional expression. Do not replace source-native labels for people/groups/things with analyst-created category names.

Do not concatenate noncontiguous source fragments into one primitive. Do not silently add omitted words. Do not broaden a fragment merely so a reader can understand it without context.

`source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be exact source text where applicable. Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME may use null `source_wording`.

Questions, uncertainty, hypotheticals, negations, self-corrections, remembered possibilities, reported material, and proposed future actions remain in source posture. Inventorying does not assert truth or realization.

## 13. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean only. True means the source supplies material qualities/descriptions associated with the coordinate; false otherwise. Do not create or split primitives to justify the boolean. `Q` is never a primitive.

## 14. Ordering

Default within-class order is first material source anchor after filtering/coreference.

Tie rules:

- broad/whole setting before dependent/contained setting when introduced together;
- whole before dependent part at the same anchor unless the source stages the part first;
- PERSON follows first independent represented participation;
- remembered/reported/hypothetical/future frames remain at source position rather than external chronology.

The apparatus owns canonical IDs and deterministic numbering.

## 15. Structural floor audit — missing local atoms

Replay every material local binding and frame. Ask whether any source-staged role needed to represent the binding has disappeared because it was brief, generic, unnamed, deictic, context-dependent, low-salience, or nested.

Specifically check for under-resolution of:

- unnamed occurrence/participant/referent positions;
- broad and local organizing frames, waits, interactions, recurrence, transitions, remembered/reported/prospective frames, and present-reflection frames;
- minor stable actors/endpoints;
- low-salience concrete referents actually participating in a binding;
- abstract choices/decisions/contents/relations explicitly treated as things;
- questioned/rejected/corrected source-applied characterization or polarity atoms;
- generic or short lexical relations that materially connect retained coordinates;
- short physical/figurative/context orientation atoms;
- lawful cross-class roles supported by the same source span.

Add a missing primitive only in its correct class and exact source grain. Do not widen or retype a neighboring primitive to compensate.

## 16. Anti-census ceiling audit

For every retained primitive ask:

1. What exact local binding or structural frame gives this atom its class-native job?
2. What exact source span establishes it, or what exact cue establishes an unnamed PLACE/TIME?
3. Is the atom retained for source function rather than merely lexical/grammatical form?
4. Is its span the smallest exact contiguous unit that performs that job?
5. Did I invent, complete, merge, or paraphrase it to make it more understandable?
6. Is it distinct from same-class identities already retained?
7. Would it disappear if I removed proposition-census, noun-census, verb-census, modifier-census, spatial-phrase-census, or temporal-phrase-census reasoning?

If there is no positive local-binding/frame job, remove it. Never aim for a hidden row count or density.

## 17. Compound construction — reassemble selective material bindings

Freeze primitives before compound work.

A compound is a selective local packet of frozen primitives whose binding is itself useful for reconnecting the case.

Create a compound when:

1. the source materially binds two or more frozen primitives in a distinct relation/state/configuration;
2. the packet represents one local research-significant binding rather than merely sentence membership;
3. it contains only primitives active in that binding; and
4. it is not an alternate parse, dependency closure, pairwise/transitive closure, one-compound-per-token artifact, clause-support bundle, duplicate/subset alias, or scene-wide mega-bundle.

A short or generic VERB may anchor a compound when it materially supplies the relation connecting active refs. A LABEL or LOCATOR may participate when it is active in that binding. Include PLACE/TIME only when they actually organize the binding. Do not omit an active primitive merely because its own text is context-dependent.

Compounds are where contextual atoms are reconnected. Do not force primitives to carry compound-level context inside their own wording.

A compound cannot create, delete, merge, retype, repair, or substitute for a primitive. If a needed atom is missing, return to primitive review first.

Canonical compound IDs/order and `_Q` construction remain apparatus-owned.

## 18. Final contrast audit

### Too dense / census failure

Warning signs: every grammatical predicate becomes VERB; every noun phrase becomes OBJECT; every description becomes LABEL; every explicit or implicit moment becomes TIME; every spatial phrase becomes PLACE/LOCATOR; whole propositions are reified as primitives; compounds track nearly every clause.

### Too sparse / conceptual-compression failure

Warning signs: a short source-native relation is omitted because it seems generic; a deictic or tiny orientation atom is widened or dropped; a brief polarity/characterization move disappears; separate broad/contained or unnamed positions/frames collapse; same-span cross-class roles are suppressed; several local atoms are merged into one explanatory phrase; source context is replaced by analyst interpretation.

Correct toward the local-binding atom graph, not toward target density.

## 19. Final audit

Before return verify:

- **Binding coverage:** every material local binding/frame was replayed for all class-native roles.
- **Source atomicity:** each non-null primitive uses the smallest exact contiguous source span that performs its job.
- **No self-containment pressure:** a primitive was not widened merely so it makes sense outside context.
- **Typing:** every primitive is typed by source function rather than word form.
- **Cross-class:** genuinely distinct jobs may share source wording; alternate parses do not multiply rows.
- **Literal:** exact wording, dialect, polarity, question, uncertainty, negation, and posture are preserved.
- **Ceiling:** no lexical/grammatical/proposition census survived without a positive binding/frame job.
- **Compounds:** selective local bindings reassemble frozen active refs without repairing primitives.
- **Candidate:** no promotion, scoring, APA identity, or database action occurred.
