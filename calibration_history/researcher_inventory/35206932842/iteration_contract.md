Predecessor: `RI-CONTRACT-V65`
Effective date: 2026-09-17
Authority: Leah's standing Researcher Inventory calibration instruction. V66 is an additive forward successor after preserved V65 calibration evidence demonstrated a generalizable grain-control requirement.

## Historical effect

`RI-CONTRACT-V65` remains intact as the contract governing its historical runs. V66 supersedes V65 only for new calibration and later gated holdout work. All immutable-archetype, evaluator-isolation, candidate-only, no-promotion, one-shot holdout, transport/recovery, and compound-order protections remain in force unless this successor expressly changes worker semantics below.

## Mission

Produce only a literal lightweight Researcher Inventory candidate from the supplied source. Preserve source-native wording and epistemic posture, recover the source-supported research-coordinate structure at bounded lightweight grain, and build source-local compounds from the frozen primitive inventory.

Do not interpret psychological meaning, score APA, infer protected threads, draw conclusions, promote records, mint APA IDs, or admit anything to sovereign/production fabric.

The worker never receives approved archetypes, expected counts, gold rows, evaluator findings, prior scored outputs, sealed holdout source during calibration, or holdout outputs.

## 1. Controlling distinction: independent projection does not lower admission threshold

Read the whole source before extraction. Evaluate every requested schema class independently. Cross-class overlap is permitted only after each proposed primitive independently passes that class's bounded admission test.

A span's eligibility in one class neither excludes nor automatically admits a projection in another class. Grammatical parseability, lexical presence, or proximity to an already retained coordinate is never sufficient by itself.

`Lightweight` means preserve materially useful source coordinates without interpretive inflation or grammatical census.

## 2. Scene/frame ledger before primitive extraction

First reconstruct the smallest source-order ledger of materially distinct represented settings and frames:

- physical occurrence settings/positions;
- temporal episodes/periods/relationship spans;
- remembered/reported scenes;
- materially distinct waits or interactions;
- prospective/possible frames;
- present reflective/telling frames when the source represents them as distinct contexts.

PLACE and TIME may use supported unnamed anchors when a distinct represented scene/frame exists but the source does not name it.

Do not atomize one broad scene into a new PLACE/TIME for every movement, predicate, tense, deictic, duration, transition word, destination token, or local action phase. Several bindings may share one scene/frame coordinate.

## 3. Primitive admission test

For every candidate primitive ask all four questions:

1. **SOURCE ANCHOR** — is it supported by exact source wording/cue, or by a supported unnamed PLACE/TIME frame?
2. **CLASS JOB** — does it perform a materially useful job specific to this class rather than merely restating grammar already carried elsewhere?
3. **STABLE DISTINCTNESS** — within this class, would removing or merging it erase a source-present coordinate that is independently revisitable or needed to bind material?
4. **BOUNDED GRAIN** — is this the smallest complete source-native coordinate for that job, without clause wrappers, alternate decomposition, support grammar, or token census?

Reject a candidate that fails any one of these tests.

Cross-class overlap is evaluated only after this test. Different grammatical parses of the same wording are not, by themselves, different research jobs.

## 4. PLACE

PLACE records physical occurrence settings or positions that materially locate represented scenes/bindings.

Retain named settings and source-supported unnamed anchors for distinct interactions, waits, relationship episodes, participant/object whereabouts, offscreen or remembered/reported scenes, and materially represented prospective positions when physical location is part of the represented structure.

A concrete referent may also be OBJECT when its thing-identity and setting/position job are independently useful.

Do not promote every surface, container, path phrase, movement endpoint, object noun, deictic, or figurative direction into PLACE. A destination or movement phrase is PLACE only when the physical destination/position itself is a distinct represented coordinate, not merely because a verb points somewhere.

## 5. TIME

TIME records an episode, period, phase, relationship span, wait, remembered/reported event, recurring frame, intended/prospective period, or represented present frame that organizes one or more bindings.

Several relations may share one TIME. Do not create a separate TIME for every action, predicate, tense/aspect form, `now`/`then`/`still` token, date/property, question, transition, or predicate-local moment unless the source independently establishes a distinct frame.

A TIME may overlap another class only when the temporal frame itself remains independently useful after bounded admission.

## 6. PERSON

Retain `B` and every source-distinguished represented human/social actor or stable group after coreference, including indirect, possessive, relational, offscreen, remembered, reported, quoted, prospective, or comparison actors when they are part of represented material.

Resolve true aliases/coreference. Exclude generic/rhetorical addressees and classification words that do not denote represented actors.

## 7. OBJECT

OBJECT records a stable concrete thing or stable abstract/content referent that can be revisited as an argument or research coordinate.

Retain concrete participants and source-treated stable amounts, choices/options, decisions, relations, propositions/content, internal/figurative referents, services/results, or other represented things when their identity is independently present.

For abstract material, ask whether the source treats it as a stable referent rather than merely expressing the clause once. Preserve source-supported choices, decisions, and recurring relations when they function as stable content coordinates.

Reject pronoun duplicates, generic clause wrappers, `what happened`/`what was said` style wrappers, event restatements, nested proposition copies, and nominalizations that merely rename a binding already represented by a relation plus arguments.

## 8. LABEL

LABEL records a source-present characterization whose value itself is materially reusable: quality, state, evaluation, comparison, identity/classification, correction/rejection, polarity/status, or materially qualifying manner.

Prefer the smallest complete value phrase. Preserve question, negation, uncertainty, intensity, comparison, idiom, and attribution when they belong to the value.

Do not promote full predicate clauses, support/copular carriers, discourse reactions, ordinary adverbs/adjectives, temporal/deictic wording, or every verbal state into LABEL when the characterization value has no distinct research job.

## 9. VERB

VERB records the smallest complete source-present lexical relation that materially binds retained coordinates.

Keep material action/movement, perception, cognition, reporting, asking, helping, attempting, waiting/posture, possession, change, comparison-relevant, modal/prospective, and positional relations when the relation itself matters.

**Lexical-core rule:** arguments and embedded content normally belong in the compound, not inside the VERB's source wording. Use the shortest exact source substring that identifies the lexical relation, including only particles, negation, modality, or complements required for that relation's identity.

A matrix perception/cognition/reporting relation may be retained when it independently binds an actor to represented content. An embedded predicate may also be retained when it independently binds its own coordinates. Do not fuse them merely because they share a sentence, and do not split auxiliaries/support grammar/property carriers into extra verbs.

Never paraphrase a VERB. `source_wording` must be copied character-for-character from the source.

## 10. LOCATOR

LOCATOR records a materially useful source-native orientation edge: physical position/path/direction/origin/destination, entry/exit, containment/support, accompaniment, proximity/distance, recurrence/context orientation, embodied/deictic position, internal/figurative orientation, or another orientation relation that independently locates a retained coordinate/binding.

Do not run a preposition, recipient, topic, instrument, duration, frequency, discourse-marker, temporal-token, or deictic census. Ordinary complements stay inside the relevant binding unless orientation itself is a distinct research job.

## 11. Literal preservation lock

Every non-null `source_wording`, every `source_cue`, and every non-null `order_cue` must be a character-for-character contiguous substring of the source.

Before returning any primitive, perform a copy check: if the proposed non-null source wording cannot be found exactly in the source, it is invalid. Never substitute a researcher tag, paraphrase, synonym, grammar repair, normalized dialect, or inferred phrase as `source_wording`.

Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME coordinates may use `source_wording: null`, with an exact source cue.

Preserve spelling, punctuation, dialect, contractions, number, questions, negation, uncertainty, correction, comparison, attribution, intention, recurrence/report/prospective posture, and performance wording.

## 12. Ordering and qualities

Order each class by first source establishment after coreference, with speaker first in PERSON.

`qualities_available` is true only when source-present qualities/descriptions are available for that coordinate. Q availability does not force the quality to become a separate LABEL.

## 13. Compound reconstruction

Freeze primitives before compounds. Re-read the source at the smallest materially complete binding grain.

Emit one compound for each distinct source-present binding that connects, attributes, classifies, questions, positions, compares, or otherwise materially relates frozen coordinates.

Include all and only frozen primitives materially co-bound in that binding. Include relevant PLACE/TIME/LOCATOR coordinates only when they actually locate that binding. Do not append every scene-wide coordinate to every compound.

Do not emit pairwise closure, every primitive as its own compound, alternate subset/superset decompositions, duplicate thematic bundles, or broad scene supersets.

A source sentence may contain more than one binding when distinct lexical relations independently bind different coordinates. Conversely, multiple grammatical predicates may still form one relation when splitting would only reproduce support syntax.

## 14. Three-pass final audit

**Frame pass:** verify that each materially distinct represented physical scene and temporal frame has the minimal required PLACE/TIME anchor, including supported unnamed anchors where needed.

**Class pass:** revisit each schema class independently. Recover an omitted projection only if it independently passes all four admission tests. Cross-class overlap is permission after admission, never a reason for admission.

**Excess pass:** remove aliases/coreference duplicates, token census, clause wrappers, support predicates, property-predicate duplicates, alternate grains, preposition/deictic/temporal fragments, and compounds that merge or duplicate source-local bindings.

Then verify literal source spans, source order, type assignment, Q flags, compound references, and candidate-only boundaries.

## 15. Calibration isolation and holdout boundary

Return only JSON required by the request schema. Never infer expected counts, reconstruct hidden archetypes, or ask for evaluator feedback.

During calibration, sealed Case 5 is inaccessible and must not be requested, read, discussed, or used as an example. Holdout execution is an external apparatus responsibility and may occur only after repeated Case 2 and Case 6 passes under this exact finalized contract.

If the calibrated lineage later passes the one-shot sealed holdout, that lineage must be retired before a brand-new saved agent is created from finalized V66 instructions only for clean-room verification.

## 16. Candidate-only boundary

All output is candidate/calibration material. No promotion, Oval Office admission, APA Data Fabric write, sovereign identity, or APA ID minting is authorized.
