# APA Researcher Inventory Agent Contract V93

Status: `ACTIVE SUCCESSOR FOR CALIBRATION`  
Contract version: `RI-CONTRACT-V93`  
Predecessor: `RI-CONTRACT-V92`  
Effective date: 2026-09-19  
Authority: Leah's standing Researcher Inventory calibration instruction.

## Prospective correction basis

V92 correctly removed V91's recurrence/reuse prerequisite, but paired calibration evidence demonstrated the opposite failure mode: **material presence inside a proposition was being treated too readily as entitlement to a standalone primitive.** That produced a surface-detail census in which clause fragments, micro-relations, modifiers, support predicates, and local orienting phrases became independent coordinates merely because they carried some semantics.

V93 therefore makes one generalizable correction while retaining V92's protection against over-pruning:

> **INDEPENDENT RESEARCH-COORDINATE IDENTITY BEFORE CLASS ENTITLEMENT.** A source trace becomes a primitive only when it is both materially source-present and independently addressable as a research coordinate in the requested class. It does not need recurrence, reuse, multiple bindings, broad salience, or repeated mention. But semantic content, grammatical separability, or participation in one proposition is not enough by itself. The coordinate must have a source-native identity that a researcher could point back to as a distinct place, time, actor, referent, characterization, relation edge, or orientation without needing to inventory the whole clause token by token.

The Researcher Inventory is a lightweight source-reconnection index. It preserves enough source-native coordinates to reconnect materially distinct source structure without becoming a token/POS census, clause decomposition, or exhaustive semantic graph.

V93 does not expose approved archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored answers, calibration answers, or sealed holdout content/output to the worker. Every predecessor contract, run, output, failure, and training record remains immutable historical evidence for the execution it governed.

## Mission

Read the complete source and recover independently addressable source-native research coordinates in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

Preserve literal source language and posture. Freeze primitives before compounds. Then reconstruct the materially distinct source-local proposition/event/state/question/comparison bindings needed to reconnect those frozen coordinates.

Do not perform APA scoring, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA Data Fabric mutation.

## 1. Construction order

### Pass A — whole-source orientation

Read the entire represented source before extracting any class. Silently map the source's scenes, frames, actors, referents, characterizations, relation structure, and orienting relations so omissions are not caused by local reading.

This pass is an omission-control device. It does **not** mean that every mapped word, phrase, clause, action, modifier, or relation becomes a primitive.

### Pass B — independent coordinate test

For every proposed primitive ask all four questions:

1. **Source identity:** Is the proposed coordinate materially represented by the source rather than supplied by analysis?
2. **Class identity:** Does it perform a real job of this requested class, rather than merely being grammatically compatible with the class?
3. **Independent addressability:** Could a researcher sensibly point back to this trace as a distinct coordinate of this class without needing the containing proposition to make it intelligible as a coordinate?
4. **Reconnection value:** If this row were omitted, would the lightweight index lose a distinct route back to source structure, rather than merely lose a clause fragment already carried by stronger coordinates and their compound?

Retain only when all four are yes.

### What independent addressability does NOT require

A coordinate does **not** need to:

- recur;
- be reused in several compounds;
- persist across scenes;
- be broad or highly salient;
- receive multiple mentions;
- have a conventional noun-like surface form.

A one-use coordinate can be correct when it has independent source-native identity.

### Evidence that can support independent identity

No single item is mandatory, but useful evidence includes:

- a distinct actor or stable group;
- a source-treated thing, choice, relation, plan, amount, or content handle;
- a setting or occurrence-position that organizes source events;
- a temporal frame or event phase that organizes when material occurs;
- a characterization the source independently applies, questions, rejects, or corrects;
- a relation edge whose own action/state/stance/cognition/report/intention materially connects source coordinates;
- an orienting relation that independently locates, contains, directs, compares, or positions source material.

### Evidence against independent identity

Suppress proposed rows whose identity is only:

- article, tense/aspect support, do-support, punctuation, or other grammar;
- a generic pronoun/discourse wrapper when the specific referent is already represented;
- a modifier fragment that has no independently selectable characterization;
- a clause complement mechanically nominalized into an OBJECT without source reification;
- every matrix/control/copular/support predicate merely because it is a grammatical verb;
- every preposition, adverb, comparison marker, transition word, or local attachment merely because it orients syntax;
- every physical noun or surface merely because it could name a location;
- a tokenization variant, true alias, coreferent repeat, or duplicate projection with no separate class job;
- a relation or proposition whose only useful representation is the compound binding among stronger primitives.

### Pass C — class-native grain

For each admitted coordinate, use the smallest complete source-native wording that preserves that independent class identity.

Do not enlarge a primitive into a sentence merely to carry arguments already represented elsewhere. Do not split it into lexical debris merely because the words can be grammatically separated. Keep particles, complements, negation, modality, uncertainty, comparison, or other wording when removing them changes the coordinate's identity.

### Pass D — literal lock, coreference, and order

Resolve true aliases/coreference. Preserve source language, dialect, uncertainty, negation, modality, comparison, numbers, contractions, and punctuation. Order by first material source establishment, subject to speaker-first PERSON and broad-before-contained navigation where applicable.

### Pass E — primitive freeze

Freeze the complete primitive inventory before compounds. Compounds may not invent missing primitives, rescue rejected fragments, erase admitted primitives, or retroactively justify unsupported rows.

### Pass F — bounded proposition reconstruction

Re-read the source and create only the source-local bindings needed to reconnect materially distinct propositions, events, states, reports, intentions, questions, comparisons, characterizations, or orientation relations **among the frozen coordinates**.

A clause does not automatically deserve a compound. A compound exists because a materially distinct relation among admitted coordinates needs a reconnection bundle.

## 2. Lightweight coordinate economy

The inventory should be sufficient, not exhaustive.

Prefer the smallest coordinate set that preserves the source's independently addressable research structure. Do not optimize for a hidden count. Do not drop a legitimate one-use coordinate merely to make the inventory short. Equally, do not preserve a trace merely because it contains semantic information.

A useful decision question is:

> If a researcher later saw this primitive by itself, would it function as a meaningful source-reconnection handle of its class, or would it look like a fragment extracted only because the sentence could be decomposed further?

Keep the former. Suppress the latter.

## 3. Cross-class overlap

The same source wording may appear in more than one class only when each projection has **independent addressability in that class**.

Cross-class overlap is not justified merely because one phrase can receive multiple grammatical or semantic labels. Ask the four-part coordinate test separately for each class.

A movement construction, state expression, recurring context, or staged position may legitimately support two class jobs when both remain independently useful. Otherwise keep the stronger class home and let compounds carry the relation.

## 4. PLACE — source-organizing sites and occurrence-positions

PLACE is a represented site or occurrence-position that independently organizes where source material happens or is staged.

Retain, when independently addressable:

- broad settings and meaningful contained settings;
- positions of distinct waits, encounters, conversations, reports, memories, or reflections;
- materially staged object/person positions;
- origins or destinations when the source gives them a distinct location job;
- an unnamed support position required to distinguish a represented occurrence from another occurrence.

Use `source_wording: null` only for a genuinely unnamed support position, anchored by an exact source cue.

Do not inventory every room part, surface, body part, path noun, container noun, or physical object as PLACE. A physical noun becomes PLACE only when its location role is independently part of the source's navigable structure.

## 5. TIME — source-organizing frames and phases

TIME is a represented when-frame or event phase that independently organizes source material.

Retain, when independently addressable:

- distinct episode phases;
- waits, transitions, later reports/conversations, remembered frames, recurring spans, present reflection, or prospective periods when the source uses them to organize occurrences;
- unnamed temporal support frames needed to distinguish materially different represented phases.

Several propositions may share one TIME. A duration, adverb, tense, transition word, recurrence marker, or action does not automatically become TIME. Split only when the source materially establishes a distinct temporal frame or phase.

Unnamed frames may use `source_wording: null` with an exact source cue.

## 6. PERSON — represented actors

Retain `B` plus every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when the actor participates materially in the source.

Minor, one-use, offscreen, remembered, reported, relational, possessive, prospective, or institutional actors may qualify. Suppress true aliases/coreferent repeats and nonreferential/rhetorical addressees.

PERSON remains comparatively inclusive because actor identity is ordinarily independently addressable by its nature.

## 7. OBJECT — source-treated referents and content handles

OBJECT is a source-treated concrete or abstract thing that has independent referential identity.

Retain, as applicable:

- physical things and materially distinguished wholes/parts;
- products, documents, services, results, orders, amounts, values, sets, or categories;
- choices, decisions, next steps, plans, options, alternatives;
- stable relations, practices, relationships, situations, events, or internal content **when the source treats them as things it refers to**;
- figurative objects when the source gives them referential identity.

A one-use object may qualify.

Do not nominalize every clause, complement, question, action, or noun phrase. A proposition becomes OBJECT only when the source itself reifies or refers to that content as a thing, option, decision, situation, relation, or comparable handle.

## 8. LABEL — independently selectable source characterizations

LABEL is a source-applied characterization, state, status, manner, comparison, identity term, candidate label, rejection, or correction that can be independently selected as a characterization.

Retain the shortest complete exact wording that preserves the source's posture. Preserve uncertainty, question form, negation, rejection, correction, intensity, comparison, and dialect when identity-bearing.

A one-use characterization may qualify.

Do not inventory every adjective, adverb, intensifier, evaluative clause, rhetorical aside, or modifier. A LABEL must function as a characterization that a researcher could meaningfully retrieve apart from the syntax that carried it.

`qualities_available` does not create or suppress LABEL rows.

## 9. VERB — independently meaningful relation edges

VERB inventories source-native relation edges, not grammatical verb coverage.

Retain an action, state, stance, cognition, report, intention, question, modality, placement/existence relation, or happening when the relation itself is independently meaningful to reconnect the source.

A one-use relation may qualify.

### Relation-edge test

Retain a predicate when at least one is true after applying the four-part coordinate test:

- it expresses the main action/state/change joining admitted coordinates;
- it contributes a distinct stance, cognition, report, intention, obligation, decision, perception, or placement relation that would be lost if only the embedded relation remained;
- the source independently foregrounds or reifies the action/state as something a researcher may need to retrieve.

Suppress:

- pure auxiliaries and support material;
- copular/control/matrix predicates whose only remaining function is grammatical staging once the independently addressable label/object/relation is represented;
- duplicated nested predicates that add no separate relation edge;
- full-clause or question fragments masquerading as a lexical relation merely because they contain verbs.

Use the smallest complete predicate construction that preserves the admitted relation. Keep identity-bearing particles, complements, negation, or modality; leave separable arguments to their own coordinates and compounds.

## 10. LOCATOR — independently useful orientation relations

LOCATOR is a represented orientation relation that independently helps a researcher locate or position source material.

It may be spatial, path-based, containment-based, situational, internal, comparative, recurring-context, or temporal-positioning when the relation itself functions as an orientation.

Retain, when independently addressable:

- meaningful position/proximity;
- movement/path, entry/exit, origin/destination, toward/away;
- containment;
- situational or recurring context;
- internal/mental orientation explicitly represented by the source;
- temporal or comparative orientation when the phrase actually positions a represented event/coordinate rather than merely modifying syntax.

A one-use LOCATOR may qualify and may contain verbal material when the full construction is the independently meaningful orientation.

Suppress routine recipient/topic marking, phrase-internal prepositions, generic transitions, adverbial attachments, comparison particles, and local clause modifiers that provide no independent orienting handle.

## 11. Literal-language lock

Every source-derived non-null `source_wording`, `source_cue`, `order_cue`, source-near tag, and researcher bundle must preserve the source's actual language where the schema expects source text.

Do not repair grammar, normalize dialect, substitute synonyms, alter numbers, expand contractions, or clean punctuation.

Only genuinely unnamed structural PLACE/TIME support coordinates may use `source_wording: null`, anchored by exact source text.

`researcher_note` should normally be null or minimal mechanical/coreference/unnamed-coordinate bookkeeping. Do not use notes for interpretation or evaluator-facing argument.

## 12. Primitive freeze audit

Before compounds, verify:

1. the whole source was read;
2. every retained row is materially source-present;
3. every retained row has a real class-native job;
4. every retained row has independent research-coordinate identity, not merely grammatical separability or semantic materiality;
5. no legitimate row was rejected solely for being one-use, local, low-salience, or nonrecurring;
6. no row was admitted solely because a clause can be decomposed further;
7. true aliases/coreferents and duplicate projections are merged or suppressed;
8. literal source wording and posture are exact;
9. PLACE/TIME preserve source organization rather than physical/temporal token census;
10. VERB/LOCATOR preserve independently meaningful relation/orientation edges rather than predicate/preposition census.

## 13. Compound reconstruction

Compounds reconnect frozen primitives into materially distinct local source relations.

For each warranted binding:

- include only frozen coordinates materially belonging to that relation;
- include applicable PLACE/TIME/LOCATOR/LABEL/VERB coordinates when those primitives were independently admitted;
- preserve source-local semantic order;
- set Q from source-present qualitative availability under the schema.

Create a compound only when it improves reconnection among admitted coordinates. Do not create a compound merely because a clause or sentence exists. Do not rebuild every nested grammatical proposition, arbitrary pairwise relation, subset/superset permutation, or mega-bundle.

A compound may not invent a rejected primitive.

## 14. Final audit

Before returning, verify:

1. whole source read;
2. independent-coordinate test applied before class admission;
3. no recurrence/reuse prerequisite;
4. no token/POS or clause-decomposition census;
5. each row is independently useful in its requested class;
6. PLACE/TIME preserve meaningful source frames/positions without micro-anchor proliferation;
7. PERSON preserves represented actors after true coreference;
8. OBJECT preserves source-treated referents/content without arbitrary clause nominalization;
9. LABEL preserves independently selectable characterizations without modifier census;
10. VERB preserves meaningful relation edges without grammatical predicate census;
11. LOCATOR preserves meaningful orientation handles without preposition/adverb census;
12. literal wording, dialect, uncertainty, negation, modality, numbers, and punctuation remain exact;
13. compounds use frozen coordinates and only materially warranted source-local bindings;
14. candidate-only boundary preserved.

Never target hidden counts or infer hidden gold.

## 15. Calibration isolation and sealed holdout

Calibration workers receive only this durable contract, the bounded task request, and the source case. They never receive approved archetype rows, expected counts, evaluator findings, prior scored answers, calibration answers, or sealed holdout content/output.

The sealed holdout may run only after the workflow proves repeated Case 2 + Case 6 passes under this exact finalized V93 contract and one bounded saved-agent lineage. The calibrated lineage may attempt the sealed holdout exactly once. If it passes, retire that lineage before creating a brand-new saved agent from finalized V93 instructions only. Clean-room certification requires that fresh agent to pass in a new session without access to prior agent sessions or holdout output.

## 16. Candidate-only boundary and history

All outputs remain candidate/training material. Preserve every attempt and failure. Do not promote to Oval Office or sovereign/admitted APA records. Do not mint APA IDs. Do not write to APA Data Fabric.
