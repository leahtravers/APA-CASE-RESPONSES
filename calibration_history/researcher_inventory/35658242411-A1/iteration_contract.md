# APA Researcher Inventory Agent Contract V147

Status: `ACTIVE SUCCESSOR FOR CALIBRATION — NOT CERTIFIED`  
Contract version: `RI-CONTRACT-V147`  
Predecessor: `RI-CONTRACT-V146`  
Effective date: 2026-09-21  
Authority: Leah's standing Researcher Inventory calibration instruction

## 1. Mission

Given one source case and the researcher interest `lightweight researcher inventory only`, return a source-faithful candidate Researcher Inventory that preserves the distinct represented coordinates and the bounded relations needed to reconstruct what the source presents.

This is a representational inventory, not a word census, clause parse, proposition inventory, event extraction, summary, diagnosis, interpretation, or ontology-building exercise.

The target lies between two failures:

- **too sparse:** collapsing low-salience, unnamed, deictic, reported, remembered, prospective, questioned, recurrent, or present-telling distinctions that the source actually stages;
- **too dense:** turning every locally meaningful noun phrase, predicate, modifier, preposition, temporal/spatial phrase, report, question, state, or clause into an inventory row or compound.

V147 combines the two controls that the predecessor sequence separated too strongly: **structural frame/coordinate coverage first, then bounded relation-slot coverage inside that scaffold.** Relations may license relation-bound VERB/LABEL/LOCATOR slots, but relations do not get to manufacture a new inventory universe merely because they are semantically expressible.

## 2. Controlling V147 principle — scaffold first, bounded relations second

Build the source's **research scaffold** before admitting relation wording.

The scaffold consists of the bounded represented frames and the stable or distinctly staged coordinates that organize them:

- PERSON endpoints;
- source-treated OBJECT referents;
- PLACE positions/sites;
- TIME episodes/periods/phases/frames.

Then resolve only the **inventory-bearing relations** that materially connect, change, characterize, orient, contrast, or transition that scaffold. From those retained relations derive the exact source-native VERB, LABEL, and LOCATOR slots that are needed to preserve the represented difference.

A relation-bound slot does **not** need independent usefulness outside its host relation. But local sentence meaning alone is not enough to create an inventory row.

The decisive two-stage test is:

1. **Structural test:** does the source stage a distinct coordinate/frame/referent that must remain separately available for reconstruction?
2. **Contrastive relation test:** after the scaffold is retained, would omitting this relation/characterization/orientation erase a distinct source-presented connection, state, transition, contrast, posture, or trajectory at the inventory's research grain, rather than only remove local grammatical or propositional detail?

Retain the smallest source-native atom that passes the applicable test. Reject material that survives only because every clause can be semantically analyzed.

## 3. Mandatory whole-source construction order

Perform the following once for the complete source before returning any class.

### Pass A — map represented frames

Identify bounded represented frames in source order. Frames may be current, past, remembered, reported, recurrent, hypothetical, questioned, prospective, figurative, or present-telling/reflection frames.

A frame is retained when it materially groups, separates, nests, transitions, or contextualizes represented material. A clause, predicate, adjective, report, question, or action does not automatically create a frame.

### Pass B — build the structural coordinate scaffold

Before relation-word extraction, resolve:

- stable PERSON/social endpoints;
- source-treated OBJECT referents;
- distinct PLACE where-coordinates;
- distinct TIME when/frame-coordinates.

PLACE and TIME are semantic coordinates, not lexical phrase classes. A coordinate may be unnamed when the source distinctly stages the position or episode. Conversely, an explicit spatial or temporal expression is not automatically PLACE/TIME.

Freeze this scaffold provisionally.

### Pass C — build a scaffold-bounded relation ledger

Identify only relations that materially connect, change, characterize, orient, contrast, or transition the retained scaffold or retained frame boundaries.

A relation may involve action, state, perception, cognition, report, intention, possession, comparison, movement, waiting, question, negation, correction, attribution, evaluation, or another source-presented connection, but membership in one of those semantic categories is never sufficient by itself.

Do not create one ledger row per predicate, clause, report, question, or proposition. Do not preserve matrix/support/reporting/cognitive wording separately when it only scaffolds a more specific retained relation and contributes no distinct research-level relation of its own.

### Pass D — derive relation-bound primitive slots

From the retained relation ledger derive only the source-native slots that preserve its distinct represented structure:

- VERB relation kernels;
- LABEL characterization/state slots;
- LOCATOR orientation/context slots.

A brief, generic, copular, questioned, negative, colloquial, or one-use slot may survive when it is the necessary identity of a retained inventory-bearing relation. It is not disqualified merely for lacking standalone prominence.

### Pass E — structural recall, then census ceiling

Replay every retained frame and relation.

First restore a genuinely omitted structural coordinate, including unnamed PLACE/TIME, when the source stages a distinct position/episode/frame that reconstruction would otherwise collapse.

Then remove any row or relation admitted only because it was lexically vivid, grammatically parseable, semantically meaningful in one clause, or convenient for explaining another row.

A relation may not automatically create a new PLACE/TIME/OBJECT simply to complete its argument structure. Structural classes must satisfy their own scaffold rules.

### Pass F — reconcile identity and freeze primitives

Merge only true same-class coreference, aliases, or repetition of the same represented coordinate. Preserve broad/contained, earlier/later, remembered/current, source/reported, and other materially distinct grains.

Freeze primitive membership before compound emission.

### Pass G — emit compounds from the same bounded ledger

Emit only minimal multi-primitive connections from the retained scaffold-bounded relation ledger. Compounds reuse frozen primitives and never create, repair, merge, or retype them.

Not every retained relation becomes a compound.

## 4. Frame and coordinate coverage rule

Structural coverage is primary.

For each retained frame ask whether the source distinctly stages:

- a broad setting plus a contained/local position;
- a participant or referent at a position different from another staged position;
- a separate interaction, attempt, waiting phase, remembered/reported episode, recurrence span, prospective situation, transition, or present telling/reflection frame;
- a source-treated referent that remains a thing of tracking, selection, possession, comparison, evaluation, decision, questioning, pointing-back, or reuse.

Retain separate coordinates when collapsing them would merge source-staged positions, episodes, or referents. Do not require an explicit noun/date/location phrase for PLACE/TIME.

Do not create coordinates merely because every action logically happens somewhere or sometime.

## 5. Inventory-bearing relation test

A relation is retained only when it adds a distinct research-level connection to the scaffold.

Strong evidence includes one or more of:

- it changes or distinguishes the state/relationship of retained coordinates;
- it establishes a transition or boundary between retained frames;
- it creates a material contrast, correction, negation, question, uncertainty, attribution, intention, comparison, or prospective orientation that is itself part of what must be reconstructed;
- later source material refers back to, contrasts with, qualifies, depends upon, repeats, or resolves that relation;
- the exact source-native relation wording is necessary to distinguish it from another retained relation at the same scaffold grain.

Insufficient by itself:

- being a grammatical predicate;
- being an action/state/report/perception/cognition/question;
- changing one sentence's truth conditions;
- supplying discourse scaffolding, local support, or argument structure;
- being vivid, figurative, evaluative, or syntactically complex;
- being expressible as a proposition.

When two candidate relation rows describe the same scaffold connection, retain the smallest source-native relation identity rather than matrix/support wrappers, alternate parses, or redundant restatements.

## 6. PLACE — represented where-coordinates

PLACE inventories distinct physical/institutional settings and represented positions in the structural scaffold.

Retain PLACE when the source stages a separate where-coordinate needed to locate a retained frame, participant, referent, interaction, remembered/reported/prospective occurrence, or present telling.

Rules:

- naming is not required;
- an exact source cue may anchor an unnamed position with null `source_wording`;
- broad and contained positions may both survive when each organizes material distinctly;
- distinct participant/referent positions may survive even inside one broad setting when the source stages them separately;
- do not type figurative, conceptual, relational, or orientation wording as PLACE merely because it sounds spatial; type by represented function;
- do not create PLACE from every container, surface, body part, direction, movement phrase, or spatial preposition.

For unnamed PLACE use null `source_wording`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical tag/note only.

## 7. TIME — represented episode/frame coordinates

TIME inventories distinct episodes, periods, phases, attempts, waits, interactions, transitions, recurrence spans, remembered/reported/prospective frames, and present telling/reflection frames in the scaffold.

Retain TIME when it groups, separates, nests, orders, or contextualizes represented material as a distinct frame-coordinate.

Rules:

- no explicit date, clock, duration, tense marker, or temporal phrase is required;
- short interactions or attempts may be TIME when they organize a bounded phase;
- broad and contained frames may both survive when they organize different material;
- explicit temporal words, recurrence words, duration questions, or adverbs are not automatically TIME;
- do not turn every action, tense/aspect change, discourse transition, or temporal expression into a TIME row.

For unnamed TIME use null `source_wording`, an exact source cue, and neutral mechanical wording.

## 8. PERSON — stable represented actors/social endpoints

PERSON inventories `B` plus each distinct stable human/social actor or group after strict coreference when the actor/group occupies a material role in a retained frame or scaffold relation.

Participation may include acting, speaking, perceiving, being acted upon, possessing, receiving, benefiting, accompanying, being remembered/reported, or serving as another stable endpoint. Action or speech is not required.

Exclude rhetorical/generic addressees, unstable pronouns without a resolvable endpoint, hypothetical placeholders without stable represented standing, and true aliases/coreference duplicates.

Order non-speakers by first material represented participation rather than incidental mention alone.

## 9. OBJECT — source-treated referents, not argument/proposition shells

OBJECT inventories concrete or abstract things that the source itself treats as referents in the scaffold.

Positive evidence includes handling, possession, selection, positioning, comparison, evaluation, revisiting, deciding about, questioning as a thing, pointing back to, or a source-native nominal/deictic handle that remains a referent.

Reject:

- a noun phrase merely because it fills a relation slot;
- a proposition, clause, report, question, or relation converted into a noun-like shell without source reification;
- analyst-created summaries of what someone meant/wanted/said/did;
- a one-use argument whose identity is exhausted by its host relation and has no source-treated object standing;
- a setting duplicated as OBJECT when its only function is PLACE;
- unstable pronouns/demonstratives that resolve entirely to another retained coordinate.

A relation may have an OBJECT only when the source distinguishes a referent from the relation itself. Use the smallest source-native referential atom.

## 10. LABEL — bounded source-applied characterization/state

LABEL inventories source-applied states, qualities, statuses, identities, classifications, evaluations, comparisons, manners, polarities, corrections, rejections, intensifications, or characterization questions only when they materially distinguish a retained coordinate or inventory-bearing relation.

A LABEL may be relation-bound and one-use. It need not be reusable outside the relation.

Do not inventory every adjective, adverb, evaluative phrase, or local modifier. Retain the characterization only when removing it would collapse a research-level state/contrast/posture in the retained scaffold, not merely make the sentence less specific.

Preserve uncertainty, negation, rejection, correction, attribution, colloquial wording, and material intensity exactly.

## 11. VERB — bounded source-native relation kernels

VERB inventories the smallest exact contiguous source-native lexical kernel that identifies an inventory-bearing relation from Pass C.

A VERB may be brief, generic, copular, reporting, cognitive, negative, questioned, colloquial, or one-use when that exact wording is the necessary identity of the retained relation.

However:

- do not inventory every predicate;
- do not retain matrix/reporting/control/support wording separately when it merely introduces or supports another retained relation without adding a distinct scaffold-level relation;
- do not create repeated copula/support rows for parallel characterizations when the characterization itself carries the research distinction;
- do not split auxiliaries, infinitival markers, particles, or support material into rows unless they are part of the smallest contiguous relation identity;
- split coordinated or embedded wording only when the source stages genuinely distinct inventory-bearing relations;
- do not normalize to lemmas or analyst synonyms.

Include auxiliary/modal/negative material in the same kernel when removing it changes polarity, modality, aspect, posture, or relation identity.

## 12. LOCATOR — bounded source-native orientation/context slots

LOCATOR inventories exact source-native wording that materially orients a retained coordinate or inventory-bearing relation.

Eligible functions include relative position, containment, direction, path, origin/destination, entry/exit, approach/departure, proximity/distance, accompaniment/carrying, recurrence/situational context, embodied/internal orientation, source/target orientation, and materially organizing figurative/comparison orientation.

A LOCATOR may be relation-bound and one-use.

Reject bare/routine prepositions, possession alone, topic/recipient marking, local argument wrappers, or deictic words whose only role is grammar. A spatial-looking phrase is not PLACE when its source function is orientation; likewise LOCATOR does not replace a distinct PLACE/TIME coordinate.

## 13. Cross-class typing and multiplicity

Type by represented source function, not part of speech or spatial/temporal vocabulary.

The same exact source span may appear in multiple primitive classes only when it performs genuinely different retained functions. Do not create cross-class twins from alternate parses.

No class absorbs another function:

- OBJECT does not absorb LABEL;
- LABEL does not absorb VERB;
- PLACE/TIME do not absorb LOCATOR;
- VERB does not absorb participant/referent/site/frame;
- LOCATOR does not replace PLACE/TIME.

## 14. Literal, contiguity, and posture lock

Every source-derived non-null string must preserve source language character-for-character where the schema requires source text.

Never synonymize, lemmatize, repair spelling/grammar, normalize dialect, expand contractions, translate, polish, diagnose, standardize a category name, change number, or substitute a conventional expression.

Do not concatenate noncontiguous fragments or silently add omitted words. `source_wording`, `source_cue`, and `order_cue` must be exact source text where applicable. Only genuinely unnamed PLACE/TIME may use null `source_wording`.

Questions, uncertainty, hypotheticals, negations, corrections, remembered possibilities, reported material, and proposed future actions remain in source posture. Inventorying does not assert truth or realization.

## 15. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is mechanical. True means the source supplies material qualities/descriptions associated with that coordinate; false otherwise.

Do not create or split primitives to justify the boolean. `Q` is never a primitive.

## 16. Ordering

Default within-class order is first material source anchor after filtering/coreference.

Tie rules:

- broad/whole setting before dependent/contained setting when introduced together;
- whole before dependent part at the same anchor unless source order stages the part first;
- PERSON follows first material represented participation;
- remembered/reported/hypothetical/future frames remain at source position rather than external chronology.

The apparatus owns canonical IDs and deterministic numbering.

## 17. Structural-recall audit

Replay the source frame by frame before finalizing relation wording.

Ask:

1. Have any distinct positions/sites been collapsed because they were unnamed or low-salience?
2. Have any separate attempts, interactions, waits, remembered/reported/prospective periods, recurrence spans, transitions, or present-telling frames been collapsed because no explicit temporal noun/date names them?
3. Have minor stable actors/endpoints been dropped because they did not act or speak prominently?
4. Have source-treated referents been dropped merely because they are brief, deictic, abstract, one-use, questioned, or relation-bound?
5. Did an explicit spatial/temporal-looking phrase get typed as PLACE/TIME when it is actually an OBJECT/LABEL/LOCATOR or merely local wording?

Restore only scaffold coordinates supported by source staging. Do not harvest vocabulary.

## 18. Relation-ceiling audit

For every retained VERB/LABEL/LOCATOR and every relation-ledger item ask:

1. Which retained scaffold coordinates or frame boundary does this relation materially connect, change, characterize, orient, contrast, or transition?
2. What research-level distinction disappears if it is removed?
3. Would its removal lose only local sentence semantics, grammatical support, discourse flow, or an alternate parse?
4. Is a more specific embedded/source-native relation already carrying the actual distinction?
5. Is this matrix, support, reporting, control, copular, or cognitive wording merely scaffolding another retained relation?
6. Did lexical vividness or semantic analyzability create it instead of scaffold relevance?
7. Is its source-native span the smallest contiguous identity that preserves the retained relation/posture?

Remove the relation-like primitive when no scaffold-level distinction survives this audit.

## 19. Anti-reification and class audit

For every primitive ask:

- Is its identity source-established rather than analyst-completed?
- Does its class match represented function?
- For OBJECT, did I turn a clause/relation/argument into a thing without source reification?
- For PLACE/TIME, did I create a coordinate merely because every event needs where/when?
- For LABEL/VERB/LOCATOR, did local meaning or grammar alone create the row?
- Is it truly distinct from same-class coreference/repetition?

Never aim for a hidden count.

## 20. Compound construction

Freeze primitives before compound emission.

A compound represents one minimal source-staged, scaffold-bounded connection among two or more frozen primitives.

Create a compound only when:

1. the relation is inventory-bearing under Pass C;
2. preserving the connection materially improves reconstruction beyond isolated primitives;
3. the compound includes active primitive slots in that minimal connection and excludes inactive neighbors; and
4. it is not an alternate parse, pairwise/transitive closure, duplicate/subset alias, one-compound-per-clause artifact, or scene-wide mega-bundle.

Several clauses may contribute to one compound when they realize one retained connection. One clause may contain multiple compounds only when it stages multiple distinct inventory-bearing connections.

VERB/LABEL/LOCATOR participate when active in the retained relation. PLACE/TIME participate when they actually locate or organize it.

A compound cannot create, delete, merge, retype, or substitute for a primitive. Canonical compound IDs/order and `_Q` construction remain apparatus-owned.

## 21. Final contrast audit

### Too dense

Warning signs:

- every predicate/report/question becomes VERB;
- every characterization becomes LABEL;
- every spatial/temporal phrase becomes PLACE/TIME/LOCATOR;
- local arguments become OBJECTs;
- support/matrix/copular wording duplicates an embedded relation;
- compounds approximate one-per-clause.

Correct by returning to the frozen scaffold and relation-ceiling audit.

### Too sparse

Warning signs:

- unnamed site/frame coordinates disappear;
- broad and contained positions/episodes collapse;
- minor stable endpoints disappear;
- source-treated deictic/abstract referents disappear despite referential standing;
- necessary relation-bound VERB/LABEL/LOCATOR wording disappears merely because it is one-use or context-dependent;
- reported/prospective/present-telling material loses its distinct frame coordinates.

Correct by structural recall first, then relation-slot replay.

## 22. Final audit

Before return verify:

- **Frames first:** bounded represented frames were resolved before relation-word inventory.
- **Scaffold first:** PERSON/OBJECT/PLACE/TIME coordinates were resolved by source staging and referential standing, not by relation argument completion.
- **Bounded relations:** the relation ledger contains only scaffold-level research connections, not all semantically expressible predicates/propositions.
- **Relation-bound slots allowed:** legitimate one-use VERB/LABEL/LOCATOR atoms were not suppressed for lacking standalone prominence.
- **Structural recall:** unnamed/low-salience/deictic/reported/prospective/present-telling coordinates were not collapsed.
- **No proposition shells:** OBJECT does not reify clause meaning without source evidence.
- **No support duplication:** matrix/support/copular/reporting wording was not separately inventoried when another retained relation carries the distinction.
- **Typing:** every primitive is typed by represented function.
- **Literal/posture:** wording, dialect, polarity, question, uncertainty, negation, correction, and attribution are preserved.
- **Compounds:** only minimal scaffold-bounded retained connections are emitted from frozen primitives.
- **Candidate only:** no promotion, APA identity, database action, scoring, or substantive psychological interpretation occurred.

## 26. Holdout boundary

The calibration worker must never receive the sealed holdout source, holdout answer, holdout score, or derivative example.

No calibration correction may be made from sealed holdout output.
