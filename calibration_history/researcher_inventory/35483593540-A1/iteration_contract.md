## Mission

Read the complete source and recover a lightweight, source-native researcher inventory in exactly seven classes:

1. `PLACE`
2. `TIME`
3. `PERSON`
4. `OBJECT`
5. `LABEL`
6. `VERB`
7. `LOCATOR`

The target is **decomposed frame-complete lightweight resolution**: preserve every distinct source-native coordinate that performs a research function in represented source structure, including subtle, local, mundane, and one-use coordinates, while excluding lexical tokens, grammatical scaffolds, discourse wrappers, and propositions that do not constitute separate class-native research coordinates.

Do not perform APA scoring, protected-thread analysis, psychological interpretation, promotion, APA-ID creation, executive analysis, sovereign/admitted writing, or APA Data Fabric/database mutation.

## 1. Whole-source relation map first

Read the entire source before projecting any requested class. Silently map the represented structure, including where present:

- actions, interactions, attempts, responses, waits, movements, states, changes, possession, perception, reports, speech, cognition, questions, decisions, intentions, alternatives, memories, comparisons, corrections, recurrence, reflection, and prospective relations;
- actors and stable groups participating in those relations;
- concrete and abstract referential/content handles used by those relations;
- source-present characterizations, candidate labels, corrections, polarities, comparisons, and posture;
- explicit or neutral PLACE/TIME support that distinguishes episodes or phases;
- orienting relations that position coordinates or frames spatially, relationally, procedurally, mentally, temporally, or figuratively.

This map is semantic, not a token or part-of-speech inventory.

## 2. Relation decomposition before primitive admission

For each represented relation/frame, perform this silent decomposition in source order:

1. **RELATION KERNEL** — what source-present action, state, report, cognition, possession, movement, question, decision, comparison, or other relation is represented?
2. **ACTOR SLOTS** — which distinct persons/groups participate in or are materially related to that relation?
3. **REFERENTIAL/CONTENT SLOTS** — which concrete or abstract source handles are acted on, possessed, checked, selected, contrasted, remembered, reported, considered, exchanged, located, or otherwise used as distinct arguments/anchors?
4. **CHARACTERIZATION SLOTS** — which source-present qualities, states, identities, comparisons, corrections, polarity units, manner, or posture are distinctly predicated or represented?
5. **PLACE SUPPORT** — what explicit setting/local support, or legitimate neutral unnamed support, distinguishes the represented episode if place support is semantically present or needed to reconnect it?
6. **TIME SUPPORT** — what explicit period/phase, or legitimate neutral unnamed support, distinguishes the represented episode/phase if temporal support is semantically present or needed to reconnect it?
7. **ORIENTATION SLOTS** — which source-present locator/orienting relations position a coordinate or frame by containment, path, origin/destination, direction, proximity, accompaniment, recurrence, procedure, relation, mental/internal context, temporal position, or figurative/comparative orientation?

The decomposition is an internal work step. It does **not** mean every relation must produce a row in every class. A slot stays empty when the source supplies no distinct class-native coordinate for it.

Do not admit a clause or proposition merely because it expresses the relation. When its information separates cleanly into a relation kernel plus participants/content/support/characterization/orientation, inventory those class-native coordinates separately.

## 3. Positive admission routes retained

After decomposition, a candidate primitive must qualify through at least one source-present route:

### 3.1 `FRAME_ARGUMENT`
The trace is a distinct actor, referent/content handle, characterization, endpoint, support, or orientation inside a represented relation/frame.

### 3.2 `INDEPENDENT_REIFICATION`
The source treats the coordinate as a separately reconnectable handle through naming, identification, material possession, contrast, selection, questioning, correction, acceptance/rejection, memory/report, or another source-present reifying act.

### 3.3 `RELATION_KERNEL`
For `VERB`, the exact predicate expresses the represented relation itself. Semantically ordinary predicates remain eligible: copular, speech/reporting, possession, cognition, perception, movement, question, state, modal, decision, and comparison relations may qualify when they organize represented coordinates. Grammatical simplicity is not a reason to prune them.

### 3.4 `CHARACTERIZATION_KERNEL`
For `LABEL`, the source presents a distinct characterization/state/identity/comparison/candidate/correction/polarity/manner/posture as represented content.

### 3.5 `ORIENTATION_KERNEL`
For `LOCATOR`, the source presents a distinct orienting relation rather than merely containing a preposition, adverb, comparison, destination word, or deixis.

### 3.6 `SUPPORT_FRAME`
For `PLACE` and `TIME`, explicit or neutral implicit support distinguishes a represented episode/phase without inventing geography or chronology.

No route requires recurrence, narrative centrality, compound membership, or global indispensability.

## 4. Non-admission evidence

None of the following is sufficient by itself:

- lexical occurrence;
- noun/verb/adjective/adverb/preposition status;
- grammatical separability;
- vividness, concreteness, or colorfulness;
- repetition or one-use occurrence;
- spatial or temporal wording;
- describability;
- chronological position;
- possible analyst usefulness;
- presence inside a quotation, report, memory, or comparison;
- ability to fit one of the seven schema classes.

Low salience, one-use occurrence, mundane concreteness, local scope, reported status, remembered status, hypothetical status, prospective status, or figurative language are likewise **not** reasons to reject a coordinate that performs a qualifying role.

## 5. Class-slot coverage audit

After initial decomposition, walk the relation/frame map again in source order. For each represented relation/frame:

- verify its distinct actors are represented if PERSON-eligible;
- verify its distinct referential/content handles are represented if OBJECT-eligible;
- verify its relation kernel is represented if VERB-eligible;
- verify source-present characterizations are represented if LABEL-eligible;
- verify distinct PLACE/TIME support is represented when required to reconnect the episode/phase;
- verify source-present orientation is represented if LOCATOR-eligible.

This is a **coverage audit, not a quota**. Never create a row only because a slot exists. The question is whether the source already supplies a distinct class-native coordinate that the first pass missed.

Coverage is local. A coordinate does not need to be globally indispensable to qualify.

## 6. Atomic source-native span

Once a coordinate qualifies, choose the smallest exact source-native span that remains semantically complete for that class-native function.

Keep only what the coordinate itself requires, including where necessary:

- particles and required prepositions;
- reflexives;
- light-verb support;
- essential complements that are part of predicate identity rather than separable class-native arguments;
- negation;
- modality;
- uncertainty;
- comparison;
- attribution;
- idiomatic wording;
- posture-bearing wording.

Externalize independently qualifying actors, objects/content, labels, place/time support, and locators instead of bundling them into a clause-sized VERB or another proposition wrapper.

Expand a head fragment when the head alone would distort or underspecify the source-present coordinate. Shrink a sentence/clause wrapper when smaller class-native coordinates preserve the represented information.

## 7. Literal lock

For every explicit source-derived coordinate, preserve exact source language, including dialect, contractions, numbers, uncertainty, negation, modality, comparison, attribution, punctuation, and figurative posture.

Never normalize, clean, improve, translate, diagnose, euphemize, paraphrase, lemmatize into a different surface form, or substitute synonyms.

Neutral mechanical tags are reserved only for legitimate unnamed PLACE/TIME support where the schema permits null source wording; `source_cue` must anchor such support to exact source text.

Literal fidelity applies after semantic admission. It is not a word census.

## 8. Class assignment and tie-breaks

Assign by the source-present research function, not by physical denotation or grammar.

When one phrase could superficially resemble several classes, use these tie-breaks:

- a distinct human/social actor or stable group → `PERSON`;
- a distinct concrete or abstract referential/content handle → `OBJECT`;
- a source-present quality/state/identity/comparison/correction/posture represented as characterization → `LABEL`;
- an explicit or neutral episode/location support → `PLACE`;
- an explicit or neutral period/phase support → `TIME`;
- the represented relation predicate → `VERB`;
- a relation that positions/contextualizes another coordinate or frame → `LOCATOR`.

The same exact trace may project to more than one class only when each projection preserves genuinely different class-native information. Do not duplicate identical semantic work across classes.

### PLACE
Retain explicit settings and distinct local/contained supports plus legitimate unnamed support for distinct represented episodes where exact location is unstated. Broad and local supports may coexist only when they do different research work. Do not convert every physical noun, surface, movement, or spatial phrase into PLACE.

### TIME
Retain explicit periods and distinct semantic episode/phase supports. Merge expressions that support the same phase; separate genuinely different phases. Distinct remembered, reported, intended, prospective, recurring, and present-telling phases may have their own support when the source differentiates them. Do not inventory tense, every temporal token, duration, or connective.

### PERSON
Retain the speaker and every distinct represented human/social actor or stable group after true coreference when the actor participates, possesses materially, reports/is reported, is remembered, is prospectively involved, or otherwise functions in represented source structure. One-use/offscreen actors remain eligible. Suppress true aliases and rhetorical/nonreferential addressees.

### OBJECT
Retain concrete and abstract referential/content handles that act as distinct arguments or anchors in represented relations. Mundane, local, one-use, and scene-bound things remain eligible when the relation actually uses them as handles. Abstract choices, decisions, relations, conditions, amounts, alternatives, and reported/remembered content may also qualify when reified by the source. Reject noun census, generic pronouns/deixis, discourse organizers, and proposition wrappers without separate referential identity.

### LABEL
Retain distinct source-present characterization kernels at the smallest complete literal span. Keep candidate labels, corrections, polarity, comparisons, idiomatic posture, and meaningful manner when represented as characterization. Reject modifier census and colorful wording whose only role is style.

### VERB
Retain each distinct represented relation kernel at the smallest complete predicate span. A simple copula, report/speech predicate, possession relation, cognition/perception predicate, movement, question, state, comparison, or decision can be a real relation kernel. Normally omit the subject and externalize independently qualifying arguments/support/characterizations/orientations. Reject auxiliary-only rows, discourse carriers whose only function is narration, and clause-sized propositions when separable coordinates carry the content.

### LOCATOR
Retain distinct source-present orienting constructions at the smallest complete span. Orientation may be spatial, contained, path/route, origin/destination, directional, proximal, accompaniment, recurring, procedural/relational, mental/internal, temporally positional, or figurative/comparative. Do not inventory every preposition, adverb, recipient, topic phrase, duration, deixis, or comparison.

## 9. Semantic identity and deduplication

Primitive identity follows represented semantic identity, not mention count.

Merge true aliases, coreference, restatements, inflection-only repeats, and duplicate head/full-span variants representing one stable coordinate. Preserve distinct local occurrences/support when the source represents materially different relation/frame roles.

Do not create analyst paraphrases beside literal source wording.

## 10. Primitive freeze audits

Before compounds, repeat these audits until stable:

1. **Relation decomposition audit** — every represented relation/frame was decomposed before admission decisions.
2. **Class-slot coverage audit** — no qualifying actor/referent/kernel/characterization/support/orientation was omitted merely because it was subtle, ordinary, local, one-use, reported, remembered, prospective, concrete, or abstract.
3. **Positive-route audit** — every proposed primitive has a source-present positive route; lexical/grammatical presence alone is insufficient.
4. **Anti-proposition audit** — remove clause/proposition/discourse wrappers when separable class-native coordinates carry the information.
5. **Anti-tokenization audit** — remove noun/verb/modifier/spatial/temporal/preposition census rows.
6. **Atomic-span audit** — fix too-short fragments and too-long bundled spans.
7. **Wrong-class audit** — reassign by function rather than duplicating the same semantic work.
8. **PLACE/TIME support audit** — restore missing distinct episode/phase supports and merge redundant supports.
9. **Literal-lock audit** — remove normalization, synonyms, invented wording, analyst inference, and paraphrase.
10. **Semantic dedup audit** — collapse true duplicates while preserving genuinely different local roles.

Freeze primitives only after all ten audits are stable.

## 11. `qualities_available`

`qualities_available` is a mechanical boolean only. It is true when source-present qualitative/descriptive material is available around a coordinate or compound under the schema. It is not confidence, importance, admission authority, or a demand to create a LABEL.

## 12. Selective compound construction

Only after all seven primitive classes are frozen, construct lightweight multi-coordinate compounds.

Create a compound when two or more frozen coordinates jointly express one distinct source-local relation/event/frame useful to reconnect as a bundle. Use the smallest set of frozen refs needed to identify that binding and preserve source-local semantic order/posture.

A primitive may remain unbundled.

Do not create graph closure, pairwise closure, one compound per primitive, one compound per clause, singleton-equivalent bundles, subset/superset permutations, redundant restatements, or scene mega-bundles. Compounds never justify missing, extra, or wrong-class primitives.

If compounds fail because primitive coordinates are wrong, fix primitive semantics prospectively; do not alter evaluator mechanics merely to accommodate the output.

## 13. Completion standard

The target is **relation-decomposed archetypal lightweight resolution**:

- complete-source reading precedes projection;
- every represented relation/frame is decomposed before admission decisions;
- qualifying atomic class-native roles are covered without quotas or fabrication;
- low-salience, one-use, mundane, reported, remembered, prospective, concrete, and abstract coordinates remain eligible when they perform a positive role;
- lexical/POS/grammatical census behavior is excluded;
- proposition/discourse wrappers are decomposed into their qualifying coordinates;
- simple relation kernels are not pruned merely for being ordinary;
- each explicit row uses exact source wording at the smallest complete class-native span;
- class assignment follows source function;
- PLACE/TIME distinguish support frames without token proliferation;
- semantic duplicates and wrong-class projections are removed;
- compounds reconstruct only a selective canonical set from frozen primitives.

Do not target hidden counts or infer hidden gold.
