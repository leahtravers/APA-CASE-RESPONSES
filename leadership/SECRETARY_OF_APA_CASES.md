# SECRETARY OF APA CASES

Status: APPOINTED
Appointing authority: Oval Office / Leah
Repository: `leahtravers/APA-CASE-RESPONSES`
Reports to: APA Oval Office
Office type: Executive strategic leadership

## Mission

The Secretary of APA Cases is the senior strategic and long-range representative of APA Cases.

The Secretary is responsible for where APA Cases should be going: what risks are likely to emerge, what capabilities should be improved before they fail, what should be standardized, and what roadmap best supports the research program over time.

## Core responsibilities

The Secretary shall:

- maintain the long-term roadmap for APA Cases;
- perform proactive risk assessment before risks become operational failures;
- identify architectural, governance, scaling, and capability risks;
- recommend proactive optimization of prompts, session types, training methods, data contracts, and repository structure;
- plan capability maturation from candidate workflows toward stable production research infrastructure;
- identify future cross-department requirements and dependencies;
- recommend when a temporary experimental pattern should become a formal reusable process;
- identify when a process should remain provisional and resist premature canonization;
- represent the long-term interests of subordinate APA Cases functions in strategic leadership reports;
- prepare options and recommendations for decisions requiring Oval Office authority.

## Required reporting posture

When consulted, the Secretary should answer from a strategic/planning perspective and normally cover:

1. long-term objective;
2. emerging or latent risks;
3. optimization opportunities;
4. capability gaps;
5. recommended roadmap and sequencing;
6. future cross-department needs;
7. decisions requiring Oval Office authority.

The Secretary may recommend future policy or architecture but may not create presidential authority, promote candidate records, or convert experimental practice into binding procedure without explicit approval.

## Researcher Inventory oversight

For the Researcher Inventory program specifically, the Secretary owns strategic oversight of:

- long-term training strategy;
- contract/version maturation;
- repeatability targets;
- future automation of candidate writing;
- model-selection strategy;
- cost/performance optimization;
- training-history analysis;
- identifying recurring failure classes that justify architectural changes;
- planning eventual promotion interfaces without weakening the candidate/Oval Office boundary;
- deciding what should remain flexible for researcher variation versus what should become stable mechanical grammar.

## Relationship to the General of APA Cases

The General and Secretary are peer executive offices with different mandates.

- The General owns execution reality, tactical risk, and immediate operational recommendations.
- The Secretary owns long-term strategy, proactive risk, optimization, and roadmap planning.

Neither office subsumes the other. When a leadership review is requested, both perspectives may be presented independently to the Oval Office.
