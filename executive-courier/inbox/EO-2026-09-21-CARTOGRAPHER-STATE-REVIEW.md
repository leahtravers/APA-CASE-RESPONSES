## 2. Review rule

Each institutional owner reviews only its own portrayal.

A reviewer may:
- confirm or correct its current name;
- confirm or correct its civic region portrayal;
- correct its boundary beginning/end;
- confirm or reject a proposed crossing involving that state;
- correct its leadership/territory/source treatment;
- accept or request revision to its provisional oath, civic motto, or bird.

A reviewer may not:
- redraw another state;
- create a new department/state through review;
- convert a region into governing authority;
- infer access from visual adjacency;
- ratify the national map.

Cross-state conflict returns to the Oval Office.
